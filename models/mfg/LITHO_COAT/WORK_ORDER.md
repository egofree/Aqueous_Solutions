# Work order LITHO_COAT

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-SC, route hold). Route hold: the `coater` class is not named in any section 4 route row — A-core and the tier-7 A-SC set apply, and the route hold is carried by name. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/LITHO_COAT.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/LITHO_COAT.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-MOD-002 (frame system), AQS-BOM-SC-001 (self-copy BOM), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node LITHO_COAT (design block and note; A-M-287; AQS-LITH-001 rev C chemistry).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `litho_coat.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1200 × 800 × 1400 ("L x W x H mm, issued envelope (A-M-287)"). The build fills it: measured 1200.0 × 800.0 × 1400.0 mm, one closed manifold mesh. The duty section 2 prose still carries the seed figure "Envelope 600 × 500 × 800 mm" — the node governs and the older figure is named (holds, W12; the WS_SOLV precedent). A four-post guard frame on the base plate pins the registry box so the X-PAR-04 envelope registry reads the build.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn per the node design block and its note ("Spin-coater line: spin bowl, vacuum chuck, softbake hotplate"): spin bowl and coat catch bowl in 316L (duty section 3), the 150 mm vacuum chuck (node chuck_mm) on the spindle inside the bowl, and the spin-chuck motor (0.75 kW peak) and softbake hotplate (0.3 kW at the 100 °C hold) drawn as their purchased envelopes per plan rule 6 — ratings from the node, no invented internals.
W4 Nozzles. All 4 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/LITHO_COAT.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric tube stubs flush at the envelope faces: N1 DN15 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N2 DN15 vent flush at the envelope top — its 800 datum predates the 1400 box and is carried as a placeholder (hold, W12); N3 DN15 (the `solids` row: spent resist and solvent in) on −Y at z 160 and N4 DN15 (the `solids` row: resist/solvent carry to the develop bench) on +Y at z 600 — non-piped transfer rows drawn DN15, bore not calculated (plan W4). Atmospheric hose per duty section 4 ("DN is copied from the edge and is not recalculated"); no flange hardware is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M10 wedge anchors (base plate to slab, 2 per frame post line) and 4 M8 bowl-stand bolts (stand to base, one per corner), counts from the model. 8.8 on carbon steel, A4-70 on the 316L assembly, per the grade rule; lengths and patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Spin bowl, catch bowl, chuck and connection hardware 316L per duty section 3 ("316L where the stream is wet" — the resist/solvent stream is the wet service; CA 0 mm, AQS-STD-001 rule). Base plate, guard frame, stand, motor and hotplate envelopes carbon steel A500 (AQS-MOD-002 dry service, dry structure outside the wetted station).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6). The spin bowl spins at 5000 rpm (node design) unshrouded by a pressure boundary — the guard frame is the machine guard; no pressure test is claimed. The vacuum line to the chuck is not designed: no vacuum source is named on the node or duty (hold, W12).
W8 Exports. `export/litho_coat.step` (assembly); `export/litho_coat.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/LITHO_COAT.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1200.0 × 800.0 × 1400.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. Masses in `check.json`: base 150.7 kg, frame 116.6 kg, spin bowl 31.3 kg, catch bowl 20.4 kg, stand 45.2 kg, chuck 5.1 kg, motor envelope 45.2 kg, hotplate envelope 29.4 kg, hardware 0.2 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make rows (shop-fabricable, flagged for AQS-BOM-SC-001): base plate, guard frame, spin bowl, catch bowl, stand, chuck, connection hardware. Buy rows (purchased per plan rule 6, ratings from the node): spin-chuck motor 0.75 kW peak (node spin_peak_kW; A-M-287 basis), softbake hotplate 0.3 kW at 100 °C (node softbake_kW/softbake_C).
W10 Fabrication route. Machine practice applied per the lane (the class carries a section 4 route hold): 316L sheet rolled and welded for the bowl and catch tray (the metal shop, GTAW per the 316L practice), bowl balanced against the vendor spin-chuck print at fit-out, frame weldment and guards, anchors drilled and set, purchased drive and hotplate mounted by rating. On-site tool: the metal shop.
W11 Self-copy relevance. A-SC applies (tier 7): the coater is a lithography line tool for the plant's own wafers (AQS-BOM-SC-001 names LITHO-COAT-01), and the parts of the unit the on-site shop can make — base, guard frame, bowls, stand, chuck and hardware — are flagged make in the BOM and cross-referenced to AQS-BOM-SC-001. The spin-chuck motor and softbake hotplate are purchased.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The duty section 2 prose carries the seed figure 600 × 500 × 800; the node 1200 × 800 × 1400 governs and the older figure is named — nothing resized (plan rule 3). (2) The bowl (OD 360 × 200, wall 10), catch tray (OD 480 × 60) and hotplate (250 × 250 × 60) envelopes are the layout choice around the node figures (chuck_mm 150, softbake_kW 0.3); no bowl or tray size is stated by an authority. (3) The N2 vent datum 800 predates the 1400 box — carried as a placeholder, flush at the top. (4) The vacuum source for the chuck is not named on the node or duty: none is drawn (the WS_SOLV no-named-purchase precedent). (5) The N3/N4 solids rows are non-piped transfers drawn DN15, bore not calculated (plan W4). (6) The route hold itself: no section 4 class row names the `coater` class — carried by name; A-core and A-SC pass on the files. (7) Fastener lengths and the bowl balance check are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

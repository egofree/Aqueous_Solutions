## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `sinter.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` and rebuilds every solid: the water-cooled double-wall jacket, charge door, ceramic-fibre lining, alumina muffle set, graphite element racks, controls cabinet, and the schedule hardware. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1000 × 1000 × 1500 (A-M-199 melt class N+1 rated envelope, A-M-282; AQS-DUTY-072 study). The build fills it: measured 1000.0 × 1000.0 × 1500.0 mm from the export STL - the plinth and the jacket pin the box so the X-PAR-04 envelope registry reads the build; the Oct-31 envelope-only placeholder box is replaced by the real machine.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn per the node material row and duty section 3 (ASM_V7 reactive-powder class): water-cooled nickel-free steel jacket (double wall t4 with the 15 mm cooling-water annulus - the water circuit is loop hardware, not modeled, hold W12), ceramic-fibre board between jacket and hot zone (study construction), 95 % alumina muffle OD 260 × 480 with lid on the alumina hearth (the inert/vacuum process chamber; buy consumable), and graphite resistance elements OD 24 in wall and crown racks at the 1300 C design (buy consumable). The 0.5 kg design-case powder charge (node design design_case_kg_d) is drawn as a process-inventory solid, not a BOM part. The front charge door is furnace furniture on the -X face.
W4 Nozzles. All 3 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/SINTER.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore, flush at the envelope faces: N1 sintered stock outlet (solids row) drawn as an outlet chute on +X at z 600, bore not calculated per W4; N2 DN15 drain through the plinth flush at the bottom (50 datum); N3 DN15 vent riser on the jacket roof flush at the envelope top - the 800 datum is the schedule placeholder (hold, W12). Atmospheric joints; no flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06 - hold, W12).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M12 wedge anchors (plinth to slab, 4 per rail, count from the model) and 12 M10 studs (door face hinge and muffle lid clamp). 8.8 on carbon steel per the grade rule (AQS-PLAN-006 W5); lengths, torque and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3 (X-MAT-22): nickel-free hot zone on a water-cooled steel jacket (Ti embrittles in air/N2, CRC_TI); 95 % alumina muffle and hearth (ASM_V7 reactive-powder class; buy consumables); graphite resistance elements at 1300 C (duty section 3 element class; Mo elements are the duty alternative for an air variant, not drawn); ceramic-fibre board between jacket and hot zone (study). CA 0 mm - dry furnace service, no wetted stream (AQS-STD-001 shop rule; dry hot gas above any dew point, M7).
W7 Pressure. The muffle runs under inert/vacuum atmosphere (duty section 3); no pressure is claimed - the vacuum/inert package purchases at fabrication (hold, W12) and the duty section 6 relief case is pending (unit task 07). No instrument loop is issued (unit task 08).
W8 Exports. `export/sinter.step` (assembly, 14 solids); `export/sinter.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/SINTER.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 registry) - the Oct-31 envelope-only placeholder box (~28 facets) is replaced by the real machine. Bounding box 1000.0 × 1000.0 × 1500.0 mm against the node envelope: filled exactly, nothing resized. Masses in `check.json`: plinth 471.0 kg, jacket walls + door 356.3 kg, lining 40.1 kg, muffle set + hearth 56.5 kg, elements 4.8 kg, cabinet 174.9 kg, nozzles 5.6 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make rows: plinth, jacket walls and door, fibre lining, nozzle set. Buy rows (purchased per plan rule 6, ratings from the node): 95 % alumina muffle set and hearth (consumable, change-out per campaign), graphite element set 8 kW connected at T_C 1300 (consumable), controls cabinet 8 kW connected (envelope; vacuum/inert atmosphere package at fabrication). The standby state is named on the jacket and cabinet rows (A-M-282).
W10 Fabrication route. Route hold: the `furnace` class has no section 4 class (W12). The operations follow the RES_HEAT/COMB_HEAT furnace practice as the study route (named, not claimed): plate cut and fold (jacket double walls, door), CS weld (jacket seams, leak-tight water annulus), fibre board install with the door throat, muffle and hearth set, element racks hung with leads out to the cabinet bay, chute and stub set-in, then the tests: jacket annulus leak check, muffle seal check under inert purge, element-resistance survey, and a trial sinter at the 0.5 kg design case. On-site tool: the metal shop welding bay with the refractory bench.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the furnace consolidates the mill's own powder stock, it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.

# Work order POLY_COND

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-VES). Route class F-VES. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/POLY_COND.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/POLY_COND.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node POLY_COND (design block, AQS-DUTY-073).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `poly_cond.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` and, for the kettle construction figures, the node design block of the graph; it rebuilds every solid. The `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks of `params.json` are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 600 × 600 × 1200 (the AQS-DUTY-073 reactor-class study minimum; the node envelope state is "standby"). The build fills it: measured 600.0 × 600.0 × 1200.0 mm; the vent riser tops the box (the X-PAR-04 registry gate).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The node design agitator row (agitator_kW 0.18) is drawn as solids - shaft and two-blade impeller - into the purchased drive envelope (plan rule 6); the impeller diameter and immersion are study values (the node carries no agitator geometry - hold, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 3 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/POLY_COND.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube connections (duty section 4: hose, atmospheric; the connecting line makes the joint at the face - the CATCH_TANK idiom): N1 digester organics in on −Y at its 160 datum (face hardware); N2 drain boss under the base head, flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N3 vent riser on the top head, flushing at the envelope top (the 800 datum is the schedule placeholder - hold, W12). No flange is drawn (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. The along-face position is the registered layout choice (per tag in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M12 wedge anchors, grade 8.8, plinth to slab, one per corner. Welded joints carry no fastener row. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Kettle and agitator 316L per duty section 3 ("316L, Hastelloy option"); the Hastelloy alternative is a named hold (W12). CA 0 mm (AQS-STD-001 rule). Shell wall 6 mm is the study fab-page value - the node books no thickness (hold, W12). Jacket envelope and stand carbon steel (the jacket is a thermal envelope; the dry stand sees no aqueous chloride).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric (duty section 6: relief case study pending, unit task 07; no set point claimed, not to the room). No test pressure is claimed; the leak test is a shop discretion of the route (W10). No instrument loop yet (unit task 08) - named on the duty sheet.
W8 Exports. `export/poly_cond.step` (assembly); `export/poly_cond.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/POLY_COND.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 600.0 × 600.0 × 1200.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. The skid chain fuses to one solid; the inlet face stub stays separate until the connecting line makes its joint (`check.json` `solids_note`). A-VES: the gross shell volume (9.4 L) is reported for the record; the duty claims no holdup and no residence time is derived.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: kettle, jacket envelope, agitator, stand, connections. Buy: the 0.18 kW drive (envelope and interface per plan rule 6).
W10 Fabrication route. F-VES: plate cut, roll, GTAW per ASME IX practice, nozzle set-in, jacket closure, leak test; stand weldment and anchors. On-site tool: the metal shop.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the kettle would make condensation resin; it makes no plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) Installed standby: the node envelope state is standby and the DIGEST_TO_POLY edge stays 0 until the digester organic assay issues the recipe (AQS-DES-POLY); the model covers the installed geometry the node issues and claims no production. (2) Shell wall 6 mm is the study fab-page value; the node books no thickness. (3) The Hastelloy option in duty section 3 is unresolved; 316L drawn. (4) The jacket envelope geometry (OD 234 / ID 212 annulus) is the layout choice; the node carries only jacket_C 160. (5) The agitator geometry (impeller diameter, immersion, rpm) is study; the node carries only agitator_kW 0.18. (6) The N1 inlet datum 160 is a schedule placeholder (duty section 4 boilerplate: elevations pending vessel design, unit tasks 02/06); the face hardware stops at the envelope face. (7) The vent riser flushes at the envelope top; the 800 datum is the schedule placeholder (designed elevations are unit tasks 02/06). (8) The duty claims no holdup: the gross shell volume is reported for the record, no residence time derived (A-VES). (9) Relief case (unit task 07) and instrument loop (unit task 08) are open on the duty sheet; nothing invented here. (10) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-VES). Route class F-VES. Graph revision per `params.json` `graph_revision`. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/MEOH_COND.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/MEOH_COND.csv`, the node design block (flow_graph/param/meoh_train.py, AQS-DUTY-077: D 400, H 600, drum 71.19 L, coil 1 × 10 mm × 1 m, area 0.0301 m², condensing 0.7079 kW at U 1.2, LMTD 19.6 K), AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node MEOH_COND.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `meoh_cond.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 400 × 400 × 600 - SIZED, not a registry box (the `size_note` records the methanol-train residual sizing, AQS-DUTY-077; the node design block books D_mm 400, H_mm 600, drum_L 71.19 for the "knock-out pot + condenser on the same skid"). The build fills the node box: measured 400.0 × 400.0 × 600.0 mm, one closed manifold solid; the shell is tangent to all four faces (node D 400).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The node design block books the coil (a coil is hardware: both ends stub through the walls): drawn as a 316L OD 10 helix, ~2.6 turns at a 120 mm mean diameter, 1.000 m developed, z 80..185 in the lower drum. The drawn coil area 0.0314 m² is 0.0013 m² over the node book figure 0.0301 m² (the node area is a nominal book figure; the drawn coil is the "1 x 10 mm x 1 m coil" hardware) - reported, nothing resized (W12). The knock-out function is the drum itself; no weir or pad is booked for this unit anywhere. Coil mean diameter and pitch are study values against the node's tube-and-length booking (hold, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 5 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/MEOH_COND.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore: N1/N5 condensate sources in on −Y at z 160, N2 condensate out on +Y at z 600 clamped to 584.4 by the barb-radius rule (hold, W12), N3 drain boss on the base head to its 50 datum, N4 vent flush at the top head - its 800 datum predates the 600 box (hold, W12). Atmospheric joints are hose barbs (duty section 4); no flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. Per-tag positions in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M10 wedge anchors, plinth to slab, one per corner (count from the model). ISO 3506 A4-70 by the plan grade rule on 316L. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication. The welded joints carry no fastener row.
W6 Materials. 316L shell and coil (graph node material; duty section 3: methanol/water traces). Corrosion allowance 0 mm (AQS-STD-001). Shell wall 3 mm is a study value; no authority states a wall (hold, W12).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6). Not to the room. Level and temperature are the duty section 5 instruments (high level trips the inlet; loop hardware, not modeled). No hydrotest pressure is claimed; the leak test of shell side and coil side separately is a shop discretion of the route (W10).
W8 Exports. `export/meoh_cond.step` (assembly); `export/meoh_cond.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/MEOH_COND.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 400.0 × 400.0 × 600.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. Liquor holdup at the working level net of the coil reported in `check.json` against the node drum_L figure.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make: drum, coil and hardware are in-house fabrication.
W10 Fabrication route. F-VES: 316L plate/pipe cut (saw or laser), roll the shell, gas-tungsten-arc weld the seam, heads and set-in ports, coil formed from OD 10 tube and welded to its stubs, tube-sheet-free drum leak tested, shell side and coil side tested separately. On-site tool: the MFG shop plate work and weld shop (AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 F-VES operations: plate or sheet cut, roll, weld, nozzle set-in, hydro or leak test).
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the drum condenses and collects methanol condensate; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) Shell wall 3 mm is a study value; no authority states a wall. (2) The drawn coil area 0.0314 m² is 0.0013 m² over the node book 0.0301 m²; the difference is reported, nothing resized. (3) The coil mean diameter, pitch and the 2.5-turn layout are study values against the node's tube-and-length booking. (4) The N2 outlet datum 600 clamps to 584.4 by the barb-radius rule. (5) The N4 vent datum 800 predates the 600 box; the vent is drawn flush at the top head. (6) The model liquor volume at the working level (0.0584 m³) sits under the node drum_L 71.19 L nominal figure - reported (A-VES), nothing resized. (7) Schedule rating column (Class 150) against the atmospheric hose duty: no flange is drawn. (8) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

# Work order WS_PEROX

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-MACH). Route class F-MACH. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/WS_PEROX.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/WS_PEROX.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-STD-002 rules M3/M6, AQS-DSN-OX-001 section 2, `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node WS_PEROX.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `ws_perox.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 600 × 600 × 350, set by `docs-authority/_storage_sizing.py` (AQS-DUTY-064). The build fills it: measured 600.0 × 600.0 × 350.0 mm, one closed manifold solid. The duty section 4 prose block still carries the seed line "Size 800 × 800 × 1100 mm" - the node governs and the older figure is named (holds, W12).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid; no study page issues internals for this station and none are drawn (hold, W12). The purchased items the duty names are drawn as their envelopes and interfaces (plan rule 6): the PVDF pump (duty section 3; 120 × 90 × 110 in the guarded floor zone) and the electrolysis cell (duty section 8 / AQS-DSN-OX-001 2.1: 12.31 A, 246 cm2, 30.8 W, anode held; 180 × 120 × 160 at the front-left). Their internals stay with the maker/design authority (holds, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 6 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/WS_PEROX.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube connections on tray risers, flush at the envelope faces: N1 DN15 oxygen to peroxide, N5 DN15 dilution water and N6 DN15 peroxide dilution water in on −Y at z 160; N2 DN15 H2O2 out on +Y (schedule datum 600 predates the box, clamped to 320.7 - hold, W12); N3 DN15 drain through the tray floor (50 datum); N4 DN15 vent riser on the tank head (its 800 datum predates the box - hold, W12) (duty section 4: atmospheric joints are hose; hose makeup by the connecting line at the face). No flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. Duty section 6: continuous filtered vent and a free-lifting manway cover as the emergency relief for decomposition; ball and plug valves have vented cavities, no trapped volume; not to a closed header (the manway is noted, not drawn - hold, W12). The along-face positions are the registered layout choice (per tag in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M10 wedge anchors, tray to slab, one per corner (count from the model). A4-70 hardware by the polymer-joint supplier rule. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication. The welded joints carry no fastener row.
W6 Materials. HDPE tank (cross-linked, unpigmented, UV stabilized), PVDF piping, pump and valves, PTFE or FKM seals (duty section 3); no metal wetted part (M3); the cell electrodes are under M6; 5 wt% nominal, 6 wt% maximum, no stabilizer (graph product_spec); AQS-DSN-OX-001 section 2. CA 0 mm on the polymers (AQS-STD-001).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric with continuous filtered vent and the free-lifting manway cover as emergency relief (duty section 6). Not to a closed header. Instruments: level high-high trip on the cell, temperature high trip 30 °C closing the cell feed and draining to the bund, concentration alarm above 6 wt%, vent flow or tank pressure alarm (duty section 5; loop hardware, not modeled). No hydrotest pressure is claimed; the leak test is a shop discretion of the route (W10).
W8 Exports. `export/ws_perox.step` (assembly); `export/ws_perox.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/WS_PEROX.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 600.0 × 600.0 × 350.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. The working levels at the duty section 2 X-SAF-09 basis are reported in `check.json`: 5 L tank nominal (level z 236.5) and 2.9 L net at the 1-day holdup (level z 154.0).
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Buy: the PVDF pump envelope (named with the duty section 3 class; drive rating per the maker TDS at order). Make: tray, tank, guard rail, connections and the cell envelope (the cell duty is designed in-house per AQS-DSN-OX-001 2.1).
W10 Fabrication route. F-MACH: frame weldment (tray, guard rail), purchased items mounted by rating, anchors drilled, leak test of the tank. On-site tool: the thermoplastic shop and the welding bay (AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 F-MACH operations: frame weldment, guards, purchased drive or electrical by rating, anchors). Duty section 8 open items carried: the anode is held; the IA-H2O2-SRC water source edge is an owner decision (3.0 kg/d against 0.176 kg/d O2 needed).
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the station dispenses reagent; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The envelope is the storage-sizing box; the duty section 4 prose seed line 800 × 800 × 1100 is an older figure - the node governs, nothing resized. (2) Tank wall 10 mm is the study fab-page value; no thickness is booked on the node. (3) No internals: the node row is none and no study page issues internals for this station. (4) The tank diameter, axis position and the guard/floor-zone layout are registered layout choices within the box. (5) The duty section 4 prose own-bund figures are room-side containment (the placement seeds seat the stations in segregated bunded rooms); the skid tray is the drip tray. (6) The 600 outlet datums and the 800 vent datum predate the 350 box; clamped as W4 records. (7) The tank volume is the duty section 2 X-SAF-09 basis (1 d at 2.94 L/d = 2.9 L net; tank 5 L nominal); unstabilized peroxide decomposes - product_spec holdup_max_d 1.0. (8) The 6 L own bund (>= 110 %) is the room-side containment the station seats in; the skid tray is the drip tray. (9) The PVDF pump drive rating and the cell maker classes follow the TDS at order; the manway cover is fabrication detail. (10) The IA-H2O2-SRC water source edge is an owner decision (duty section 8).(7) Schedule rating column (Class 150) against the atmospheric hose duty: no flange is drawn.

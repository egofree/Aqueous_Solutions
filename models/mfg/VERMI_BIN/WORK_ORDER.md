Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-FARM). Route class F-FARM (the `worm_bin` class has no section 4 row - the F-FARM practice is the named study route; hold, W12). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/VERMI_BIN.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/VERMI_BIN.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), A-M-425 (2 m2 bin basis), AQS-STD-002 (polymer selection), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node VERMI_BIN.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `vermi_bin.py` (CadQuery, on the shared close-out builder `models/mfg/farm_closeout.py`) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 2100 × 1100 × 600 - "2 m2 bin at 300 mm bedding depth (A-M-425)". The rebuild fills the box exactly: measured 2100.0 × 1100.0 × 600.0 mm, flush on all six faces (A-core 2). A-FARM governs: the bin is drawn at the node box, NOT shrunk to fit; area reading: the interior 2080 × 1080 = 2.25 m2 against the node design bin_m2 2.0 - the registry box governs (hold, W12).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid row - the duty section 3 build-up is drawn: the SS316 harvest screen as a false-floor mesh plate 2 mm over the bin floor (the worm tea drains below it to the N4 harvest row; laps 1 mm into the walls per the manifold idiom), and the bedding envelope at the 300 mm depth the size_basis names (coco-coir and shredded-paper, held 70-80 % moisture near neutral pH; the charge is a buy row - no bedding density row exists in the material table, mass carries 0.0; hold, W12). The PP working fork is a hand tool, not a bin part: not drawn.
W4 Nozzles. All 6 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/VERMI_BIN.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore DN15 tube stubs flush at the envelope faces: inlets N1 (quail droppings), N2 (soft canopy residue) on −Y at z 160; outlets N3 (vermicompost to the growbed soil), N4 (worm tea to the growbed soil) at the schedule z 600 - the rim height, drawn with their tops flush at the envelope top (hold W12); N5 drain flush at the envelope bottom; N6 vent flush at the envelope top - the schedule z 800 datum predates the 600 box (stale datum, hold W12). Atmospheric joints are tube/hose; no flange is drawn (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M10 base anchors (8.8 carbon steel per the grade rule) and 8 M6 screen clamps (polymer supplier rule, A4-70). Patterns are the model count / study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: HDPE bin with coco-coir and shredded-paper bedding, PP working fork, SS316 harvest screen. The bin is aerobic through diffusion (node design). No metal wetted part except the SS316 screen above the false floor; corrosion allowance does not apply (AQS-STD-001 rule).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric; the bin respires through the bedding surface and the vent. Duty section 6 states the relief case is pending and no set point is claimed. Instruments VERMI_BINPT-01/TE-01 are the duty section 5 loops (loop hardware, not modeled). No test pressure is claimed.
W8 Exports. `export/vermi_bin.step` (assembly, 2 solids); `export/vermi_bin.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/VERMI_BIN.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 2100.0 × 1100.0 × 600.0 mm against the node envelope: flush, no misfit. Masses in `check.json`: basin shell 57.6 kg, harvest screen 36.0 kg plate-equivalent (5.4 kg mesh class), bedding 0.0 (table absence; hold W12), nozzles under 0.1 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: bin basin, nozzle set (HDPE fittings stock). Buy: SS316 screen panel and the bedding charge (plan rule 6).
W10 Fabrication route. F-FARM (plan section 4 F-FARM row: frame, liner, bed, drainage): HDPE sheet cut and thermoplastic weld (walls, floor), screen panel cut and clamped to rim ledges, bedding loaded after the water-fill leak test at the worm-tea line. On-site tool: the thermoplastic (polymer) bench with the farm fit-out crew; screen and bedding are buy goods.
W11 Self-copy relevance. The bin makes vermicompost, not plant parts; A-SC does not apply (tier 9). The HDPE bin is itself a shop thermoplastic-weld product class.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The `worm_bin` class has no AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 route class; the F-FARM practice is the named study route. (2) Area reading: the interior yields 2.25 m2 against the node design bin_m2 2.0 - the registry box governs, nothing resized. (3) No bedding density row exists in the material table - the bedding mass carries 0.0 until the supplier TDS. (4) The N3/N4 outlet datum (z 600) is the rim height - drawn flush at the envelope top; the N6 vent datum (z 800) predates the box. (5) The PP working fork is a hand tool, not a bin part - not drawn. (6) Fastener patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

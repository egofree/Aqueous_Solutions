Authority: `docs-authority/duties/EXT_HALL.md`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-MOD-002 (frame standard), A-M-104 (conditioned-hall envelope classes), A-M-105 (roof loads), the placement_seed blocks EXT_HALL_E/EXT_HALL_N (the L-shape authority per the node size_note), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node EXT_HALL (size_note, hvac block).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `ext_hall.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 186000 × 174000 × 6000: "building massing envelope (bounding box of the issued L-shape): EXT_HALL_E 162 × 174 m east extension + EXT_HALL_N 24 × 156 m north extension, 6 m eave, portal frames on the 6 m grid (31 × 29 bays, X-PAR-07, A-M-104; placement_seed blocks EXT_HALL_E/N are the authority)". The model is drawn at the true building scale and fills it: measured 186000.0 × 174000.0 × 6000.0 mm from the export STL (X-PAR-04 registry green).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid, and the building is drawn as its envelope/structure with no process internals: on-grade slab 150 mm under the L-shape, perimeter wall panels 300 mm from slab to eave on the L-footprint (IECC U 0.534 class, A-M-104), roof deck 100 mm at z 5900..6000 (IECC U 0.199 class), and the steel portal-frame column grid — a 300 × 300 column at every 6 m grid point inside the L (the 31 × 29 bay lines of the size note; 948 columns). The fitout programme stays the duty-sheet open item (duty section 8).
W4 Nozzles. None. The duty section 4 states "None. Electrical feed SF-EXT_HALL at 480 V AC. Air-source heat pumps (D77). GEO_GHEX not coupled." No nozzle schedule is issued; nothing is piped; nothing is drawn at the faces. The electrical feed and heat-pump service are electrical/mechanical-lane scope.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M20 column base bolts per column (4 × 948 = 3792, count from the model). 8.8 on carbon steel per the grade rule; the anchor embedment design is the structural lane's (hold, W12).
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: steel portal frames on the 6 m grid; insulated metal roof and wall panels to the IECC U-factors (A-M-104: roof U 0.199, wall U 0.534 W/(m² K)); roof framing for LRFD 1.72 kPa (A-M-105: 1.2 × 0.15 kPa PV dead + 1.6 × 0.96 kPa roof live, ASCE 7-22). Steel sections and panels are made in-house (duty section 3, X-MFG-32) — the BOM make rows carry the X-MFG-32 note. Slab concrete (massing).
W7 Pressure. Not a pressure system. Roof PV access per IFC 2027 1205.3 / NFPA 1 11.12: the 1829 mm perimeter pathway and the 15 array tables are the PV_ARRAY model's scope (accepted separately) — nothing doubled here. Egress for the hall is the AQS-EGR-001 update under X-STD-01 (duty section 8, open).
W8 Exports. `export/ext_hall.step` (assembly); `export/ext_hall.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/EXT_HALL.stl` replaced from the in-envelope build solid (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 registry). Bounding box 186000.0 × 174000.0 × 6000.0 mm against the node envelope: filled exactly.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Slab (buy, concrete massing), wall and roof panels (make, X-MFG-32 in-house panels), column grid (make, X-MFG-32; massing sections). Masses carry the massing basis — the structural lane owns member design (W12).
W10 Fabrication route. Route hold (W12): `building` has no section 4 class; the tracker carries the hold and this work order states the intent — the frames and panels follow the F-MACH operations as the nearest family (section cut and weld, panel fabrication and fit, anchors), with erection in the duty section 3 order: slab, columns and frames, roof, wall panels. On-site tool: the metal shop welding bay (X-MFG-32 in-house steel) with the erection crew; concrete is a buy.
W11 Self-copy relevance. The hall is the building that houses the shop and the extension programme — it does not make plant parts. The steelwork itself is X-MFG-32 in-house fabrication (the make rows above); no AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference beyond that note.
W12 Holds, named. (1) Route hold: no AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 class for `building` — the tracker acceptance cell reads A-core (route hold). (3) The fitout programme (duty section 8) and the egress update (AQS-EGR-001 under X-STD-01) are open lane items — none drawn. (4) No nozzle schedule (duty section 4: None). (5) The roof PV interface is the PV_ARRAY model's scope; roof load figures (A-M-105) are stamped there. (6) The slab mass is a table-density placeholder (published reinforced-concrete class) until the foundation design quotes.

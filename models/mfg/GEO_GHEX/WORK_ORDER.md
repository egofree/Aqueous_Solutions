# Work order GEO_GHEX

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-HX). Route class F-HX (the `ghex` class sits in the F-HX row of plan section 4). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/GEO_GHEX.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/GEO_GHEX.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-MOD-002 (frame standard), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node GEO_GHEX (design block, design_survey_locked ST-ORC-01).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `geo_ghex.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1000 × 600 × 800 — the study registry box. The placement seed seats a 1200 × 800 × 800 "bore field tie, massing only" box: the node governs, the seed figure is a named hold (W12). The build fills the node box: measured 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm from the export STL — the hold-down frame pins the box so the X-PAR-04 envelope registry reads the build.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The unit is the manifold skid per the node design block (design_survey_locked, ST-ORC-01): DN32 HDPE flow/return header runs on the skid (x 120..700 at z 300/360), the two bore-pair tie-in stubs to below grade (interface indication — the boreholes are drilled subsurface works, not shop fabrication; 2 bores × 45 m at 6 m spacing, 90 m installed per the node, depth and spacing named, not drawn to depth — hold, W12), the circulation-pump envelope (circ_pump_W 125.7, node — purchased per plan rule 6), and the glycol fill pot (OD 140 × 220, study — W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 3 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/GEO_GHEX.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric hose barbs flush at the envelope faces: N3 geothermal loop fluid out on +Y at z 600 (the loop tie to GEO_HP, the S43 class edge); N1 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N2 vent flush at the envelope top (800 datum is the box top). Duty section 4: "Atmospheric joints are hose. DN is copied from the edge and is not recalculated" — the rows read "Bore not calculated. Rating hose, atmospheric"; no flange is drawn (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column is carried verbatim as text. Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06 — hold, W12).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M12 wedge anchors (plinth to slab) and 6 M10 header-clip bolts (3 per run), counts from the model. 8.8 on carbon steel per the grade rule; study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Node material row: HDPE DR-11 U-tubes, 1 in bore + horizontal runs; borehole grout thermal. CA does not apply to the HDPE (AQS-STD-001). Skid frame and plinth painted A500 (AQS-MOD-002 dry service).
W7 Pressure. The loop is a closed glycol circuit at the pump class; no design_kPa is stated on the node and the duty section 6 carries no vent edge — "not to the room". No set point is claimed. Duty section 5: level and temperature where the node is a liquid holdup — the fill pot carries the fill-point duty; no trip is specified on this node.
W8 Exports. `export/geo_ghex.step` (assembly, 3 solids); `export/geo_ghex.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/GEO_GHEX.stl` replaced from the in-envelope build solid (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 registry). Bounding box 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm against the node envelope: filled exactly.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: plinth, hold-down frame, DN32 header runs, bore tie-in stubs, fill pot, nozzle set. Buy: circulation pump envelope (125.7 W, node).
W10 Fabrication route. F-HX (plan section 4: plate pack or coil, headers, frame, pressure or leak test): header runs cut and joined (HDPE fusion per the pipe lane practice), skid frame weldment, clips, stub fit, fill pot, pump set and anchor, then a loop pressure test at the glycol class and a fill/vent sequence. On-site tool: the metal shop frame bay with the polymer (fusion) bench; the drilling and grouting are the site/well lane's.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the loop ties the ground field to the heat pump; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The borehole depth/spacing are node facts named, not drawn to depth — the boreholes are drilled subsurface works (site/well lane; hold W12). (2) The seed 1200 × 800 × 800 figure predates the node study box — the node governs (W12). (3) Skid header stations, pump envelope and fill pot are study values. (4) The nozzle bores are not calculated (schedule verbatim, hose). (5) Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06). (6) The pump-envelope mass is a table-density placeholder until the maker TDS quotes. (7) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication. (8) A-HX: the loop area (186 m DN32 = 24.8 m²) is reported from the model plus the node-quoted buried run and is not called a calculated duty — the duty sheet states no mass throughput; q_per_m 35 W/m and Q_ceiling 3.0 kW th are the node's survey-locked class figures.

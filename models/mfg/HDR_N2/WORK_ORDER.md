## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `hdr_n2.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` and, for the A-PIPE line list, the unit's edges in the graph and the issued AQS-PIP-004 rows; it rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Parameters. Envelope 2000 × 60.3 × 60.3 mm at origin 5000, 600, 6000 mm (placement seed), node size_note "DN32 manifold x 20 m run (0.009 m3), header sizing (flow_graph/param/headers.py, AQS-DUTY-075)". design_kPa and relief_mm are null: the header is atmospheric (duty section 6).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid; none is drawn. The model is the spool set as the node defines it: one manifold run, drawn as a 2000 mm representative spool. The 20 m run is carried by the per-line route lengths of AQS-PIP-004, not redrawn.
W4 Nozzles. All 14 rows of `docs-authority/nozzles/HDR_N2.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as open-bore tube stubs flush at the envelope faces (lib/nozzles.place_on_face). Atmospheric per duty section 4 ("No flange class. Atmospheric."); the W4 110 % rule draws no flange because no process pressure is stated. The schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. N1 carries DN50: it is drawn at the manifold centreline and sits inside the cross-section — the reservation cross-section (60.3 mm) is sized in `flow_graph/param/headers.py` (AQS-DUTY-075) to cover the largest issued branch hardware, the EN-rounded OD series (2026-11-08 reservation adoption; nothing was resized). N2 (DN15, sweep nitrogen) and N14 (DN15, aisle make-up air) join the schedule rows. N12 carries "solids" (nitrogen return to the ASU): drawn DN15 and labeled bore not calculated (plan W4). N3 (worm bin vent) and N4 (larvae bin vent) carry services with no present edge on the graph; drawn verbatim, no edge created. The along-run x positions are the registered layout choice (params layout.nozzle_spread).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M10 wedge anchors, grade 8.8 on the carbon-steel plinth (plan grade rule). Spool and branch joints are 316L welds; there are no other bolted joints. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Manifold and branches 316L tube (duty section 3; M1 class, AQS-PIP-003: ASTM A312 pipe with A403 fittings); corrosion allowance 0 mm (AQS-STD-001 rule). Plinth carbon steel (AQS-MOD-002 dry service class).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric (duty section 6: no vent edge, relief not to the room). The vent N10 returns to the loop suction per duty section 4. No test pressure is claimed.
W8 Exports. `export/hdr_n2.step` (assembly); `export/hdr_n2.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/HDR_N2.stl` replaced from the build solid (ASCII, metres; the X-PAR-04 registry gate: the registry bbox equals the node size within 1 mm). Bounding box 2000.0 × 60.3 × 60.3 mm, inside the 2000 × 60.3 × 60.3 reservation — the cross-section is sized to cover the DN50 branch hardware (headers.py, AQS-DUTY-075; 2026-11-08 reservation adoption). Masses in `check.json`: plinth 18.93 kg, manifold 6.95 kg, branches 0.44 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make.
W10 Fabrication route. F-PIPE: spool cut (DN32 316L tube to the Sch 40 table), branch set-in welds at the schedule tags, trough plinth weld and anchor, line list, leak test of the assembled run. On-site tool: the metal shop; the line list and spool sheet travel with the spool.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the header moves gas; it makes no plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The envelope is the AQS-DUTY-075 sizing box (DN32 manifold × 20 m run); the model draws a 2000 mm representative spool of it; per-line lengths stay in the line list. (2) The pipe schedule is not stated on the node or in the duty; the geometry is drawn on the lib/dn Sch 40 table and the M1 class sheet carries the schedule at order. (3) The AQS-DUTY-075 ID grid (DN − 8 = 24 mm) and the Sch 40 bore (35.3 mm) disagree by the grid simplification; both seat DN32; nothing resized. (4) Resolved 2026-11-08: headers.py (AQS-DUTY-075) sizes the reservation cross-section to the largest issued branch hardware (60.3 mm, the EN-rounded OD series of AQS-PIP-004), and the DN50 row N1 draws inside it at the manifold centreline; the build reads 2000 × 60.3 × 60.3 against the same reservation and no misfit is carried; nothing was resized beyond the registered reservation. (5) Schedule datums sit in a 60.3 mm cross-section: the 10 mm inlet rows are clamped up to 11.3 mm, the DN25 37 mm outlet datums are clamped down to 32.4 mm, and the 160 mm and 600 mm placeholder datums are clamped; designed elevations are unit tasks 02/06. (6) The Class 150 rating column stands against an atmospheric duty; no flange is drawn (duty section 4, W4 110 % rule). (7) Duty section 4 says "Atmospheric" without fixing tube or hose; the model draws tube stubs flush at the faces and the hose makeup is the connecting spool's joint at the face. (8) The trough-mount support scheme (plinth, no top rails) is the layout choice carried from the HDR_CO2 acceptance. (9) Anchor lengths and the branch pitch are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

# Work order AFTERBURNER

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-HX). Route class F-HX (the `thermal` class sits in the F-HX row of plan section 4). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/AFTERBURNER.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/AFTERBURNER.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-MOD-002 (frame system), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node AFTERBURNER (equipment list), duty section 8 (AQS-DUTY-063 Rev B section 6, X-SAN-01).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `afterburner.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1000 × 600 × 800 (study default envelope box). The build fills it exactly: measured 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm, one closed manifold mesh. The chamber sizing of duty section 8 is the machine authority; the study box is the registry envelope and the build fills it.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn as the unit: horizontal refractory-lined combustion chamber ID 200 × 600 (duty section 8, AQS-DUTY-063 Rev B section 6, X-SAN-01) with two end heads, steel shell wall 6 mm and refractory lining 50 mm (study values — the duty says "refractory and burner detail open"), on two saddles over a 20 mm plinth.
W4 Nozzles. All 12 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/AFTERBURNER.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube stubs flush at the envelope faces: N1 NCG, N2 organics, N3 fuel, N10 biogas, N11 combustion O2 and N12 solids (dewatered digestate cake to incineration, barrier 2 — non-piped transfer drawn DN15, bore not calculated, plan W4) on −Y at z 160; N4/N5 DN15 (Q1 flue, not Q4 — the rows were repaired to the schedule quoting, hold W12), N6 flue (to AFTERBURNER_FLUE) and N9 captured CO2/air on +Y at z 600; N7 DN15 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N8 DN15 vent flush at the envelope top (800 datum is the box top). Atmospheric tube per duty section 4 ("no duct bore... no flange class"); no flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M12 wedge anchors (plinth to slab, 4 per saddle station) and 8 M12 saddle bolts (4 per saddle), counts from the model. 8.8 on carbon steel per the grade rule; lengths and patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Chamber, heads, saddles, plinth and nozzles carbon steel (duty section 3 "Carbon steel. Hydrocarbon. No aqueous chloride"); CA 1.5 mm (AQS-STD-001 hydrocarbon rule). Refractory lining per the duty class (castable/class — detail open, hold W12).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6). Operating class ≥ 850 C for ≥ 2 s (duty section 8, Directive 2010/75/EU Art. 50). No test pressure is claimed. The vent stays on the gas edge, not the room (duty section 4).
W8 Exports. `export/afterburner.step` (assembly); `export/afterburner.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/AFTERBURNER.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. Masses in `check.json`: plinth 94.2 kg, saddles 272.6 kg, chamber 33.3 kg, refractory 198.4 kg, burner envelope 22.0 kg, fan envelope 32.9 kg, damper envelope 9.0 kg, nozzles 0.8 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make rows: chamber, refractory lining, saddles + plinth, nozzles. Buy rows (purchased envelopes per plan rule 6, ratings from the node equipment list): burner + ignition transformer + BMS panel 0.05 kW; flue ID fan variable-speed high-temp class 0.1332 kW; dampers and flame monitor 0.01 kW.
W10 Fabrication route. F-HX (plan section 4: plate pack or coil, headers, frame, pressure or leak test) applied to the thermal machine: plate cut and roll of the shell, circumferential and end-head welds, nozzle set-in, refractory placement and cure, saddle fabrication, leak check of the finished shell. On-site tool: the metal shop (roll, weld); refractory placement per the castable supplier procedure (detail open, hold W12).
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the afterburner destroys waste gas; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) Shell wall 6 mm and refractory lining 50 mm are study values; the duty states "refractory and burner detail open". (2) The burner internals are not designed: the burner rides its purchased envelope (plan rule 6, 0.05 kW from the node equipment list). (3) The N4/N5 schedule rows were repaired to the schedule quoting (unquoted comma in "Q1 flue, not Q4" shifted face and height; change record in `notes/glass_oilgas_m3_2026-11-02.md`). (4) The N12 solids row is a non-piped transfer drawn DN15, bore not calculated (plan W4). (5) A-HX report: chamber volume 0.01885 m³, residence at the AFTERBURNER_FLUE node design flow 0.0018 m³/s (200 C flue class) is 10.5 s against the ≥ 2 s requirement — reported, not called a calculated design (the duty claims no heat rate for this unit). (6) Fastener lengths and saddle fit are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

# Work order CRYO_N2

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-COL). Route class F-COL. Graph Rev AC. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/CRYO_N2.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/CRYO_N2.csv`, the node size_mm box (300 × 300 × 2000, study registry), AQS-STD-002, AQS-STD-001.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `cryo_n2.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `layout` and `study` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node `size_mm` [300, 300, 2000] is the study registry box and there is no other design: the build IS the box (shell machine, measured 300.0 × 300.0 × 2000.0 mm) and `cad/stl/CRYO_N2.stl` is replaced from the build.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none` and the duty sheet names no packing, diameter or internals (duty section 8: "No diameter on this node"). The build is the shell machine: welded 316L shell t 3 closed top and bottom on a 20 mm skid. No internal solid is invented. A vacuum jacket is not named anywhere (F-COL: "vacuum jacket where named") and none is drawn.
W4 Nozzles. All 8 rows of `docs-authority/nozzles/CRYO_N2.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore, and every schedule datum fits this envelope: N1 DN50 inlet at −Y z 400; N2 DN50 outlet at +Y z 1500; N5/N6/N7 DN15 and N8 DN50 outlets at +Y z 600; N3 DN15 drain flush at z 0 with its boss to the 50 datum; N4 DN15 vent flush at the 2000 top datum. Atmospheric joints are hose barbs (duty sections 4 and 6: "the vent stays on the gas edge, not the room"); no flange is drawn and the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. The along-face x positions are the registered layout choice (per tag in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M10 wedge anchors (skid to slab), ISO 3506 A4-70 per the plan grade rule on 316L. Count is a study value.
W6 Materials. 316L (duty section 3: condensation, no carbon steel), corrosion allowance 0 mm (AQS-STD-001 rule). Shell t 3 and skid 20 are house study values - no thickness is stated by any authority.
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric (duty section 6); the vent stays on the gas edge to NORTH_DOCK, not the room. The stored `design_kPa` is the old head-plus-10 figure, not a set point. Leak test of the shell per the F-COL route.
W8 Exports. `export/cryo_n2.step` (assembly); `export/cryo_n2.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/CRYO_N2.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Masses in `check.json`: shell 64.2 kg, skid 14.4 kg, nozzles 4.8 kg; about 84 kg empty.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Everything is shop-made.
W10 Fabrication route. F-COL: 316L sheet cut and rolled/braked into the box shell, TIG welds, nozzle set-in, skid weldment, leak test of the shell. On-site tool: the metal shop.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: a nitrogen product/vent vessel makes no plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) No diameter on the node and no design block (duty section 8): the shell machine is drawn at the study registry box; the diameter, wall and internals design is unit task 02. (2) No internals named: A-COL packing, distributor and demister are not drawn; stages or HTU stay a hold where the duty says so. (3) Shell t 3 and skid 20 are house study values, no thickness stated. (4) A vacuum jacket is not named; none is drawn - insulation design is pending. (5) The schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text against the atmospheric duty; no flange is drawn. (6) Hose barb profile and the anchor count are study values. (7) The duty section 2 statement "Not a packed tower. The column diameter and the tray count are on CRYO_ASU only" refers to the earlier column story and does not size this box; this unit's diameter remains undesignated.

# Work order GLASS_BURNOUT

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-PIPE). Route class F-PIPE (the `intra` class sits in the F-PIPE row of plan section 4). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/GLASS_BURNOUT.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/GLASS_BURNOUT.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-PIP-003/004 (line list), AQS-MOD-002 (frame system), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node GLASS_BURNOUT.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `glass_burnout.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 800 × 600 × 1200 ("issued envelope"). The build stays inside it exactly: measured 800.0 × 600.0 × 1200.0 mm, four closed solids, one closed manifold mesh.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn as the unit: a horizontal 316L burnout drum OD 560 × 700 between two tire rings, on trunnion stands over a 20 mm plinth (the ported AQS-MFG-001 form `models/openscad/detail/mfg_burnout_rotary.scad`: drum, tires, UCP213 trunnions).
W4 Nozzles. All 7 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/GLASS_BURNOUT.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore: N1 DN25 milled cullet and N2 DN15 fuel on −Y at z 160; N3/N4 DN15 (Q2 burnout, not Q4) and N5 DN25 burned cullet on +Y at z 600; N6 DN15 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum is the schedule placeholder); N7 DN15 vent flush at the envelope top (schedule datum 800 mm predates the 1200 box — hold, W12). Atmospheric joints are hose (duty section 4): all process rows are hose barbs; no flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. The N3/N4 schedule rows carried an unquoted comma that shifted their face and height fields; the rows were repaired to the schedule quoting (the generator only appends, hand rows are preserved verbatim; change record in `notes/glass_oilgas_m3_2026-11-02.md`).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M10 wedge anchors (plinth to slab, 4 per stand station) and 4 M12 housing bolts (trunnion housing to pedestal), counts from the model. 8.8 on carbon steel per the grade rule; lengths and patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Drum, heads, tires, shafts and nozzles 316L (duty section 3 "316L where the stream is wet"); plinth and stands carbon steel A500 (AQS-MOD-002 dry service class). CA 0 mm on the 316L (AQS-STD-001 rule); the dry carbon-steel structure carries no aqueous chloride and takes no CA.
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6). No test pressure is claimed. The two DN15 +Y rows are the Q2 heat-recovery loop takeoffs (graph edges present as study; drawn verbatim, no new edge).
W8 Exports. `export/glass_burnout.step` (assembly); `export/glass_burnout.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/GLASS_BURNOUT.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 800.0 × 600.0 × 1200.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, exact. Masses in `check.json`: plinth 75.4 kg, drum 163.7 kg, stands 30.4 kg, hardware 1.1 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make: drum, stands, plinth and hardware are in-house fabrication.
W10 Fabrication route. F-PIPE (plan section 4: spool cut, join, support, line list) applied to the machine hardware: drum plate rolling and seam weld, end-head set-in, tire and shaft machining, stand fabrication and alignment, connection stubs, leak check of the finished drum. On-site tool: the metal shop (roll, weld, drill); the drum bore is finished in-house on the shop lathe bed if the 560 envelope exceeds the lathe swing — study route note, confirmed at fabrication.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the burnout furnace conditions cullet; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) No drum dimension (OD, length, wall) is stated on the node or duty; the drum is drawn to the node envelope and the 12 mm wall is a study value. (2) Drum-full volume 0.1579 m³ against the duty section 2 seven-day charge 0.2100 m³: reported, nothing resized (plan rule 3); the charge basis is a capacity figure, not a residence time. (3) The rotary drive is not designed: no drive is named on the node or duty; the ported legacy form names no drive either. (4) Vent schedule datum 800 mm predates the 1200 box; drawn flush at the envelope top (house idiom). (5) N3/N4 schedule rows repaired to the schedule quoting (unquoted comma shifted face and height); the Q2 rows ride study edges. (6) Trunnion housing bores, pedestal fit and fastener lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication. (7) No burner hardware is drawn: the fuel row (N2) is the schedule interface; the burner itself is not named on the node.

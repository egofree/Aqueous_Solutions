## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `met_syn.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1500 × 1000 × 1800: "bench fume-hood train: glass-lined reactor column (2 L working volume), reagent feed tanks, Hastelloy C crystalliser; the 9 g/d DL-Met duty sets nothing larger (A-M-416 route A; X-UI-002)". The build fills it: measured 1500.0 × 1000.0 × 1800.0 mm from the export STL, all connection hardware flush at the envelope faces, nothing protrudes. The four-post hold-down frame pins the registry box so the X-PAR-04 envelope registry reads the build.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn as the unit per the node sizing/design blocks and duty section 3: borosilicate glass-lined reactor column (OD 150 × 600, 2 L working volume) with heating-jacket band (OD 190 × 300), three SS304 reagent feed tanks (OD 200 × 300; CH3OH / H2S / NH3 per the schedule feeds), Hastelloy C crystalliser (OD 220 × 350), PTFE-lined transfer lines, and the stirrer drive and metering pumps as purchased-class envelopes. The three Strecker steps run through the one column in campaign sequence; the node names the sequence, not separate hardware.
W4 Nozzles. All 6 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/MET_SYN.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube stubs flush at the envelope faces: N1 methanol feed, N2 H2S feed and N3 ammonia feed on −Y at z 160 (under the bench deck, where the metering pumps sit); N4 DL-methionine crystals (a solids service row) on +Y at z 600 at the crystalliser discharge, drawn DN15, bore not calculated (plan W4); N5 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N6 vent flush at the envelope top — the 800 datum predates the 1800 box (hold, W12). No flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06 — hold, W12).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M12 wedge anchors (plinth to slab, 2 per frame post line) and 8 M8 hold-down bolts (2 per vessel on the deck), counts from the model. 8.8 on carbon steel, A4-70 on 316L per the grade rule; lengths and patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3 (X-MAT-22): borosilicate glass-lined reactor column, PTFE-lined transfer lines, SS304 reagent feed tanks, Hastelloy C crystalliser — the Strecker carries HCN, acrolein and methanethiol, all aggressive at the reagent class. Bench frame, deck and fume hood 316L, the wet-bench class under HCN/acrolein fume (REE_RECOVER idiom; the frame material is a registered layout choice — hold, W12). Hold-down frame and plinth carbon steel A500 (AQS-MOD-002 dry service). CA 0 mm on the glass, PTFE, SS304, Hastelloy C and 316L; it does not apply to the polymers (AQS-STD-001).
W7 Pressure. Relief case study pending (unit task 07); no set point is claimed on this page (duty section 6). Atmospheric tube per duty section 4; the vent riser is drawn flush at the envelope top. No instrument loop yet (unit task 08) — no trip is specified.
W8 Exports. `export/met_syn.step` (assembly, 12 solids); `export/met_syn.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/MET_SYN.stl` replaced from the in-envelope build solid (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 registry). Bounding box 1500.0 × 1000.0 × 1800.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make rows carry the AQS-BOM-SC-001 flag where the on-site shop makes the part: bench deck/plinth, hold-down frame, fume-hood shell, glass-lined column, feed tanks, PTFE lines, nozzles. Buy rows (purchased per plan rule 6, the ratings from the node): heating jacket, stirrer drive, metering pumps (connected set 0.2 kW, no per-item figure invented), Hastelloy C crystalliser (maker TDS mass governs at order).
W10 Fabrication route. Route hold (W12): `chemical_synthesis` has no section 4 class; the tracker carries the hold and this work order states the intent — the bench, frame and hood follow the F-MACH operations as the nearest family (frame weldment, guards, purchased units by rating, anchors) and the glass-lined column, tanks and lines follow the F-VES vessel practice where wetted. On-site tool: the metal shop with the polymer (thermoplastic) bench for the PTFE work; the column lining is a maker service.

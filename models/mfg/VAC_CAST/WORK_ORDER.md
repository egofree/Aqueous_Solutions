## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `vac_cast.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid: plinth, vacuum chamber and lid, mould-tilt table hardware, crucible set, pour hardware, pump and cabinet envelopes, and the schedule hardware. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1500 × 800 × 1500 (A-M-199 forming class N+1 rated envelope, A-M-282). The build fills it: measured 1500.0 × 800.0 × 1500.0 mm, all connection hardware flush at the envelope faces, nothing protrudes.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The 3 kg design-case melt is drawn as a process-inventory solid, not a BOM part and not an internal.
W4 Nozzles. All 3 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/VAC_CAST.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore DN15 tube connections, flush at the envelope faces (duty section 4: hose, atmospheric; the connecting line makes the joint at the face): N1 DN15 vacuum melt to cast on +Y at z 160; N2 DN15 drain through the plinth, flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N3 DN15 vent riser on the chamber lid, flush at the envelope top (its 800 datum is the schedule placeholder - hold, W12). The schedule inlet face is mapped to a compass face by the registered layout choice (params.json layout block). No flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M12 wedge anchors, plinth to slab (8.8 on carbon steel), 12 M10 stud-and-nut sets on the chamber-lid clamp and cabinet rails (A4-70 on the 304L joint). Lengths and torque are study values, confirmed at fabrication. The welded joints carry no fastener row.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: 304L vacuum tilt-pour chamber (t6 study wall, W12), graphite crucible on its alumina backup (consumable buy set), steel mould-tilt table hardware, CS plinth. CA 0 mm (dry foundry-floor service and the stainless/graphite classes; AQS-STD-001 shop rule).
W7 Pressure. Vacuum chamber: leak test per the route (W10); no design_kPa is booked on the node and none is claimed (duty section 6: relief case pending, unit task 07 - hold, W12). Not to the room. The melt row is atmospheric at the face (duty section 4); the schedule Class 150 column stands as text.
W8 Exports. `export/vac_cast.step` (assembly); `export/vac_cast.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/VAC_CAST.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1500.0 × 800.0 × 1500.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly, zero face overhang.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: plinth, chamber and lid, mould-tilt table hardware, nozzle set. Buy: the graphite crucible + alumina backup consumable set, the vacuum pump skid and the 7.5 kW control cabinet (node design rating, drawn as envelopes per plan rule 6). The standby state is named in the BOM rows and the build log.
W10 Fabrication route. Route hold (W12); operations named so the shop can bid it: plate cut and weld (304L GTAW, full-penetration seam welds on the chamber), leak test of the chamber, mould-table and tilt-cradle machine and fit, plinth weld (CS), set-in of the schedule connections, pump skid and cabinet mount, then dry run and vacuum leak-down before the first design-case pour. On-site tool: the metal shop welding bay with the machine bay.
W11 Self-copy relevance. A-SC applies (tier 7): the station casts plant parts (the Cu/Fe/Zn class castings the on-site shop needs), so the parts it makes are the shop-makeable castings flagged make in the BOM and cross-referenced to AQS-BOM-SC-001; the parts of the station itself the on-site shop can make are the chamber weldment, table hardware and plinth (make rows); the crucible set, pump skid and cabinet are purchased (buy rows). The station is itself a self-copy shop tool in the AQS-BOM-SC-001 casting path.
W12 Holds, named. (1) No AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 route class for class `caster`; the tracker carries the route hold and W10 names the operations anyway. (2) The chamber wall t6 is the study fab-page value; no thickness is booked on the node. (3) The N3 vent datum 800 is the schedule placeholder; the riser stands on the chamber lid and flushes at the envelope top. (4) Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders, not designed elevations (unit tasks 02/06). (5) The relief/vacuum-case study is pending (unit task 07); no design_kPa is booked on the node. (6) The pump skid, control cabinet and tilt actuator package are purchased; the maker ratings read the sheets at order. (7) The chamber door and mould-change interface are unit-task design items pending the operating procedure. (8) Fastener lengths and torque are study values, confirmed at fabrication. (9) No instrument loop is issued for this unit yet (duty section 8). (10) The mould seats themselves are the cast-product tooling, ordered per casting class at the unit task; the table is drawn at its envelope.

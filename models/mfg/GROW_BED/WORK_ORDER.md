Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-FARM). Route class F-FARM (the `farm` class sits in the F-FARM row of plan section 4). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `grow_bed.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 7310 × 4030 × 915 - "crew-duty growing area per A-M-229 (29.12 m2) framed per A-M-469: 4 x 2 nominal 2000 x 1000 bays, 15 mm basin walls, LED canopy to z 900 + 15 mm rail (X-FARM sec3)" - and `area_m2` 29.12 — "crew duty (A-M-229): S14 4.2 kg/d / 0.1442 kg/m2/d = 29.12 m2 (supersedes the BH ~28 m2 / 120.4 m2 residue-claim studies [A-M-151])". A-FARM governs: the bed area matches the node and is NOT shrunk to fit the block. The full bed — interior 7280 × 4000 mm = 29.12 m², 400 high, canopy to z 900 + 15 mm rail — is the `export/` build and the STEP; `cad/stl/GROW_BED.stl` keeps the in-envelope bay module (2000 × 1000 × 400, the `grow_bed_layout_mm` design-block bay; scope recorded in check.json cad_stl_scope). The build bounding box 7310.0 × 4030.0 × 910.6 mm sits inside the reservation — 2026-11-08 reservation adoption: the former single-bay study box 2000 × 1000 × 400 stated no bay count or canopy height and is withdrawn.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The bed is drawn as the unit: HDPE basin (walls and floor 15 mm) with an EPDM liner (5 mm, the GROWBED_A ponded-zone idiom), LECA media envelope at a 250 mm study depth (the duty names no media depth — hold, W12), a drain sump (300 × 300 × 120) at the bay corner with the N9 drain above it (A-FARM: liner and drain drawn), internal cross-ribs framing the nominal 2000 × 1000 bays, and the LED canopy — perimeter posts and rails to z 900 with the LED panel envelope over the bed (the node continuous 2.0463 kW = 29.12 m² × 70.3 W/m², DLI 17 at 2.8 umol/J, A-M-133/A-M-144; purchased per plan rule 6). Canopy height is the registered A-M-469 framing (top z 900 + 15 mm rail).
W4 Nozzles. All 22 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/GROW_BED.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube stubs flush at the envelope faces: N1-N4 in on −Y at z 80 (CO2, nutrients, load, CO2 enrichment dose); N5-N8 out on +Y at z 300 (the bed water line: organics, condensate, food, food waste to larvae); N13/N14/N16/N17/N19 in at z 160; the canopy rows N11, N12, N15, N18, N20, N21, N22 at the schedule z 600 datum sit above the 400 mm bed and the build height datum — drawn flush at the envelope top with the placeholder note (the GLASS_BURNOUT stale-datum idiom; hold, W12); N9 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum) over the sump; N10 vent flush at the envelope top (400 datum is the bed height). Duty section 4: DN15, no flange class, atmospheric tube or hose; no flange is drawn (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06 — hold, W12).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M10 basin hold-down bolts (polymer-joint hardware, A4-70 with the polymer supplier rule named at order) and 8 M12 canopy base-plate bolts, counts from the model. Lengths and patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: HDPE or PVC for the water — the basin is HDPE with an EPDM liner (GROWBED_A idiom); LECA media charge (buy). The frame is not the wetted part: canopy painted A500 (farm frame class, AQS-MOD-002). CA does not apply to the polymer (AQS-STD-001).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric; the duty section 6 states no vent edge — "not to the room" carried. Duty section 5: no trip specified. Duty section 2 circulation (19100.9 kg/d, two-minute holdup 0.0265 m³) is reported, not a residence-time claim; the duty section 8 note stands ("if this node is also a reservoir, that volume is not the two-minute holdup").
W8 Exports. `export/grow_bed.step` (assembly, 4 solids); `export/grow_bed.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/GROW_BED.stl` replaced from the in-envelope bay module (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 registry; scope recorded in check.json cad_stl_scope). Registry bbox 2000.0 × 1000.0 × 400.0 mm: the bay module. Full-bed bbox 7310.0 × 4030.0 × 910.6 mm: inside the 7310 × 4030 × 915 reservation (2026-11-08 reservation adoption, A-M-469).
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: HDPE basin with ribs and sump, canopy, nozzle set. Buy: EPDM liner (sheet roll), LECA media charge (the envelope drawn per plan rule 6, the charge a buy row), LED panel envelope (2.0463 kW node stamp).
W10 Fabrication route. F-FARM (plan section 4: frame, liner, bed, drainage): sheet cut and thermoplastic weld (basin walls, floor, ribs, sump), liner roll fitting with the ponded-zone laps, media loading after the leak test, canopy frame weldment and base plates, LED panel mounting and driver hookup, then a water-fill leak test at the bed line. On-site tool: the polymer (thermoplastic) bench with the farm fit-out crew; the liner and media are buy goods.
W11 Self-copy relevance. The bed makes food, not plant parts; A-SC does not apply (tier 9). The HDPE basin is itself a shop thermoplastic-weld product class.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The LECA media depth (250 mm) is a study value — the duty names no media depth. (2) The sump size is a study value; the canopy height is the registered A-M-469 framing (top z 900 + 15 mm rail) (W12). (3) The schedule z 600 canopy rows predate the build height — drawn flush at the envelope top (W12). (4) Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06). (5) The LED panel and liner masses are table-density placeholders until the maker TDS quotes. (6) The media charge is a buy row; the envelope is drawn, the fill is not. (7) The duty section 8 note stands: a reservoir volume on this node would not be the two-minute holdup. (8) Resolved 2026-11-08: the reservation `size_mm` is the built array (the A-M-469 framing of the A-M-229 area; 2026-11-08 reservation adoption), the full-bed build 7310 × 4030 × 910.6 mm sits inside it, and no misfit is carried; the former single-bay study box stated no bay count or canopy height and is withdrawn; the registry keeps the bay module per the CRYO_ASU treatment. (9) Fastener lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

# Work order IND_MELT

Authority: AQS-DUTY-072 (X-MFG lane). LIVE unit - the melt train anchor.

1. Fabricate the 1000 x 1000 x 1500 mm casing (CS t4); fit the t150 ceramic-fibre hot face.
2. Set the crucible on its pedestal; wind and clamp the 12-turn coil; fit the trunnions and tilt cradle.
3. Route the coil leads through the +X pass; megger the coil to the casing.
4. Mount the hood with the N17 vent; fit the N1/N8/N13/N14 melt stubs and the N9 slag spout.
5. Wire the 25.0 kW medium-frequency supply; interlock lid position and coil water flow.
6. Test: coil dry-out, then first melt at the 10.0 kg batch; prove the tilt-pour lip at +X.
7. Inspect against models/mfg/IND_MELT/export/ind_melt.step; record dimensions on the traveller.

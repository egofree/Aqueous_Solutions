# Work order IND_MELT

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core). Route class hold: `furnace` has no section 4 class (W12). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/IND_MELT.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/IND_MELT.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-DUTY-072 (sizing study), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node IND_MELT (design block, A-M-282/A-M-283 melt basis).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `ind_melt.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` and rebuilds every solid: casing, hot face, coil, crucible, tilt gear, hood, and the schedule hardware. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `assumptions`, `crucible`, `coil` and `design` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1000 × 1000 × 1500 (A-M-199 melt class: crucible charge at 7000 kg/m³, H/D + 300/500 allowances, box furnace at the connected load). The build fills it: measured 1000.0 × 1000.0 × 1500.0 mm from the export STL, all connection hardware flush at the envelope faces, nothing protrudes.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The melt in the crucible is drawn as a process-inventory solid, not a BOM part and not an internal.
W4 Nozzles. All 17 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/IND_MELT.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube connections (duty section 4; hose makeup by the connecting line at the face): N1/N8/N13/N14 DN15 melt stubs set in through the +Y wall at z 600 (pour lip, caster, atomizer, investment pour); the ten solids rows N2..N7/N10..N12/N15 land on the common charge chute at the −X face, z 160, drawn at DN15 and labeled bore not calculated (W4 rule); N9 solids slag return on the +X face at z 600, bore not calculated; N16 DN15 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N17 DN15 vent on the hood top (its 800 datum is the schedule placeholder - hold, W12). No flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M10 stud-and-nut sets on the hood rim, 4 M12 anchors, casing to slab. Both rows grade 8.8 carbon steel by the W5 grade rule. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: clay-graphite crucibles assigned per melt family, ceramic-fibre hot face t150 in a 4 mm CS casing, alumina castable hearth under the pour lip. The crucible is a buy row (change-out consumable); the casing, lining, coil and hardware are make. CA 0 mm - dry, non-hydrocarbon furnace service (AQS-STD-001 shop rule).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric furnace; the duty section 6 relief case is pending (unit task 07) and no set point is claimed. The coil cooling-water interlock is the duty section 5 class; loop hardware is not modeled (W12).
W8 Exports. `export/ind_melt.step` (assembly, 14 solids); `export/ind_melt.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/IND_MELT.stl` replaced from the in-envelope build solid (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 registry; scope recorded in `check.json` `cad_stl_scope`). Bounding box 1000.0 × 1000.0 × 1500.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly, zero face overhang.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: casing, hot face, coil and leads, pedestal and cradle, trunnions, hood, stubs, spouts, chute, drain. Buy: the graphite crucible and the 25 kW medium-frequency power supply (rating from the node design; drawn as its envelope per plan rule 6).
W10 Fabrication route. Route class hold (W12). Operations in order, named so the shop can bid it before the class lands: plate cut and fold, CS weld (casing and hood), refractory fit (fibre stack and castable hearth), stub set-in, coil wind and clamp, trunnion bore and fit, then megger and dry-out heat-up test, first melt at the 10 kg batch. On-site tool: the metal shop welding bay with the refractory bench.
W11 Self-copy relevance. The furnace makes melt stock for the cast line, not plant parts; its acceptance set is A-core and A-SC does not apply (tier 6). The melt it makes feeds the tier-7 shop tools, where the AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference lives.
W12 Holds, named. (1) No AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 route class for class `furnace`; the tracker carries the route hold and W10 names the operations anyway. (2) Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders, not designed elevations (duty section 4; unit tasks 02/06). (3) The N17 vent datum 800 is the schedule placeholder; the riser stands on the hood and clears the coil zone. (4) The ten solids rows are drawn at DN15, bore not calculated (W4 rule). (5) No measured melt rate is claimed; the duty section 1 rates are graph study values. (6) The relief case is pending (unit task 07). (7) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication. (8) The power-supply cabinet rating reads the purchase datasheet at order. (9) The coil cooling-water circuit is loop hardware, not modeled; its skid is a unit-task item. (10) No instrument loop is issued for this unit yet (duty section 8).

# Work order IND_MELT

Authority: AQS-DUTY-072 (X-MFG-30 pattern). In-house fabrication; no bought furnace.

1. Fold and weld the 660 x 1000 mm chamber casing; line with 150 mm ceramic fibre.
2. Fit the 10 kg crucible (ID 110.0 x 165 mm) on the hearth; wind and set the coil band.
3. Mount the tilt frame, trunnions and hydraulics; fit the lid and DN150 stack with damper.
4. Wire the 25 kW PSU cabinet; interlock lid-open and coil water flow.
5. Test: dry-out heat-up curve, then 2 trial heats of the 10 kg charge (4 heats/d duty).
6. Inspect against models/mfg/IND_MELT/export/ind_melt.step; record dimensions on the traveller.

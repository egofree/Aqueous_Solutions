# Work order OIL_DRYER

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-MACH). Route class F-MACH (the `dryer` class sits in the F-MACH row of plan section 4). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/OIL_DRYER.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/OIL_DRYER.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-MOD-002 (frame system), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node OIL_DRYER.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `oil_dryer.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1000 × 600 × 800 (study default envelope box). The build fills it exactly: measured 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm, one closed manifold mesh (the guard frame pins the registry box, so the X-PAR-04 envelope registry reads the build).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn as the unit: a horizontal jacketed drying drum — drum OD 500 × 700 with end heads, wall 6 mm (study, W12), heating jacket OD 540 with annulus 20 mm (study, W12; the schedule N1/N2 rows are the HX-4 Q3 process heat connections and the schedule service text says dryer jacket), on two saddles over a 20 mm plinth. Duty section 8: "No holdup basis for this class" — none claimed.
W4 Nozzles. All 7 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/OIL_DRYER.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube stubs flush at the envelope faces: N1/N2 DN15 (HX-4 Q3 process heat, dryer jacket) and N3 DN25 (float plastics in) on −Y at z 160; N4 DN25 (dried plastics to OIL_PYRO) and N5 DN15 (dryer reject) on +Y at z 600; N6 DN15 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N7 DN15 vent flush at the envelope top (800 datum is the box top). Atmospheric tube or hose per duty section 4 (nozzle DN15, no flange class); no flange hardware is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M12 wedge anchors (plinth to slab, 4 per saddle station) and 8 M12 saddle bolts (4 per saddle), counts from the model. 8.8 on carbon steel per the grade rule; lengths and patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Drum, jacket, saddles, plinth, frame and nozzles carbon steel (duty section 3 "Carbon steel. Hydrocarbon. No aqueous chloride in this vessel"); CA 1.5 mm (AQS-STD-001 hydrocarbon rule) on the wetted drum and jacket; the dry structure carries the same alloy with no CA claim beyond the rule.
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6). Jacket side is a thermal-oil loop connection at atmospheric rating (no process pressure stated). No test pressure is claimed beyond a leak check of the jacket space.
W8 Exports. `export/oil_dryer.step` (assembly); `export/oil_dryer.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/OIL_DRYER.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. Masses in `check.json`: plinth 94.2 kg, saddles 246.3 kg, drum 51.9 kg, jacket 190.2 kg, frame 112.5 kg, nozzles 0.6 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make: drum, jacket, supports, guard frame and hardware are in-house fabrication. No buy rows: no purchased item is named on the node or duty (hold, W12).
W10 Fabrication route. F-MACH (plan section 4: frame weldment, guards, purchased drive or electrical by rating, anchors): plate cut and roll of the drum, circumferential and end-head welds, jacket fabrication with end plates and leak check of the jacket space, nozzle set-in, saddle and guard-frame weldment, anchors. On-site tool: the metal shop (roll, weld, drill).
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the dryer conditions plastics feed; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) Drum wall 6 mm and jacket annulus 20 mm are study values; no authority states a wall. (2) No purchased items are named on the node or duty (no equipment row): the dryer drive and vacuum hardware are not drawn (A-MACH named hold). (3) Guard and anchor drawn per A-MACH: guard frame + 8 anchors. (4) Duty section 8: "No holdup basis for this class" — none claimed; the node internals row is none and no internal solid is drawn. (5) Fastener lengths and saddle fit are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

# Work order TI_CELL

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-CELL). Route class F-CELL (the `cell` class sits in the F-CELL row of plan section 4). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/TI_CELL.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/TI_CELL.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-DUTY-072 (sizing study), AQS-BAL-KROLL (loop closure), A-M-280 (chlorine ruling), A-M-284 (D-DOCK feedstock ruling), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node TI_CELL (design block). LIVE unit - the Kroll chlorinator + Mg-reduction retort train at the 0.5 kg/d Ti scale.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `ti_cell.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid: chlorinator retort and heater band, TiCl4 condenser and water band, Mg-reduction retort and furnace box, Mg charge hopper, interconnects, MgCl2 drag-out trough, and the skid frame with the retort lift beam. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `assumptions` and `design` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 800 × 800 × 1600 (cell-class study minimum, AQS-DUTY-072; batch retort campaigns at the 0.5 kg/d Ti scale, AQS-BAL-KROLL). The build fills it: the skid frame spans 800 × 800 × 1600 (the retort lift beam at z 1560..1600 pins the top); measured face overhang 0/0/0/0 mm (check.json, A-core 2); the vessel group bounding box 680 × 230 × 752 mm is recorded beside it.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn as the unit per the duty sheet and the design block: chlorinator retort OD 180 × 640 at 800 C with the heater band (TiO2 + 2C + 2Cl2 -> TiCl4 + 2CO), TiCl4 condenser OD 140 × 420 with the water band, Mg-reduction retort OD 200 × 520 at 900 C in the furnace box, Mg charge hopper cone, chlorinator->condenser->retort interconnects OD 24 t 2, and the MgCl2 drag-out trough to FLUX_PREP. The Ti sponge in the retort is drawn as a process-inventory solid (study campaign fill), not a BOM part. A-CELL note: the design row is thermochemical (I_A 0, no electrolysis - duty section 2), so the plate count/pitch/active-area criteria have no duty-stated values; the cell-class acceptance runs on the A-core set with this record (hold, W12). The chlorine loop closes with MG_POT: its N6 chlorinator feed is this train's N9, and the retort MgCl2 returns through N6 (A-M-280).
W4 Nozzles. All 14 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/TI_CELL.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore, attached to their vessels inside the envelope: N1 slag tap spout (solids, bore not calculated per W4) on the chlorinator +Y shell at z 600; N2 dense grit and N10 rutile and N11 carbon charge chutes (solids, bore not calculated) on the chlorinator −X face at z 160; N3/N9 chlorine DN15 stubs on the chlorinator −Y shell at z 160 (loop with MG_POT, A-M-280); N4/N5 harvest ports DN90-class on the retort +X shell at z 600 (campaign-end harvest); N6 MgCl2 return and N7 drag-out DN15 stubs on the retort +Y shell at z 600; N8 Mg reductant chute (solids, bore not calculated) on the retort −X face at z 160; N12 CO export DN15 (fuel-gas class) on the chlorinator +Y shell at z 600; N13 drain well through the chlorinator pedestal to the sump face z 50; N14 vent riser from the condenser top to the schedule datum z 800. Atmospheric joints; no flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06 - hold, W12).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 12 M10 retort-flange and harvest-port studs, 8 M8 hopper bolts, 4 M12 wedge anchors (skid base plate to slab); counts from the model. 8.8 on carbon steel per the grade rule (AQS-PLAN-006 W5); lengths and patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3 (X-MAT-22): carbon steel throughout the dry hot Cl2/TiCl4 class shells; the chlorinator hot zone carries the refractory class (heater band with element pockets); retort CS per the batch-campaign class. CA 0 mm - dry, non-hydrocarbon furnace service (AQS-STD-001 shop rule). The vacuum/Ar atmosphere hardware on the retort (AQS-DUTY-072) is loop hardware, not modeled (hold, W12).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric train; the duty section 6 relief case is pending (unit task 07) and no set point is claimed. The CO export leaves as fuel-gas class (N12). The campaign instruments (retort temperature, condenser coolant) are loop hardware; their sensor circuits are not modeled (hold, W12).
W8 Exports. `export/ti_cell.step` (assembly, 2 unioned solids); `export/ti_cell.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/TI_CELL.stl` replaced from the build solid (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 registry; the schedule hardware stays inside the envelope, `external_parts` empty). Bounding box 800 × 800 × 1600 mm (skid frame) against the node envelope: filled exactly, nothing resized. Masses in `check.json`: chlorinator + band 156.9 kg, condenser + band 62.3 kg, retort 128.2 kg, furnace box 158.3 kg, skid frame 564.3 kg, hardware the remainder.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make (vessels, bands, furnace box, hopper, interconnects, trough, chutes and stubs, skid frame); there is no purchased row - the duty carries no machine rating that maps to a cabinet (the 0.5 kW hold duty is resistance elements in the bands and box, bought with them at fabrication).
W10 Fabrication route. F-CELL (plan section 4): tank fabrication, plate shear and punch, bus bar cut and drill, gasket cut, stack assembly - in order: vessel shell roll and weld (chlorinator, condenser, retort), heater-band and furnace-box fabrication with element pockets, interconnect fit with the stated slope (dry Cl2 service, no dead legs), skid frame weld and pedestal set, nozzle set-in, then leak checks: chlorinator and interconnects dry-air, retort under vacuum, followed by the campaign test (chlorination run to TiCl4, then the reduction batch; sponge harvest at N4/N5). On-site tool: the metal shop welding bay with the refractory bench and the vacuum leak rig.
W11 Self-copy relevance. The train makes Ti sponge for the melt shop, not plant parts; the skid, bands and furnace box are shop-made. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference (tier-6 unit; the tier-7 A-SC set does not apply).
W12 Holds, named. (1) The envelope is the AQS-DUTY-072 cell-class study minimum; the sizing study gives the chemistry and the loop rates, not a vessel design. (2) Vessel diameters and heights, band thicknesses and the interconnect OD 24 t 2 are study values; no authority states a shell thickness. (3) A-CELL: the design row is thermochemical (I_A 0), so the plate count/size/pitch/active-area criteria have no duty-stated values; the bus criteria do not apply (no electrolysis) - recorded here per the class set. (4) Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06); the N14 vent 800 datum rides the condenser riser. (5) The relief case is pending (unit task 07); no instrument loop is issued (unit task 08). (6) The retort vacuum/Ar atmosphere hardware and the condenser coolant loop are loop hardware, not modeled. (7) Fastener lengths, stud torques and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication. (8) The Ti sponge inventory volume is the drawn study campaign fill, not a holdup basis (A-VES rule: no residence time claimed).

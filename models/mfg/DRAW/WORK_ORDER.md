## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `draw.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid: plinth, bench bed, die stand and die set, capstan group, drive and cabinet envelopes, guard rail, and the schedule hardware. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1500 × 800 × 1500 (A-M-199 forming class N+1 rated envelope, A-M-282). The build fills it: measured 1500.0 × 800.0 × 1500.0 mm, all connection hardware flush at the envelope faces, nothing protrudes.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid.
W4 Nozzles. All 3 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/DRAW.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore connections flush at the envelope faces (duty section 4: hose, atmospheric): N1 solids wire row on the +X face at z 600 riding the take-off guide, drawn at DN15 and labeled bore not calculated (W4 rule); N2 DN15 drain through the plinth, flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N3 DN15 vent riser on the capstan head, flush at the envelope top (its 800 datum is the schedule placeholder - hold, W12). The schedule outlet face is mapped to a compass face by the registered layout choice (params.json layout block). No flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M12 wedge anchors, plinth to slab (8.8 on carbon steel), 10 M12 stud-and-nut sets on the die-stand clamp and capstan bearing blocks (8.8 on carbon steel). Lengths and torque are study values, confirmed at fabrication. The press-fitted shaft/bearing joints carry no fastener row.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: tungsten-carbide drawing dies in tool-steel casings (consumable buy set) on a steel bench; the die-capstan hardware carries the machine lubricant programme - no wetted stream. CA 0 mm - dry process (AQS-STD-001 shop rule). The polished-diamond last-pass alternative is the duty alternative row and is not drawn (hold, W12).
W7 Pressure. No hydraulic circuit; the drive group is the purchased electrical package at the node rating. No design_kPa is booked on the node and none is claimed (duty section 6: relief case pending, unit task 07 - hold, W12). Not to the room.
W8 Exports. `export/draw.step` (assembly); `export/draw.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/DRAW.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1500.0 × 800.0 × 1500.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly, zero face overhang.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: plinth, bench bed and die stand, capstan group, guard rail, nozzle set. Buy: the carbide die set (consumable), the drive group and the control cabinet (node design rating, drawn as envelopes per plan rule 6). The standby state is named in the BOM rows and the build log.
W10 Fabrication route. F-MACH: frame weldment (bench bed and die stand), purchased drive and electrical by rating, guards made and set, anchors drilled, capstan and bearing blocks aligned, then a dry-chain pull trial and a first-article wire pass before release to standby (AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 F-MACH operations: frame weldment, guards, purchased drive or electrical by rating, anchors). On-site tool: the metal shop welding bay and the machine bay.
W11 Self-copy relevance. A-SC applies (tier 7): the bench draws the wire and rod the shop fabricates from, which is the AQS-BOM-SC-001 feedstock path (MFG WIRE_DRAW stays the production sizing line per the duty section 2 note; this bench is the shop's N+1 former); the parts of the machine itself the on-site shop can make - bench weldment, die stand, capstan group, guard - are flagged make in the BOM and cross-referenced to AQS-BOM-SC-001. The carbide die set is the purchased consumable.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The bench sections and capstan size are study values; no thickness is booked on the node. (2) The N3 vent datum 800 is the schedule placeholder. (3) The solids row (N1) is drawn at DN15, bore not calculated (W4 rule). (4) Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders, not designed elevations (unit tasks 02/06). (5) The drive group and control cabinet are purchased; the maker ratings read the sheets at order. (6) The relief case is pending (unit task 07). (7) The die-layout per wire class is a unit-task tooling item; the 6-die cascade is the node design count. (8) No instrument loop is issued for this unit yet (duty section 8). (9) Fastener lengths and torque are study values, confirmed at fabrication. (10) Wire sizing stays MFG WIRE_DRAW S57 per the duty section 2 note; this bench is the shop alternate, not the production line.

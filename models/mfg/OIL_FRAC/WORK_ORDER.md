# Work order OIL_FRAC

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-COL). Route class F-COL (the `frac` class sits in the F-COL row of plan section 4). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/OIL_FRAC.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/OIL_FRAC.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-MOD-002 (frame system), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node OIL_FRAC (cut_basis, vol_net_base).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `oil_frac.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration. This build closes the style-builders column study (M2) per the HX8 close pattern: the column rebuilt through the current shared lib, the 14-row issued schedule replacing the study layout, and the section 5 artifacts completed.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1000 × 600 × 800 — the envelope is computed by the write_graph scrubber pass each regen (STAGE_OWNED_SIZE), so the builder reads it from params and does not pin it. The build fills it exactly: measured 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm, one closed manifold mesh. A four-post hold-down frame (40 × 40 posts + top rails) pins the registry box around the column — the HX8 idiom, so the X-PAR-04 envelope registry reads the build (the shell itself is the OD 480 construction and does not fill the box alone).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid: no packing, distributor or demister is drawn (A-COL hold, W12). The machine is drawn as the unit: vertical carbon-steel column shell OD 480, wall 6 mm (study — the duty states no diameter and no wall; drawn to the envelope, W12), top and bottom heads, skirt seat, inside the hold-down frame on a 20 mm plinth.
W4 Nozzles. All 14 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/OIL_FRAC.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube stubs flush at the envelope faces: N1 DN25 (Pyro → frac), N2–N4 DN15 (resist and solvent waste; solvent and photoresist; organic mist), N12 DN15 (unrecovered wax) and N14 solids (spent resist and solvent — non-piped transfer drawn DN15, bore not calculated, plan W4) on −Y at z 160; N5–N8 DN15 (the four CUT lines), N11 DN15 (NCG only) and N13 DN15 (light cuts to POLY_OLEFIN) on +Y at z 600; N9 DN15 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N10 DN15 vent flush at the envelope top (800 datum is the box top). Atmospheric tube or hose per duty section 4 (nozzle DN15, no flange class); no flange hardware is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M12 wedge anchors (plinth to slab, 2 per frame post line), count from the model. 8.8 on carbon steel per the grade rule; length and pattern are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Column, heads, skirt, frame, plinth and nozzles carbon steel (duty section 3 "Carbon steel. Hydrocarbon. No aqueous chloride in this vessel"); CA 1.5 mm (AQS-STD-001 hydrocarbon rule) on the wetted shell and heads.
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6). No test pressure is claimed beyond a leak check of the finished shell.
W8 Exports. `export/oil_frac.step` (assembly); `export/oil_frac.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/OIL_FRAC.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. Masses in `check.json`: plinth 94.2 kg, frame 76.4 kg, column 102.9 kg, nozzles 0.9 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make: column, frame, plinth and hardware are in-house fabrication.
W10 Fabrication route. F-COL (plan section 4: column shell, packing support, distributor, demister, vacuum jacket where named — the packing/distributor/demister rows do not apply here, nothing is named on the node): plate cut and roll of the shell, circumferential and head welds, nozzle set-in, skirt seat, frame weldment, leak check. On-site tool: the metal shop (roll, weld, drill).
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the column separates oil cuts; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The duty states no column diameter and no wall: the shell is drawn to the node envelope (OD 480, wall 6 study), nothing resized (plan rule 3). (2) A-COL hold: stages and HTU are not stated anywhere — named hold; the cut split rides the node cut_basis pass fractions (A-M-138, SU2018_PYRO) — a flowsheet allocation, not tray design. (3) A-COL hold: no packing, distributor or demister is named on the node (internals none), so none is drawn. (4) Sump report: the duty section 2 sump 0.0006 m³ (30 min) stands 3.5 mm deep in the modeled bore — the sump zone is the bottom head; reported, nothing resized (plan rule 3). (5) The envelope is computed by the write_graph scrubber pass each regen: the builder reads it from params and does not pin it. (6) The N14 solids row is a non-piped transfer drawn DN15, bore not calculated (plan W4). (7) Fastener lengths and frame fit are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

# Work order Q4_BUS

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-PIPE). Route class F-PIPE. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/Q4_BUS.md`, `docs-authority/sizing/HEADERS.md` (AQS-DUTY-075, with AQS-HEAT-LADDER-001), `docs-authority/nozzles/Q4_BUS.csv`, `docs-authority/LINE_LIST.md` (AQS-PIP-004, the DN and class authority), `docs-authority/PIPING_CLASSES.md` (AQS-PIP-003), AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node Q4_BUS.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `q4_bus.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` and, for the A-PIPE line list, the unit's edges in the graph and the issued AQS-PIP-004 rows; it rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Parameters. Envelope 2000 × 50 × 50 mm at origin 12000, 0, 0 mm (placement seed), node size_note "DN32 manifold x 20 m run (0.009 m3), header sizing (AQS-DUTY-075)". design_kPa and relief_mm are null: the bus is atmospheric (duty section 6). The duty section 1/2 sentences read zero mass because the bus edges carry kW, not kg/d; the flow basis is the node design block (AQS-DUTY-075 winter concurrent case). The seed placement block (10000 × 80 × 80) is the placement grid cell, not the unit envelope.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid; none is drawn. The model is the bus as the node defines it: one manifold run, drawn as a 2000 mm representative spool. The related HX features (CRYO_HX_Q4, MET_HX_Q4, MAU_HX, the Q4 batteries) are separate units; the bus is the pipe/spool work. The 20 m run is carried by the per-line route lengths of AQS-PIP-004, not redrawn.
W4 Nozzles. All 9 rows of `docs-authority/nozzles/Q4_BUS.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as open-bore tube stubs flush at the envelope faces (lib/nozzles.place_on_face). Duty section 4 (line-list table): "Atmospheric lines are hose. DN is copied from the edge and is not recalculated."; the W4 110 % rule draws no flange because no process pressure is stated. The schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. The DN15 datums 10 mm are clamped up to 11.3 mm for the branch bore; the 160 mm datum (round-trip loss) is clamped down; the placeholders are unit tasks 02/06 (holds). The duty section 4 table (10 L-rows, several "bore not calculated") predates the issued schedule and names GEO_ORC on the rejection line where the graph and AQS-PIP-004 name DRY_COOLER; the issued rows govern (hold).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M10 wedge anchors, grade 8.8 on the carbon-steel plinth (plan grade rule). Spool and branch joints are PP-H fusion welds; there are no other bolted joints. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Manifold and branches PP-H SDR 11, 80 C class, fusion-welded (node design / AQS-DUTY-075); corrosion allowance 0 mm (AQS-STD-001 polymer rule). Plinth carbon steel (AQS-MOD-002 dry service class). Material basis conflict named: the issued AQS-PIP-004 rows class the Q4 water lines M1 (316L, the AQS-STD-002 water-loop audit) while the node design carries PP-H SDR 11; PP-H is drawn and both facts are recorded (hold W12); the re-selection belongs to the X-MAT lane.
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric (duty section 6: no vent edge, relief not to the room). No test pressure is claimed.
W8 Exports. `export/q4_bus.step` (assembly); `export/q4_bus.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/Q4_BUS.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 2000.0 × 50.0 × 50.0 mm against the 2000 × 50 × 50 envelope: inside, filled exactly. Masses in `check.json`: plinth 15.7 kg, manifold 0.79 kg, branches 0.02 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All hardware rows make.
W10 Fabrication route. F-PIPE: spool cut (DN32 PP-H to the Sch 40 table; SDR 11 wall per the purchased pipe), branch fusion joints at the schedule tags, trough plinth weld and anchor, line list, leak test of the assembled run. On-site tool: the plastics shop; the line list and spool sheet travel with the spool.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the bus moves heat; it makes no plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The envelope is the AQS-DUTY-075 sizing box (DN32 manifold × 20 m run); the model draws a 2000 mm representative spool of it; per-line lengths stay in the line list. (2) The pipe schedule is not stated on the node or in the duty; the geometry is drawn on the lib/dn Sch 40 table and the SDR 11 wall is the purchased pipe's property, confirmed at order. (3) The AQS-DUTY-075 ID grid (DN − 8 = 24 mm) and the Sch 40 bore (35.3 mm) disagree by the grid simplification; both seat DN32; nothing resized. (4) Material basis conflict: the issued rows class the Q4 water lines M1 (316L) and the node design says PP-H SDR 11; PP-H is drawn, both facts recorded, the re-selection belongs to the X-MAT lane. (5) Schedule datums sit in a 50 mm cross-section: the DN15 datums 10 mm are clamped up to 11.3 mm and the 160 mm datum is clamped down; designed elevations are unit tasks 02/06. (6) The Class 150 rating column stands against an atmospheric duty; no flange is drawn (duty section 4, W4 110 % rule). (7) Duty section 4 says "Atmospheric lines are hose"; the model draws tube stubs flush at the faces and the hose makeup is the connecting spool's joint at the face. (8) The duty section 4 table predates the issued schedule (its L-rows carry "bore not calculated" for the heat takes) and names GEO_ORC on the rejection line where the graph and AQS-PIP-004 name DRY_COOLER; the issued rows govern. (9) HL-Q4-A-BUS and HL-Q4-B-BUS are issued AQS-PIP-004 lines whose graph edges carry presence study: the lines are drawn in the issued list, the flows are study. (10) The trough-mount support scheme (plinth, no top rails) is the layout choice carried from the HDR_CO2 acceptance; anchor lengths and the branch pitch are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

Edge coverage is complete; no new edge is created.

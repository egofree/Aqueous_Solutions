# Work order WIRE_DRAW

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-MACH + A-SC). Route class F-MACH (the `forming` class sits in the F-MACH row of plan section 4). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/WIRE_DRAW.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/WIRE_DRAW.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-MFG-010 (Table 2.1 tool row, rev C metering split), A-M-177 (metal-forming basis), AQS-MOD-002 (frame standard), the DRAW accepted model commit e1e7d94b (bench idiom), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node WIRE_DRAW (continuous note).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `wire_draw.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1000 × 600 × 800 (the M0 study registry box). The build fills it: measured 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm from the export STL — the four-post hold-down/guard frame and the vent riser pin the box so the X-PAR-04 envelope registry reads the build.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn as the unit per the duty sheet and the AQS-MFG-010 tool row (Table 2.1, rev C metering split): bull-block wire drawer, nameplate 1.1 kW, energy basis 250 MPa × strain 3.0 / 0.55 (DIETER1988) = 0.0423 kWh/kg + no-load 15 % × 1 h (A-M-177), built on the DRAW accepted idiom (commit e1e7d94b) scaled to the node box — steel bench bed on legs, die stand with the tungsten-carbide die set in tool-steel casings (consumable buy set), bull-block take-up drum OD 300 with shaft, wire-guide sheaves, draw-fume extraction hood over the die zone, drive group and control cabinet as purchased envelopes.
W4 Nozzles. All 7 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/WIRE_DRAW.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube stubs flush at the envelope faces: N1 billet in on −Y at z 160 and N7 rod/wire stock in (a solids service row drawn DN15, bore not calculated, plan W4) beside it; N2 wire product out, N3 draw scrap out and N4 draw fume out on +Y at z 600; N5 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N6 vent flush at the envelope top (800 datum is the box top). Duty section 4: DN15, no flange class, atmospheric tube or hose; no flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06 — hold, W12).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M12 wedge anchors (plinth to slab, 2 per frame post line) and 4 M12 drum-bearing block bolts (bull-block bearing blocks to the frame bay), counts from the model. 8.8 on carbon steel per the grade rule; lengths and patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: 316L where the stream is wet — the drawer is a dry forming machine whose capstan hardware carries the machine lubricant programme, so CA 0 mm shop practice applies and the 316L row is carried for the connection stubs. Dies tungsten-carbide in tool-steel casings (DIETER1988 ch. 20/21; consumable buy set). Frame, bench, drum, guides and guards carbon steel A500 (AQS-MOD-002 dry service).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric machine; the duty section 6 relief case is pending (unit task 07) and no set point is claimed. Duty section 5: temperature or current with a high-temperature power trip — control hardware, sensor circuit not modeled (IND_MELT loop precedent — hold, W12).
W8 Exports. `export/wire_draw.step` (assembly, 11 solids); `export/wire_draw.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/WIRE_DRAW.stl` replaced from the in-envelope build solid (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 registry). Bounding box 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm against the node envelope: filled exactly.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make rows carry the AQS-BOM-SC-001 flag (A-SC): plinth, bench bed and die stand, bull block with shaft and guides, fume hood, guard rail, nozzle set. Buy rows (purchased per plan rule 6, ratings from the node/AQS-MFG-010): carbide die set (consumable), drive group (1.1 kW nameplate), control cabinet (maker TDS mass governs).
W10 Fabrication route. F-MACH (plan section 4: frame weldment, guards, purchased drive or electrical by rating, anchors): plate cut and weld (bench, die stand, hood), bull-block turning and journal bore (shop lathe), guide sheave turning, die-stand clamp fitting, guard assembly, drive set mounting, anchors, then a no-load spin check and a first draw through the die set. On-site tool: the metal shop (lathe, mill, welding bay); the dies are maker items.
W11 Self-copy relevance. The drawer makes wire product (S58) for the shop and goods lines, not plant parts; A-SC applies to the shop-made hardware flagged in the BOM with the AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference (tier-7 set).
W12 Holds, named. (1) Bench, die-stand, drum and guide dimensions are study values scaled from the DRAW idiom to the node box; no authority states a drum diameter or a bench length for this unit. (2) The die count (4) is a study value; the duty names no die count. (3) The N7 solids row is drawn DN15, bore not calculated (W4 rule). (4) Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06). (5) The drive-set and cabinet masses are table-density placeholders until the maker TDS quotes. (6) The high-temperature trip loop sensor circuit is not modeled. (7) The relief case is pending (unit task 07). (8) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication. (9) The duty section 8 open item stands: no machine envelope on the node beyond the M0 study box; the box is filled, nothing resized.

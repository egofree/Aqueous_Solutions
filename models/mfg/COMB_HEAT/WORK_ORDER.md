## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `comb_heat.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid: plinth, furnace shell, brick lining and castable crown, 310S radiant burner tube and burner block, hearth rail, flue duct, controls bay, and the schedule hardware. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1000 × 1000 × 1500 (A-M-199 melt class N+1 rated envelope, A-M-282). The build fills it: measured 1000.0 × 1000.0 × 1500.0 mm, all connection hardware flush at the envelope faces, nothing protrudes.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The hearth rail is furnace furniture - a BOM part, not an internal.
W4 Nozzles. All 3 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/COMB_HEAT.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore DN15 tube connections, flush at the envelope faces (duty section 4: hose, atmospheric; the connecting line makes the joint at the face): N1 DN15 combustion off-gas takeoff on +Y at z 600, riding the dry flue duct (duty section 3: flue path dry above the acid dew point into MET_SCRUB when energized); N2 DN15 drain through the plinth, flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N3 DN15 vent riser on the shell roof, flush at the envelope top (its 800 datum is the schedule placeholder - hold, W12). The schedule outlet face is mapped to a compass face by the registered layout choice (params.json layout block). No flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M12 wedge anchors, plinth to slab (8.8 on carbon steel), 12 M10 stud-and-nut sets on the shell-roof hatch and burner-block clamp (8.8 on carbon steel). Lengths and torque are study values, confirmed at fabrication. The welded joints carry no fastener row.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: alumina-silica brick + castable crown hot face (study t115, W12) on a mild-steel shell (study t4, W12); 310S radiant burner tube (combustion products stay inside the tube); CS plinth and hearth rail. CA 0 mm - dry furnace service (AQS-STD-001 shop rule).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric furnace; the radiant tube runs at the study burner duty with no booked design_kPa (duty section 6: relief case pending, unit task 07 - hold, W12). Not to the room. The flue is the dry path above the acid dew point per duty section 3; the connection to MET_SCRUB is at the envelope face.
W8 Exports. `export/comb_heat.step` (assembly); `export/comb_heat.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/COMB_HEAT.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1000.0 × 1000.0 × 1500.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly, zero face overhang.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: plinth, shell, brick lining and crown, radiant tube and burner block, hearth rail, flue duct, nozzle set. Buy: the controls cabinet (0.05 kW connected, node design, drawn as its envelope per plan rule 6) and the burner train (purchased at fabrication, W12). The standby state is named in the BOM rows and the build log.
W10 Fabrication route. Route hold (W12); operations named so the shop can bid it: plate cut and weld (CS fillet welds, ISO 2553), brick lining stack and crown cast, shell roof hatch fit, radiant-tube bore and set, flue duct weld, plinth weld and anchors, then dry-out cure curve of the refractory and a burner trial at the study duty before release to standby. On-site tool: the metal shop welding bay with the refractory bench.
W11 Self-copy relevance. The furnace provides reheating heat for the melt train; its acceptance set is A-core and A-SC does not apply (tier 6). The heat it makes backs up the shop-tools lane, where the AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference lives.
W12 Holds, named. (1) No AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 route class for class `furnace`; the tracker carries the route hold and W10 names the operations anyway. (2) The shell wall t4 and the brick t115 are study fab-page values; no thickness is booked on the node. (3) The N3 vent datum 800 is the schedule placeholder; the riser stands on the shell roof and flushes at the envelope top. (4) Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders, not designed elevations (unit tasks 02/06). (5) The burner train (valve train, ignition, flame supervision) is purchased at fabrication; the maker ratings read the sheets at order. (6) The relief/combustion-case study is pending (unit task 07). (7) The refractory dry-out cure curve is an operating-procedure item. (8) No instrument loop is issued for this unit yet (duty section 8). (9) Fastener lengths and torque are study values, confirmed at fabrication. (10) The C5-C10 fuel train interface is the plant product-store side, a unit-task line item.

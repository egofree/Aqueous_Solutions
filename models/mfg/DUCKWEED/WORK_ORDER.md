## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `duckweed.py` (CadQuery, on the shared close-out builder `models/mfg/farm_closeout.py`) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 30100 × 10100 × 700 - "300 m2 raceway at the A-M-420 revision, 400 mm water + 100 freeboard + 200 berm: a lined civil basin, HDPE/EPDM (X-FARM sec3)". The rebuild fills the box exactly: measured 30100.0 × 10100.0 × 700.0 mm, flush on all six faces (A-core 2; A-FARM: the raceway is drawn at the node box flush, not shrunk to fit the block).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid row. The liner the duty names is drawn: EPDM sheet 5 mm (floor + four walls to the berm base at z 500; the berm band stays bare - the GROW_BED ponded-zone idiom). The 400 mm water line is a size_note figure reported, not drawn: the water and the duckweed mat are process inventory, not BOM parts. The raceway flow layout (baffles, paddlewheel) has no node layout: not drawn (hold, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 17 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/DUCKWEED.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube stubs flush at the envelope faces: N1 in on −Y at z 80 (nutrient water) with N2 beside it (DN50, nitrate water); N9/N17 in on −Y at z 160 (loop water, mineral nitrate solution); harvest rows N3-N6 on +Y at z 300 (the water-line class: duckweed, duckweed reserve); N10-N16 out on +Y at z 600 (the ration/forage/galley rows); N7 drain flush at the envelope bottom; N8 vent flush at the envelope top - the schedule z 400 datum predates the 700 box (stale datum, hold W12). Atmospheric joints are tube/hose; no flange is drawn (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 24 M6 liner batten bolts (polymer supplier rule, A4-70; the liner is held by batten strips, not adhesive; study pattern) and 8 M12 base anchors (8.8 carbon steel per the grade rule). Confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: HDPE or PVC for the water, EPDM liner class. No metal wetted part; corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymer (AQS-STD-001 rule). Liner roll is a buy good (plan rule 6); the liner mass is the drawn-sheet figure at the EPDM table density.
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric; duty section 6 states the relief case is pending and no set point is claimed. Duty section 5: no trip specified. Duty section 2 circulation (9697.9 kg/d, two-minute holdup 0.0135 m3) is reported on the duty sheet, not a residence-time claim. No test pressure is claimed.
W8 Exports. `export/duckweed.step` (assembly, 2 solids); `export/duckweed.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/DUCKWEED.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 30100.0 × 10100.0 × 700.0 mm against the node envelope: flush, no misfit. Masses in `check.json`: basin shell 3414.9 kg, liner sheet 1962.8 kg, nozzles 0.2 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: basin shell, nozzle set. Buy: the EPDM liner roll (plan rule 6).
W10 Fabrication route. On-site tool: the thermoplastic (polymer) bench with the farm fit-out crew; the liner is a buy roll.

# Work order GAS_ATOMIZER

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-MACH + A-SC). Route class F-MACH (the `forming` class sits in the F-MACH row of plan section 4). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/GAS_ATOMIZER.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/GAS_ATOMIZER.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-MFG-010 (Table 2.1 tool row, rev C metering split), A-M-177 (atomiser basis), AQS-MOD-002 (frame standard), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node GAS_ATOMIZER (continuous note).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `gas_atomizer.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1000 × 600 × 800 (the M0 study registry box). The build fills it: measured 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm from the export STL — the four-post hold-down/guard frame pins the box so the X-PAR-04 envelope registry reads the build.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn as the unit per the duty sheet and the AQS-MFG-010 tool row (Table 2.1, rev C metering split): close-coupled gas atomiser with Ar recirculation, nameplate 5.5 kW, energy basis Ar GMR 2.5 at 30 bar: 0.246 kWh/kg × 3.56 kg/d + tundish 1 kW × 1 h (A-M-177: GMR 2.5, 30 bar, compressor isothermal eta 0.6, 10 min pour, 1 kW tundish) — atomization tower OD 300 (wall 6, study) with powder-collection pot, close-coupled gas ring (R 60, 4 ports, GMR 2.5 study) at the tower neck, heated tundish OD 160 on a stand over the tower (1 kW tundish duty), Ar recirculation loop DN32 to the compressor envelope (30 bar class), control cabinet as a purchased envelope.
W4 Nozzles. All 7 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/GAS_ATOMIZER.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube stubs flush at the envelope faces: N1 melt in on −Y at z 160 and N5 standby melt row (the IND_TO_ATOM study edge, "when powder required" — zero on the design day) beside it; N2 metal powder out, N6 powder stock out and N7 off-spec remelt out (solids service rows drawn DN15, bore not calculated, plan W4) on +Y at z 600; N3 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N4 vent flush at the envelope top (800 datum is the box top). Duty section 4: DN15, no flange class, atmospheric tube or hose; no flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated on the node (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06 — hold, W12).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M12 wedge anchors (plinth to slab, 2 per frame post line) and 8 M10 tower-flange bolts (tower neck and pot flanges), counts from the model. 8.8 on carbon steel, A4-70 on 316L per the grade rule; lengths and patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: 316L where the stream is wet — melt-contact hardware (tundish, tower, ring) 316L; the powder loop is dry, so CA 0 mm shop practice applies. Frame, plinth carbon steel A500 (AQS-MOD-002 dry service).
W7 Pressure. No design_kPa is stated on the node; the 30 bar Ar loop class is the A-M-177 study basis and the relief case is pending (unit task 07) — no set point is claimed. The loop hardware is drawn as envelopes; no flange is drawn (W4 110 % rule). Duty section 5: temperature or current with a high-temperature power trip — control hardware, sensor circuit not modeled (IND_MELT loop precedent — hold, W12).
W8 Exports. `export/gas_atomizer.step` (assembly, 11 solids); `export/gas_atomizer.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/GAS_ATOMIZER.stl` replaced from the in-envelope build solid (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 registry). Bounding box 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm against the node envelope: filled exactly.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make rows carry the AQS-BOM-SC-001 flag (A-SC): plinth, hold-down frame, tower and powder pot, gas ring, tundish + stand, Ar loop runs, nozzle set. Buy rows (purchased per plan rule 6, ratings from the node/AQS-MFG-010): Ar compressor set (30 bar class, iso eta 0.6), control cabinet (5.5 kW nameplate), tundish heater set (1 kW; maker TDS mass governs).
W10 Fabrication route. F-MACH (plan section 4: frame weldment, guards, purchased drive or electrical by rating, anchors): plate cut and roll (tower shell), weld (tower heads, pot, stands, loop runs), ring machining (4 gas ports, shop mill), tundish spin and fit, loop run fit and pressure-leak check at the shop air class (the 30 bar Ar proof stays with the maker test), anchors, then a no-gas spin dry run and a first pour at the 10 min pour class. On-site tool: the metal shop (mill, welding bay); the compressor set is a maker item.
W11 Self-copy relevance. The atomiser makes metal powder for the shop powder line (S60), not plant parts; A-SC applies to the shop-made hardware flagged in the BOM with the AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference (tier-7 set).
W12 Holds, named. (1) Tower, tundish, ring and pot dimensions are study values sized to the 3.56 kg/d class; no authority states a tower bore or a ring geometry beyond the GMR 2.5 study basis. (2) The three solids rows (N6, N7 and the N5 standby melt row is a study edge at zero) are drawn DN15, bore not calculated (W4 rule); N5 stays closed on the design day. (3) Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06). (4) The compressor and cabinet masses are table-density placeholders until the maker TDS quotes. (5) The 30 bar Ar loop proof test and the relief case (unit task 07) stay with the maker/owner tasks; no set point is claimed here. (6) The high-temperature trip loop sensor circuit is not modeled. (7) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication. (8) The duty section 8 open item stands: no machine envelope on the node beyond the M0 study box; the box is filled, nothing resized.

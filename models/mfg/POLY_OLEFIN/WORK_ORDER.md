# Work order POLY_OLEFIN

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-VES). Route class F-VES. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/POLY_OLEFIN.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/POLY_OLEFIN.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node POLY_OLEFIN (design block, AQS-DUTY-073 / AQS-SIZE-OIL-POLY).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `poly_olefin.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` and, for the reactor construction figures, the node design block of the graph; it rebuilds every solid. The `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks of `params.json` are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 600 × 600 × 1200 (the reactor-class study minimum: DN150 at 1 d hold-up of the naphtha feed at 750 kg/m3 melt density; wall by ASME VIII-1 UG-27 at 5 MPa). The build fills it: measured 600.0 × 600.0 × 1200.0 mm; the vent riser tops the box (the X-PAR-04 registry gate).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The node names no agitator and no jacket - none is drawn (hold, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 6 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/POLY_OLEFIN.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube connections flush at the envelope faces (duty section 4: hose, atmospheric; the connecting line makes the joint at the face - the CATCH_TANK idiom): N1 light cuts, N2 methanol co-feed and N4 C5-C10 naphtha in on −Y at their 160 datum; N3 polyolefin resin out on +Y at its 600 datum - the row carries the line-list classification `solids` (non-piped transfer) in its size column: drawn at DN15, bore not calculated, per plan W4, and labeled; N5 drain boss under the base head, flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N6 vent riser on the top head, flushing at the envelope top (the 800 datum is the schedule placeholder - hold, W12). No flange is drawn (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. The along-face positions are the registered layout choice (per tag in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M12 wedge anchors, grade 8.8, plinth to slab, one per corner. Welded joints carry no fastener row. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Vessel SA-240 316L, welded (ASME IX) per duty section 3 (the node design row matches); wall 3.8 mm per the node design row (UG-27 at the 5 MPa design). CA 0 mm (AQS-STD-001 rule). Stand carbon steel (dry service).
W7 Pressure. Design 5 MPa on the vessel (the node sizing block, UG-27 basis; the op window is 150-250 °C at 1-5 MPa). The face connection hardware is atmospheric tube per duty section 4. Duty section 6: relief case study pending (unit task 07) - named, nothing invented. No instrument loop yet (unit task 08). The leak test is a route operation (W10).
W8 Exports. `export/poly_olefin.step` (assembly); `export/poly_olefin.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/POLY_OLEFIN.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 600.0 × 600.0 × 1200.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. The skid chain fuses to one solid; the face stubs stay separate until the connecting lines make their joints (`check.json` `solids_note`). A-VES: the gross shell volume (3.6 L) is reported; the 1 d hold-up of the naphtha feed (0.39 L at 750 kg/m3) is the node envelope basis (existing), and no residence time beyond it is derived.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make.
W10 Fabrication route. F-VES: plate cut, roll, GTAW per ASME IX practice, nozzle set-in, hydro at 1.3 × the 5 MPa design (UG-99); stand weldment and anchors. On-site tool: the metal shop pressure-work bay.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the reactor makes polyolefin resin; it makes no plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The vessel sits high in the study box (base head at z 616) so the vent riser fills the envelope to the box top - the elevation is the registered layout choice; nothing resized. (2) The node names no agitator and no jacket - none drawn. (3) The N3 solids row carries `solids` (non-piped transfer): drawn DN15, bore not calculated, per plan W4. (4) The schedule datums 160/600 are placeholders pending vessel design (unit tasks 02/06); they draw as issued at the envelope faces. (5) The vent riser flushes at the envelope top; the 800 datum is the schedule placeholder. (6) Relief case (unit task 07) and instrument loop (unit task 08) are open on the duty sheet; nothing invented here. (7) The duty claims no holdup figure beyond the node envelope basis (1 d at 750 kg/m3); the gross shell volume is reported, no new residence time derived (A-VES). (8) The head depths and stand geometry are study values (the node names the shell D/H/wall only). (9) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

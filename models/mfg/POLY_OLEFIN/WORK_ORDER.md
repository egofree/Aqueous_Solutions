# Work order POLY_OLEFIN

Authority: AQS-DUTY-073 (X-MFG-30 pattern). In-house fabrication; no bought reactor.

1. Roll and weld the DN150 shell, 3.8 mm wall, height 225 mm; cut the bottom and lid discs (SA-240 316L, welded (ASME IX)).
2. Weld the longitudinal seam and the bottom; drill the lid for 8 M10 bolts.
3. Set 2 DN25 nozzles (feed at the side, product at the bottom) and the rupture disc seat.
4. Weld 3 legs; fit the agitator gland and the heater band.
5. Test: hydrostatic to 1.3 x 5.0 design (ASME VIII-1 UG-99 class intent); dry the heater circuit.
6. Inspect against models/mfg/POLY_OLEFIN/export/poly_olefin.step; record dimensions on the traveller.

# Work order OIL_PYRO

Authority: AQS-DUTY-062 (X-MFG-31). In-house fabrication; no bought vessel.

1. Cut and roll the shell DN200, 4.5 mm, height 170 mm; cut the bottom and lid discs (SA-240 304H plate, rolled, GTAW (ASME IX)).
2. Weld the longitudinal seam and the bottom; weld the lid flange ring.
3. Cut and weld 5 DN25 nozzles: oil feed in, vapour out to OIL_FRAC, char out, thermowell, bursting disc.
4. Weld 3 legs; drill 8 lid bolt holes.
5. Fit the band heaters, thermowell, insulation and bursting disc.
6. Test: hydrostatic test at 1.3 x design pressure (ASME VIII-1 UG-99); heater insulation resistance; dry-run to 500 C.
7. Inspect against models/mfg/OIL_PYRO/export/oil_pyro.step; record dimensions on the traveller.

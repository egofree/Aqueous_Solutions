# Work order MET_MELT

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-VES + A-SC). Route class F-VES (the `melt` class sits in the F-VES row of plan section 4). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/MET_MELT.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/MET_MELT.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-MFG-010 (Table 2.1 tool row, rev C metering split), A-M-177 (melt basis), AQS-MOD-002 (frame standard), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node MET_MELT (continuous note).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `met_melt.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1000 × 600 × 800 (the M0 study registry box). The build fills it: measured 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm from the export STL — the four-post hold-down frame and the hood vent riser pin the box so the X-PAR-04 envelope registry reads the build.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn as the unit per the duty sheet and the AQS-MFG-010 tool row (Table 2.1, rev C metering split): coreless induction melt furnace, 10 kg Cu crucible class, nameplate 7.5 kW — CS casing OD 420 (wall 6, study) with heads, refractory hot face 40 mm, induction coil band (copper), clay-graphite crucible OD 110 bore 80, tilt trunnions and pour lip toward +Y over the three melt-out rows, fume hood with the vent riser at the box top, heat shield plate under the frame line, power-supply cabinet as a purchased envelope. The melt in the crucible (0.003 m³ at the melt line) is drawn as a process-inventory solid, not a BOM part and not an internal (IND_MELT W3 idiom).
W4 Nozzles. All 7 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/MET_MELT.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube stubs flush at the envelope faces: N1 cathode sheet charge in on −Y at z 160; N7 off-spec powder return (a solids service row) on −Y at z 160, drawn DN15, bore not calculated (plan W4); N2 billet, N3 melt-to-mill and N4 melt-to-atomizer pour rows on +Y at z 600 under the pour lip; N5 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N6 vent on the hood riser flush at the envelope top — the 800 datum is the box top. Duty section 4: DN15, no flange class, atmospheric tube or hose; no flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06 — hold, W12).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M12 wedge anchors (plinth to slab, 2 per frame post line) and 4 M12 trunnion studs (trunnion blocks to the casing plates), counts from the model. 8.8 on carbon steel per the grade rule; lengths and patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: graphite or refractory on the hot face (refractory liner charge drawn; supplier TDS governs at order); frame A500 behind a heat shield; CA 0 mm on the frame — dry, non-hydrocarbon furnace service (AQS-STD-001). Crucible clay-graphite (buy consumable, change-out per campaign, IND_MELT W6 idiom). Coil copper tube (study section, W12). Plinth, frame, casing, hood and shield carbon steel A500 (AQS-MOD-002 dry service).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric furnace; the duty section 6 relief case is pending (unit task 07) and no set point is claimed. Duty section 5: temperature or current with a high-temperature power trip — the loop is control hardware; its sensor circuit is not modeled (IND_MELT loop precedent — hold, W12).
W8 Exports. `export/met_melt.step` (assembly, 9 solids); `export/met_melt.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/MET_MELT.stl` replaced from the in-envelope build solid (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 registry). Bounding box 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm against the node envelope: filled exactly.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make rows carry the AQS-BOM-SC-001 flag (A-SC): plinth, hold-down frame, casing, coil, trunnions and pour lip, heat shield, hood, nozzle set. Buy rows (purchased per plan rule 6, ratings from the node/AQS-MFG-010): clay-graphite crucible (consumable), refractory liner charge, 7.5 kW power-supply cabinet (maker TDS mass governs).
W10 Fabrication route. F-VES (plan section 4, the `melt` class): plate or sheet cut, roll, weld, nozzle set-in, leak test — in order: plate cut and fold (casing, hood, shield), CS weld (casing and heads), refractory fit (hot-face liner), coil wind and clamp, trunnion bore and fit, nozzle set-in, then a leak check of the casing and a dry-out heat-up test, first melt at the 10 kg batch. On-site tool: the metal shop welding bay with the refractory bench; the crucible is a maker item.
W11 Self-copy relevance. The furnace makes melt stock for the mill, atomizer and wire drawer (tier-7 forming train), not plant parts; A-SC applies to the shop-made hardware flagged in the BOM with the AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference (tier-7 set).
W12 Holds, named. (1) Casing OD, wall, hot-face thickness, coil section and crucible dimensions are study values sized to the 10 kg Cu class at the bench box; no authority states a bore or a wall. (2) The N7 solids row is drawn DN15, bore not calculated (W4 rule). (3) Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06). (4) The power-supply cabinet and crucible masses are table-density placeholders until the maker TDS quotes. (5) The high-temperature trip loop sensor circuit is not modeled. (6) The relief case is pending (unit task 07). (7) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication. (8) The duty section 8 open item stands: the holdup assumes 1000 kg/m³; the melt-line figure is reported, nothing recomputed here.

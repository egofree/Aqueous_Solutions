# Work order MG_POT

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-CELL). Route class F-CELL (the `cell` class sits in the F-CELL row of plan section 4). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/MG_POT.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/MG_POT.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-DUTY-072 (sizing study), A-M-280 (Kroll chlorine ruling), A-M-282 (Faraday class), A-M-284 (D-DOCK feedstock ruling), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node MG_POT (design block). LIVE unit - the Kroll magnesium recycle leg.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `mg_pot.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid: casing, lining, hood group, head plate, electrode bank, busbar ladder, DN80 offtake riser, and the schedule hardware. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `assumptions`, `anode`, `cathode`, `busbar`, `design` and `layout_note` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 800 × 800 × 1600 (cell-class study minimum, AQS-DUTY-072; the 0.51 kg/d Mg leg of the 0.5 kg/d Ti line, A-M-284). The build fills it: measured 800.0 × 800.0 × 1600.0 mm from the export STL - the flush-hardware build keeps every connection group inside the faces (check.json `connection_overhang_mm` 0.3/0.3/0/0.1 mm, A-core 2).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn as the unit per the duty sheet and the design block: molten MgCl2 electrolysis cell at 700 C - CS casing t6 (walls + roof + head plate at z 1154), ceramic-fibre backup t24 + graphite ram liner t40 (wetted faces), 4 x D50 graphite anodes at 300 mm immersion, 4 cathode plates 110 × 12 × 400 on the −Y ram face (A-CELL: the design row `cathodes 4`), hood with graphite hot plate t10, DN80 offtake riser with return dip leg. The bath inventory (salt + Mg metal layer at the bath top z 400) is drawn as process-inventory solids, not BOM parts and not internals (AL_POT idiom).
W4 Nozzles. All 8 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/MG_POT.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore, flush at the envelope faces: N1 Mg metal tap (solids row, DN80 well group - bore not calculated per W4) on +X at z 600; N5 Mg reductant tap (solids row, bore not calculated) on −X at z 600; N2 Cl2 return and N6 chlorine to the Kroll chlorinator, DN15 on +Y at z 600; N3 Kroll MgCl2 return and N4 brine inlet, DN15 launders on −Y at z 160 with the brine lance into the bath; N7 DN15 drain flush at the bottom (50 datum); N8 DN15 vent on the DN80 riser at z 800. Atmospheric joints; no flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06 - hold, W12).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 6 M10 anode-clamp studs, 12 M8 hood bolts, 4 M10 slab anchors; counts from the model. 8.8 on carbon steel per the grade rule (AQS-PLAN-006 W5); lengths and patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3 (X-MAT-22): graphite anodes and ram liner on the wetted faces (ULLMANN_MAGNESIUM pot class; DING2021_ENG, KRUIZENGA2012; change-out at the unit task 12 work order); steel cathode plates; ceramic-fibre backup (SCHACHT2004 class); CS casing, hood and head plate below the liner. The pot-feed import leg stays PVDF (P3) and the headspace stays dry - MgCl2 is hygroscopic; no PVDF hardware is drawn on this unit (the import leg is the WS/dock lane's). CA 0 mm on the graphite; the CS shell is dry outside the lining (AQS-STD-001 shop rule). Bus hardware Cu on ceramic pads, frame-isolated per AQS-STD-002 section 11.
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric cell; the duty section 6 relief case is pending (unit task 07) and no set point is claimed. The Cl2 headspace leaves on the dry hot line to the Kroll chlorinator (A-M-280: N6 feed, N2 return net of drag-out). The rectifier interlocks (hood draft, bath temperature, cover position) are loop hardware; their sensor circuits are not modeled (AL_POT precedent - hold, W12).
W8 Exports. `export/mg_pot.step` (assembly, 1 unioned solid); `export/mg_pot.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/MG_POT.stl` replaced from the in-envelope build solid (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 registry; the N1/N5 tap groups, N3/N4 launders and N2/N6 stubs are the check.json `external_parts` record, drawn flush). Bounding box 800.0 × 800.0 × 1600.0 mm against the node envelope: filled exactly, nothing resized - the A-core 2 overshoot the §7 row carried from the Oct-31 build is cleared by the flush rebuild. Masses in `check.json`: casing + head plate 316.5 kg, lining 314.7 kg, hood group 84.4 kg, electrodes 32.0 kg, hardware and stubs the remainder.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make rows: shell, linings, hood group, cathode set, bus ladder, clamp bars, riser group, tap groups and launders. Buy rows: the anode bank (consumable graphite, Faraday I = 54.89 A at CE 0.85, A-M-282) and the 1 kW thyristor rectifier + bath-hold cabinet (purchased per plan rule 6; AQS-DUTY-072; mass per the purchase datasheet at order).
W10 Fabrication route. F-CELL (plan section 4): tank fabrication, plate shear and punch, bus bar cut and drill, gasket cut, stack assembly - in order: plate cut and fold (casing, roof, hood), CS weld (6 mm fillets, ISO 2553), lining install (fibre + ram, launder and drain passes cast in), head plate and bus ladder fit with ceramic pads, electrode set and clamp, nozzle set-in, then a dry-out and melt-up heat test and the trial electrolysis with the N1 tap and the Cl2 route to the chlorinator. On-site tool: the metal shop welding bay with the refractory bench; anodes and rectifier are maker items.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the pot makes Kroll-loop magnesium stock, not plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The envelope is the AQS-DUTY-072 cell-class study minimum; the sizing study gives chemistry and the Faraday current, not a tank design. (2) Shell t6, lining t24/t40, hood plate t10 and the 400 mm cathode plate are study values; no authority states a thickness or plate size. (3) The design row states `cathodes 4` and the busbar class 50 mm2 (drawn 2 x 10 x 5 rails); it names no plate size or anode count - the 4-anode bank is the study layout (AL_POT family idiom), drawn j 0.0284 A/cm2 reported, no rate claimed. (4) Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06); the N8 vent 800 datum rides the riser (riser top 1594). (5) The relief case is pending (unit task 07); no instrument loop is issued (unit task 08). (6) Rectifier and anode masses are table-density placeholders until the maker datasheets quote. (7) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication. (8) The bath-inventory volumes are the drawn study geometry, not a holdup basis (A-VES rule: no residence time claimed).

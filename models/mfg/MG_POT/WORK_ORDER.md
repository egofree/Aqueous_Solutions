# Work order MG_POT

Authority: AQS-DUTY-072 (X-MFG-30 pattern). LIVE unit - D-DOCK feedstock program (A-M-284).

1. Fabricate the 800 x 800 x 1000 mm shell; line 70 mm (casing + ceramic fibre + ram) for 700 C service.
2. Set the 3 cathode plates on the pocket wall; hang the 4 x D50 mm anodes at 300 mm immersion.
3. Fit the busbar ladder (50 mm2 Cu class for 55 A) and the DC drops; torque and megger-check.
4. Mount the hood, DN80 offtake with dip leg, and the feed chute with rotary seal.
5. Wire the 1 kW rectifier; interlock hood draft, bath temperature and cover position.
6. Test: dry-out and melt-up curve, then trial electrolysis; route the off-gas per the Cl2 ruling (1.4813 kg/d Cl2 to the Kroll loop (AQS-BAL-KROLL)).
7. Inspect against models/mfg/MG_POT/export/mg_pot.step; record dimensions on the traveller.

# Work order EW_5

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-CELL). Route class F-CELL. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/EW_5.md`, `docs-authority/sizing/EW_CELLS.md` (AQS-DUTY-051), `docs-authority/nozzles/EW_5.csv`, `docs-authority/BUSBAR.md` (AQS-BUS-001), `docs-authority/MATERIALS_STD.md` (AQS-STD-002) sections 8, 9 and 11, `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node EW_5.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `ew_5.py` (CadQuery) reads the graph node through `models/mfg/lib/graph_node.py` and rebuilds every solid: the envelope, graph revision and nozzle schedule come from the node and the unit CSV, the electrode bank and currents from the AQS-DUTY-051 Rev F sizing stamp, the bus pack from `docs-authority/BUSBAR.json` (AQS-BUS-001 Rev D). Built on the EW_1 pattern per its WORK_ORDER recipe: never a copy of EW_1 figures. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Parameters. Graph sizing 270 × 720 × 760 mm at origin 11300, 4000, −6000 mm (placement seed). design_kPa and relief_mm are null: the cell is atmospheric (duty section 6); the stored design_kPa on other nodes is an old head-plus-10 figure and is not a set point.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The electrode bank is drawn anyway because A-CELL requires it from duty section 2: 1 cathode sheet 600 × 420 × 3 mm titanium Grade 2 with PVC-U edge-strip clips, 2 anode sheets 600 × 420 × 1.5 mm MMO-coated titanium mesh drawn as its sheet envelope (AQS-PLAN-006 rule 6), hanger tabs rising to the bus. The cell-to-cell launder (duty section 3) is the inter-cell piping scope (AQS-STD-002 section 11 free-fall with air break): the N2 overflow connection is drawn; no launder solid is invented.
W4 Nozzles. All 8 schedule rows of `docs-authority/nozzles/EW_5.csv` are present at tag, face, height, bore and rating (`check.nozzles_drawn`), drawn flush at the envelope faces (lib/nozzles.place_on_face). The side-face rows re-base into the cover gas zone band z 690-748 in OD-aware slots - the issued datums (487/600/650) predate the Rev F zone stack (W12); the overflow discharge N2 sits at the 680 fill line on its issued face at the discharge end; rows N1, N7 re-face to the +X discharge-end wall with their hardware; the drain N4 and the vent N5 keep their issued datums. Atmospheric joints are hose barbs (duty section 4); no flange anywhere because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule). The power terminal N8 is a cable schedule row: carried verbatim, never drawn. The along-face stations and the re-based heights are the registered layout choice (params.json layout block).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M10 wedge anchors (tank to slab), 4 M8 beam bolts, 4 M8 vent-post bolts, 3 M6 electrode tab clamps (one per tab), 6 M10 rectifier-lug bolts (3 per pole, overlap at least the bar width per AQS-BUS-001 section 4). All ISO 3506 A4-70 by the plan grade rule (polymer joints; supplier rule named in the rows). Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Tank and racks PVC-U (duty section 3 via the EW_1 page: the liquor arrives at 55 °C; AQS-STD-002 M5 PVC-U limit 60 °C); wall 10 mm (AQS-DUTY-051 section 1). Cathode titanium Grade 2 t3, anode titanium mesh t1.5 MMO (AQS-STD-002 section 9). Corrosion allowance 0 mm on the titanium and none on polymers (AQS-STD-001 shop rule). Bus copper C101 above the liquor line, contacts Ti-clad, FRP standoffs, no busbar on a tank lip (AQS-STD-002 sections 9 and 11).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric. The vent N5 hangs to its 650 mm datum on a rim bridge; Duty section 6 records no vent edge on the graph; the schedule vent row is drawn as issued. The gas capture hood itself is not dimensioned in any authority and is not invented.
W8 Exports. `export/ew_5.step` (assembly with the liquor inventory); `export/ew_5.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/EW_5.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box and masses in `check.json`; liquor drawn net of solids against the 0.117 m³ fill basis.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Purchased rows (anode meshes, edge-strip stock, standoffs, rectifier) carry the order-time mass note.
W10 Fabrication route. F-CELL: tank plate cut and thermoplastic weld (PVC-U sheet, hot-gas / extrusion welds, set-in nozzles solvent-welded), cathode shear and punch (Ti G2 sheet; annealed, no cold forming after anneal), bus cut and drill (Cu C101 flats, lug overlap ≥ bar width), gasket/hose makeup at the face plane, stack assembly, leak test of the shell. On-site tool: the plastics shop and the bench line; the Ti sheets and the MMO anodes are bought in.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the cell makes metal, not plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) Nozzle re-basing: the issued datums (487/600/650) predate the AQS-DUTY-051 Rev F zone stack; the side-face connections re-base into the cover gas zone band z 690-748 in OD-aware slots, and the rows that do not fit re-face to the +X discharge-end wall with their hardware. The schedule datums stay in the CSV; the drawn stations are check.nozzles_drawn. (2) Working level: the sizing fill 0.117 m³ reads to the top of the cover zone (z 680); the overflow discharge is drawn at that fill line (the launder datum). (3) Gas capture hood: not dimensioned anywhere; not modeled. (4) Designed nozzle elevations are unit tasks 02/06; the drawn stations are the registered re-basing layout (hold 1) until those tasks land. (5) N3 carries service "plated sheet" at DN15; the issued schedule is drawn verbatim. (6) Hose barb profile and fastener lengths are study proportions, finished against supplier prints. (7) The issued schedule carries no rectifier connection row (the EW_1 schedule alone names one); the drawn bus pair carries rectifier lugs at the −X bar ends (z 691), and the connection routing is the rectifier task's to place. (8) Anode coat life is a vendor figure, not assumed.

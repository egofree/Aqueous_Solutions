# Work order SOLAR_PV_CELL

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-MACH + A-SC). Route class F-MACH (the `tool` class sits in the F-MACH row of plan section 4). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/SOLAR_PV_CELL.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/SOLAR_PV_CELL.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-MOD-002 (frame system), AQS-BOM-SC-001 (self-copy BOM), AQS-PV-001 Rev C (`docs-authority/PV_CELL_MOD.md`, the PV cell module page), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node SOLAR_PV_CELL (design tool table; X-MFG-38, A-M-178, AQS-DUTY-071).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `solar_pv_cell.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 2400 × 1200 × 1600 ("L x W x H mm, issued envelope (X-MFG-38 tool line)"); the duty section 2 envelope line agrees. The build fills it exactly: measured 2400.0 × 1200.0 × 1600.0 mm, one closed manifold mesh. A four-post hold-down frame (40 × 40 posts + top rails) pins the registry box — the HX8 idiom, so the X-PAR-04 envelope registry reads the build.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn per the node design tool table (X-MFG-38): seven named stations as envelopes with the node ratings — PV-TEX-01 KOH texture bath (10 L, 80 °C, immersion heater, 0.687 kWh/d), PV-EMIT-01 POCl₃ diffusion, PV-PSG-01 HF PSG etch bath (ambient), PV-ARC-01 PECVD SiNx (chamber with the 5 kg heated platen, dry vacuum pump envelope purchased at the A-M-178 1.5 kW basis), PV-CON-01 screen printer and 200 °C dryer, PV-FIRE-01 IR fast-firing furnace, PV-IV-01 flash IV tester. The diffusion station is internally resolved per its node dims: quartz tube 200 mm × 1.0 m (wall 5, study — the node gives OD and length, not wall) with the 200 mm ceramic-fibre annulus (the node text "200 mm ceramic fibre" taken at face value as the fibre thickness). Station arrangement and every unstated envelope size are the layout choice (W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 4 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/SOLAR_PV_CELL.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric tube stubs flush at the envelope faces: N1 DN15 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N2 DN15 vent flush at the envelope top — its 800 datum predates the 1600 box and is carried as a placeholder (hold, W12); N3 DN15 (the `solids` row: dopant concentrate in) on −Y at z 160 and N4 DN15 (the `solids` row: dopant into the wafer fab) on +Y at z 600 — non-piped transfer rows drawn DN15, bore not calculated (plan W4). Atmospheric hose per duty section 4 ("Bore not calculated. Rating hose, atmospheric"); no flange hardware is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M12 wedge anchors (base plate to slab, 2 per frame post line) and 4 M10 tube-stand bolts (diffusion tube stands, 2 per stand), counts from the model. 8.8 on carbon steel, A4-70 on the hot-bay hardware, per the grade rule; lengths and patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Wetted baths (PV-TEX-01 KOH texture, PV-PSG-01 HF PSG etch) 316L per duty section 3 ("316L where the stream is wet"; CA 0 mm, AQS-STD-001 rule); the node station texts name PP tanks/baths — the duty selection governs (plan rule 5) and the tension is a named hold (W12). Diffusion tube quartz and screen stainless shop-cut per AQS-PV-001 section 3. Base plate, guard frame and dry station envelopes carbon steel A500 (AQS-MOD-002 dry service).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6). The PECVD chamber is drawn as its envelope; no pressure boundary is designed or claimed here (the chamber internals are the vendor/in-house design task, W12). No design_kPa is booked on the node. The diffusion tube is open-ended per AQS-PV-001 section 6; exhaust to the scrubber is that page's routing, not a schedule row here.
W8 Exports. `export/solar_pv_cell.step` (assembly); `export/solar_pv_cell.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/SOLAR_PV_CELL.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 2400.0 × 1200.0 × 1600.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. Masses in `check.json`: base 452.2 kg, frame 166.8 kg, station envelopes 6001.9 kg (study envelopes at carbon-steel density), quartz tube 6.7 kg, fibre annulus 32.7 kg, PV-TEX-01 heater envelope 1.8 kg, PV-ARC-01 pump envelope 98.1 kg, hardware 0.2 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make rows (shop-fabricable, flagged for AQS-BOM-SC-001): base plate, guard frame, station shells (baths 316L), screen print mesh (shop-cut per AQS-PV-001), connection hardware. Buy rows (purchased per plan rule 6, ratings from the node/A-M-178): PV-TEX-01 immersion heater, quartz diffusion tube, ceramic-fibre annulus, PV-ARC-01 dry vacuum pump (1.5 kW basis), the PV-CON-01 dryer 0.5 kW and printer drive 0.2 kW, the PV-FIRE-01 IR lamp set 3.0 kW, the PV-IV-01 flash IV tester 0.1 kW.
W10 Fabrication route. F-MACH (plan section 4: frame weldment, guards, purchased drive or electrical by rating, anchors): bench and frame weldment, bath sheet welding (GTAW per the 316L practice), tube stands set and anchored, purchased units mounted by rating, chamber and furnace housings shop-made. On-site tool: the metal shop. The diffusion tube loads at commissioning (buy rows).
W11 Self-copy relevance. A-SC applies (tier 7): the line makes the plant's own PV cells (AQS-BOM-SC-001 row: SOLAR_PV_CELL, PV-TEX-01..PV-IV-01), and the parts of the unit the on-site shop can make — bench, frame, bath shells, chamber and furnace housings, printer envelope, screen mesh, tube stands, connection hardware — are flagged make in the BOM and cross-referenced to AQS-BOM-SC-001. Heaters, pump, quartz tube, fibre, dryer, lamps and the IV tester are purchased.
W12 Holds, named. (1) Station arrangement and every unstated envelope size are the layout choice; the node tool table names stations, design texts and peak kW, not sizes. (2) Wetted material: the node station texts name PP tanks/baths; duty section 3 selects 316L where wet — the duty governs (plan rule 5); nothing resized, the tension is carried (the X-MFG-38 tool designs will settle it). (3) The node text "200 mm ceramic fibre" is taken at face value as the fibre thickness; AQS-PV-001 rev C carries the older 50 mm ID × 800 mm tube figure — the node design 200 mm × 1.0 m governs, the older figure named. (4) Tube wall 5 mm is a study value (the node gives OD and length, not wall). (5) The N2 vent datum 800 predates the 1600 box — carried as a placeholder, flush at the top. (6) The N3/N4 solids rows are non-piped transfers drawn DN15, bore not calculated (plan W4). (7) No per-heater kW rating is stated for the PV-TEX-01 immersion heater (node row carries 0.687 kWh/d). (8) The PECVD chamber is drawn as its envelope; no pressure boundary is designed (vendor/in-house design task). (9) AQS-PV-001 section 4 names an SB350 heater connection and the scrubber exhaust — those are the module page's routing, not schedule rows here; named, not drawn. (10) Fastener lengths and the station mass study figures are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

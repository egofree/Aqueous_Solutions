# Work order EW_1

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-CELL). Route class F-CELL. Graph Rev AC. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/EW_1.md`, `docs-authority/sizing/EW_CELLS.md` (AQS-DUTY-051), `docs-authority/nozzles/EW_1.csv`, `docs-authority/BUSBAR.md` (AQS-BUS-001), `docs-authority/MATERIALS_STD.md` (AQS-STD-002) sections 9 and 11, `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node EW_1.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `ew_1.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Parameters. Graph sizing 2670 × 720 × 760 mm at origin 7300, 5700, −6000 mm (placement seed). design_kPa and relief_mm are null: the cell is atmospheric (duty section 6); the stored design_kPa on other nodes is an old head-plus-10 figure and is not a set point.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The electrode bank is drawn anyway because A-CELL requires it from duty section 2: 25 cathode sheets 600 × 420 × 3 mm titanium Grade 2 with PVC-U edge-strip clips, 26 anode sheets 600 × 420 × 1.5 mm MMO-coated titanium mesh drawn as its sheet envelope (AQS-PLAN-006 rule 6), hanger tabs rising to the bus. The cell-to-cell launder (duty section 3) is the inter-cell piping scope (AQS-STD-002 section 11 free-fall with air break): the N3 overflow connection is drawn; no launder solid is invented.
W4 Nozzles. All 12 schedule rows of `docs-authority/nozzles/EW_1.csv` are present at tag, face, height, bore and rating, drawn flush at the envelope faces (lib/nozzles.place_on_face). Atmospheric joints are hose barbs (duty section 4); no flange anywhere because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule). N2 is the rectifier connection and is drawn as a conductor gland, not a hose. N10 and N11 are solids rows, drawn at DN15 and labeled bore not calculated. The along-face x positions are the registered layout choice (params.json layout.nozzle_spread).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M10 wedge anchors (tank to slab), 8 M8 beam bolts, 4 M8 vent-post bolts, 51 M6 electrode tab clamps (one per electrode), 6 M10 rectifier-lug bolts (3 per pole, overlap at least the bar width per AQS-BUS-001 section 4). All ISO 3506 A4-70 by the plan grade rule (polymer joints; supplier rule named in the rows). Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Tank and racks CPVC (duty section 3, X-MAT-09 / M5); wall 10 mm (AQS-DUTY-051 section 1). Cathode titanium Grade 2 t3, anode titanium mesh t1.5 MMO (AQS-STD-002 section 9). Corrosion allowance 0 mm on the titanium and none on polymers (AQS-STD-001 shop rule). Bus copper C101 above the liquor line, contacts Ti-clad, FRP standoffs, no busbar on a tank lip (AQS-STD-002 sections 9 and 11).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric. The vent N8 hangs to its 650 mm datum on a rim bridge; N9 collects hood hydrogen at the schedule datum. The gas capture hood itself is not dimensioned in any authority and is not invented.
W8 Exports. `export/ew_1.step` (assembly with the liquor inventory); `export/ew_1.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/EW_1.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 2670.0 × 720.3 × 760.0 mm against the 2670 × 720 × 760 envelope: inside. Masses in `check.json`; liquor 1.208 m³ drawn net of solids against the 1.243 m³ fill basis; dry mass about 314 kg; operating about 1 680 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Purchased rows (anode meshes, edge-strip stock, standoffs, rectifier) carry the order-time mass note.
W10 Fabrication route. F-CELL: tank plate cut and thermoplastic weld (CPVC sheet, hot-gas / extrusion welds, set-in nozzles solvent-welded), cathode shear and punch (Ti G2 sheet; annealed, no cold forming after anneal), bus cut and drill (Cu C101 flats, lug overlap ≥ bar width), gasket/hose makeup at the face plane, stack assembly, leak test of the shell. On-site tool: the plastics shop and the bench line; the Ti sheets and the MMO anodes are bought in.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the cell makes metal, not plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) Bus current basis: the published AQS-BUS-001 EW-1 pack (2 × 110 × 6.3 mm, 13.9 cm², 152 A/cm²) is sized at 2105 A; at the duty section 9 bounding 2476 A the same pack runs 179 A/cm². The pack is carried verbatim; re-sizing belongs to `docs-authority/_ew_busbar.py` when it regenerates from the duty current. (2) Working level: the sizing fill 1.243 m³ reads to the top of the cover zone (z 680) while the IA-EW-01 overflow notch stands at 487 mm; both are the authorities' figures and both are drawn. (3) Gas capture hood: not dimensioned anywhere; not modeled. (4) Nozzle elevations N9/N10/N11/N12 carry the issued schedule datums (600/160 mm); designed elevations are unit tasks 02/06. (5) N6 carries service "plated sheet" at DN25 while N10 carries the sheet transfer as solids; the issued schedule is drawn verbatim. (6) Hose barb profile and fastener lengths are study proportions, finished against supplier prints. (7) The N2 schedule datum (130 mm on −Y) predates the drawn bus arrangement (lugs at the −X bar ends, z 691); the connection routing is the rectifier task's to place. (8) Anode coat life is a vendor figure, not assumed.

## Recipe for EW_2 to EW_9

The build is the same script shape with per-unit numbers; nothing else changes.

1. Copy `models/mfg/EW_1/ew_1.py` and `draw_ew_1.py` to `models/mfg/<UNIT>/`, rename the assembly and the export names.
2. Take every number from the unit's own row of `docs-authority/sizing/EW_CELLS.md` section 2 and its `docs-authority/nozzles/<UNIT>.csv` schedule (the tracker writes `params.json` from them): cathode/anode counts (one more anode), tank inside length = cathodes × 100 mm + 2 × 75 mm ends (outside +2 × 10 mm wall), width 720 and height 760 constant, pitch 100 mm, plates 600 × 420 immersed.
3. Materials: EW_1 runs CPVC (77.5 °C liquor); EW_2 to EW_9 see 55 °C and stay PVC-U (duty section 3, AQS-STD-002 section 8 temperature table). Fastener grade rule unchanged.
4. Bus: carry the unit's own published pack from the AQS-BUS-001 table verbatim.
5. Regenerate in order: unit script → `python3 -m flow_graph.param.regen` (duty section 7 pointer and the tracker stage) → `python3 docs-authority/_drawings.py` → drawing PDF pass → `python3 models/mfg/tracker.py --check` → `scripts/check_all.sh` → one commit per unit (AQS-PLAN-006 rule 4).
6. Name that unit's holds in its WORK_ORDER from the same list as W12 plus anything its own duty adds; a hold is named, never guessed (plan rules 2 and 3).

# Work order MEOH_SYNTH

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-VES). Route class F-VES. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/MEOH_SYNTH.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/MEOH_SYNTH.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-REV-H2-001 section 5 (API 941), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node MEOH_SYNTH (design block, X-MFG-31 / A-M-156).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `meoh_synth.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` and, for the construction figures, the node design block of the graph (the W2 file carries it as `design`; the tube row pre-dates the duty section 3 alloy selection and is superseded - see W6); it rebuilds every solid. The `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks of `params.json` are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 414 × 414 × 2585 (set by `flow_graph/param/reactor_design.py`, X-MFG-31). The build fills it: measured 414.0 × 414.0 × 2585.0 mm; the vent riser tops the box, the side hardware spans the depth.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine construction the node design block defines is drawn: the catalyst bed envelope (V_bed 3.2143 L, 3.54 kg Cu/ZnO/Al2O3) and the two support grids, inside the DN50 tube bore at their running clearances (purchased items as envelopes per plan rule 6; the inert-alumina ball layers are loading items, not solids).
W4 Nozzles. All 10 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/MEOH_SYNTH.csv` are scheduled at tag, face, height and bore; 9 are drawn as atmospheric open-bore tube connections flush at the skid faces (duty section 4: hose, atmospheric; the connecting line makes the joint at the face): N1 CO2 and N2 H2 in on −Y and N8 CO2-not-taken at the 160 pack; N3 crude MeOH, N6 co-feed, N7 synthesis water and N9 mercaptan trace out on +Y at the 600 pack; N4 drain boss under the bottom flange, flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N5 vent riser on the top flange, flush at the envelope top (the 800 datum is the schedule placeholder). N10 (top, z 800) is the power terminal (cable schedule X-ELS-06, AQS-ELEC-010): electrical, not piped - carried verbatim in `check.json`, never drawn. The 160 and 600 datums fit the 2585 box and draw as issued; the side stubs pierce to the tube axis so every takeoff lands on the vessel wall; the elevations are the schedule placeholders pending vessel design (duty section 4 - hold, W12). No flange hardware is drawn at the face connections (W4 110 % rule - no process pressure stated at the face); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text, and the pressure-part flanges carry the B16.5 Class 600 rating of the node construction. The along-face positions are the registered layout choice (per tag in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 16 studs 5/8 in with heavy hex nuts (8 per joint, the B16.5 pattern), A193 B8M / A194 8MA for the 316L pressure part, torque per ASME PCC-1; 4 M12 wedge anchors, grade 8.8, plinth to slab. Lengths and torque values are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. The pressure part (tube, flanges) and jacket are 316L per duty section 3: synthesis 50 bar a, 250 °C (study), 316L per API 941 (AQS-REV-H2-001 section 5); above 200 °C with H₂ partial pressure no carbon steel. The node design tube row ("DN50 Sch 40 SA-106 B") predates that selection and is superseded - the drawn wall stays the node t_nom 3.91 mm (t_req 2.88, t_ok true) and the alloy changes (hold, W12). CA 0 mm (AQS-STD-001 rule). Plinth carbon steel (dry support).
W7 Pressure. Design 5.5 MPa (node design_MPa) on the pressure part, Class 600 flanges per B16.5 at the 250 °C design; the face connection hardware is atmospheric tube per duty section 4. The vent N5 is the schedule row; duty section 6 records the stored design_kPa idiom as not a set point. Hydrostatic test of the tube at 1.3 × design (UG-99) and the jacket at 1.3 × its design are route operations (W10). Instruments are the duty section 5 loops (T and P, high-pressure trip), loop hardware, not modeled.
W8 Exports. `export/meoh_synth.step` (assembly); `export/meoh_synth.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/MEOH_SYNTH.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 414.0 × 414.0 × 2585.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. Two solids: the vessel chain and the bed-with-grids envelope at running clearance in the bore (both closed and manifold). The bed volume 3.213 L (model) against the node V_work_L 3.2143 L is reported in `check.json`; no residence time is derived (A-VES; the duty section 2 claims no holdup).
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: tube, flanges, jacket, grids, connections. Buy: catalyst bed, feed preheater (envelopes and interfaces per plan rule 6).
W10 Fabrication route. F-VES: plate/tube cut, roll and weld (GTAW, ASME IX qualified procedures; 100 % radiography of the butt welds, UW-11), nozzle set-in, jacket closure, hydro at 1.3 × design (UG-99), then the dry pack: support grids, catalyst load, inert balls, close on the spiral-wound gaskets and torque per PCC-1. On-site tool: the metal shop pressure-work bay; the catalyst reduction runs per the operating procedure (X-OPS-01).
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the reactor makes methanol; it makes no plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The node design tube row "DN50 Sch 40 SA-106 B" is superseded by the duty section 3 selection (316L per API 941); the drawn wall keeps the node t_nom 3.91 and the alloy changes; the wall geometry is the node figure, unchanged. (2) The stud alloy follows the pressure part (A193 B8M / A194 8MA); the pre-duty-3 build's B7/2H row is superseded with it. (3) The side-nozzle elevations (160/600) are the schedule placeholders pending vessel design (unit tasks 02/06); they draw as issued and the takeoffs land on the tube at those heights. (4) The 800 vent datum is the schedule placeholder; the riser stands on the top flange and flushes at the envelope top. (5) The catalyst bed is drawn as its envelope (purchased, plan rule 6); pellet size, reduction schedule and the inert-ball loading are supplier/procedure items. (6) The bed and grids sit at running clearances in the bore (0.2 / 0.5 mm study values) and are not welded to the pressure part - the export is two closed solids. (7) The duty section 2 claims no holdup; the bed volume is reported, no residence time derived (A-VES). (8) The Class 150 rating column stands against the atmospheric hose duty at the faces; the pressure-part rating is the node's Class 600 construction. (9) Fastener lengths, torque pattern and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication. (10) The duty section 6 relief idiom ("atmospheric, stored design_kPa not a set point") stands as issued against the 5.5 MPa design; the relief case is unit task 07 and stays open.

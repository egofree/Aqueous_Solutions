Authority: `docs-authority/duties/H2O_ELYZ.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/H2O_ELYZ.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-STD-002, AQS-REV-H2-001, `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node H2O_ELYZ (design block written by `flow_graph/param/specialty_sizing.py`, X-SPEC lane; stack point A-M-459; window A-M-133; bed classes A-M-168; skid allowances A-M-279), ISO 22734 / IEC 62282-3-100 construction frame, NFPA 2 (H2 and O2 on separate lines).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `h2o_elyz.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` and the node design block; it rebuilds every solid. The `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks of `params.json` are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 3367 × 1050 × 2200 (the A-M-279 skid allowances at the rated kW: stack deck 2042 + gap 100 + BoP deck 1225 at W 1050, H 2200). Measured 3367 × 1050 × 2201 mm (the vent riser stands 0.5 mm proud of the BoP frame, inside the 1 mm gate). The placement seed block (1800 × 800 × 1600) under-books the skid - hold, W12(1).
W3 Internals. The node internals row drawn in full ("PEM stack, H2 and O2 gas-liquid separators, deoxo on H2, dryer"): the 80-cell bipolar-plate field between Ti end plates (169.7 × 169.7 mm active, 8 mm pitch, 2.304 m² cell-summed - the plates and lands draw, the MEAs are a BOM loading row, W12(3)); the H2 and O2 gas-liquid separators DN150 × 400 on legs; the deoxo on H2 (2 beds DN80 × 345 at the GHSV 5000 1/h class on the rated 17.4 Nm³/h, A-M-168); the dryer on H2 (2 beds DN80 × 712, 4A/13X at margin 3, 250 °C regen, 5.01 kg sieve on the 0.167 kg/d water book); the DI-water loop pump and power-electronics envelopes per plan rule 6; the H2 relief-valve envelope per the node relief_basis.
W4 Nozzles. All 7 rows of the issued schedule are carried at tag, face, height and bore: the five piped rows draw as 60 mm 316L tube stubs flush at the skid faces (feed water in at −Y; H2 and O2 out at +Y; drain boss through the plinth; vent riser on the BoP frame), N6 (power terminal) and N7 (service terminal, the Q4 stack-heat edge) are text rows with no stub. The schedule elevations (160/600/800) draw as issued - they are the schedule placeholders pending vessel design (duty section 4; hold, W12(4)). Per-tag notes in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: M12 studs at the stack end-plate compression joints (austenitic) and M12 plinth anchors. Study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: Ti porous transport layers, Ir/Ru mixed-oxide anode with Pt cathode, PTFE-class ionomer, Ti cell hardware and PTFE compression seals; deoxo on 316L hardware; dry product lines on the M1 316L family. No wetted copper (the bus pair is dry-side).
W7 Pressure. Design 990 kPa g (node design_kPa); the H2 side relief per the node relief_basis (blocked outlet at the compressor suction, 3.3 mm bore book, A-M-196; the O2 side on its own valve at the same set). The separators and beds carry the same design class. Instruments per AQS-CTL-001 (level, temperature, pressure loops).
W8 Exports. `export/h2o_elyz.step` (compound of 110 closed solids); `export/h2o_elyz.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/H2O_ELYZ.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 3367 × 1050 × 2201 mm against the node envelope: inside (1 mm gate).
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: stack plates, separators, bed tubes, bus pair, connections, skid frame. Buy: MEAs/CCMs, catalyst and sieve loadings, power electronics, DI-water pump.
W10 Fabrication route. Stack assembly (Ti plate shear and punch, CCM lamination, compression sealing per the electrolyser standards), separator and bed tube work (316L, GTAW), skid frame weldment, loop plumbing, leak test at 1.1 × design. On-site tool: the metal shop with the Ti-weld station; the first-article stack test is X-PAR-12 / X-MFG-41-class hardware evidence.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the electrolyser makes hydrogen; it makes no plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The placement seed block (1800 × 800 × 1600) under-books the A-M-279 skid (3367 × 1050 × 2200) - the floor-space consequence is named for the placement lane; nothing is resized here. (2) The stack operating point (j 1.8 A/cm², V_cell 1.85 V, 80 × 288 cm² cells, 518.4 A at 148 V DC) is the registered A-M-459 study basis; the measured polarisation curve at the X-PAR-12 first article replaces it and the area follows. (3) The MEAs/CCMs, deoxo catalyst and dryer sieve are BOM loading rows inside the drawn pitch and bed tubes (plan rule 6). (4) The schedule elevations (160/600/800) are the issued placeholder datums pending vessel design (duty section 4). (5) The deoxo/dryer beds apply the A-M-168 purifier classes on the skid (GHSV 5000 1/h; 0.10 kg/kg at margin 3; duplicated beds); the water book scales off the H2_DRYER saturation figure. (6) Recorded, not fixed here: the H2_DRYER unit sizes on the HDR pool residual (2.750 kg/d per its design block) while this electrolyser books 9.358 kg/d design / 8.715 kg/d edge - the mismatch persists after the F-26 corrections were applied; the dryer book is the pool residual by the by-difference writer, not a mis-scale of this unit, and the sizing hold stays open for the H2-pool owner pass. (7) Fastener sizes, torque pattern and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication. (8) The power electronics and DI-water pump draw as purchased envelopes; ratings come from the rated-draw balance (55 kWh/kg book, A-M-133) and the maker sheets at order.

# Work order NOX_SCRUB

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-COL). Route class F-COL. Graph Rev AC. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/NOX_SCRUB.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/NOX_SCRUB.csv`, node design block (flow_graph/param/house_scrubbers.py, X-PAR-03), AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-STD-002 rules M1/M2/M5 (CPVC class), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node NOX_SCRUB.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `nox_scrub.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 600 × 600 × 4103, set by the X-PAR-03 house-scrubber sizing; the column design block carries D 200, packed height 2303, column H 3803. The build fills the envelope: measured 600.0 × 600.0 × 4103.0 mm, one closed manifold solid (plinth at the base, shell to 4063 with a 20 mm top head, the vent riser closing the 4103 top).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The A-COL internals are drawn as envelope solids at recorded layout elevations: packing support grid (z 700), the 2303 mm packing in two beds with a redistribution disc (z 715..1866.5, 1881.5..3033), liquid distributor (z 3060), PP mesh demister (z 4043). The rings and the mesh are not modelled piece-wise. Bed elevations are the layout choice — the design gives packed height only (holds, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 15 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/NOX_SCRUB.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore: inlets on −Y (N1..N4 at z 400, N13..N15 at z 160), outlets on +Y (N5..N8 at z 1500), N9 drain flush at z 0 as a boss lapping the skirt ring, N10 vent as a riser on the top head. Atmospheric joints are hose barbs flush at the envelope faces on extension spools that cross the air gap and the shell wall (the column stands 200 off the face); no flange is drawn (W4 110 % rule — no process pressure stated); the schedule rating column (Class 150, and "hose" on N11/N12) is carried verbatim as text. N11/N12 carry no schedule datum: drawn at z 3400 in the gas space above the distributor (layout choice). The z 400 row packs the full DN 200 chord: the outer nozzles are near-tangent to the shell (hold, W12).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M10 wedge anchors, plinth to slab (count from the model), plus the M12 PP-flanged service joint at the top head (study pattern). Polymer joints to the supplier rule; A4-70 hardware per the observed house practice. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. CPVC shell, sump and packing support per duty section 3 (AQS-STD-002 M1/M2/M5): leach off-gas arrives at up to 85 °C. Packing is PP (in-house cut rings per the node design); demister PP mesh. No metal wetted part; corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymer. The node design block names PP-H — duty section 3 governs wetted materials (plan rule 5); the disagreement is a hold (W12).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6); dP 1171 Pa across the packing is the node design figure and is not a pressure rating. No hydrotest is claimed; leak test is a shop discretion (route W10).
W8 Exports. `export/nox_scrub.step` (assembly); `export/nox_scrub.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/NOX_SCRUB.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 600.0 × 600.0 × 4103.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. Masses in `check.json`: shell + skirt + plinth 37.6 kg, packing 58.6 kg, distributor 0.6 kg, demister 1.0 kg, hardware 9.9 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make: shell, internals and hardware are in-house fabrication (the PP rings are cut in-house per the duty; the mesh pad is stocked PP mesh).
W10 Fabrication route. F-COL: CPVC pipe/sheet cut, thermoplastic weld of shell, head, skirt and plinth, packing support and distributor fit, set-in ports, bed loading, leak test. On-site tool: the thermoplastic shop (AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 F-COL operations).
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the scrubber cleans gas; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) Wall thickness: no authority states one; 6 mm CPVC is a study value, confirmed against the 85 °C class at fabrication. (2) The node design block material (PP-H) disagrees with duty section 3 (CPVC shell, sump, packing support; PVC-U possible above the quench once the gas temperature there is calculated — held): the model draws duty section 3, the reconciliation is the sizing-module task. (3) Bed elevations, bed split (two beds + redistribution disc), distributor and demister elevations are layout choices; the design gives packed height 2303 only. (4) No stage or HTU count exists for this column anywhere (A-COL: it stays a hold where the duty says so — the duty states none). (5) The duty section 4 prose (14 rows, different services) and the issued 15-row CSV disagree: the CSV is drawn verbatim; the prose refresh is the duty generator's run. (6) N11/N12 carry no schedule datum; drawn at z 3400 in the gas space (layout choice). (7) N10's 2000 datum predates the sized column; the vent is drawn as a riser on the top head. (8) The z 400 inlet row packs the full DN 200 chord: the outer nozzles are near-tangent to the shell — set-on fillets are the fabricator's; a single distribution manifold is the detail-design alternative (unit task 02/06). (9) Schedule rating column (Class 150) against the atmospheric duty: no flange is drawn. (10) Fastener lengths, the head-joint bolt pattern and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

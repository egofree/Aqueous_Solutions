# Work order ABE

Authority: AQS-DUTY-062 (X-MFG-31). In-house fabrication; no bought vessel.

1. Cut and roll the shell DN150, 4.0 mm, height 150 mm; cut the bottom and lid discs (HDPE PE100 sheet, rolled and extrusion-welded (DVS 2207-4)).
2. Weld the longitudinal seam and the bottom; weld the lid flange ring.
3. Cut and weld 5 DN25 nozzles: sugar in, broth out, gas out, N2 blanket, drain.
4. Weld 3 legs; drill 8 lid bolt holes.
5. Fit the gas recirculation sparge and insulation.
6. Test: hydrostatic fill to the brim, 24 h, no leak (DVS 2205).
7. Inspect against models/mfg/ABE/export/abe.step; record dimensions on the traveller.

# Work order MEAM

Authority: AQS-DUTY-062 section 6 (X-MFG-31, A-M-157). In-house fabrication; welders and procedure qualified to ASME IX.

1. Cut DN15 Sch 40 316L to 600 mm; butt-weld the DN15 Class 300 weld-neck flanges (GTAW, back-purged).
2. Drill the blind flanges for the feed/product ports and thermowell.
3. Hydrostatic test at 1.3 x 2.28 MPa (UG-99).
4. Load inert balls, 2.6 g catalyst, inert balls between mesh retainers; close with spiral-wound gaskets.
5. Fit the band heater, vaporiser coil, insulation and safety valve.
6. Inspect against models/mfg/MEAM/export/meam.step; record on the traveller.

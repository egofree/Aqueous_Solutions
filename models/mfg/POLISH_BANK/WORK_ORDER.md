# Work order POLISH_BANK

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-VES). Route class F-VES. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/POLISH_BANK.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/POLISH_BANK.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-STD-002 rules M1 and M2, `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node POLISH_BANK.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `polish_bank.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1000 × 600 × 800, whose `size_note` records it as the study registry box read back from the `cad/stl` registry. The build fills it: measured 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm, one closed manifold solid.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. No authority dimensions the polish media: the duty section 5 names a differential-pressure instrument "across the packing", but no packing height, media class or column count is stated on the node or the duty sheet - nothing is invented (hold, W12). History: the placement seed once carried the polish bank as separate columns (the PK_1..PK_9 label era); the present graph defines POLISH_BANK as one unit and the model covers that column set as the node defines it - one vessel skid.
W4 Nozzles. All 7 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/POLISH_BANK.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore: N1 DN50 EW tail in and N7 DN15 in on −Y at z 160; N2/N3 DN50 out and N6 DN15 liquor return out on +Y at z 600 (all datums fit the box, no clamp); N4 drain boss on the base head to its 50 datum; N5 vent flush at the top head - the schedule 800 datum is the envelope top. Atmospheric joints are hose barbs (duty section 4); no flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. The duty section 4 prose sizing line ("the bore is 6.25 mm... Nozzle is DN15") is not a specification and predates the issued DN50 tail rows - the schedule governs (hold, W12). The along-face positions are the registered layout choice (per tag in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M10 wedge anchors, plinth to slab, one per corner (count from the model). A4-70 hardware by the polymer-joint supplier rule. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication. The welded joints carry no fastener row.
W6 Materials. PVC-U drawn (duty section 3, rules M1 and M2; no metal wetted part). Corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymer (AQS-STD-001). Shell wall 10 mm is the study fab-page value; no thickness is booked on the node (hold, W12).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6). Not to the room. Level instruments: differential pressure across the packing and pH where there is a liquor loop (duty section 5; loop hardware, not modeled). No hydrotest pressure is claimed; the leak test is a shop discretion of the route (W10).
W8 Exports. `export/polish_bank.step` (assembly); `export/polish_bank.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/POLISH_BANK.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. A-VES: the liquor holdup at the duty-basis working level (z 449.4, 0.0827 m³) and the full-shell volume (0.1529 m³) are reported in `check.json`.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make: shell, plinth and hardware are in-house fabrication.
W10 Fabrication route. F-VES: PVC-U sheet/pipe cut (saw), thermoplastic weld of shell, heads and set-in internals, nozzle set-in welded, anchors drilled, leak test of the shell. On-site tool: the thermoplastic shop (AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 F-VES operations: plate or sheet cut, roll, weld or thermoplastic weld, nozzle set-in, hydro or leak test).
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the vessel polishes EW tail liquor; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The envelope is the study registry box; nothing is resized. (2) Shell wall 10 mm is the study fab-page value; no thickness is booked on the node. (3) The polish media is not dimensioned by any authority (node internals row none; the duty section 5 instrument line names packing, no packing data exists) - nothing invented. (4) The PK_1..PK_9 seed history split the bank across columns; the present graph defines one unit, modeled as one vessel skid. (5) The duty section 4 prose bore line (6.25 mm, DN15) predates the issued DN50 tail rows; the schedule governs. (6) Schedule rating column (Class 150) against the atmospheric hose duty: no flange is drawn. (7) The A-VES holdup is the duty section 2 basis (0.0827 m³ at a 30-minute hold) applied as the model working level; duty section 8 records the vessel is not built, so it is not a measured volume; no new residence-time claim is made. (8) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

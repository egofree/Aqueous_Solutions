# Work order HNO3

Authority: AQS-DUTY-062 (X-MFG-31). In-house fabrication; no bought vessel.

1. Cut and roll the shell DN65, 2.0 mm, height 60 mm; cut the bottom and lid discs (SA-240 304L plate, rolled, GTAW (ASME IX)).
2. Weld the longitudinal seam and the bottom; weld the lid flange ring.
3. Cut and weld 5 DN25 nozzles: NO/air from burner, water in, HNO3 out, tail gas vent, drain.
4. Weld 3 legs; drill 8 lid bolt holes.
5. Fit the gauze burner, heater, packing and insulation.
6. Test: fill test 24 h and dye-penetrant on welds (ASME V); spark test of the lining (BUCL).
7. Inspect against models/mfg/HNO3/export/hno3.step; record dimensions on the traveller.

# Work order BMED_ACID

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-CELL). Route class F-CELL. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/BMED_ACID.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/BMED_ACID.csv`, `docs-authority/sizing/BMED_STACK.md` (AQS-DUTY-068, X-MFG-35), `docs-authority/BENCH_TEST_BMED_STACK.md` (AQS-TST-006), AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-STD-002 rules M1 and M2, `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node BMED_ACID.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `bmed_acid.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1000 × 600 × 350, whose `size_note` records it as the study registry box ("M0 study envelope; design sizing per the duty sheet follows"). The build fills it: measured 1000.0 × 600.0 × 350.0 mm, one closed manifold solid (hold, W12).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn as the unit: the electrodialysis stack module between end electrode plates on the drip tray - Ti/MMO anode plate and 316L/Ni cathode plate, two 300 × 300 clamp frames, the triplet laminate (85 triplets of one bipolar and two monopolar membranes with 0.8 mm PE mesh spacers, drawn as the stack envelope per plan rule 6, membranes a buy row) and four tie rods - the construction line of AQS-DUTY-068 / A-M-170, the same construction AQS-TST-006 section 2 builds the bench apparatus to. The sizing page books 421 triplets in 5 stacks of 85 plus 1 spare for the BMED pair; the node books no per-unit stack count, so the model draws the representative stack module (hold, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 10 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/BMED_ACID.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube connections (duty section 4: atmospheric tube or hose, no flange class): N1 DN25 and N9/N10 DN15 in on −Y at z 160; N2/N3/N5/N8 DN15 and N4 DN25 out on +Y (schedule datums 600 predates the 350 box, clamped to 331.4 / 325.3 by the box bound so the branch and riser stay inside - hold, W12); N6 drain, open bore through the tray floor flush at the bottom face (50 datum); N7 vent flush at the top face on the hold-down beam (800 datum predates the box - hold, W12). Side connections ride tray risers; hose makeup by the connecting line at the face. No flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. The along-face positions are the registered layout choice (per tag in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M10 wedge anchors (tray to slab, one per corner, count from the model) and 4 M12 316L tie rods at the stack clamp (A-M-170 torque pattern per AQS-TST-006 section 2; ends drawn buried in the end plates, the protruding stud length and nuts are fabrication detail). Polymer joints to the supplier rule; A4-70 hardware per the observed house practice. Lengths and patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. PVC-U wetted (duty section 3, rules M1 and M2: no metal wetted part, above 60 °C the thermoplastic is CPVC): tray, clamp frames, hold-down beam and connections drawn PVC-U; corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymer (AQS-STD-001). The electrode end plates are the A-M-170 electrochemical hardware (Ti/MMO anode, 316L/Ni cathode - buy rows); the laminate is the membrane/PE spacer class. The AQS-DUTY-068 construction names PP frames; duty section 3 rules PVC-U: PVC-U drawn, PP named (hold, W12). No thickness is booked on the node; plate 20 and electrode plate 10 are study values (hold, W12).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6). Not to the room. Stored `design_kPa` is the old head-plus-10 figure; it is not a set point. The A-M-088 stack power (26.93 kW at 2 M, duty section 9) is booked on this unit; the sizing design voltage class (1.22-1.67 V per triplet) and the AQS-TST-006 acceptance band (1.8-2.4 kWh/kg at CE 0.80 ± 0.05) are the verification route - figures reported, not recomputed here (A-CELL holds, W12). The leak-test regime is the route's (W10): each hydraulic loop at 0.5 bar for 30 min, no external drop and no cross-loop transfer (AQS-TST-006 section 4).
W8 Exports. `export/bmed_acid.step` (assembly); `export/bmed_acid.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/BMED_ACID.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1000.0 × 600.0 × 350.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: tray, clamp frames, hold-down beam and posts, tie rods, connections. Buy: membranes (laminate row, per the maker class), anode and cathode end plates.
W10 Fabrication route. F-CELL: tank fabrication (PVC-U sheet cut and thermoplastic weld of tray and curb), plate shear and punch (clamp frames), gasket cut (per the membrane maker class), stack assembly (85 triplets, torque pattern), leak test. On-site tool: the thermoplastic shop and the stack bench (AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 F-CELL operations).
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the skid regenerates acid; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The envelope is the study registry box; the `size_note` says design sizing follows per the duty sheet - carried, nothing resized. (2) The AQS-DUTY-068 construction names PP frames, duty section 3 rules PVC-U wetted: PVC-U drawn, PP named. (3) No thickness is booked on the node: frame plate 20, electrode plate 10 and tray sections are study values. (4) The stack allocation (5 stacks of 85 plus 1 spare for the BMED pair) is not booked per node; the representative module is drawn. (5) A-CELL plate count, plate size, pitch and active area are carried from the AQS-DUTY-068 / A-M-170/171 construction; the duty section 2 carries no plate row, so the section 5 match is against the sizing construction; POWER.md states no bus bar for this class, so the bus-bar criterion has nothing to match. (6) The triplet pitch 2.85 mm is derived from the A-M-171 stack-up (3 × 0.8 mm compartments plus membrane sheets); the membrane sheet thickness is a study value. (7) Schedule row N4 carried an unquoted comma that shifted its face and height fields; the row was repaired to schedule quoting (the generator only appends, hand rows are preserved verbatim). (8) The 600 outlet datums and the 800 vent datum predate the 350 box; clamped as W4 records. (9) The schedule rating column (Class 150) against the atmospheric duty: no flange is drawn. (10) Duty section 9 books the stack power from the A-M-088 SEC revision while the sizing page derives 5.6 kW at the 1.67 V pilot class; AQS-TST-006 section 5 holds the measured verification band that reconciles them - no figure is recomputed here. (11) Fastener lengths, the anchor embedment and the torque pattern are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

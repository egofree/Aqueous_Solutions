# Work order P_CA

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core). Route hold: the node class `precip` has no row in the section 4 route table; the vessel work follows the F-VES operations as the nearest family. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/P_CA.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/P_CA.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-STD-002 (X-MAT-22 selection), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node P_CA (envelope, sizing and design blocks).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `p_ca.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 300 × 300 × 400, "envelope from the placement seed (AQS-PLACE-SEED-001); sized by need"; the node envelope block records it as the calcium-soap precipitation vessel at the 0.025 kg/d thickener draw. The build fills it: measured 300.0 × 300.0 × 400.0 mm, one closed manifold solid.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The duty section 3 material line names the PP agitator; it is drawn from the model: shaft on the vessel axis from the base head up through the top head into the drive hub, with two blades at z 80 and z 150. Shaft and blade dimensions are study values (hold, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 2 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/P_CA.csv` are drawn at tag, face and bore - the CSV carries only the drain and vent utilities because there is no piped process connection on the graph yet (duty section 4): N1 DN15 drain, open bore through the plinth and base head, flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N2 DN15 vent, flush at the vessel top head (its 800 datum predates the 400 box - hold, W12). PE slurry lines: the PCA_TO_GREASE edges carry no kg/d rate yet, so none are drawn (hold, W12). No flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. The nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06 - hold, W12).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M8 wedge anchors (frame plinth to slab, one per corner, count from the model; 8.8 on carbon steel) and 4 M10 hold-down bolts (vessel base ring to the frame plinth, count from the model; polymer joint to the supplier rule). Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication. The welded joints carry no fastener row.
W6 Materials. PP-H vessel and PP agitator per duty section 3 (X-MAT-22); PE slurry lines when edges exist; steel frame and housing outside the wetted headspace. CA 0 mm on the polymers (AQS-STD-001); the frame plinth is outside the wetted headspace so the carbon-steel allowance is not applied to it. Scale is knocked down mechanically at clean-out: a mechanical design note, not a material allowance (duty section 3). Wall 6 mm is a study value - no thickness is booked on the node (hold, W12). The duty section 3 alternative (316L vessel + PP agitator as a mixed build if hot-work temperatures reach the PP limit) is a named hold (W12). The duty section 8 line "Material selection pending (unit task 09)" predates the issued X-MAT-22 selection recorded in the same duty section 3.
W7 Pressure. Relief case study pending (unit task 07); no set point is claimed on this page (duty section 6). Not to the room. Instrument loops P_CALT-01, P_CAPT-01, P_CATE-01 are loop hardware, not modeled (duty section 5). No hydrotest pressure is claimed; the leak test is a shop discretion of the route (W10).
W8 Exports. `export/p_ca.step` (assembly); `export/p_ca.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/P_CA.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 300.0 × 300.0 × 400.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. The vessel working volume (0.0159 m³ to the shaft top blade band) is reported in `check.json` for the record; the duty books no holdup basis, so no level or residence is derived.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: frame plinth, vessel, agitator, connections. Buy: agitator drive envelope (maker class at order).
W10 Fabrication route. Route hold: no section 4 class exists for `precip`; the tracker records the hold and this work order states the intent - thermoplastic-sheet vessel work following the F-VES operations as the nearest family (plate or sheet cut, roll, weld or thermoplastic weld, nozzle set-in, leak test) on the thermoplastic shop floor, with the steel frame in the welding bay. The route class itself stays a hold until the plan table gains a row (W12).
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the vessel makes grease thickener feed; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) Route hold: no AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 class for `precip` - the tracker acceptance cell reads A-core (route hold). (2) Vessel wall 6 mm is a study value; no thickness is booked on the node. (3) The agitator shaft and blade dimensions are study values; the duty names the agitator but no authority sizes it. (4) The drive envelope is a purchased unit; its rating follows the maker TDS at order. (5) The duty section 3 316L mixed-build alternative is unresolved; PP-H is drawn. (6) The N2 vent datum 800 predates the 400 box; drawn flush at the vessel top head. (7) The PE slurry lines wait for rated graph edges; no process nozzles are invented. (8) The nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06). (9) The relief case study is pending (unit task 07); no set point claimed. (10) The duty section 8 "material selection pending" line predates the issued X-MAT-22 selection. (11) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

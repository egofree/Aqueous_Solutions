# Work order GREY_TREAT

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-VES). Route class F-VES. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/GREY_TREAT.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/GREY_TREAT.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-STD-002 rules M1 and M2, `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node GREY_TREAT.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `grey_treat.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 649 × 520 × 748, whose `size_note` records it as the study registry box read back from the `cad/stl` registry. The build fills it: measured 649.0 × 520.0 × 748.0 mm, one closed manifold solid. The duty section 4 prose block still carries the seed line "Size 800 × 800 × 1100 mm" - the node governs and the older figure is named (holds, W12).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none` and no study page issues internals for this unit, so the tracker records no internal solid and none is drawn (hold, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 11 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/GREY_TREAT.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore: N1 DN50 grey/solids in and N2/N8/N9/N10/N11 DN15 inlets on −Y at z 160; N3 DN25 treated grey, N4 DN50 grey sludge and N7 DN15 water-cycle return out on +Y at z 600 (datums fit the box, no clamp); N5 drain boss on the base head to its 50 datum; N6 vent flush at the top head - its 800 datum predates the 748 box (hold, W12). Atmospheric joints are hose barbs (duty section 4); no flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. The issued schedule (11 rows) governs; the duty section 4 prose table (7 rows on the seed block) predates it and is named (hold, W12). The along-face positions are the registered layout choice (per tag in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M10 wedge anchors, plinth to slab, one per corner (count from the model). A4-70 hardware by the polymer-joint supplier rule. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication. The welded joints carry no fastener row.
W6 Materials. PVC-U drawn (duty section 3, rules M1 and M2; no metal wetted part). Corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymer (AQS-STD-001). Shell wall 10 mm is the study fab-page value; no thickness is booked on the node (hold, W12).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6). Not to the room. Level and temperature are the duty section 5 instruments (high level trips the inlet; loop hardware, not modeled). No hydrotest pressure is claimed; the leak test is a shop discretion of the route (W10).
W8 Exports. `export/grey_treat.step` (assembly); `export/grey_treat.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/GREY_TREAT.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 649.0 × 520.0 × 748.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. A-VES: the liquor holdup at the study working level (0.0955 m³) is reported in `check.json`.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make: shell, plinth and hardware are in-house fabrication.
W10 Fabrication route. F-VES: PVC-U sheet/pipe cut (saw), thermoplastic weld of shell, heads and set-in ports, nozzle set-in welded, anchors drilled, leak test of the shell. On-site tool: the thermoplastic shop (AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 F-VES operations: plate or sheet cut, roll, weld or thermoplastic weld, nozzle set-in, hydro or leak test).
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the vessel treats greywater; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The envelope is the study registry box; the duty section 4 prose seed line 800 × 800 × 1100 is an older figure - the node governs, nothing resized. (2) Shell wall 10 mm is the study fab-page value; no thickness is booked on the node. (3) No internals: the node row is none and no study page issues internals for this unit. (4) The duty section 4 prose table (7 rows) predates the issued 11-row schedule; the schedule governs. (5) The N6 vent datum 800 predates the 748 box; the vent is drawn flush at the top head. (6) Schedule rating column (Class 150) against the atmospheric hose duty: no flange is drawn. (7) The A-VES holdup is a reported model volume at a study working level; the duty claims no holdup and no residence time is derived. (8) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

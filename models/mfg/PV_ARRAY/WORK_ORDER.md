# Work order PV_ARRAY

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-MACH). Route class F-MACH (the `pv` class sits in the F-MACH row of plan section 4). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/PV_ARRAY.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/PV_ARRAY.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), A-M-103 (roof GCR 0.6 low-tilt rows), A-M-105 (roof loads), A-M-184 (17 kg/m² PV + ballast), A-M-233 (PV rebasis note), the `models/freecad/params.py` site-north heritage rule, `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node PV_ARRAY (module/count/nameplate/area/mounting/roof blocks).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `pv_array.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1000 × 600 × 800 (the M0 study registry box). The build fills it: measured 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm from the export STL — the four-post hold-down frame pins the box so the X-PAR-04 envelope registry reads the build. The unit is drawn the way the accepted room-scale units are (the WAFER_FAB bench precedent): a representative array-table section at the registry box, with the array facts read from the node and reported — the array itself spans the roof planes and is not shrunk onto the box (the figures live in check.machine.array_facts, the drawing keys and this work order).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn as the representative section: roof-plane deck, four ballast feet (80 × 80 × 240), table rails, and the tilted module plane — 3 strips covering 60 % of the table plane (GCR 0.6, A-M-103), tilt +10° about +X with the +Y edge raised (equator-facing, site north = +Y, the models/freecad heritage rule). Array facts (reported, not drawn at box scale): 15 tables of 40293 × 43968 mm per the solver roof_close segment math inside the 1829 mm perimeter pathway (interior pathways 1219 mm), footprint 210000 × 138000 mm, area 26574 m², 6052 modules × 0.5 kW = nameplate 3026 kWp (roof_capacity_kWp 3029.5 class), mounting "roof only (X-PAR-07); ground field removed", array mass 451.8 t at 17 kg/m² (A-M-184), roof loads service 1.11 / LRFD 1.716 kPa (A-M-105).
W4 Nozzles. All 4 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/PV_ARRAY.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube stubs flush at the envelope faces (duty section 4 verbatim: "DN is copied from the edge and is not recalculated"): N1 solar into the array (DN25) on −Y at z 160 and N2 PV daily average (DN25) on +Y at z 600 — the electrical edges drawn as the schedule's tube rows with the conductor note carried (the duty inline rows read "Rating conductor. Not a hose"); N3 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N4 vent flush at the envelope top (800 datum is the box top). No flange is drawn (W4 110 % rule — no process pressure stated); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06 — hold, W12).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M12 wedge anchors (plinth to slab) and 8 M10 ballast-foot bolts (2 per foot), counts from the model. 8.8 on carbon steel per the grade rule; the roof-ballast design is the X-BLD lane's (hold, W12).
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: no wetted alloy; conductors copper; CA does not apply. Structure carbon steel/aluminium class (ballasted racking is the shop make, X-MFG-32 class; modules and the inverter row are purchased per plan rule 6 with the node ratings: module_kw 0.5, pv_module_eff 0.19 STC).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric; the duty section 6 states no vent edge — "not to the room" carried. Duty section 5: current, overcurrent opens the breaker — the protection is the electrical lane's (inverter/breaker row), not modeled here (W12).
W8 Exports. `export/pv_array.step` (assembly, 6 solids); `export/pv_array.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/PV_ARRAY.stl` replaced from the in-envelope build solid (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 registry). Bounding box 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm against the node envelope: filled exactly.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make rows carry the AQS-BOM-SC-001 flag where the shop makes the part: plinth, hold-down frame, roof-plane deck, table rails + ballast feet, nozzle set. Buy rows (purchased per plan rule 6, ratings from the node): the module plane (0.5 kW × 6052 = 3026 kWp).
W10 Fabrication route. F-MACH (plan section 4: frame weldment, guards, purchased drive or electrical by rating, anchors): racking section cut and weld (rails, feet), ballast casting or mass placement, module plane mounting (clamp rails per the maker print), conductor dressing to the N1/N2 edges, then a continuity and earth check per the electrical lane. On-site tool: the metal shop (cut, weld, drill); the modules are maker items installed by the fit-out crew.
W11 Self-copy relevance. The array makes bus energy, not plant parts; A-SC does not apply (tier 8). The racking itself is an X-MFG-32 in-house steel product class.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The numeric tilt (10° drawn) is a study value — A-M-103 says low-tilt rows at GCR 0.6 without a number; the driver's verification test is a PVWatts run at the roof tilt and azimuth. (2) The representative build is box-scale; the array spans the roof planes and its figures are reported (the WAFER_FAB room-scale precedent) — nothing resized. (3) The module-plane strips are purchased envelopes; module dimensions are not stated on the node. (4) The roof-ballast and anchor design is the X-BLD lane's (A-M-105). (5) The overcurrent protection is the electrical lane's. (6) Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06). (7) The N1/N2 electrical edges are drawn as the schedule's DN25 tube rows; the conductor sizing is the cable-schedule lane's. (8) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

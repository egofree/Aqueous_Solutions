# Work order MET_HX_Q4

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-HX). Route class F-HX. Graph revision per `params.json` `graph_revision` (the build and its check record ran at the previous graph revision; the tracker W2 regen carried `params.json` forward and nothing resized). Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/MET_HX_Q4.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/MET_HX_Q4.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule and corrosion allowance), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node MET_HX_Q4.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `met_hx_q4.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1000 × 600 × 800, whose `size_note` records it as the study default envelope box read back from the `cad/stl` registry; the duty section 2 sizing states "No kilowatts on the node. Area is not calculated." The claimed envelope is that box and the build fills it: measured 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm, one closed manifold solid (holds, W12).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn as the unit: a vertical 316L shell-and-tube exchanger — shell OD 540, wall 3 study (z 40..760, heads laps to the plinth), inside a four-post hold-down frame that pins the envelope — with a straight-tube bundle of 198 tubes OD 12 × 525, wall 1.5 study, between two 15 mm tube sheets at z 120 and 660, laid out at a 34 mm pitch. The bundle area 3.9188 m² is REPORTED AS DRAWN, not called calculated: the node carries no heat rate (A-HX; duty sections 2 and 8), and no duty area figure exists to check it against. Tube wall 1.5 mm and shell wall 3 mm are study values (hold, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 3 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/MET_HX_Q4.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore: N1 DN15 "heat < 80 °C" out on +Y at z 600 (fits the 800 box, no clamp); N2 drain drawn as a boss on the base head with the boss tip at its 50 datum (the check records the drawn height at the face, 0); N3 vent flush at the top head — the 800 datum IS the envelope top, so it is drawn exact, unlike the HX8 case where the datum predated the box. Atmospheric joints are hose barbs flush at the envelope faces (duty section 4: "Atmospheric joints are hose"; the shell stands 30 off the +Y face, within the barb reach); no flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule — the node states no design_kPa and duty section 6 is atmospheric); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. The along-face x positions pack centred on the face mid-length — the registered layout choice, per tag in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M10 wedge anchors (plinth to slab, one per corner, count from the model) and 8 M12 A4-70 bolts + nuts at the top-head-to-shell service joint. Grade ISO 3506 A4-70 by the plan grade rule on 316L. Lengths and the bolt pattern are study values, confirmed at fabrication. The welded joints carry no fastener row.
W6 Materials. 316L plate/pipe throughout — shell, heads, tube sheets, bundle, nozzle hardware — drawn per duty section 3's wet branch ("316L if the stream is wet or chloride"). The conditional is not resolved by any authority: the carbon-steel alternative (dry hydrocarbon or dry gas stream) is a named hold (W12). Corrosion allowance 0 mm on the 316L (AQS-STD-001 rule).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6). Not to the room. Both outlet temperatures are the duty section 5 instruments (high hot-side outlet trips the hot feed; loop hardware, not modeled). No design pressure and no test pressure is claimed; the leak test is a shop discretion of the route (W10).
W8 Exports. `export/met_hx_q4.step` (assembly); `export/met_hx_q4.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/MET_HX_Q4.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. Masses in `check.json`: plinth 96.0 kg, frame 20.0 kg, shell 139.1 kg, tube sheets 53.8 kg, bundle 41.1 kg, nozzles 0.3 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make: shell, sheets, bundle and hardware are in-house fabrication; no purchased row exists (the duty names no instrument or trim for this node).
W10 Fabrication route. F-HX (class hx): 316L plate/pipe cut to the shell, sheet and plinth blanks (saw or laser); roll or break-form the shell to OD 540; gas-tungsten-arc weld the shell seam, the heads and the two tube sheets; drill the 198 tube holes at the 34 mm pitch and set the bundle by expansion or bond; machine the drain boss and set in the three ports; leak test the shell side and the tube side separately. On-site tool: the MFG shop plate work and weld shop (AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 F-HX operations: plate pack or coil, headers, frame, pressure or leak test).
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the exchanger moves heat in the Q4 loop; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.

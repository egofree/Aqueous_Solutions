# Work order CATCH_TANK

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-VES). Route class F-VES. Graph revision per `params.json` `graph_revision`. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/CATCH_TANK.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/CATCH_TANK.csv`, `docs-authority/fab/CATCH_TANK.md` (AQS-MFG-001 study page: dip pipe, stilling well), AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-STD-002 rule M1 (PVC-U or HDPE water tanks), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node CATCH_TANK.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `catch_tank.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 649 × 520 × 748, whose `size_note` records it as the study default envelope box read back from the `cad/stl` registry. The build fills it: measured 649.0 × 520.0 × 748.0 mm, one closed manifold solid (holds, W12).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The AQS-MFG-001 study page issues the internals: dip pipe OD 60 × wall 4 hung from the top head to z 100, inside a stilling well OD 110 × wall 5 from the floor to above the working level (concentric; feed enters down the dip pipe, the well damps the inlet jet for the level instruments). Dimensions are study values - the study page names the internals, no authority sizes them (holds, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 6 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/CATCH_TANK.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore: N1/N2 in on −Y at z 160 (DN50 hydro discharge, DN15 penstock), N3/N4 out on +Y at z 600 (DN50 charge, DN15 pump return), N5 drain boss on the base head to its 50 datum, N6 vent flush at the top head - its 800 datum predates the 748 box (hold, W12). Atmospheric joints are hose barbs (duty section 4); no flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. The along-face x positions are the registered layout choice (per tag in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M10 wedge anchors, plinth to slab, one per corner (count from the model). A4-70 hardware by the polymer-joint supplier rule. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication. The welded joints carry no fastener row.
W6 Materials. PVC-U drawn (duty section 3: "PVC-U or HDPE, per AQS-STD-002 rule M1"; the M1 audit list books this unit in the PVC-U or HDPE water-tank family). The unresolved HDPE alternative is a named hold (W12). Corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymer (AQS-STD-001). Shell wall 10 mm per the study fab page; no thickness is booked on the node (hold, W12).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6). Not to the room. Level and temperature are the duty section 5 instruments (high level trips the inlet; loop hardware, not modeled). No hydrotest pressure is claimed; the leak test is a shop discretion of the route (W10).
W8 Exports. `export/catch_tank.step` (assembly); `export/catch_tank.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/CATCH_TANK.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 649.0 × 520.0 × 748.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. Liquor holdup at the working level net of the internals reported in `check.json`.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make: shell, well, dip pipe and hardware are in-house fabrication.
W10 Fabrication route. F-VES: PVC-U sheet/pipe cut (saw), thermoplastic weld of shell, heads and set-in internals, nozzle set-in welded, anchors drilled, leak test of the shell. On-site tool: the thermoplastic shop (AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 F-VES operations: plate or sheet cut, roll, weld or thermoplastic weld, nozzle set-in, hydro or leak test).
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the tank receives and holds pumped-hydro water; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The envelope is the study registry box (params `size_note`); the duty sheet section 4 still carries the unit-task-04 reservoir line "Size 12000 × 8000 × 4000 mm" and the AQS-MFG-001 study page a generic 1000 × 600 × 800 - the node governs and the two older figures are named, nothing resized. (2) The duty sheet section 2 two-minute figure (0.2778 m³ at 200 t/d circulation) is not a designed volume; the model holdup is reported, not turned into a residence time (A-VES). (3) Shell wall 10 mm is the study fab-page value; no thickness is booked on the node. (4) The dip pipe and stilling well are named by the study page; their dimensions are study values. (5) The PVC-U-or-HDPE conditional of duty section 3 is unresolved; PVC-U is drawn. (6) The N6 vent datum 800 predates the 748 box; the vent is drawn flush at the top head. (7) Schedule rating column (Class 150) against the atmospheric hose duty: no flange is drawn. (8) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

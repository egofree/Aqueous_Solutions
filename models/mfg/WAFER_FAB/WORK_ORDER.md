# Work order WAFER_FAB

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-MACH + A-SC). Route class F-MACH (the `fab` class sits in the F-MACH row of plan section 4). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/WAFER_FAB.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/WAFER_FAB.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-EQP-FAB-001 Rev C (wafer-fab equipment list; named by the node continuous-kW note), AQS-MOD-002 (frame system), AQS-BOM-SC-001 (self-copy BOM), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node WAFER_FAB (equipment list).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `wafer_fab.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1000 × 600 × 800 (study default envelope box; the size_note defers design sizing to the duty sheet, and the duty section 2 states no envelope). The build fills it exactly: measured 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm, one closed manifold mesh. A four-post hold-down frame (40 × 40 posts + top rails) pins the registry box — the HX8 idiom, so the X-PAR-04 envelope registry reads the build.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn as the unit per the node equipment list (the calculated connected load): the stations are purchased-class tool envelopes per plan rule 6 — CZ puller (0.2317 kW), ingot cool/anneal station (0.02 kW), crop/grind/edge tools (0.04 kW), FAB_TOOL dry row ox/RTA/metal/probe standby (0.12 kW) — plus the N2 purge distribution (0.03 kW) drawn as the DN15 header with four drop legs. Station internals are not invented; vendor prints govern (hold, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 14 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/WAFER_FAB.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube stubs flush at the envelope faces: inlets on −Y at z 160 — N1 DN15 N2 purge, N2 DN15 B/P/As concentrate (BBr₃ / POCl₃ class), N3 DN15 colloidal silica slurry, N4 DN25 fab UPW, N11/N12/N14 (the solids transfer rows: specialty stock, polymer feedstock, dopant); outlets on +Y at z 600 — N5 DN15 spent slurry and etch liquor, N6 DN15 solvent and photoresist, N7 DN15 kerf, N8 DN25 spent UPW, N13 DN15 spent resist and solvent; N9 DN15 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N10 DN15 vent flush at the envelope top (800 datum is the box top). The four `solids` rows are non-piped transfer rows drawn DN15, bore not calculated (plan W4). Atmospheric tube or hose per duty section 4 (nozzle DN15, no flange class); no flange hardware is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M12 wedge anchors (plinth to slab, 2 per frame post line), count from the model. 8.8 on carbon steel per the grade rule; lengths and patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Wetted connection hardware 316L per duty section 3 ("316L where the stream is wet"; CA 0 mm, AQS-STD-001 rule). Plinth, deck and frame carbon steel A500 (AQS-MOD-002 dry service, dry structure outside the wetted hardware). Station envelopes are purchased items (plan rule 6): no material is invented for them; the vendor print governs.
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6: "Atmospheric. No vent edge. Not to the room."). No test pressure is claimed beyond a shop leak check of the welded connections.
W8 Exports. `export/wafer_fab.step` (assembly); `export/wafer_fab.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/WAFER_FAB.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. Masses in `check.json`: plinth 94.2 kg, deck 73.3 kg, frame 76.4 kg, station envelopes 658.1 kg (study envelope mass at carbon-steel density, apportioned by envelope volume in the BOM basis), purge 3.4 kg, nozzles 1.0 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make rows (shop-fabricable, flagged for AQS-BOM-SC-001): bench deck + plinth, hold-down/guard frame, purge distribution, nozzle set. Buy rows (purchased per plan rule 6, ratings from the node equipment list): the four station envelopes.
W10 Fabrication route. F-MACH (plan section 4: frame weldment, guards, purchased drive or electrical by rating, anchors): bench and frame weldment, purge header cut and branch welds, nozzle set-in, anchors drilled and set, shop leak check; the purchased stations mount on the deck by their vendor interfaces. On-site tool: the metal shop.
W11 Self-copy relevance. A-SC applies (tier 7): the fab row makes the plant's wafers (the sensor and PV feedstock path of AQS-BOM-SC-001), and the parts of the unit the on-site shop can make — bench deck, plinth, hold-down frame, purge distribution, connection hardware — are flagged make in the BOM and cross-referenced to AQS-BOM-SC-001. The station tools themselves are purchased envelopes (vendor prints govern).
W12 Holds, named. (1) The envelope is the study default registry box (node size_note); neither the node nor the duty section 2 states a design envelope — the build fills the box via the hold-down frame (X-PAR-04); nothing resized (plan rule 3). (2) The station arrangement is the layout choice (station positions in `params.envelope.stations`); the node names stations and ratings, not arrangement. (3) Station internals are not drawn: the node equipment list names stations and kW, not internals; vendor prints govern (plan rule 6). (4) The station envelope masses are study figures at carbon-steel density, apportioned by envelope volume in the BOM basis. (5) AQS-EQP-FAB-001 section 5 names the full process train (CROP_GRIND, ID_WIRE_SAW, LAP_CMP and the rest): those tags are process names, not yet solids (the page says so itself); the drawn stations are the node equipment list. (6) The node note (ISO 14644-1 Class 7 wet bench, Class 6 tool row) is a room-side classification carried as text; no clean-room hardware is modeled. (7) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication. (8) The four solids schedule rows are non-piped transfers drawn DN15, bore not calculated (plan W4). (9) The 0.4417 kW station sum is the node equipment figure; no electrical design is claimed here.

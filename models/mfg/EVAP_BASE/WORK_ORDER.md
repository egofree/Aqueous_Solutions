# Work order EVAP_BASE

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core). Route class hold (evaporator carries no section 4 class). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/EVAP_BASE.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/EVAP_BASE.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-STD-002, `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node EVAP_BASE (design and relief blocks written by `flow_graph/param/evap_design.py`, X-MFG-30, AQS-DUTY-060), A-M-106/107 (the in-house MVR basis).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `evap_base.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` and the node design block; it rebuilds every solid. The unit folder owns its model script (AQS-PLAN-006 W1), so the style pass never clobbers this build; the former `evap_mvr.py EVAP_BASE` expansion is retired from `build_all.py` (the EVAP_ACID branch is unchanged). The `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks of `params.json` are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1250 × 800 × 3100 (set with the design by evap_design, X-MFG-30: body plus interchanger and compressor frame). Measured 1250 × 800 × 3101 mm (the vent riser stands 0.5 mm proud of the separator cap and the drain boss 0.5 mm below the plinth, inside the 1 mm gate). The residual x/y margin is framing (the blower and interchanger envelopes sit inside it) - recorded, nothing resized.
W3 Internals. The X-MFG-30 MVR falling-film idiom in nickel for the NaOH service: DN350 body (sump 400 + tube zone 1500 + head 150), the 20-tube nickel bundle ID 25 × OD 29 × 1500 mm at the design triangular pitch (1.25 × OD) carrying the booked 2.33 m² at +1.1 % (A-HX: the node carries the 20.95 kW evap duty, so the booked area is the calculated figure, A = latent / (U 1500 × 6 K), AQS-DUTY-060 section 1); vapour separator DN350 × 975 with 100 mm mesh pad; MVR blower envelope (ratio 1.616, 1.189 kW shaft, X-MFG-32 line) and feed/condensate/product interchanger envelope (3.67 kW, 0.146 m², outlets 50.6 °C) per plan rule 6; two relief-valve envelopes per the node relief_basis.
W4 Nozzles. All 8 rows of the issued schedule are carried at tag, face, height and bore: the six piped rows draw as nickel tube stubs that pierce to the shell bore (real takeoffs; the three +Y outlet rows share z 600 and spread along the shell axis), N6 is the drain boss through the plinth, N7 the vent riser on the separator top cap; N5 (purchased NaOH flake make-up) and N8 (power terminal) are text rows with no stub. The 800 vent datum is the schedule placeholder (hold, W12(4)). DN stays the line-list bore (duty section 4); per-tag notes in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: M16 studs at the tubesheet-access flanged joints (austenitic, nickel service) and M12 plinth anchors. Study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: nickel 200 wetted parts in hot NaOH; no aluminium, zinc or PVC above 60 °C; CA not applicable to nickel (AQS-STD-001 rule); frame carbon steel (dry).
W7 Pressure. Body 100 kPa g and full vacuum, shell 350 kPa g (node design); relief per the node relief_basis (body fire case, shell blocked outlet, API 526 D orifices, sized in-house, AQS-DUTY-060 section 3); vent to ACID_POLISH_SCRUB. Hydrotest at 1.43 × design is a route operation (W10). Instruments per duty section 5 (density/conductivity control, level, temperature, compressor current).
W8 Exports. `export/evap_base.step` (compound of 33 closed solids); `export/evap_base.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/EVAP_BASE.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1250 × 800 × 3101 mm against the node envelope: inside (1 mm gate). The nominal recirculation sump 82.9 L is reported in `check.json`; no residence time claimed (A-VES rule).
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: body, bundle, separator, connections, plinth. Buy: mesh pad, blower, interchanger, recirculation pump, relief valves.
W10 Fabrication route. Nickel shell roll and weld (GTAW, nickel-qualified procedures), tubesheet drill and joint, bundle assembly at the design pitch, separator and mesh pad fit, blower set and balanced (X-MFG-32 line), interchanger mount, hydro at 1.43 × design, then the relief-valve set. On-site tool: the metal shop pressure-work bay with the nickel-weld station.
W11 Self-copy relevance. Nickel tube work and sheet lining are in-house lines (X-MFG-32); the blower casting and balancing are noted on the AQS-BOM-SC-001 bought-out list (the rotating set is shop-balanced on the in-house machine).
W12 Holds, named. (1) The evaporator class carries no AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 route class (the tracker row reads "hold: no section 4 class"); the fabrication route above follows the F-VES practice. (2) The bundle area drawn (2.356 m²) is +1.1 % on the booked 2.33 m² - the writer tube count is a ceil (20 tubes of the 19.78 minimum); the U 1500 W/(m² K) and 6 K drive are the AQS-DUTY-060 section 1 class, replaced by the pilot U and blower-efficiency data when they exist (A-M-106). (3) The blower, interchanger, recirculation pump and relief valves draw as purchased envelopes and interfaces; ratings come from the node design (X-MFG-30) and the maker sheets at order (plan rule 6, no vendor basis is a design input). (4) The vent schedule datum (top, 800) is the placeholder elevation: the riser stands on the separator top cap and flushes at the envelope top. (5) The single MVR train carries no redundancy clause on the node (unlike VCD_TRAIN's 2 × 100 %): the NaOH loop rides the BMED product book; a redundancy decision is the owner's, not a model change. (6) Bench leach at 20 wt% and the pilot U are open duty items (duty section 8); they gate the material coupon and the U class, not the geometry. (7) Fastener lengths, torque pattern and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

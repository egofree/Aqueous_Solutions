## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `vcd_train.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` and the node design block; it rebuilds every solid. The `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks of `params.json` are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1400 × 600 × 1200 (the issued envelope, duty sections 2 and 4). The build fills it: measured 1400 × 600 × 1201 mm (the vent riser stands 0.5 mm proud of the bay-0 separator cap and the drain boss 0.5 mm below the plinth, both inside the 1 mm gate).
W3 Internals. Both installed modules (2 × 100 %, X-RED-05) draw: each a vertical MVC still vessel (sump, tubesheet pair, separator ring, top cap) with the falling-film bundle as 18 tube solids ID 12 × OD 14 × 890 mm at running clearance in the bore, the demister pad in the separator, and the scroll-class compressor as its purchased envelope (plan rule 6). The bundle fills the booked 0.603 m² at +0.1 % (A-HX: the node carries the 8.436 kW latent duty, so the booked area is the calculated figure, latent / (U 2.8 × 5 K), PERRY9_EVAP class).
W4 Nozzles. All 11 rows of the issued schedule are carried at tag, face, height and bore: the nine piped rows draw as 60 mm atmospheric open-bore stubs flush at the skid faces (duty section 4: hose, atmospheric; DN copied from the edge; inlets −Y at 160, outlets +Y at 600), N8 is the drain boss through the plinth, N9 the vent riser on the bay-0 separator cap. The schedule elevations draw as issued. Per-tag positions in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: M10 studs at the separator-ring flanged joints (4 per joint, 316L) and M10 plinth anchors. Study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: 316L where the stream is wet, corrosion allowance 0 mm (AQS-STD-001 rule); frame carbon steel, dry side.
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric skid (duty section 6: stored design_kPa is not a set point); the MVC loop runs at the compressor ratio inside the vessel pair. Level and temperature instruments per duty section 5; high level trips the inlet.
W8 Exports. `export/vcd_train.step` (compound of 52 closed solids); `export/vcd_train.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/VCD_TRAIN.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1400 × 600 × 1201 mm against the node envelope: inside (1 mm gate). The sump holdup 13.8 L across both modules equals the duty section 2 working-volume book (2 h of distillate); reported, no residence time claimed beyond the duty's own (A-VES rule).
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: vessels, bundles, connections, plinth. Buy: demister pads, scroll compressors.
W10 Fabrication route. Shell roll and weld (GTAW, ASME IX qualified procedures), tubesheet drill and expansion, bundle assembly, separator fit, hydro leak test of the vessel pair, then the compressor set. On-site tool: the metal shop pressure-work bay.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the still makes water; it makes no plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The evaporator class carries no AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 route class (the tracker row reads "hold: no section 4 class"); the fabrication route above follows the F-VES practice. (2) The compressor is drawn as its purchased envelope and interface; the maker rating reads the sheet at order (plan rule 6; no vendor basis is a design input). (3) The bundle area drawn (0.6039 m²/module) is +0.1 % on the booked 0.603 m² (latent / (U 2.8 × 5 K)); the U and ΔT are the PERRY9_EVAP class on the node design, replaced by the pilot test data when they exist (A-M-149 verification). (4) The sump holdup 13.8 L equals the duty working-volume book; the duty's "it is not full" sentence stays the control-band statement, not a fill requirement. (5) The schedule elevations (160/600/800) are the issued datums, drawn as issued. (6) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

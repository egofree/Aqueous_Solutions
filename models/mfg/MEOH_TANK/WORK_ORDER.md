# Work order MEOH_TANK

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-VES). Route class F-VES. Graph revision per `params.json` `graph_revision`. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/MEOH_TANK.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/MEOH_TANK.csv`, `docs-authority/fab/MEOH_TANK.md` (AQS-MFG-001 study page: dip pipe, stilling well), the node design_storage block (AQS-DUTY-064), AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node MEOH_TANK.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `meoh_tank.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 600 × 600 × 600, set by docs-authority/_storage_sizing.py (AQS-DUTY-064); the node design_storage block books the nominal storage basis (hold_d 14, V_nom_m3 0.02, D 300, H 300, wall_mm 3.0, SA-240 304L). The duty sheet sections 2 and 4 still carry the unit-task-04 "Size 800 × 800 × 1100 mm" shell line - the node governs (the graph is the sole topology authority), that line is a named hold, nothing resized (W12). The build fills the node box: measured 600.0 × 600.0 × 600.0 mm, one closed manifold solid.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The AQS-MFG-001 study page issues the internals: dip pipe OD 60 × wall 4 hung from the top head to z 100, inside a stilling well OD 110 × wall 5 from the floor to above the working level, exactly as CATCH_TANK (crude methanol feeds down the dip pipe, the well damps the inlet jet for the level instruments). Dimensions are study values - the study page names the internals, no authority sizes them (holds, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 13 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/MEOH_TANK.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore: N1..N4 feeds in on −Y at z 160, N5..N8 and N11..N13 draws out on +Y at z 600, N9 drain boss on the base head to its 50 datum, N10 vent flush at the top head - its 800 datum predates the 600 box (hold, W12). Atmospheric joints are hose barbs (duty section 4); no flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. Per-tag positions in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M10 wedge anchors, plinth to slab, one per corner (count from the model). ISO 3506 A4-70 by the plan grade rule on 304L. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication. The welded joints carry no fastener row.
W6 Materials. SA-240 304L, welded (duty section 3 / node design_storage). Corrosion allowance 0 mm (AQS-STD-001). Wall 3.0 mm is the node design_storage booking (AQS-DUTY-064 nominal basis); the fab study page's "6 mm on metal" predates the node booking - the node governs, the older figure named (hold, W12).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric (duty section 6; the N10 vent row is a schedule row, not a relief edge). Not to the room. Level and temperature are the duty section 5 instruments (high level trips the inlet; loop hardware, not modeled). No hydrotest pressure is claimed; the leak test is a shop discretion of the route (W10).
W8 Exports. `export/meoh_tank.step` (assembly); `export/meoh_tank.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/MEOH_TANK.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 600.0 × 600.0 × 600.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. Liquor holdup at the working level net of the internals reported in `check.json`.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make: shell, well, dip pipe and hardware are in-house fabrication.
W10 Fabrication route. F-VES: 304L plate cut (laser or shear), roll the shell, gas-tungsten-arc weld the seam, heads and set-in ports, nozzle set-in welded, anchors drilled, hydro or leak test of the shell. On-site tool: the MFG shop plate work and weld shop (AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 F-VES operations).
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the tank stores methanol product; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The duty sheet shell line 800 × 800 × 1100 (sections 2 and 4) is a unit-task-04 placeholder against the node AQS-DUTY-064 box - the node governs, nothing resized. (2) The wall: node design_storage books 3.0 mm; the fab study page says 6 mm on metal - the node booking governs, the older figure named. (3) The dip pipe and stilling well dimensions are study values. (4) Schedule rating column (Class 150) against the atmospheric hose duty: no flange is drawn. (5) The model liquor volume at the working level (0.1028 m³) is reported against the duty working volume (0.0421 m³, 7 days) and the design_storage nominal (0.02 m³) - the booked basis stays the duty figure, no residence time derived (A-VES). (6) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

# Work order SHELL_BREAK

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-VES). Route class F-VES (the `separator` class sits in the F-VES row of plan section 4). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/SHELL_BREAK.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/SHELL_BREAK.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-DUTY-072 (sizing study), A-M-286 (knock-out split), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node SHELL_BREAK (design block). LIVE unit - the investment-casting shell knock-out station behind INV_CAST.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `shell_break.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid: frame and feed deck, vibratory table and drive, reclaim sieve and pan, enclosure hood, and the schedule hardware. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `assumptions` and `design` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1500 × 800 × 1500 (AQS-DUTY-072 study box). The build fills it: measured 1500.0 × 800.0 × 1500.0 mm from the export STL - the flush-hardware build keeps every connection group inside the faces (check.json `connection_overhang_mm` 0/0/0/0 mm, A-core 2).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid; the machine the design block names (vibratory shell knock-out station with reclaim sieve) is drawn as the unit per the duty sheet: vibratory table 700 × 400 × 60 at z 610 with the 0.4 kW drive under it, inclined reclaim sieve with the mesh window, reclaim pan, and the dust enclosure hood. A-VES: the model holdup is the reclaim pan, 5.2 L at the drawn geometry - reported, and not used as a residence time (the duty carries the A-M-286 mass split, not a holdup basis).
W4 Nozzles. All 7 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/SHELL_BREAK.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore, flush at the envelope faces: N1 cast + shell inlet (solids row, bore not calculated per W4) on −X at z 160; N2 investment castings and N3 spent ceramic shell on +X at z 600; N4 reclaimed stucco/flour and N5 spent foundry silica on +Y at z 600 (solids rows, bore not calculated); N6 DN15 drain flush at the bottom (50 datum); N7 DN15 vent on the service mast at the z 1500 datum. Atmospheric solids chutes; no flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06 - hold, W12).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M10 wedge anchors (frame to slab), 4 M10 table mounts (vibratory table isolation mounts), 8 M8 hood bolts (enclosure to the posts); counts from the model. 8.8 on carbon steel per the grade rule (AQS-PLAN-006 W5); lengths, isolation-mount selection and patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: carbon steel frame, table, sieve, pan and enclosure; the sieve mesh window and the chute throats carry the refractory/wear class (spent silica is abrasive). CA 0 mm - dry, non-hydrocarbon solids service (AQS-STD-001 shop rule).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric station; the duty section 6 relief case is pending (unit task 07) and no set point is claimed. Dust control is the enclosure hood (N7 vent to the house extraction - loop hardware, not modeled; hold, W12). The table-vibration survey is the W10 test.
W8 Exports. `export/shell_break.step` (assembly, 1 unioned solid); `export/shell_break.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/SHELL_BREAK.stl` replaced from the in-envelope build solid (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 registry; the N1..N5 groups are the check.json `external_parts` record, drawn flush). Bounding box 1500.0 × 800.0 × 1500.0 mm against the node envelope: filled exactly, nothing resized. Masses in `check.json`: frame 636.9 kg, table + drive 144.3 kg, sieve + pan 58.1 kg, enclosure 98.9 kg, hardware the remainder.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make; there is no purchased row on the model - the vibratory drive is bought with the table at fabrication (the 0.4 kW rating is the node design row; the maker unit mounts under the drawn table envelope).
W10 Fabrication route. F-VES (plan section 4, the `separator` class): plate or sheet cut, form, weld or join, machine, test - in order: plate cut and fold (frame, posts, feed deck, enclosure), weld (frame seams, post set), sieve and pan fabrication with the mesh window, table and drive mount on the isolation mounts, hood set on the posts, chute set-in, then the tests: table vibration survey, dust-hood capture check, and a trial knock-out of an INV_CAST campaign tree. On-site tool: the metal shop welding bay.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the station reclaims the shell shop's own stucco/flour, it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The envelope is the AQS-DUTY-072 study box; the sizing study gives the A-M-286 mass split, not a machine design. (2) Frame sections, table size, sieve slope and the enclosure sheet thickness are study values; no authority states a section. (3) The vibratory drive unit is named by its node rating (0.4 kW table_kW); the maker unit mounts under the drawn table envelope at fabrication. (4) Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06); the N7 vent rides the service mast at the 1500 datum. (5) The relief case is pending (unit task 07); no instrument loop is issued (unit task 08). (6) The dust-extraction tie-in to the house scrubber train is loop hardware, not modeled. (7) Fastener lengths, isolation-mount selection and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication. (8) The reclaim-pan volume (5.2 L) is the drawn geometry, not a holdup basis (A-VES rule: no residence time claimed).

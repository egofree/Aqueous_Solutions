## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `extrude.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid: plinth, press frame, container group, main ram, billet loader and run-out chute, die-zone guard rail, HPU and control-pedestal envelopes, and the schedule hardware. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1500 × 800 × 1500 (A-M-199 forming class N+1 rated envelope, A-M-282). The build fills it: measured 1500.0 × 800.0 × 1500.0 mm, all connection hardware flush at the envelope faces, nothing protrudes.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid.
W4 Nozzles. All 4 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/EXTRUDE.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore connections flush at the envelope faces (duty section 4: hose, atmospheric): N1 solids billet row on the −X face at z 160 riding the billet loader, and N2 solids rod/profile row on the +X face at z 600 on the run-out chute nose, both drawn at DN15 and labeled bore not calculated (W4 rule); N3 DN15 drain through the plinth, flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N4 DN15 vent riser, flush at the envelope top (its 800 datum is the schedule placeholder - hold, W12). The schedule faces map to compass faces by the registered layout choice (params.json layout block). No flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated at the face (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M16 wedge anchors, plinth to slab (8.8 on carbon steel), 8 M24 stud-and-nut sets on the tie-rod nuts and die-holder clamp (8.8 on carbon steel). Lengths and torque are study values, confirmed at fabrication. The ram/cylinder press fit carries no fastener row.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: H13 extrusion die (consumable buy row) + tool-steel container liner on the press frame (CS platens and tie rods, study sections). CA 0 mm - dry hot-work contact, no corrosive stream (AQS-STD-001 shop rule). The nitrided die land where galling is measured is the duty first-article item, a process note, not geometry.
W7 Pressure. The press hydraulic circuit is the purchased HPU package at the study working pressure; no design_kPa is booked on the node and none is claimed (duty section 6: relief case pending, unit task 07 - hold, W12). Not to the room.
W8 Exports. `export/extrude.step` (assembly); `export/extrude.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/EXTRUDE.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1500.0 × 800.0 × 1500.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly, zero face overhang.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: plinth, frame, container group, loader and chute, guard, nozzle set. Buy: the H13 die (consumable), the HPU skid and the control pedestal (node design rating, drawn as envelopes per plan rule 6); the hydraulic cylinder package purchases at fabrication (W12). The standby state is named in the BOM rows and the build log.
W10 Fabrication route. Route hold (W12); operations named so the shop can bid it: platen machine and tie-rod bore, container liner turn and shrink-fit, die holder machine, loader and chute weld, plinth weld and anchors, guard set, then HPU mount, hydraulic leak test and a dummy-billet stroke trial before release to standby. On-site tool: the metal shop machine bay with the welding bay.
W11 Self-copy relevance. A-SC applies (tier 7): the press extrudes the profile stock the shop fabricates from, which is the AQS-BOM-SC-001 feedstock path; the parts of the machine itself the on-site shop can make - frame, container housing, loader, chute, guard - are flagged make in the BOM and cross-referenced to AQS-BOM-SC-001. The H13 die is the purchased consumable.
W12 Holds, named. (1) No AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 route class for class `press`; the tracker carries the route hold and W10 names the operations anyway. (2) The frame sections and ram OD are study values; no thickness is booked on the node. (3) The N4 vent datum 800 is the schedule placeholder. (4) The two solids rows (N1, N2) are drawn at DN15, bore not calculated (W4 rule). (5) The hydraulic cylinder package and HPU internals are purchased; the maker ratings read the sheets at order. (6) The relief case is pending (unit task 07). (7) The nitrided die land is a first-article measurement item (duty section 3). (8) No instrument loop is issued for this unit yet (duty section 8). (9) Fastener lengths and torque are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

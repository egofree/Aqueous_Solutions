Authority: `docs-authority/duties/ZNBR2_BR2_REGEN.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/ZNBR2_BR2_REGEN.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-STD-002 section 3.1 (halogen class), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node ZNBR2_BR2_REGEN (design block written by `flow_graph/param/ess_tf.py`, X-MFG-43b), AQS-DUTY-061 (house column internals classes), X-PAR-02 TF (the live purge book).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `znbr2_br2_regen.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` and the node design block; it rebuilds every solid. The `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks of `params.json` are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 400 × 400 × 3700 (set with the design by ess_tf, X-MFG-43b). The build fills it: measured 400 × 400 × 3701 mm (the vent riser stands 0.5 mm proud of the top cap and the drain boss 0.5 mm below the plinth, both inside the 1 mm gate). The duty section 4 header still carries the seed study box (400 × 400 × 1500) - hold, W12(5).
W3 Internals. All solids (A-COL): perforated packing support plate (50 % open envelope) at z 490..500, the 25 mm PVC-U Pall-ring bed 3000 mm (499.5..3499.5, drawn as its envelope at the bulk class), the drip-pipe liquid distributor (3500..3580) and the mesh demister pad (3590..3690). The sump is the 450 mm house class under the bed - hardware, not a residence time derived from the gas mass (duty section 2 rule; the holdup is reported in `check.json`, A-VES rule).
W4 Nozzles. All 4 rows of the issued schedule are carried: N1 Br2-vapour inlet DN50 on −Y at z 400 and N2 NaBr-return outlet DN50 on +Y at z 1500 draw as solvent-welded PVC-U socket stubs flush at the skid faces (duty section 4: hose, atmospheric; DN copied from the edge); N3 is the drain boss through the plinth slot; N4 is the DN50 vent riser on the top cap, flush at the envelope top (the 2000 datum is the schedule placeholder - hold, W12(4)). No liquor inlet is drawn: the scrub liquor loop is the S69B return (duty section 2: no liquor inlet). Per-tag notes in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: two M8 U-bolt hold-down clamps (stainless at the polymer, supplier rule named) and the solvent-cement joint system row (ASTM D2564) - the construction has no bolted process joints (solvent-welded PVC-U, duty section 3).
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: PVC-U shell, sump, liquor piping and packing; FKM gaskets; no metal wetted part. AQS-STD-002 section 3.1: PVDF excluded above pH 12, PP excluded for halogens - the demister mesh is PVC (coupon test with the hypobromite rating, W12(3)). Corrosion allowance not applicable to PVC-U (AQS-STD-001 polymer rule).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric column on the ZNBR2 headspace purge (35.9 m³/h at the 25 % LFL book, NFPA 69). Bed ΔP is the duty instrument (high ΔP trips the fan, duty section 5). The stored design_kPa idiom of duty section 6 is not a set point.
W8 Exports. `export/znbr2_br2_regen.step` (compound of 12 closed solids); `export/znbr2_br2_regen.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/ZNBR2_BR2_REGEN.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 400 × 400 × 3701 mm against the node envelope: inside (1 mm gate). The nominal sump holdup 13.6 L is reported in `check.json`; no residence time is derived (duty section 2 rule; A-VES rule).
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: shell, support, packing rings (X-MFG-32 cut-ring class), distributor, connections, plinth. Buy: demister mesh.
W10 Fabrication route. F-COL: column shell (PVC-U pipe cut, solvent-weld per ASTM D2564), packing support and distributor fabrication, ring cutting on the X-MFG-32 jellybean line, demister fit, gasketed hold-down. On-site tool: the plastics bay.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the tower washes bromine from the battery purge; it makes no plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The duty section 2 gas figure (30.0 m³/h, D 84 mm) predates the X-PAR-02 TF re-solve: the live purge book is 35.9 m³/h (the 25 % LFL case on the solved store), and the column sizes at the bounding live figure - D_req 92.0 mm, PVC-U DN100; the duty sheet keeps its authored §2 text (generated artifacts are not hand-edited), so this hold carries the correction. (2) HTU 0.5 m on 25 mm rings and NTU 6 stay the duty-held figures (duty section 8: HTU is a packing figure, not a measured transfer unit); the 99.75 % removal claim is the duty's own irreversibility assumption. (3) The demister mesh material (PVC class per the AQS-STD-002 halogen exclusion of PP) and the wetted PVC-U/FKM path stand on the coupon test with the hypobromite rating (duty section 3). (4) The vent schedule datum (top, 2000) is the placeholder elevation: the riser stands on the top cap and flushes at the envelope top. (5) The duty section 4 header size line (400 × 400 × 1500, the placement seed study box) predates the design envelope; the node size_mm (3700) is the design authority and the placement block under-books the column height by 2.2 m - the named consequence for the placement lane. (6) The ring bed draws as its envelope at the bulk class (380 kg/m³ study figure for cut PVC rings); the ring count is a fabrication output of the X-MFG-32 cutting pass.

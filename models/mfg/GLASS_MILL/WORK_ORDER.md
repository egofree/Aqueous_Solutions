# Work order GLASS_MILL

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-PIPE). Route class F-PIPE (the `intra` class sits in the F-PIPE row of plan section 4). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/GLASS_MILL.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/GLASS_MILL.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-PIP-003/004 (line list), AQS-MOD-002 (frame system), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node GLASS_MILL.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `glass_mill.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 800 × 600 × 800 ("issued envelope"). The build stays inside it exactly: measured 800.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm, one closed manifold mesh.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn as the unit: a horizontal 316L jar OD 350 × 500 between trunnion journals in two V-roll cradle stands over a 20 mm plinth (the ported AQS-MFG-001 form `models/openscad/detail/mfg_mill.scad`: jar + media). The media charge is not drawn (internals none on the node).
W4 Nozzles. All 6 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/GLASS_MILL.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore: N1 DN25 rinsed cullet and N6 solids (spent ceramic shell to the solids line — a non-piped transfer drawn DN15, bore not calculated, plan W4) on −Y at z 160; N2 DN25 milled cullet and N3 DN15 mill dust on +Y at z 600; N4 DN15 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum is the schedule placeholder); N5 DN15 vent flush at the envelope top (800 datum is the box top). Atmospheric joints are hose (duty section 4): hose barbs; no flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M10 wedge anchors (plinth to slab, 4 per stand station) and 4 M12 roll-shaft bolts (rolls to pedestal), counts from the model. 8.8 on carbon steel per the grade rule; lengths and patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Jar, heads, journals and nozzles 316L (duty section 3 "316L where the stream is wet"); plinth and stands carbon steel A500 (AQS-MOD-002 dry service class). CA 0 mm on the 316L (AQS-STD-001 rule); the dry carbon-steel structure carries no aqueous chloride and takes no CA.
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6). No test pressure is claimed. Mill dust leaves on N3 to BAGHOUSE (issued solids line, not piped — conveyor or hand per the line list).
W8 Exports. `export/glass_mill.step` (assembly); `export/glass_mill.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/GLASS_MILL.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 800.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, exact. Masses in `check.json`: plinth 75.4 kg, jar 46.2 kg, stands 147.3 kg, hardware 1.0 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make: jar, stands, plinth and hardware are in-house fabrication.
W10 Fabrication route. F-PIPE (plan section 4: spool cut, join, support, line list) applied to the machine hardware: jar plate rolling and seam weld, end-head set-in, journal machining, roll and stand fabrication, connection stubs, leak check of the finished jar. On-site tool: the metal shop (roll, weld, turn); the OD 350 jar is within the shop lathe swing (study route note, confirmed at fabrication).
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the mill conditions cullet; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) No jar dimension (OD, length, wall) is stated on the node or duty; the jar follows the ported legacy form and the 8 mm wall is a study value. (2) Jar-full volume 0.0438 m³ against the duty section 2 seven-day charge 0.2577 m³: reported, nothing resized (plan rule 3); the charge basis is a capacity figure, not a residence time. (3) The media charge is not drawn: the node carries internals none. (4) The jar drive is not designed: no drive is named on the node or duty; the legacy form names no drive either. (5) Roll fit, pedestal depths and fastener lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication. (6) The N6 solids row is a non-piped transfer drawn DN15, bore not calculated (plan W4).

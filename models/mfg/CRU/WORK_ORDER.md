# Work order CRU

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-VES). Route class F-VES (the `cru` class sits in the F-VES row of plan section 4). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/CRU.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/CRU.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-STD-002 rules M1/M2 (thermoplastic class rules), AQS-MOD-002 (frame system), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node CRU.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `cru.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1000 × 600 × 800 (study default envelope box). The build fills it exactly: measured 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm, one closed manifold mesh. A four-post hold-down frame (40 × 40 posts + top rails) pins the registry box around the vessel — the HX8 idiom, so the X-PAR-04 envelope registry reads the build (the shell itself is the OD 520 construction and does not fill the box alone).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn as the unit: vertical PVC-U vessel — shell OD 520, wall 10 mm (study — the duty states no diameter and no wall; drawn to the envelope, W12), z 40..680, welded top and bottom, on a seat over a 20 mm plinth inside the hold-down frame.
W4 Nozzles. All 12 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/CRU.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube stubs flush at the envelope faces: N1 DN25 scrub liquor (in) and the six gas rows N5–N8 DN15 (cell gas from the Ti/MO/AL/MG pots and Kroll chlorinator), N11/N12 DN15 (the fluoropolymer HCl returns) on −Y at z 160; N2 DN25 sodium chlorate liquor (out) and N9/N10 DN15 chlorine (to VDF_SYN/TFE_SYN) on +Y at z 600; N3 DN15 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N4 DN15 vent flush at the envelope top (800 datum is the box top). Atmospheric tube or hose per duty section 4 (nozzle DN15, no flange class); no flange hardware is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. The gas rows stay on their gas edges, not the room (duty sections 5 and 6).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M12 wedge anchors (plinth to slab, 2 per frame post line), count from the model. 8.8 on carbon steel per the grade rule; length and pattern are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Vessel, seat and nozzles PVC-U per duty section 3 ("PVC-U wetted, per AQS-STD-002 rules M1 and M2. No metal wetted part"); CA does not apply to the polymer (AQS-STD-001 rule). Frame and plinth carbon steel A500 (AQS-MOD-002 dry service, dry structure outside the wet vessel). The temperature watch is a named hold (W12): the graph gives no temperature for this unit; above 60 °C the thermoplastic is CPVC, never metal.
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6). No test pressure is claimed beyond a fill-and-hold leak check of the finished vessel.
W8 Exports. `export/cru.step` (assembly); `export/cru.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/CRU.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. Masses in `check.json`: plinth 94.2 kg, frame 76.4 kg, vessel 24.3 kg, nozzles 0.2 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make: vessel, frame, plinth and hardware are in-house fabrication.
W10 Fabrication route. F-VES applied to the thermoplastic shop: PVC-U sheet cut and rolled, extrusion welds of the seams, heads and seat (DVS 2207 class practice), nozzle set-in welds, frame weldment, fill-and-hold leak check. On-site tool: the thermoplastic shop.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the vessel finishes chlorate liquor and recovers chlorine; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The duty states no vessel diameter and no wall: the shell is drawn to the node envelope (OD 520, wall 10 study), nothing resized (plan rule 3). (2) A-VES: the vessel holds 104.1 L to 100 mm below the top weld; the duty states "No holdup basis for this class", so no residence time is derived. (3) Temperature watch: the graph gives no temperature for this unit; above 60 °C the thermoplastic is CPVC, never metal (duty section 3) — carried, nothing resized. (4) Fastener lengths and frame fit are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

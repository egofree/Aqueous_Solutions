# Work order BAGHOUSE

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-MACH). Route class F-MACH. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/BAGHOUSE.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/BAGHOUSE.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-STD-002 section 4.3 (combustible dust), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node BAGHOUSE (design block written by `flow_graph/param/specialty_sizing.py`, X-SPEC lane, A-M-458), AQS-DSN-INT-001 disposition (the pulse-jet cartridge basis this model realises).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `baghouse.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` and the node design block; it rebuilds every solid. The `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks of `params.json` are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1250 × 1000 × 2276 (set with the design by specialty_sizing, X-SPEC). Measured 1250 × 1000 × 2276.5 mm (the vent riser stands 0.5 mm proud of the roof, inside the 1 mm gate; the drain boss sits 0.5 mm below the plinth). The placement seed block (1500 × 1000 × 1500) under-books the design height by 0.78 m - hold, W12(1).
W3 Internals. The pulse-jet machine as solids: 3 mm CS housing (painted, bonded, grounded) with roof, the tubesheet with the 20-bag hole field, the 20 antistatic-polyester bags D150 × 1200 (11.31 m² cloth drawn, felt-wall envelopes rooted into the tubesheet), the 60° dust hopper with the DN200 rotary-valve discharge to IA-BAG-01 (drive guard drawn), the DN40 pulse-clean header with 5 diaphragm-valve envelopes and the solenoid enclosure, and the deflagration vent panel on the +X wall per the combustible-dust class.
W4 Nozzles. All 7 rows of the issued schedule are carried at tag, face, height and bore: the three dust draws (N1-N3) as DN15 stubs flush at the inlet-plenum face (−Y), N4 the captured-dust edge at its +Y datum, N5 the drain boss through the plinth, N6 the vent riser on the roof, and N7 (dust, solids row) carried verbatim with no stub. Duty section 4 carries the bore note ("0.28 mm is not a specification; nozzle is DN15") - the DN15 rows stay the duty connection table; the hood conveying ducts are the design DN100/DN80 set (hold, W12(5)). Per-tag notes in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: the access-door bolted joint (M10, 8 per face) and the drawn M12 plinth anchors. Study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: carbon steel housing, painted, bonded and grounded; antistatic polyester bags; deflagration venting per the combustible-dust class; dry and ambient so M3 (marine class) does not apply; dust wetted only after the PVC-U sluice break (AQS-STD-002 section 4.3). CA 1.5 mm paint-system class on the CS frame.
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric machine; the baghouse ΔP rides the tool-extraction air mover (duty section 1 books 0.0 kW electrical for this unit - the purchased fan belongs to that lane; hold, W12(3)). Instruments: level and temperature per duty section 5; the hopper level trips the valve service.
W8 Exports. `export/baghouse.step` (compound of 41 closed solids); `export/baghouse.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/BAGHOUSE.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1250 × 1000 × 2276.5 mm against the node envelope: inside (1 mm gate).
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: housing, tubesheet, hopper, duct set, connections, plinth. Buy: bags, rotary valve, pulse diaphragm valves and solenoid.
W10 Fabrication route. F-MACH: frame weldment (sheet cut, brake-form, weld), tubesheet drill, hopper plate-work, paint/bond/ground system, bag and valve fit, duct spools to the three hoods. On-site tool: the metal shop sheet-metal bay.
W11 Self-copy relevance. Sheet-metal work is the shop's own; the duct spools are flagged make on AQS-BOM-SC-001 (self-copy relevant).
W12 Holds, named. (1) The placement seed block (1500 × 1000 × 1500) under-books the design envelope height by 0.78 m - the named consequence for the placement lane; nothing is resized here. (2) The capture-air basis (hood face velocity 0.5 m/s over the 0.20/0.12/0.10 m² hood faces; 756 m³/h; cloth at the 1.2 m³/min/m² EPA-453 basis) is the registered A-M-458 study basis - the measured hood entries and ΔP at the first-article duct run replace it. (3) The unit draws no fan of its own: duty section 1 books 0.0 kW electrical, so the ΔP rides the tool-extraction air mover of that lane; the baghouse hardware states this, it does not omit the mover. (4) The vent schedule datum (top, 800) is the placeholder elevation: the clean-air riser stands on the roof and flushes at the envelope top. (5) The schedule DN15 rows are the duty connection table; the hood conveying ducts are the design DN100/DN80 set (18-20 m/s class, A-M-458), a BOM make row of shop spools, not drawn (they run to the tool hoods outside this unit). (6) The captured-dust edge (N4, +Y at 600) is the duty table row; the dust path is the hopper rotary valve to IA-BAG-01 - the datum is the placeholder. (7) Fastener sizes, pitches and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

# Work order MEOH_FERM

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-VES). Route class F-VES. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/MEOH_FERM.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/MEOH_FERM.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node MEOH_FERM (design block, X-MFG-31 / AQS-DUTY-062).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `meoh_ferm.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` and, for the construction figures, the node design block of the graph; it rebuilds every solid. The `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks of `params.json` are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 425 × 425 × 600 (set by `flow_graph/param/reactor_design.py`, X-MFG-31). The build fills it: measured 425.0 × 425.0 × 600.0 mm; the vent riser tops the box. The duty section 2 prose still names "Envelope 2200 × 900 × 1400 mm" - the node governs and the older figure is named (hold, W12).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The node mixer row names the agitation: a daily recirculation pump, drawn as its purchased envelope (plan rule 6) with the DN15 recirculation loop (pump to top head, base tap to pump). No top-entry agitator is named on the node, so none is drawn (hold, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 6 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/MEOH_FERM.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube connections flush at the envelope faces (duty section 4: hose, atmospheric; the connecting line makes the joint at the face - the CATCH_TANK idiom): N1 ferment feed in on −Y at its 160 datum; N2 biogenic MeOH and N3 fermenter residue out on +Y (schedule datums 600 predates the box, clamped to 570.7 by the od + 8 rule - hold, W12); N4 drain boss under the base head, flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N5 vent riser on the top head, flush at the envelope top (the 800 datum is the schedule placeholder); N6 fermenter digestate carries the line-list classification `solids` (non-piped transfer) in its size column: drawn at DN15, bore not calculated, per plan W4, and labeled. No flange is drawn (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. The along-face positions are the registered layout choice (per tag in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M10 wedge anchors, grade 8.8 on the carbon-steel plinth (plan grade rule), one per corner. Thermoplastic joints carry no fastener row. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Vessel, heads and loop PVC-U per duty section 3 (AQS-STD-002 rules M1 and M2, no metal wetted part); CA does not apply to the polymer (AQS-STD-001). The node T_C 35 °C sits inside the PVC-U class; above 60 °C the thermoplastic is CPVC, never metal (duty section 3). The node design material row "HDPE (PE100)" predates the duty selection and is superseded for the wetted parts (hold, W12). The 50 mm PIR insulation is the node material row (35 kg/m³ study bulk value). Plinth and stand carbon steel (dry support).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6). Not to the room. No test pressure is claimed; the leak test is a fill test at the shop's discretion of the route (W10). Instruments are the duty section 5 loops (temperature), loop hardware, not modeled.
W8 Exports. `export/meoh_ferm.step` (assembly); `export/meoh_ferm.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/MEOH_FERM.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 425.0 × 425.0 × 600.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. The skid chain fuses to one solid; the four face-stub connections stay separate until the connecting lines make their joints (`check.json` `solids_note`). A-VES: the 48 h working volume V = 0.0007 m³ (duty section 2) is an existing holdup basis - the model working level (z 305.1) is set by it and reported; no new residence-time claim.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: vessel, stand, insulation, loop, connections. Buy: recirculation pump and heater mat (envelopes and interfaces per plan rule 6).
W10 Fabrication route. F-VES: sheet/pipe cut, roll, thermoplastic weld (DVS 2207 practice), nozzle set-in, stand weldment and anchors, fill-and-leak test. On-site tool: the thermoplastic shop and the welding bay.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the fermenter makes biogenic methanol; it makes no plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The duty section 2 prose line "Envelope 2200 × 900 × 1400 mm is the shell" predates the node box; the node governs, nothing resized. (2) The node design material row "HDPE (PE100)" is superseded for the wetted parts by duty section 3 (PVC-U per rules M1 and M2); the 50 mm PIR insulation of the same row stays. (3) Agitation is the node mixer row's daily recirculation pump, drawn as its envelope; no top-entry agitator is named on the node and none is drawn. (4) The schedule datums 600 (outlets) predate the 600 box and clamp to 570.7; the 800 vent datum is the schedule placeholder. (5) Row N6 carries `solids` (non-piped transfer): drawn DN15, bore not calculated, per plan W4. (6) The face-stub connections stop at the envelope faces (the CATCH_TANK idiom); the connecting lines make the joints. (7) The PIR bulk density 35 kg/m³ is a study value for mass reporting; the class sheet carries the board at order. (8) The duty section 2 working volume (48 h) is an existing holdup basis; the model level is reported against it and no new residence time is derived. (9) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

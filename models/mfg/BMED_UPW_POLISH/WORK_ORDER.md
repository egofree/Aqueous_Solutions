# Work order BMED_UPW_POLISH

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-MACH). Route class F-MACH (the `skid` class sits in the F-MACH row of plan section 4). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/BMED_UPW_POLISH.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/BMED_UPW_POLISH.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-MOD-002 (frame system), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node BMED_UPW_POLISH (internals row, design block and continuous-kW note; X-MFG-34, A-M-176, AQS-DUTY-070; A-M-072 held).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `bmed_upw_polish.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1000 × 600 × 800 (study default envelope box; the duty section 2 states no envelope). The build fills it exactly: measured 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm, one closed manifold mesh. A hold-down frame (40 × 40 posts + top rails) on the 20 mm plinth pins the registry box — the HX8 idiom, so the X-PAR-04 envelope registry reads the build.
W3 Internals. The node carries the internals row "potable bypass; 2 x flat-sheet RO module, 2 x EDI stack, UV-TOC lamp", so every named internal is a solid: the 2 flat-sheet TFC RO module envelopes (Ø120 × 500, one per train; node design membrane_m2 0.2652 at the 6.859 bar feed, recovery 0.9547; A-M-176 basis 0.27 m²/module at 6.9 bar, rejection 0.98), the 2 EDI stack envelopes (150 × 150 × 400; node EDI_A 0.00973 A / 0.39 W; A-M-176 basis 10 cell pairs at 4 V), the UV-TOC lamp envelope (Ø30 × 400; node UV_kW 0.015 = 15 W), the RO pump envelope (120 × 120 × 150; node continuous-kW note: RO pump 2.6 W), and the potable bypass run (DN25 Sch 40 with risers; node design bypass_kg_d 206.77 — the potable share bypasses RO/EDI, 2 × 100 % trains per A-M-176). The module internals are the X-MFG-34 in-house design task and are not invented: the purchased modules are drawn as their envelope and interface per plan rule 6 (hold, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 7 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/BMED_UPW_POLISH.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube stubs flush at the envelope faces: N1 DN50 VCD distillate in on −Y at z 160; N2 DN25 UPW, N3 DN25 fab UPW, N4 DN15 remineralizer, N5 DN15 polish reject on +Y at z 600; N6 DN15 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N7 DN15 vent flush at the envelope top (800 datum is the box top). Atmospheric hose per duty section 4 ("Atmospheric joints are hose... Bore not calculated"); no flange hardware is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M10 wedge anchors (plinth to slab, 2 per frame post line) and 8 M8 module-clip bolts (modules and pump to the deck clip rail), counts from the model. 8.8 on carbon steel, A4-70 on the 316L-adjacent hardware, per the grade rule; lengths and patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Wetted hardware — bypass run, risers and nozzle stubs — 316L per duty section 3 ("316L where the stream is wet", UPW service; CA 0 mm, AQS-STD-001 rule). Plinth, deck and frame carbon steel A500 (AQS-MOD-002 dry service, dry structure outside the wetted hardware). The purchased module envelopes carry no invented material: the vendor print governs (their BOM masses are study envelope figures at carbon-steel density).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6). The RO feed pressure (6.859 bar, node design) is inside the purchased pump/module envelopes; no pressure boundary is designed here and none is claimed — the pump and module prints govern (hold, W12). No hydrotest; a shop leak check of the 316L runs at fit-out is the route's verification.
W8 Exports. `export/bmed_upw_polish.step` (assembly); `export/bmed_upw_polish.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/BMED_UPW_POLISH.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. Masses in `check.json`: plinth 94.2 kg, deck 73.3 kg, frame 76.4 kg, RO module envelopes 113.0 kg, EDI stack envelopes 141.3 kg, UV lamp 0.8 kg, RO pump 17.0 kg, bypass run 7.2 kg, nozzles 0.8 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make rows: skid deck + plinth, hold-down/guard frame, bypass run, nozzle set. Buy rows (purchased per plan rule 6, ratings from the node): 2 RO modules, 2 EDI stacks, UV-TOC lamp, RO pump.
W10 Fabrication route. F-MACH: frame weldment and deck (AQS-MOD-002), 316L bypass run cut and branch-welded, nozzle set-in, anchors drilled and set, purchased modules/pump/lamp mounted on the clip rail by rating with their vendor interfaces. On-site tool: the metal shop. Commissioning loads the modules (buy rows).
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the skid polishes water; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The envelope is the study default registry box; neither the node nor the duty section 2 states a design envelope — the build fills the box via the hold-down frame (X-PAR-04); nothing resized (plan rule 3). (2) The module, stack, lamp and pump envelope sizes and the piping layout are the layout choice; the node names areas, rates and voltages, not envelope sizes. (3) The module internals are the X-MFG-34 in-house design task (A-M-176 replaced_by); nothing invented inside the purchased envelopes (plan rule 6). (4) The vendor-class energy bases A-M-072 (RO 3.0 / EDI 0.5 kWh/m³) are held pending the X-MFG-34 in-house design and bench test — named per the assumption register. (5) The 2 × 100 % train arrangement (A-M-176) is carried as one module and one stack per train; the changeover valving is inside the purchased groups' prints. (6) The RO feed pressure boundary (6.859 bar) is inside the purchased pump/module prints; no pressure design is claimed here. (7) Fastener lengths and clip pattern are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

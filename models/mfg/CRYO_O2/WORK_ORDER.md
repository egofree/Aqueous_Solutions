# Work order CRYO_O2

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-COL). Route class F-COL. Graph Rev AC. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/CRYO_O2.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/CRYO_O2.csv`, the node design block (`flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node CRYO_O2: rack 6696 × 1500 × 600, OD 114.3 × wall 13.49 × 6.096 m tubes, banks main 14 + crew reserve 2 in 2 halves, steel_kg, relief_mm 9 API 520/521 per half), AQS-STD-002, AQS-STD-001, ASTM G93 (node storage_mode), NFPA 2 ch. 7.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `cryo_o2.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `layout` and `study` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node `size_mm` [6696, 1500, 600] is the rack envelope set by `flow_graph/param/receivers.py` (A-M-167); the build fills it exactly (measured 6696.0 × 1500.0 × 600.1 mm) and `cad/stl/CRYO_O2.stl` is replaced from the build.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`; the machine here is the storage bank itself: 16 cylinder tubes SA-312 TP316L seamless DN100 Sch 160 (OD 114.3 × t 13.49, 6.096 m), all booked tubes drawn on the derived tier grid (lib/rack_grid.py, from the A-M-167 drivers): 2 tiers (z 231/381) × 8 stations (y 225..1275, 150 mm pitch), bores drawn open, grooved tier combs and 3 saddles each. Main bank 14 tubes fills first; crew reserve 2 tubes take the last station of each half on the upper tier, behind divider plates at y 600 / 1200 — separate banks per the node crew_reserve_basis (NFPA 2 ch. 7 separation). Rows y < 750 are relief half 1, rows y > 750 are half 2, halves interleaved so each carries exactly 8 tubes (matching the node fire case per-half exposed area of 17.512 m²). The 2 relief valves are purchased envelopes at the +X end posts (AQS-PLAN-006 rule 6).
W4 Nozzles. All 9 rows of `docs-authority/nozzles/CRYO_O2.csv` are drawn at tag, face and bore on the rack side rails: N1 (z 400) on the mid rail, N7/N9 (z 160) on the bottom rails, N2 (schedule z 1500) and N5/N6/N8 (schedule z 600) clamped to the rail centres, N3 drain as a skid-top stub with its tip at the 50 datum, N4 vent up the −Y tie bar to the 600 rack top. The schedule heights were issued against the 300 × 300 × 2000 study boxes that predate the A-M-167 rack envelope; heights that fit the rack are drawn as issued and every clamp is named in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`. Inlets −Y, outlets +Y (house face mapping). No flange is drawn anywhere: lib/dn.py carries no flange table above Class 150, the node design is 22000 kPa, and the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text - the reconciliation is a hold.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 128 M12 saddle bolts (8 per tube) and 8 M10 wedge anchors (skid to slab), ISO 3506 A4-70 per the plan grade rule on 316L. Counts are study values.
W6 Materials. 316L (duty section 3: condensation, no carbon steel), corrosion allowance 0 mm (AQS-STD-001 rule). Skid 12 mm and SHS 80 × 80 × 5 sections are study sizes; the tier grid (8 per row, 150 mm pitch) is the A-M-167 driver set, not a study value. The bank and the relief valves are O2-cleaned to ASTM G93 (node storage_mode).
W7 Pressure. Design 22000 kPa g (node design_kPa, the receivers.py API 520 blocked-outlet case); relief per the node relief_basis: API 520 governing with the API 521 fire case per half bank, 2 valves × 9 mm bore (rounded from 8.606). The duty section 6 "atmospheric" text predates the receivers.py design and is superseded by the node relief_basis.
W8 Exports. `export/cryo_o2.step` (assembly); `export/cryo_o2.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/CRYO_O2.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Masses in `check.json`: tubes drawn and booked agree at 3333.7 kg on the node 16-tube count, every booked tube drawn on the derived grid (node steel_kg 2760.27 is the sizing record - carried, not recalculated), frame 2454.7 kg (saddles priced once on BOM item 3: 48 solids drawn, 631.1 kg - drawn and booked sets agree), divider plates 82.5 kg (2, one per relief half), relief valve envelopes 51.8 kg (envelope, not shipping mass), nozzles 3.1 kg. Store 134.9 kg O2 at 200 bar (node storage block; crew reserve 6.8 kg separate, X-RED-04 not drawn by the fuel cell).
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Purchased rows (tubes, relief valves) carry the order-time mass note.
W10 Fabrication route. F-COL: SHS and plate cut and welded into the rack frame, combs and saddles welded, tubes seated and strapped (O2-cleaned components handled per ASTM G93), connection stubs welded to the rails, relief valves fitted at the +X end, leak test of the bank. On-site tool: the metal shop; the seamless tubes and relief valves are bought in.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: a storage bank makes no plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The schedule rating column (Class 150) against the node design 22000 kPa: no flange is drawn and no flange table above Class 150 exists in lib/dn.py; the joint reconciliation is the nozzle-schedule unit task. (2) Schedule heights 1500/600/2000/50 predate the A-M-167 rack envelope (600 high); the clamped rows are named per tag in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn` and the datums stay in the schedule. (3) Drawn tube-wall mass 3333.7 kg (16 tubes, all drawn) vs the node steel_kg 2760.27: the node figure is the receivers.py sizing record and is carried; no recalculation. (4) Frame sections (skid 12, SHS 80 × 80 × 5) are study sizes pending the frame check task; the tier grid itself is derived from the A-M-167 drivers and is not a hold. (5) Tube-end fittings (B16.11 Class 9000 / cone-and-thread) are named, not drawn - the end-detail is the piping scope. (6) A-COL packing/distributor/demister: a cylinder bank has none; the bank itself is the machine. (7) Relief-valve make and model are purchase scope; the bore and case are from the node. (8) Interconnecting pigtails and headers between tubes are the piping scope (F-PIPE tier).

# Work order CRYO_ASU

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-COL). Route class F-COL. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/CRYO_ASU.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/CRYO_ASU.csv`, the node design block (`flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node CRYO_ASU: beds/bed_D_mm/bed_L_mm/sorbent_kg_per_bed, regen/blower/co2_comp/co2_chill/getter kW), AQS-STD-002, AQS-STD-001, relief basis API 2000 (A-M-155).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `cryo_asu.py` (CadQuery) reads the graph node through `models/mfg/lib/graph_node.py` and rebuilds every solid; the tracker-written `params.json` copy keeps the builder-owned `layout` and `study` blocks beside the W2 fields and passes through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node `size_mm` [300, 300, 2000] is the M0 study registry box (params `size_note`: "study default envelope box, read back from the cad/stl registry"). The beds at the node design diameter (421.9 mm) cannot fit it: the build measures 2460.0 × 592.1 × 2000.1 mm and the misfit is reported on the tracker board per AQS-PLAN-006 rule 3. Nothing is resized. The z extent honors the 2000 mm schedule datum through the vent standpipe.
W3 Internals. Every internal named on the node is a solid: three 13X TSA beds (shell D 421.9 × L 1265.8 per the node design block, flat heads, a 20 mm support grid under and over the charge), the 13X sorbent charges as solids between the grids (the A-COL packing), the CO2 two-stage compressor and condenser, and the heated Ar getter (purchased envelopes and interfaces with ratings from the node design, AQS-PLAN-006 rule 6). Noble-gas inventories pass through; no inventoried noble-gas hardware is invented.
W4 Nozzles. All 17 rows of `docs-authority/nozzles/CRYO_ASU.csv` are scheduled at tag, face, height and bore; 16 are drawn: N1–N4 (DN25/DN50/DN15/DN15, z 400) on the bed shells' −Y side, N5–N8 (z 1500) on +Y, N11–N16 (z 160) as frame-level rows through the base plinth (the datum sits below the bed shells), N9 drain beside the bed 1 pedestal with its tip at the 50 datum, N10 a DN15 vent standpipe on the bed 1 top head to the 2000 datum. N13/N14/N15 are solids rows drawn at DN15 and labeled bore not calculated. N17 (top, z 800) is the power terminal (cable schedule X-ELS-06, AQS-ELEC-010): electrical, not piped - carried verbatim in `check.json`, never drawn (no STEP, BOM or geometry change). Atmospheric joints are hose barbs: the node relief basis is API 2000 normal venting (open vent, no relief orifice, A-M-155), so no flange is drawn anywhere and the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. Along-bed positions are the registered layout choice (per tag in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 24 M12 bed-pedestal bolts (4 per bed) and 8 M10 wedge anchors (frame to slab), ISO 3506 A4-70 per the plan grade rule on 316L. Counts are study values.
W6 Materials. 316L throughout (duty section 3: condensation, no carbon steel), corrosion allowance 0 mm (AQS-STD-001 rule). Shell t 6 and grid t 20 are the house plate convention, study (the duty states no thickness). 13X molecular sieve bought to the node charge (115.04 kg/bed); the drawn charge is capped by the bed length at the 700 kg/m³ study bulk density.
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric box: node relief basis API 2000 normal venting (A-M-155); stored `design_kPa` 18.0 is the study vapour-plus-hydrostatic design case of the registry vessel (vapour 2.0 kPa at 4 x vent dP + hydrostatic 15.5 kPa, A-M-174), not a set point for the bed shells at this stage. Bed leak test per the F-COL route.
W8 Exports. `export/cryo_asu.step` (assembly); `export/cryo_asu.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/CRYO_ASU.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 2460.0 × 592.1 × 2000.1 mm. Masses in `check.json`: beds 731.9 kg 316L, frame 1916.2 kg (solid-envelope plinth, fabricated as a welded frame), machinery envelopes 1728.2 kg (envelope mass, not the purchased items' shipping mass), nozzles 8.2 kg, sorbent 311.9 kg drawn against 345.1 kg booked.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Purchased rows (sorbent, compressor/condenser, getter) carry the order-time mass note.
W10 Fabrication route. F-COL: 316L sheet and plate cut, shell rolls, TIG welds, grid set-in, pedestal and frame weldment, nozzle set-in, leak test of each bed, sorbent charge and change-out per the duty service hours. On-site tool: the metal shop; the sorbent, compressor, condenser and getter are bought in.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the ASU separates air streams; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The node `size_mm` is the M0 study registry box; the design-sized build leaves it on x and y and the misfit is reported on the board, nothing resized (plan rule 3). (2) The duty section 2 prose ("column diameter 150 mm, 20 trays × 0.3 m, pipe beside the 600×600×800 base") is an earlier design story; the node design block and the internals row (3 × 13X TSA beds, compressor, getter) are the issued design the model draws - the sheet refresh is the duty generator's run. (3) The internals row says CO2 two-stage compressor; the design block carries comp_stages 4 at ratio 2.11 - the named two-stage machine is drawn as its envelope and the staging reconciliation is a hold. (4) Shell thickness 6 mm and grid 20 mm are the house plate convention, study - no thickness is stated by any authority. (5) Sorbent bulk density 700 kg/m³ is a study value: at it the drawn charge (311.9 kg) sits under the booked 345.1 kg because the bed length caps the bed; the vendor TDS governs at order. (6) A-COL distributor and demister: not named on the node for TSA service; none is drawn. (7) The TSA valve/regeneration manifold row between the beds is not drawn - interconnect piping and valving are the piping scope (F-PIPE tier). (8) Hose barb profile, fastener counts and lengths are study proportions. (9) Pedestal and frame sections are study sizes pending the frame check task.

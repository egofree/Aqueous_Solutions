Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-FARM). Route class F-FARM practice named as the study route (the `coop` class has no section 4 row - hold, W12). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/CHICKEN_COOP.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/CHICKEN_COOP.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), A-M-417 (12-bird ration house class), AQS-MOD-002 (frame service classes), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node CHICKEN_COOP.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `chicken_coop.py` (CadQuery, on the shared close-out builder `models/mfg/farm_closeout.py`) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 2500 × 1300 × 1900 - "12 birds x 0.2 m2 floor + nest row + perch at the 0.12 kg/d ration house class (A-M-417)". The rebuild fills the box exactly: measured 2500.0 × 1300.0 × 1900.0 mm, flush on all six faces (A-core 2; A-FARM: not shrunk to fit the block).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid row - the size_basis names the fittings: the frame is drawn as the unit (four 40 × 40 legs to z 1800, drop board at z 100 with the HDPE liner sheet duty section 3 names, wire floor at z 300, hardware-cloth panel bands at z 300..550 and z 1650..1900, sheet roof at z 1800), the nest-row envelope 600 × 400 × 400 at the −X end (purchased, plan rule 6), and the perch the size_basis names (HDG rod OD 40 on two posts rising from the wire floor; study layout, hold W12). The SS316 feeders and waterers duty section 3 names have no rating on the node: not drawn (hold, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 10 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/CHICKEN_COOP.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore DN15 tube stubs flush at the envelope faces: inlets N1 (flock drinking water), N5 (larvae meal ration), N6 (duckweed ration base), N9 (formulated layer ration), N10 (DL-methionine top-dress) on −Y at z 160; outlets N2 (eggs to the food store), N3 (poultry manure to the larvae bin), N4 (flock respiration/evaporation) on +Y at z 600; N7 drain flush at the envelope bottom; N8 vent flush at the envelope top - the schedule z 800 datum predates the 1900 box (stale datum, hold W12). The duty notes the elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06); the coop wall is hardware cloth, so the wall backing for each stub is a fabrication detail (hold, W12). Atmospheric joints are tube/hose; no flange is drawn (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M10 nest-row carriage bolts (8.8 carbon steel per the grade rule; galvanised frame joint) and 4 M10 base anchors (8.8; anchors are not wetted parts). Patterns are study values / the model count, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: hot-dip galvanised steel frame and hardware cloth, SS316 feeders and waterers, HDPE drop-board liner. The wetted class is the wash-down frame; corrosion allowance on the galvanised frame is the galvanizing itself (AQS-STD-001 shop rule; no added CA on the HDPE liner). The hardware cloth is drawn as plate solids; the mesh open area carries ~0.15 of the plate mass (the hardware-cloth idiom).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric; the coop vents at the top and respires through the cloth. Duty section 6 states the relief case is pending and no set point is claimed. Instruments CHICKEN_COOPPT-01/TE-01 are the duty section 5 loops (loop hardware, not modeled). No test pressure is claimed.
W8 Exports. `export/chicken_coop.step` (assembly, 12 solids); `export/chicken_coop.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/CHICKEN_COOP.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 2500.0 × 1300.0 × 1900.0 mm against the node envelope: flush, no misfit. Masses in `check.json`: frame legs 90.4 kg, drop board 76.5 kg + liner 15.3 kg, cloth floor and panels 53.1 kg plate-equivalent, roof 76.5 kg, nest-row envelope 753.6 kg (table-density figure; see W9/W12), perch 32.8 kg, nozzles 0.6 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: frame, drop board + liner, perch, nozzle set. Buy: hardware cloth (cut-to-size panels, plan rule 6) and the nest row (purchased complete; the drawn-envelope mass is the HDG table-density figure - the purchase mass is the maker's; hold, W12).
W10 Fabrication route. F-FARM practice (plan section 4 F-FARM row: frame, liner, bed, drainage): frame weldment (legs, roof rail), cloth panels cut and fixed to the frame, drop board and liner fitted, perch posts welded and rod set, nest row bolted in, then a wash-down check. On-site tool: the farm frame weldment bench (AQS-MOD-002 farm frame class); cloth and the nest row are buy goods.
W11 Self-copy relevance. The coop makes eggs, not plant parts; A-SC does not apply (tier 9). The frame weldment is itself a shop product class (AQS-MOD-002).
W12 Holds, named. (1) The `coop` class has no AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 route class; the F-FARM practice is the named study route. (2) The SS316 feeders and waterers duty section 3 names have no rating on the node - not drawn. (3) The nest row is drawn as its purchased envelope; the drawn-envelope mass is a table-density figure, not a purchase mass. (4) Perch height and post positions are study layout choices. (5) The nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (duty tasks 02/06); the cloth wall backing for each stub is a fabrication detail. (6) The N8 vent datum (z 800) predates the 1900 box; drawn flush at the envelope top. (7) Fastener patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

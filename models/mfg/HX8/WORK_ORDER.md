# Work order HX8

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-HX). Route class F-HX. Graph Rev AC. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/HX8.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/HX8.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-STD-002 section 4.5 (PVDF bundle adoption) and section 4.1 (titanium withdrawal), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node HX8.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `hx8.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 600 × 400 × 600, whose `size_note` records it as the study registry box; the duty section 2 sizing gives area, U and LMTD, not the box. The claimed envelope is that box and the build fills it: measured 600.0 × 400.0 × 600.0 mm, one closed manifold solid (hold, W12).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn as the unit: a vertical PVC-U shell (OD 340, wall 6 study) with a PVDF straight-tube bundle — 46 tubes OD 10 × 360 between two 15 mm PVDF tube sheets at z 100 and 480 — 0.5202 m² of tube area against the 0.51 m² duty figure (A-HX). The duty carries a heat rate (4.59 kW at U 1500 W/m²K, LMTD 6 K; the edges sum 4.572 kW), so the area is duty-sized and reported, not invented here. Tube wall 1.5 mm is a study value (hold, W12). Titanium plates were withdrawn and graphite is not used (duty section 3, AQS-STD-002 section 4.5 owner adoption 2026-10-15).
W4 Nozzles. All 7 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/HX8.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore: N1 DN15 EW heat in on −Y at z 160; N2/N3 DN15 and N6/N7 DN25 out on +Y (schedule datum 600 sits at the envelope top; clamped to 584.4 / 581.5 by the barb-radius rule — hold, W12); N4 drain flush at z 0 as a boss on the base head; N5 vent flush at the top head (schedule datum 800 predates the 600 box — hold, W12). Atmospheric joints are hose barbs (duty section 4: hose, atmospheric); no flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule — design_kPa 17.8 is the old head-plus-10 figure and is not a pressure rating); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M10 wedge anchors (plinth to slab, count from the model) and 8 M12 PVDF bolts at the top-head service joint (study pattern). Polymer joints to the supplier rule; A4-70 hardware per the observed house practice. Lengths and patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. PVDF tubes and tube sheets (duty section 3; AQS-STD-002 section 4.5), PVC-U shell on the Q4 water side. No metal wetted part; corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymers (AQS-STD-001 rule).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6). Both outlet temperatures are the duty section 5 instruments (high hot-side outlet trips the hot feed; loop hardware, not modeled). No test pressure is claimed.
W8 Exports. `export/hx8.step` (assembly); `export/hx8.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/HX8.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 600.0 × 400.0 × 600.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. Masses in `check.json`: shell + plinth + frame 21.3 kg, tube sheets 4.5 kg, bundle 1.2 kg, hardware 0.2 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make: shell, sheets and bundle are in-house fabrication.
W10 Fabrication route. F-HX: PVC-U pipe/sheet cut, thermoplastic weld of shell and heads, tube-sheet drilling and PVDF tube expansion/bonding, set-in ports, leak test of both sides separately. On-site tool: the thermoplastic shop (AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 F-HX operations: plate pack or coil, headers, frame, pressure or leak test).
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the exchanger moves heat; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The envelope is the study registry box; the duty section 2 sizing gives area, U and LMTD, not the box — carried, nothing resized. (2) Shell wall 6 mm and tube wall 1.5 mm are study values; no authority states a thickness. (3) The model tube area 0.5202 m² is 0.0102 m² over the 0.51 m² duty figure (whole-tube count); nothing resized, the difference is reported (A-HX). (4) The z 600 outlet datums sit at the envelope top; clamped to 584.4 (DN15) / 581.5 (DN25) by the barb-radius rule. (5) The 800 vent datum predates the 600 box; drawn flush at the top head. (6) Schedule rating column (Class 150) against the atmospheric duty: no flange is drawn. (7) Two hand-era schedule rows (N2/N3) carried an unquoted comma that shifted their face and height fields; the rows were repaired to the quoting of the identical N6/N7 rows (the generator only appends, hand rows are preserved verbatim; change record in `notes/tier4_feed_solids_m3_2026-11-01.md`). (8) Fastener lengths, the head-joint bolt pattern and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

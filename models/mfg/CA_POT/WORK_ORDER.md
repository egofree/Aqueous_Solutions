# Work order CA_POT

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-CELL). Route class F-CELL (the `cell` class sits in the F-CELL row of plan section 4). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/CA_POT.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/CA_POT.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-DUTY-072 (sizing study), A-M-282 (Faraday class), A-M-284 (D-DOCK feedstock ruling), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node CA_POT (design block). LIVE unit - the grease-thickener calcium line.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `ca_pot.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid: casing, lining, hood group, head plate, electrode bank, busbar ladder, offtake riser, and the schedule hardware. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `assumptions`, `anode`, `cathode`, `busbar`, `design` and `layout_note` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 800 × 800 × 1600 (cell-class study minimum, AQS-DUTY-072; live on the D-DOCK chloride import, A-M-284). The build fills it: measured 800.0 × 800.0 × 1600.0 mm from the export STL - the flush-hardware build keeps every connection group inside the faces (check.json `connection_overhang_mm` 0/0.3/0/0.1 mm, A-core 2).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn as the unit per the duty sheet and the design block: molten CaCl2 electrolysis cell at 700 C - CS casing t6 (walls + roof + head plate at z 1154), ceramic-fibre backup t24 + graphite ram liner t40 (wetted faces), 3 x D50 graphite anodes at 250 mm immersion, 3 cathode plates 110 × 12 × 300 on the −Y ram face (A-CELL: the design row `cathodes 3`), hood with graphite hot plate t10, DN50 offtake riser with return dip leg. The bath inventory (salt + Ca metal layer, 122 L at the bath top z 350) is drawn as process-inventory solids, not BOM parts and not internals (AL_POT idiom).
W4 Nozzles. All 5 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/CA_POT.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore, flush at the envelope faces: N1 Ca metal tap (solids row, DN80 well group - bore not calculated per W4) on +X at z 600; N2 DN15 CaCl2 brine inlet on −X at z 160, lance into the bath; N3 DN15 chlorine byproduct export on +Y at z 600; N4 DN15 drain flush at the bottom (50 datum); N5 DN15 vent on the DN50 riser at z 800. Atmospheric joints; no flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06 - hold, W12).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 6 M10 anode-clamp studs, 12 M8 hood bolts, 4 M10 slab anchors; counts from the model. 8.8 on carbon steel per the grade rule (AQS-PLAN-006 W5); lengths and patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3 (X-MAT-22): graphite anodes and ram liner on the wetted faces (chlorinated duty, DING2018_CL_REVIEW; change-out at the unit task 12 work order); ceramic-fibre backup (SCHACHT2004 class); CS casing, hood and head plate below the liner. CA 0 mm on the graphite; the CS shell is dry outside the lining (AQS-STD-001 shop rule). Bus hardware Cu on ceramic pads, frame-isolated per AQS-STD-002 section 11.
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric cell; the duty section 6 relief case is pending (unit task 07) and no set point is claimed. The Cl2 headspace leaves on the dry hot line (M7; N3 to the water-treatment stock). The rectifier interlocks (hood draft, bath temperature, cover position) are loop hardware; their sensor circuits are not modeled (AL_POT precedent - hold, W12).
W8 Exports. `export/ca_pot.step` (assembly, 1 unioned solid); `export/ca_pot.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/CA_POT.stl` replaced from the in-envelope build solid (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 registry; the N1/N2/N3 groups are the check.json `external_parts` record, drawn flush). Bounding box 800.0 × 800.0 × 1600.0 mm against the node envelope: filled exactly, nothing resized - the A-core 2 overshoot the §7 row carried from the Oct-31 build is cleared by the flush rebuild. Masses in `check.json`: casing + head plate 316.5 kg, lining 314.7 kg, hood group 84.4 kg, electrodes 20.5 kg, hardware and stubs the remainder.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make rows: shell, linings, hood group, cathode set, bus ladder, clamp bars, riser group, tap/brine/drain hardware. Buy rows: the anode bank (consumable graphite, Faraday I = 3.61 A at CE 0.85, A-M-282) and the 1 kW thyristor rectifier + bath-hold cabinet (purchased per plan rule 6; AQS-DUTY-072; mass per the purchase datasheet at order).
W10 Fabrication route. F-CELL (plan section 4): tank fabrication, plate shear and punch, bus bar cut and drill, gasket cut, stack assembly - in order: plate cut and fold (casing, roof, hood), CS weld (6 mm fillets, ISO 2553), lining install (fibre + ram, lance and drain passes cast in), head plate and bus ladder fit with ceramic pads, electrode set and clamp, nozzle set-in, then a dry-out and melt-up heat test and the trial electrolysis with the N1 tap. On-site tool: the metal shop welding bay with the refractory bench; anodes and rectifier are maker items.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the pot makes grease-thickener calcium stock, not plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The envelope is the AQS-DUTY-072 cell-class study minimum; the sizing study gives chemistry and the Faraday current, not a tank design. (2) Shell t6, lining t24/t40, hood plate t10 and the 300 mm cathode plate are study values; no authority states a thickness or plate size. (3) The design row states `cathodes 3` and the busbar class 25 mm2 (drawn 2 x 5 x 5 rails); it names no plate size or anode count - the 3-anode bank is the study layout (AL_POT family idiom), drawn j 0.0029 A/cm2 reported, no rate claimed. (4) Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06); the N5 vent 800 datum rides the riser (riser top 1594). (5) The relief case is pending (unit task 07); no instrument loop is issued (unit task 08). (6) Rectifier and anode masses are table-density placeholders until the maker datasheets quote. (7) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication. (8) The bath-inventory volumes (122 L) are the drawn study geometry, not a holdup basis (A-VES rule: no residence time claimed).

# Work order H2_DRYER

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-MACH). Route class F-MACH (the `dryer` class sits in the F-MACH row of plan section 4). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/H2_DRYER.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/H2_DRYER.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-REV-H2-001 (M3/M6 hydrogen materials), AQS-THM-003 (insulation class), AQS-MOD-002 (frame system), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node H2_DRYER (design block).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `h2_dryer.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 600 × 400 × 1028. The build fills it exactly: measured 600.0 × 400.0 × 1028.0 mm, one closed manifold mesh (the guard frame pins the registry box, so the X-PAR-04 envelope registry reads the build).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn as the unit, per the node design block: dryer vessel OD 60.3 × 628 (D_mm/H_mm), wall 3.91 (wall_mm), 316L annealed, with 38 mm insulation (insulation_mm); two deoxo tubes DN25 × 328 (beds.deoxo); two guard tubes DN25 × 300 (beds.guard) with guard-media envelopes; two TSA bed tubes DN50 × 300 (beds.TSA) with molecular-sieve bed envelopes (sieve_kg 0.353); regeneration heater envelope at the vessel base (regen_W 1.03); PVC-U inlet knock-out pot (duty section 3: inlet knock-out PVC-U, wet).
W4 Nozzles. All 9 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/H2_DRYER.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube stubs flush at the envelope faces: N1 process H2 in on −Y at z 160; N2 process H2 out, N5 H2 relief contingency (S24), N6 H2 normal path (S64), N7 dried hydrogen (the product gas edge — a non-piped transfer row drawn DN15, bore not calculated, plan W4), N8 sweep air and N9 TSA regeneration condensate on +Y at z 600; N3 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N4 vent flush at the envelope top — the vent is S24 to RELIEF_H2 (duty section 6), destination named; the 800 datum is a placeholder under the 1028 box. Atmospheric tube per duty section 4 ("no duct bore... no flange class"); no flange hardware is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M10 wedge anchors (plinth to slab) and 16 M8 tube-clip bolts (2 per bed tube on the clip rail), counts from the model. 8.8 on carbon steel, A4-70 on 316L per the grade rule; lengths and patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Dryer vessel, bed tubes and nozzles 316L annealed (duty section 3: "Dryer vessel and dry outlet 316L, annealed (M6, dry H2)"; CA 0 mm, AQS-STD-001 rule). Inlet knock-out pot PVC-U per duty section 3 (wet side; CA does not apply to the polymer). Insulation mineral wool + cladding (class per AQS-THM-003; regeneration conditions held per the duty). Guard frame and plinth carbon steel A500 (AQS-MOD-002 dry service).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric per duty section 6 with one stated relief edge: the N4 vent is S24 to RELIEF_H2 — drawn flush at the envelope top with the destination named. No test pressure is claimed beyond a leak check of the vessel and tube banks.
W8 Exports. `export/h2_dryer.step` (assembly); `export/h2_dryer.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/H2_DRYER.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 600.0 × 400.0 × 1028.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. Masses in `check.json`: plinth 38.4 kg, frame 42.3 kg, vessel 14.4 kg, insulation 59.0 kg, beds 22.7 kg, media 1.7 kg, heater envelope 8.9 kg, KO pot 0.5 kg, nozzles 0.6 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make rows: vessel, insulation jacket, bed tubes, KO pot, guard frame + plinth, nozzles. Buy rows (purchased per plan rule 6, ratings from the node): regeneration heater 1.03 W (regen_W); molecular sieve charge 0.353 kg (sieve_kg); guard media 0.032 kg (guard_kg); deoxo catalyst charge 0.183 L (beds.deoxo V_L, not drawn).
W10 Fabrication route. F-MACH (plan section 4: frame weldment, guards, purchased drive or electrical by rating, anchors): tube cut and closure welds per tube bank, nozzle set-in, insulation fabrication and cladding, clip-rail and guard-frame weldment, anchors, helium leak check of the vessel and tube banks (hydrogen service practice). On-site tool: the metal shop (cut, weld, drill); the sieve and catalyst charges are loaded at commissioning (buy rows).
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the dryer conditions hydrogen; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) Bed stations are the layout choice (KO x 60, deoxo x 150, guard x 220, TSA x 300, vessel x 450 on the z 120 seat); the design names components and counts, not their arrangement. (2) Media bed lengths run bore-to-bore between 10 mm support-disc allowances — a layout choice; the design gives V_L and L_mm, not the fill depth. (3) The deoxo catalyst is a buy charge (beds.deoxo V_L 0.183 L) and is not drawn (plan rule 6). (4) Guard frame and anchors drawn per A-MACH. (5) Fastener lengths and clip pattern are study values, confirmed at fabrication. (6) Duty section 8: "No vessel diameter" — the vessel follows the design block OD 60.3; nothing resized.

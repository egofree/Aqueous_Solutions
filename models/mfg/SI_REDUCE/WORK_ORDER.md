## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `si_reduce.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1200 × 1000 × 1600: "graphite hot-face furnace (the issued sec3 selection) at the batch class". The build fills it: measured 1200.0 × 1000.0 × 1600.0 mm from the export STL - the plinth pins the box footprint and the lid stack (casing 1260, lid plate, clamp ring, hopper cone and charge hatch) tops at the 1600 datum. The retort under-fills the box by design — the batch class sets the shaft, not the registry box (the GLASS_BURNOUT capacity-report precedent); the build solid fills the box footprint through the plinth so the X-PAR-04 envelope registry reads the build.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn as the unit per the duty section 3 lining stack (X-MAT-22; SCHACHT2004 ch. 8 carbonaceous hot faces; CRC_SI the element benchmark for the reduction duty class): mild-steel casing OD 700 (wall 6, study) with alumina brick backup 60 mm and a graphite hot face 40 mm in the Kroll-class retort idiom shared with the AQS-DUTY-072 pot-cell lineage (TI_CELL train); hearth floor disc; charge hopper cone with hatch on the lid (duty section 3: dry char and silica-class feed handling; design sio2_kgd 3.85 + char_kgd 1.924 per si_kgd 1.8); heater band envelope (node continuous 5.0 kW). The hot face stays dry (M7).
W4 Nozzles. None. The duty section 4 states "no piped process connection on the graph ... no nozzle schedule is issued yet"; the CHAR_TO_SI feed is a solids edge with no kg/d rate (duty section 8). Nothing is piped; nothing is drawn at the envelope faces. The schedule placeholder rule does not apply — there is no schedule.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M12 wedge anchors (plinth to slab) and 8 M12 lid clamp studs (clamp ring to casing flange), counts from the model. 8.8 on carbon steel per the grade rule; lengths and patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3 (X-MAT-22): graphite hot-face carbothermic-reduction furnace with alumina brick backup and a mild-steel casing; the hot face stays dry (M7). The brick is a buy-row liner charge (published alumina brick class midpoint density; supplier TDS governs at order). Plinth and anchor frame carbon steel A500 (AQS-MOD-002 dry service). CA 0 mm — dry, non-hydrocarbon furnace service (AQS-STD-001); CA does not apply to the graphite or the brick. The duty alternative (silicon-carbide hot face when batch chemistry moves beyond the graphite class) is a process-change decision, not drawn.
W7 Pressure. Relief case study pending (unit task 07); no set point is claimed on this page (duty section 6). The loop SI_REDUCETE-01 (temperature, AQS-CTL-001) is the duty section 5 trip class; its sensor circuit is control hardware and is not modeled (IND_MELT loop precedent — hold, W12).
W8 Exports. `export/si_reduce.step` (assembly, 1 solid); `export/si_reduce.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/SI_REDUCE.stl` replaced from the in-envelope build solid (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 registry). Bounding box 1200.0 × 1000.0 × 1600.0 mm against the node envelope: filled exactly (X-PAR-04 registry gate green).
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: plinth, casing with heads and clamp ring, graphite hot face and hearth, charge hopper. Buy: alumina brick liner charge, heater band set (node continuous 5.0 kW, plan rule 6 envelope). The heater-band envelope mass is a table-density placeholder until the maker TDS quotes (W12).
W10 Fabrication route. Route hold (W12): `furnace` has no section 4 class; the tracker carries the hold and this work order states the intent — the casing and hopper follow the F-VES plate operations as the nearest family (plate cut and roll, casing weld, head fit), the liner follows refractory practice (brick fit, graphite hot-face segments, dry-out heat-up), then the heater set, anchors and clamp studs, and a dry-out heat-up test before first charge. On-site tool: the metal shop welding bay with the refractory bench; the graphite hot-face machining is a maker service.

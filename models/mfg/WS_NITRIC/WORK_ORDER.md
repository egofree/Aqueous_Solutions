# Work order WS_NITRIC

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-MACH). Route class F-MACH. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/WS_NITRIC.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/WS_NITRIC.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-STD-002 rules M1/M3, AQS-DSN-OX-001 section 1, `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node WS_NITRIC.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `ws_nitric.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 600 × 600 × 350, set by `docs-authority/_storage_sizing.py` (AQS-DUTY-064). The build fills it: measured 600.0 × 600.0 × 350.0 mm, one closed manifold solid. The duty section 4 prose block still carries the seed line "Size 800 × 800 × 1100 mm" - the node governs and the older figure is named (holds, W12).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid; no study page issues internals for this station and none are drawn (hold, W12). The PVDF pump named by duty section 3 is drawn as its envelope and interface (plan rule 6; 120 × 90 × 110 in the guarded floor zone); its drive rating follows the maker TDS at order (hold, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 7 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/WS_NITRIC.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube connections on tray risers, flush at the envelope faces: N1 DN15 HCl product in and N2 DN15 nitric acid in on −Y at z 160; N3 DN15 HNO3, N4 DN15 IA-NIT-VENT and N7 DN15 nitric tank vent out on +Y (schedule datums 600 predates the box, clamped to 320.7 - hold, W12); N6 DN25 hose, the nitric tank vent to NOX_SCRUB, drawn at the +Y band bound (no datum on the schedule row - layout choice, hold, W12); N5 DN15 drain through the tray floor (50 datum); the schedule top row draws as the vent riser on the tank head (its 800 datum predates the box - hold, W12) (duty section 4: atmospheric joints are hose; hose makeup by the connecting line at the face). No flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. Duty section 6: atmospheric tank with breather, the vent goes on IA-NIT-VENT to NOX_SCRUB and overflow to the oxidizing-acid bund (loop hardware and the bund are room-side, not modeled). The along-face positions are the registered layout choice (per tag in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M10 wedge anchors, tray to slab, one per corner (count from the model). A4-70 hardware by the polymer-joint supplier rule. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication. The welded joints carry no fastener row.
W6 Materials. PVC-U tank and piping, PVDF pumps and valves, FKM or PTFE gaskets (duty section 3); 304L excluded (M3); 2 M HNO3 at no more than 40 °C; AQS-DSN-OX-001 section 1. CA 0 mm on the polymers (AQS-STD-001).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric tank with breather (duty section 6); the vent goes on IA-NIT-VENT to NOX_SCRUB (0 kg/d design day, breathing and upset). Not to the room. Instruments: level high-high trip closing IA-HNO3 and IA-BMED-01, temperature high trip 40 °C, density or conductivity alarm above 2.0 M, bund sump pH (duty section 5; loop hardware, not modeled). No hydrotest pressure is claimed; the leak test is a shop discretion of the route (W10).
W8 Exports. `export/ws_nitric.step` (assembly); `export/ws_nitric.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/WS_NITRIC.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 600.0 × 600.0 × 350.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. The working levels at the duty section 2 X-SAF-09 basis are reported in `check.json`: 25 L tank nominal (level z 260.4) and 16.4 L net at the 14-day holdup (level z 184.6).
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Buy: the PVDF pump envelope (named with the duty section 3 class; drive rating per the maker TDS at order). Make: tray, tank, guard rail and connections.
W10 Fabrication route. F-MACH: frame weldment (tray, guard rail), purchased items mounted by rating, anchors drilled, leak test of the tank. On-site tool: the thermoplastic shop and the welding bay (AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 F-MACH operations: frame weldment, guards, purchased drive or electrical by rating, anchors).
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the station dispenses reagent; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The envelope is the storage-sizing box; the duty section 4 prose seed line 800 × 800 × 1100 is an older figure - the node governs, nothing resized. (2) Tank wall 10 mm is the study fab-page value; no thickness is booked on the node. (3) No internals: the node row is none and no study page issues internals for this station. (4) The tank diameter, axis position and the guard/floor-zone layout are registered layout choices within the box. (5) The duty section 4 prose own-bund figures are room-side containment (the placement seeds seat the stations in segregated bunded rooms); the skid tray is the drip tray. (6) The 600 outlet datums and the 800 vent datum predate the 350 box; clamped as W4 records. (7) The tank volume is the duty section 2 X-SAF-09 basis (14 d at 1.17 L/d = 16.4 L net; tank 25 L nominal); the holdup days are the X-SAF-09 assumption (weak 2 M acid is stable, covering a two-week absorber outage). (8) The 30 L own bund (>= 110 %) is the room-side containment the station seats in (duty section 2; the placement seed records the separate oxidizer-acid bund); the skid tray is the drip tray. (9) The PVDF pump drive rating follows the maker TDS at order. (10) The N6 vent-to-scrubber schedule row carries no datum; drawn at the +Y band bound.(7) Schedule rating column (Class 150) against the atmospheric hose duty: no flange is drawn. (8) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

# Work order FLUE_KO

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-VES). Route class F-VES. Graph revision per `params.json` `graph_revision`. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/FLUE_KO.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/FLUE_KO.csv`, AQS-STD-002 section 8 audit row (FRP vinyl ester, PVDF mesh pad, borosilicate glass 3.3 inlet spool), X-SAF-07 (quench water basis), AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node FLUE_KO.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `flue_ko.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1000 × 600 × 800, whose `size_note` records it as the study default envelope box read back from the `cad/stl` registry (it matches the AQS-MFG-001 study page). The duty sheet section 4 still carries the unit-task-04 line "Size 600 × 700 × 1100 mm" - the node governs, that line is a named hold (W12). The build fills the node box: measured 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm, one closed manifold solid.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. Duty section 3 names the hardware: borosilicate glass 3.3 inlet spool (OD 110 × wall 3 at z 300, reach 300 mm into the shell - S67 at 300 °C is spray-quenched here by N8, 24.31 kg/d tower water, X-SAF-07, so the spool stays wetted and the gas leaves below the FRP limit); PVDF mesh pad OD 508 × 16 at z 620 on a PVDF support ring (the demister of duty section 3). The weir plate (x 420..430, to z 360) and the launder trough (channel floor z 300..310, overflow lip z 360) are layout solids from the section 1/3 knock-out separation duty - the FLOAT_SINK idiom; the separation duty behind the layout is unsized (duty section 8: no holdup basis) and the layout dimensions are study values (holds, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 8 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/FLUE_KO.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore: N1/N2/N3/N8 flue and quench services in on −Y at z 160, N4/N5 gas out on +Y at z 600, N6 drain boss on the base head to its 50 datum, N7 vent flush at the top head - the 800 datum IS the envelope top, drawn exact. The duty sheet section 4 inline rows (550 heights) are unit-task-04 placeholders against the issued schedule (hold, W12). Atmospheric joints are hose barbs (duty section 4); no flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. Per-tag positions in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M10 wedge anchors, plinth to slab, one per corner (count from the model). A4-70 hardware by the polymer-joint supplier rule. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication. The bonded/welded joints carry no fastener row.
W6 Materials. FRP vinyl ester with C-veil liner (shell, heads, weir, launder), borosilicate glass 3.3 (inlet spool), PVDF (mesh pad, support ring, hardware) - duty section 3, the AQS-STD-002 section 8 audit row. No metal wetted part. Corrosion allowance does not apply (AQS-STD-001). FRP wall 8 mm is a study value; no authority states an FRP thickness (hold, W12).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6). Not to the room. Loss of quench water trips the afterburner flue damper (duty section 3; loop hardware, not modeled). No hydrotest pressure is claimed; the leak test is a shop discretion of the route (W10).
W8 Exports. `export/flue_ko.step` (assembly); `export/flue_ko.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/FLUE_KO.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. Sump volume below the weir lip reported in `check.json`.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make: shell, spool, pad, weir/launder and hardware are in-house fabrication.
W10 Fabrication route. F-VES: FRP contact moulding over the C-veil liner (plaster or spool plug form), glass spool cut and bonded, PVDF pad and support set in, weir and launder bonded, nozzle set-in bonded, anchors drilled, leak test of the vessel. On-site tool: the composites/thermoplastic shop (AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 F-VES operations: plate or sheet cut, roll, weld or thermoplastic weld, nozzle set-in, hydro or leak test).
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the drum knocks condensate out of the flue; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The envelope is the study registry box; the duty sheet section 4 line 600 × 700 × 1100 is a unit-task-04 placeholder - the node governs, nothing resized. (2) FRP wall 8 mm is a study value; no authority states an FRP thickness. (3) The weir/launder separation layout is unsized (duty section 8); its dimensions are study values. (4) The spool and mesh-pad dimensions are study values against the duty-named hardware. (5) The duty sheet section 4 inline heights (550) predate the issued schedule (160/600). (6) Schedule rating column (Class 150) against the atmospheric hose duty: no flange is drawn. (7) The borosilicate density row is a published-band midpoint (lib table); the supplier TDS confirms at order. (8) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

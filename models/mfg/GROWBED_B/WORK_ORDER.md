W1 Source. `growbed_b.py` (CadQuery, on the shared close-out builder `models/mfg/farm_closeout.py`) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 3600 × 2100 × 500 - "media bed 7 m2, the anaerobic mineralisation half (A-M-415; A-M-229)". The rebuild fills the box exactly: measured 3600.0 × 2100.0 × 500.0 mm, flush on all six faces (A-core 2). W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid row - the duty section 3 build-up is drawn: the EPDM liner sheet 5 mm (floor + four walls, the GROW_BED ponded-zone idiom), the geotextile separation cloth 2 mm over the liner (buy roll), and the LECA media envelope at the 300 mm depth GROWBED_A's size_note names for the pair (charge is a buy row, plan rule 6; table bulk density carried until the supplier TDS). The anaerobic mineralisation zone runs at depth (duty section 3: the bed runs sulfidic); cold rabbit manure lands direct on the media (node design, N3). 184 mm freeboard above the media to the rim.
W4 Nozzles. All 9 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/GROWBED_B.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore DN15 tube stubs flush at the envelope faces: inlets N3 (cold rabbit manure), N6 (press water back to the loop), N9 (mineral nitrate solution) on −Y at z 160; the schedule z 600 rows - inlet N1 (nitrate water through the aerobic root zone), outlets N2 (loop water to the duckweed raceway), N7 (media-bed greens to the reserve), N8 (growbed evapotranspiration) - sit at or above the 500 box: drawn with their tops flush at the envelope top (stale datum, hold W12); N4 drain flush at the envelope bottom; N5 vent flush at the envelope top (the schedule z 800 datum predates the box - stale datum, hold W12). Atmospheric joints are tube/hose; no flange is drawn (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.

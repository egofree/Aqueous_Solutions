# Work order WS_HF_ED

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-MACH). Route class F-MACH. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/WS_HF_ED.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/WS_HF_ED.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-STD-002 rules M1, M2 and M5 (X-MAT-09), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node WS_HF_ED (design_storage per AQS-DUTY-064).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `ws_hf_ed.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` and, for the tank figures, the node `design_storage` block of the graph (the W2 file does not carry it); it rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 600 × 600 × 350, set by `docs-authority/_storage_sizing.py` (AQS-DUTY-064). The build fills it: measured 600.0 × 600.0 × 350.0 mm, one closed manifold solid. The duty section 4 prose block still carries the seed line "Size 800 × 800 × 1100 mm" - the node governs and the older figure is named (holds, W12).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid; no study page issues internals for this station and none are drawn (hold, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 5 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/WS_HF_ED.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube connections on tray risers, flush at the envelope faces: N1 (fluoride to HF) and N5 (aqueous HF to the fab reagent fork) in on −Y at z 160; N2 HF (aq) out on +Y (schedule datum 600 predates the box, clamped to 320.7 - hold, W12); N3 drain through the tray floor (50 datum); N4 vent riser beside the tank, flush at the envelope top (its 800 datum predates the box - hold, W12). No flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. The along-face positions are the registered layout choice (per tag in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M10 wedge anchors, tray to slab, one per corner (count from the model). A4-70 hardware by the polymer-joint supplier rule. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication. The welded joints carry no fastener row.
W6 Materials. PVDF wetted per duty section 3 (AQS-STD-002 rules M1 and M2, no metal wetted part); IA-HF-SRC arrives from LEACH_CSTR_1 at up to 85 °C (X-MAT-09) and HF at that temperature takes PVDF (M1 compatibility, M5), not CPVC. CA 0 mm on the polymer (AQS-STD-001). Tank diameter, height and wall are the node `design_storage` figures (D 300, H 300, wall 4.75, AQS-DUTY-064); the design_storage material row reads PE and is superseded by duty section 3 - hold, W12.
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6). Not to the room. Level and temperature are the duty section 5 instruments (loop hardware, not modeled). No hydrotest pressure is claimed; the leak test is a shop discretion of the route (W10).
W8 Exports. `export/ws_hf_ed.step` (assembly); `export/ws_hf_ed.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/WS_HF_ED.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 600.0 × 600.0 × 350.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. The gross shell volume and the design_storage nominal 0.02 m³ are reported in `check.json`; duty section 2 claims no holdup and no residence time is derived.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make: tray, tank, guard rail and connections are in-house fabrication (PVDF).
W10 Fabrication route. F-MACH: frame weldment (tray, guard rail), purchased items mounted by rating, anchors drilled, leak test of the tank. On-site tool: the thermoplastic shop and the welding bay (AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 F-MACH operations: frame weldment, guards, purchased drive or electrical by rating, anchors). PVDF joins by thermoplastic welding per the M5 class practice.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the station dispenses reagent; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The envelope is the storage-sizing box; the duty section 4 prose seed line 800 × 800 × 1100 is an older figure - the node governs, nothing resized. (2) No internals: the node row is none and no study page issues internals for this station. (3) The guard-rail and tray layout, the tank axis position and the nozzle packing are registered layout choices within the box; the tank D/H/wall figures themselves are node design_storage values, not choices. (4) The node design_storage material row reads PE; duty section 3 governs the alloy and PVDF is drawn (X-MAT-09). (5) The duty section 4 prose own-bund figures are room-side containment; the skid tray is the drip tray. (6) The 600 outlet datum and the 800 vent datum predate the 350 box; clamped as W4 records. (7) The duty section 2 claims no holdup: the design_storage nominal 0.02 m³ (30-day hold, AQS-DUTY-064) is reported for the record and no residence time is derived. (8) Schedule rating column (Class 150) against the atmospheric hose duty: no flange is drawn. (9) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication. (10) No purchased item is named on the node or the duty sheet, so none is drawn; the A-MACH purchased-item criterion has nothing to match.

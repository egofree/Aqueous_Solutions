# Work order C29_RECYCLE

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-PIPE). Route class F-PIPE. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/C29_RECYCLE.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/C29_RECYCLE.csv`, `docs-authority/LINE_LIST.md` (AQS-PIP-004, the DN and class authority), `docs-authority/PIPING_CLASSES.md` (AQS-PIP-003), AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node C29_RECYCLE.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `c29_recycle.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` and, for the A-PIPE line list, the unit's edges in the graph and the issued AQS-PIP-004 rows; it rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Parameters. Envelope 1000 × 600 × 800 mm at origin 2000, 13520, −6000 mm (placement seed), the node `size_mm`, whose size_note records it as the study default envelope box read back from the `cad/stl` registry ("design sizing per the duty sheet follows"). design_kPa and relief_mm are null: the line is atmospheric (duty section 6). The duty section 4 prose block states "Size 2000 × 60 × 60 mm" with L1/L2 rows at height 30 - an older study frame; the node governs the W2 envelope and the older figures are named (holds, W12).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid; none is drawn. The model is the in-envelope run of the recycle line: one x-run at the schedule inlet datum (z 160), a mid-run inlet branch from the −Y face, and a bend riser carrying the line to the schedule outlet datum (z 600) at the +Y face.
W4 Nozzles. All 4 rows of `docs-authority/nozzles/C29_RECYCLE.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric tube stubs (duty section 4: hose, atmospheric; the connecting line makes the joint at the face): N1 inlet on −Y at the mid-run x 500 at its 160 datum; N2 outlet is the bend-riser branch at +Y at its 600 datum; N3 drain boss under the run, flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N4 vent riser off the run, flush at the envelope top (800 datum = the box top). The 160 and 600 datums fit the 800 box and draw as issued. No flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. The along-run positions are the registered layout choice (per tag in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M10 wedge anchors, grade 8.8 on the carbon-steel plinth (plan grade rule), one per metre of spool. All spool and branch joints are welds (carbon steel); there are no other bolted joints. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Spool and branches carbon steel tube (duty section 3: carbon steel, hydrocarbon, no aqueous chloride; line class M2 per AQS-PIP-003); corrosion allowance 1.5 mm per the AQS-STD-001 hydrocarbon rule, carried in the procurement basis (the drawn wall is the Sch 40 table value). Plinth and stands carbon steel (AQS-MOD-002 dry service class) without the hydrocarbon allowance - the dry support steel sees no aqueous chloride (the HDR_CO2 basis).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric (duty section 6: no vent edge, relief not to the room). No test pressure is claimed; the F-PIPE route leak-tests the assembled run (W10).
W8 Exports. `export/c29_recycle.step` (assembly); `export/c29_recycle.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/C29_RECYCLE.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly (the study box bounds the spool run, inlet face and vent top). Masses in `check.json`.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make.
W10 Fabrication route. F-PIPE: spool cut (DN15 carbon steel tube to the Sch 40 table), branch set-in welds at the schedule tags, bend joints, plinth and stands welded and anchored, line list, leak test of the assembled run. On-site tool: the metal shop; the line list and spool sheet travel with the spool.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the line moves the C29 heavy cut; it makes no plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The envelope is the node study registry box (its size_note says so); the duty section 4 prose line "Size 2000 × 60 × 60 mm" and its L1/L2 rows at height 30 are an older study frame - the node governs, nothing resized. (2) The pipe schedule is not stated on the node or in the duty; the geometry is drawn on the lib/dn Sch 40 table (ASME B36.10M) and the M2 class sheet carries the schedule at order. (3) The duty section 4 prose lists the line-list rows L1/L2 (height 30, mid-run x 3000 of the old frame); the issued schedule rows (160/600/50/800 datums) govern the drawn hardware, and the mid-run branch-x choice carries the old frame over as a layout choice. (4) The routed run (2.4 m + 6.8 m per AQS-PIP-004) stays in the line-list route lengths; the model draws the in-envelope run of the study box. (5) The CA 1.5 mm hydrocarbon allowance is carried in the procurement basis, not grown into the drawn wall (the wall is the Sch 40 table value; AQS-STD-001 rule named per part). (6) The Class 150 rating column stands against an atmospheric hose duty; no flange is drawn (duty section 4, W4 110 % rule). (7) Support scheme (trough plinth + 2 saddle stands at the 160 datum) and the anchor count are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

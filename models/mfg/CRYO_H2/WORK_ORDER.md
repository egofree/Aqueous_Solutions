# Work order CRYO_H2

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-COL). Route class F-COL. Graph Rev AC. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/CRYO_H2.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/CRYO_H2.csv`, the node design and compressor_design blocks (`flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node CRYO_H2: rack 6696 × 1500 × 900, OD 114.3 × wall 13.49 × 6.096 m tubes, 32 tubes in 2 halves, steel_kg, relief_mm 13 API 520/521 per half; 5-stage diaphragm compressor 2 units), AQS-STD-002, AQS-STD-001, AQS-REV-H2-001.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `cryo_h2.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `layout` and `study` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node `size_mm` [6696, 1500, 900] is the rack envelope set by `flow_graph/param/receivers.py` (A-M-167); the build fills it exactly (measured 6696.0 × 1500.0 × 900.1 mm) and `cad/stl/CRYO_H2.stl` is replaced from the build.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`; the machine here is the storage and compression service itself: 32 cylinder tubes SA-312 TP316L seamless DN100 Sch 160 (OD 114.3 × t 13.49, 6.096 m), 4 tiers (z 142/342/542/742) × 8 rows (y pitch 187.5), bores drawn open, grooved tier combs and 3 saddles per tube. Rows y < 750 are relief half 1, rows y > 750 are half 2 (16 tubes per half, matching the node fire case per-half basis). The 2 hydrogen compressors of the node compressor_design (5-stage diaphragm, 17-4PH H1150 / 316L heads, 301 SS triple-layer diaphragms with leak detection, 10 → 200 bar, 0.9325 kW average / 2.797 kW peak) are purchased envelopes in the +X end bay (AQS-PLAN-006 rule 6).
W4 Nozzles. All 8 rows of `docs-authority/nozzles/CRYO_H2.csv` are drawn at tag, face and bore on the rack side rails: N1 (z 400) and N6 (z 600) on the rails as issued, N7/N8 (z 160) on the bottom rails, N2/N3 (schedule z 1500) clamped to the mid-rail centres, N4 drain as a skid-top stub with its tip at the 50 datum, N5 vent up the −Y tie bar to the 900 rack top. The schedule heights were issued against the 300 × 300 × 2000 study boxes that predate the A-M-167 rack envelope; heights that fit the rack are drawn as issued and every clamp is named in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`. Inlets −Y, outlets +Y (house face mapping). No flange is drawn anywhere: lib/dn.py carries no flange table above Class 150, the node design is 22000 kPa, and the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text - the reconciliation is a hold.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 256 M12 saddle bolts (8 per tube), 8 M10 wedge anchors (skid to slab), 8 M12 compressor hold-downs, ISO 3506 A4-70 per the plan grade rule on 316L. Counts are study values.
W6 Materials. 316L per duty section 3: annealed, nickel toward the top of the grade range, no ferritic or high-strength steel (AQS-REV-H2-001 warm-up embrittlement rule); corrosion allowance 0 mm (AQS-STD-001 rule). Skid 12 mm, SHS 80 × 80 × 5 sections and the 200 mm tier pitch are study sizes.
W7 Pressure. Design 22000 kPa g (node design_kPa, the receivers.py API 520 blocked-outlet case); relief per the node relief_basis: API 520 with the API 521 fire case per half bank, 13 mm governing bore, valves on the bank piping (purchase scope). The duty section 6 "atmospheric" text predates the receivers.py design and is superseded by the node relief_basis.
W8 Exports. `export/cryo_h2.step` (assembly); `export/cryo_h2.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/CRYO_H2.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Masses in `check.json`: tubes 6547.8 kg drawn wall mass (node steel_kg 5520.54 is the sizing record - carried, not recalculated), frame + saddles 4606.3 kg, compressor envelopes 1294.7 kg (envelope, not shipping mass), nozzles 1.4 kg. Store 17 kg H2 at 200 bar (node storage block: 2 d fuel-cell autonomy, X-PAR-12).
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Purchased rows (tubes, compressors) carry the order-time mass note.
W10 Fabrication route. F-COL: SHS and plate cut and welded into the rack frame, tier combs and saddles welded, tubes seated and strapped, connection stubs welded to the rails, compressors set and held down, leak test of the bank. On-site tool: the metal shop; the seamless tubes and the compressor sets are bought in.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: a storage and compression service makes no plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The schedule rating column (Class 150) against the node design 22000 kPa: no flange is drawn and no flange table above Class 150 exists in lib/dn.py; the joint reconciliation is the nozzle-schedule unit task. (2) Schedule heights 1500/2000/50 predate the A-M-167 rack envelope (900 high); the clamped rows are named per tag in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn` and the datums stay in the schedule. (3) Drawn tube-wall mass 6547.8 kg vs the node steel_kg 5520.54: the node figure is the receivers.py sizing record and is carried; no recalculation. (4) Frame sections (skid 12, SHS 80 × 80 × 5) and the 200 mm tier pitch are study sizes pending the frame check task. (5) Tube-end fittings (B16.11 Class 9000 / cone-and-thread) and the compressor process/interstage piping are named, not drawn - the end-detail is the piping scope. (6) A-COL packing/distributor/demister: a cylinder bank has none; the bank is the machine. (7) Compressor make, model and installation detail are purchase scope; the ratings are from the node. (8) Relief-valve placement on the bank piping is the relief task's scope; the case and bore are from the node. (9) Interconnecting pigtails and headers between tubes are the piping scope (F-PIPE tier).

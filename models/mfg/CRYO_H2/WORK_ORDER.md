# Work order CRYO_H2

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-COL). Route class F-COL. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/CRYO_H2.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/CRYO_H2.csv`, the node design and compressor_design blocks (`flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node CRYO_H2: rack per node size_mm - 6696 × 1500 × 1050 set by `flow_graph/param/receivers.py` (A-M-167), OD 114.3 × wall 13.49 × 6.096 m tubes, 34 tubes booked in 2 halves, steel_kg, relief_mm 13 API 520/521 per half; 5-stage diaphragm compressor 2 units), AQS-STD-002, AQS-STD-001, AQS-REV-H2-001.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `cryo_h2.py` (CadQuery) reads the graph node through the shared reader (`models/mfg/lib/graph_node.py`) and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `layout` and `study` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node `size_mm` [6696, 1500, 1050] is the rack envelope set by `flow_graph/param/receivers.py` (A-M-167: tube count 34 booked from the store demand, 8 tubes per rack row, 150 mm row pitch, 300 mm rack allowance height and width, 600 mm length allowance); the build fills it exactly (measured 6696.0 × 1500.0 × 1050.1 mm) and `cad/stl/CRYO_H2.stl` is replaced from the build.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`; the machine here is the storage and compression service itself: 34 cylinder tubes SA-312 TP316L seamless DN100 Sch 160 (OD 114.3 × t 13.49, 6.096 m) booked on the node count, drawn as 4 tiers (z 142/342/542/742) × 4 rows (y 187.5/562.5/937.5/1312.5) of tube solids with open bores, grooved tier combs and 3 saddles per tier-row. Rows y < 750 are relief half 1, rows y > 750 are half 2 (17 tubes per half booked, matching the node fire case per-half basis); the tier grid draws 16 tube solids against the 34 booked - the grid/count reconciliation is the frame-layout unit task (hold, W12(4)). The 2 hydrogen compressors of the node compressor_design (5-stage diaphragm, 17-4PH H1150 / 316L heads, 301 SS triple-layer diaphragms with leak detection, 10 → 200 bar, 0.9434 kW average / 2.830 kW peak) are purchased envelopes in the +X end bay (AQS-PLAN-006 rule 6).
W4 Nozzles. All 9 rows of `docs-authority/nozzles/CRYO_H2.csv` are scheduled at tag, face, height and bore; 8 piped rows are drawn on the rack side rails: N1 (z 400) and N6 (z 600) on the rails as issued, N7/N8 (z 160) on the bottom rails, N2/N3 (schedule z 1500) clamped to the mid-rail centres, N4 drain as a skid-top stub with its tip at the 50 datum, N5 vent up the −Y tie bar to the rack top. N9 (top, z 800) is the power terminal (cable schedule X-ELS-06; bus per AQS-ELEC-002): electrical, not piped - carried verbatim in `check.json`, never drawn. The schedule heights were issued against the 300 × 300 × 2000 study boxes that predate the A-M-167 rack envelope; heights that fit the rack are drawn as issued and every clamp is named in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`. Inlets −Y, outlets +Y (house face mapping). No flange is drawn anywhere: lib/dn.py carries no flange table above Class 150, the node design is 22000 kPa, and the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text - the reconciliation is a hold.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 272 M12 saddle bolts (8 per tube, node 34-tube count), 8 M10 wedge anchors (skid to slab), 8 M12 compressor hold-downs, ISO 3506 A4-70 per the plan grade rule on 316L. Counts are study values.
W6 Materials. 316L per duty section 3: annealed, nickel toward the top of the grade range, no ferritic or high-strength steel (AQS-REV-H2-001 warm-up embrittlement rule); corrosion allowance 0 mm (AQS-STD-001 rule). Skid 12 mm, SHS 80 × 80 × 5 sections and the 200 mm tier pitch are study sizes.
W7 Pressure. Design 22000 kPa g (node design_kPa, the receivers.py API 520 blocked-outlet case); relief per the node relief_basis: API 520 with the API 521 fire case per half bank, 13 mm governing bore, valves on the bank piping (purchase scope). The duty section 6 "atmospheric" text predates the receivers.py design and is superseded by the node relief_basis.
W8 Exports. `export/cryo_h2.step` (assembly); `export/cryo_h2.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/CRYO_H2.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Masses in `check.json`: tubes booked 7084.0 kg on the node 34-tube count - the tier grid draws 16 tube solids, drawn wall mass 3333.7 kg = 16 solids x the booked tube mass (node steel_kg 5865.6 is the sizing record - carried, not recalculated), frame + saddles 4613.5 kg, compressor envelopes 1294.7 kg (envelope, not shipping mass), nozzles 1.4 kg. Store 17 kg H2 at 200 bar (node storage block: 2 d fuel-cell autonomy, X-PAR-12).
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Purchased rows (tubes, compressors) carry the order-time mass note.
W10 Fabrication route. F-COL: SHS and plate cut and welded into the rack frame, tier combs and saddles welded, tubes seated and strapped, connection stubs welded to the rails, compressors set and held down, leak test of the bank. On-site tool: the metal shop; the seamless tubes and the compressor sets are bought in.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: a storage and compression service makes no plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The schedule rating column (Class 150) against the node design 22000 kPa: no flange is drawn and no flange table above Class 150 exists in lib/dn.py; the joint reconciliation is the nozzle-schedule unit task. (2) Schedule heights 1500/2000/50 predate the A-M-167 rack envelope (the rack is the node envelope height); the clamped rows are named per tag in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn` and the datums stay in the schedule. (3) Booked tube mass 7084.0 kg (34 tubes) against the drawn wall mass 3333.7 kg (the tier grid draws 16 tube solids at the booked tube mass) and the node steel_kg 5865.6: the node figure is the receivers.py sizing record and is carried; no recalculation. (4) Frame sections (skid 12, SHS 80 × 80 × 5) and the 200 mm tier pitch are study sizes pending the frame check task; the tier grid vs the booked tube count is the same task. (5) Tube-end fittings (B16.11 Class 9000 / cone-and-thread) and the compressor process/interstage piping are named, not drawn - the end-detail is the piping scope. (6) A-COL packing/distributor/demister: a cylinder bank has none; the bank is the machine. (7) Compressor make, model and installation detail are purchase scope; the ratings are from the node. (8) Relief-valve placement on the bank piping is the relief task's scope; the case and bore are from the node. (9) Interconnecting pigtails and headers between tubes are the piping scope (F-PIPE tier).

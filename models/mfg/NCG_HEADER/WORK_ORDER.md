Authority: `docs-authority/duties/NCG_HEADER.md`, `docs-authority/sizing/HEADERS.md` (AQS-DUTY-075), `docs-authority/nozzles/NCG_HEADER.csv`, `docs-authority/LINE_LIST.md` (AQS-PIP-004, the DN and class authority), `docs-authority/PIPING_CLASSES.md` (AQS-PIP-003), AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node NCG_HEADER.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `ncg_header.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` and, for the A-PIPE line list, the unit's edges in the graph and the issued AQS-PIP-004 rows; it rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Parameters. Envelope 2000 × 50 × 50 mm at origin 5200, 12200, −6000 mm (placement seed), node size_note "DN25 manifold x 20 m run (0.004 m3), header sizing (AQS-DUTY-075)". design_kPa and relief_mm are null: the header is atmospheric (duty section 6). The seed placement block (80 × 80 × 800 at level L-1) is the placement grid cell, not the unit envelope.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid; none is drawn. The model is the spool set as the node defines it: one manifold run, drawn as a 2000 mm representative spool. The 20 m run is carried by the per-line route lengths of AQS-PIP-004, not redrawn.
W4 Nozzles. All 4 rows of `docs-authority/nozzles/NCG_HEADER.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as open-bore tube stubs flush at the envelope faces (lib/nozzles.place_on_face). Duty section 4 (line-list table): "Atmospheric lines are hose. DN is copied from the edge and is not recalculated."; the W4 110 % rule draws no flange because no process pressure is stated. The schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. The DN15 datums 10 mm are clamped up to 11.3 mm for the branch bore (hold). The duty section 4 table datums (400 mm on the seed block xyz) predate the issued schedule; the schedule governs (hold).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M10 wedge anchors, grade 8.8 on the carbon-steel plinth (plan grade rule). Spool and branch joints are 304L welds; there are no other bolted joints. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Manifold and branches 304L (node design: "304L, heat-traced (tar service)"; duty section 3 carries the carbon-steel text with a 1.5 mm hydrocarbon allowance — material basis conflict, hold W12; 304L is drawn, both facts recorded); corrosion allowance 0 mm (AQS-STD-001 stainless rule). Plinth carbon steel (AQS-MOD-002 dry service class). The heat tracing is a purchased self-regulating tracing cable (the node material names the trace; no authority dimensions the cable or its wattage): named in the BOM as a buy row, not drawn.
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric (duty section 6: no vent edge, relief not to the room). No test pressure is claimed.
W8 Exports. `export/ncg_header.step` (assembly); `export/ncg_header.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/NCG_HEADER.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 2000.0 × 50.0 × 50.0 mm against the 2000 × 50 × 50 envelope: inside, filled exactly. Masses in `check.json`: plinth 15.7 kg, manifold 5.15 kg, branches 0.09 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. The heat-tracing cable is a buy row with its mass recorded at order.
W10 Fabrication route. F-PIPE: spool cut (DN25 304L to the Sch 40 table), branch set-in welds at the schedule tags, trough plinth weld and anchor, line list, leak test of the assembled run; the tracing cable applied after the leak test. On-site tool: the metal shop; the line list and spool sheet travel with the spool.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the header moves gas; it makes no plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The envelope is the AQS-DUTY-075 sizing box (DN25 manifold × 20 m run); the model draws a 2000 mm representative spool of it; per-line lengths stay in the line list. (2) The pipe schedule is not stated on the node or in the duty; the geometry is drawn on the lib/dn Sch 40 table and the class sheet carries the schedule at order. (3) The AQS-DUTY-075 ID grid (DN − 8 = 17 mm) and the Sch 40 bore (26.9 mm) disagree by the grid simplification; both seat DN25; nothing resized. (4) Material basis conflict: duty section 3 carries the carbon-steel hydrocarbon text with a 1.5 mm allowance; the node design says "304L, heat-traced (tar service)" (the AQS-DUTY-075 selection per AQS-STD-002). 304L is drawn with CA 0 mm (stainless rule); both facts are recorded and the re-selection belongs to the X-MAT lane, not to this model. (5) Schedule datums sit in a 50 mm cross-section: the DN15 datums 10 mm are clamped up to 11.3 mm; the duty section 4 table datums (400 mm, seed block xyz) predate the issued schedule, which governs; designed elevations are unit tasks 02/06. (6) The Class 150 rating column stands against an atmospheric duty; no flange is drawn (duty section 4, W4 110 % rule). (7) Duty section 4 says "Atmospheric lines are hose"; the model draws tube stubs flush at the faces and the hose makeup is the connecting spool's joint at the face; the barb profile is a study proportion, finished against the hose supplier print. (8) The issued AQS-PIP-004 rows carry class P3 (PVDF, process vent gas) while the manifold is 304L: the PVDF-to-304L transition joint at the header face is the connection detail, confirmed at fabrication. (9) The heat-tracing cable and its wattage are not dimensioned in any authority: purchased accessory, named in the BOM, not drawn; length per the issued route at fabrication. (10) The trough-mount support scheme (plinth, no top rails) is the layout choice carried from the HDR_CO2 acceptance; anchor lengths and the branch pitch are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

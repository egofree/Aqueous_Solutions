## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `continuous_recycle.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` and, for the A-PIPE line list, the unit's edges in the graph and the issued AQS-PIP-004 rows; it rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Parameters. Envelope 1500 × 60 × 60 mm at origin 5000, 4600, −2700 mm (placement seed), the node `size_mm` (its size_note records the study registry box). design_kPa and relief_mm are null: the line is atmospheric (duty section 6).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid; none is drawn. The model is the in-envelope run: one 1500 mm spool at the cross-section centre (y 30, z 30) on a 20 mm trough plinth.
W4 Nozzles. All 5 rows of `docs-authority/nozzles/CONTINUOUS_RECYCLE.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric tube stubs flush at the envelope faces (duty section 4: hose, atmospheric; the connecting line makes the joint at the face): N1 (DN50 raffinate) in on −Y at x 400 and N5 (DN15 polish-bank return) in on −Y at x 700, its 160 datum clamped to 48.65 in the 60 mm section (placeholder above the section - hold, W12); N2 (DN50 recycle liquor) out on +Y at x 1100; N3 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N4 vent flush at the envelope top (the 800 datum is the placeholder above the section). Schedule rows N1/N2 carry DN50 in the size column: drawn verbatim - the DN50 hardware seats the 60 mm cross-section at the section centre with 0.15 mm per side outside the box, inside the 1 mm house gate, reported in `check.json` (the hand DN50 rows stand against the DN15 issued lines; the AQS-PIP-003 basis reads the piped bore as the nozzle bore basis and bores are not recalculated - hold, W12). No flange is drawn (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. The along-run x positions are the registered layout choice (per tag in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 6 M10 wedge anchors, tray-to-slab polymer joint rule (A4-70 hardware per house practice), 4 per 2 m spool segment. Spool joints are thermoplastic welds (PVC-U); there are no other bolted joints. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Spool and branches PVC-U tube (duty section 3: PVC-U wetted per AQS-STD-002 rules M1 and M2, no metal wetted part); CA does not apply to the polymer (AQS-STD-001). Above 60 °C the thermoplastic is CPVC, never metal - the line temperature is not on the graph (hold, W12; the IA-REC-01 line class P2 carries hot leach liquor). Plinth carbon steel (AQS-MOD-002 dry service class) without a corrosion allowance - the dry support steel sees no aqueous chloride (the HDR_CO2 basis).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric (duty section 6: no vent edge, relief not to the room). No test pressure is claimed; the F-PIPE route leak-tests the assembled run (W10).
W8 Exports. `export/continuous_recycle.step` (assembly); `export/continuous_recycle.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/CONTINUOUS_RECYCLE.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1500.0 × 60.0 × 60.3 mm against the node envelope: inside the 1 mm house gate (the 0.3 mm is the DN50 hardware seat, reported in `check.json`; nothing resized). Masses in `check.json`.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make.
W10 Fabrication route. F-PIPE: spool cut (DN15 PVC-U tube to the Sch 40 seat table), branch set-in joints (thermoplastic welding per the M5 class practice), trough plinth welded and anchored, line list, leak test of the assembled run. On-site tool: the thermoplastic shop; the line list and spool sheet travel with the spool.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the line returns liquor to the leach train; it makes no plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The envelope is the node study registry box (its size_note says so). (2) The pipe schedule is not stated on the node or in the duty; the geometry is drawn on the lib/dn Sch 40 table (ASME B36.10M) and the polymer class sheet carries the SDR at order. (3) Schedule rows N1/N2 carry DN50 hand-row hardware against the DN15 issued lines (AQS-PIP-004); drawn verbatim, no bore recalculated (AQS-PIP-003 basis), and the DN50 seat stands 0.15 mm per side outside the 60 mm cross-section, inside the 1 mm house gate. (4) Schedule datums sit in a 60 mm cross-section: the 160 mm inlet datum is a placeholder and draws clamped to 48.65; the 800 mm vent datum is the placeholder above the section (designed elevations are unit tasks 02/06). (5) The line temperature is not on the graph; PVC-U is drawn per duty section 3, and above 60 °C the thermoplastic class changes to CPVC, never metal (the IA-REC-01 line class P2 carries hot leach liquor). (6) The routed run (17.0 m + 9.7 m per AQS-PIP-004) stays in the line-list route lengths; the model draws the in-envelope run. (7) The Class 150 rating column stands against an atmospheric hose duty; no flange is drawn (duty section 4, W4 110 % rule). (8) The trough-mount support scheme, the branch x positions and the anchor count are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

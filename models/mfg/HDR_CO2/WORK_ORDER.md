# Work order HDR_CO2

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-PIPE). Route class F-PIPE. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/HDR_CO2.md`, `docs-authority/sizing/HEADERS.md` (AQS-DUTY-075), `docs-authority/nozzles/HDR_CO2.csv`, `docs-authority/LINE_LIST.md` (AQS-PIP-004, the DN and class authority), `docs-authority/PIPING_CLASSES.md` (AQS-PIP-003), AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node HDR_CO2.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `hdr_co2.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` and, for the A-PIPE line list, the unit's edges in the graph and the issued AQS-PIP-004 rows; it rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Parameters. Envelope 2000 × 50 × 50 mm at origin 2800, 600, 6000 mm (placement seed), node size_note "DN25 manifold x 20 m run (0.004 m3), header sizing (AQS-DUTY-075)". design_kPa and relief_mm are null: the header is atmospheric (duty section 6); the stored design_kPa idiom on vessel nodes is an old head-plus-10 figure and is not a set point.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid; none is drawn. The model is the spool set as the node defines it: one manifold run, drawn as a 2000 mm representative spool. The 20 m run is carried by the per-line route lengths of AQS-PIP-004, not redrawn.
W4 Nozzles. All 9 rows of `docs-authority/nozzles/HDR_CO2.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as open-bore tube stubs flush at the envelope faces (lib/nozzles.place_on_face). Atmospheric tube per duty section 4 ("No flange class. Atmospheric."); the W4 110 % rule draws no flange because no process pressure is stated. The schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. Rows N3 (compost CO2) and N4 (fermentation CO2) carry services with no present edge on the graph; they are drawn verbatim and no edge is created. The along-run x positions are the registered layout choice (params layout.nozzle_spread).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M10 wedge anchors, grade 8.8 on the carbon-steel plinth (plan grade rule), 4 per 2 m spool segment. All spool and branch joints are welds (316L); there are no other bolted joints. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Manifold and branches 316L tube (duty section 3 / node design; M1 class, AQS-PIP-003: ASTM A312 pipe with A403 fittings); corrosion allowance 0 mm (AQS-STD-001 rule). Plinth carbon steel (AQS-MOD-002 dry service class); the dry CO2 service sees no aqueous chloride, so the 1.5 mm hydrocarbon-rule allowance is not applied to it.
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric (duty section 6: no vent edge, relief not to the room). The vent N7 returns to the loop suction per duty section 4. No test pressure is claimed.
W8 Exports. `export/hdr_co2.step` (assembly); `export/hdr_co2.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/HDR_CO2.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 2000.0 × 50.0 × 50.0 mm against the 2000 × 50 × 50 envelope: inside, filled exactly. Masses in `check.json`: plinth 15.7 kg, manifold 5.15 kg, branches 0.19 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make.
W10 Fabrication route. F-PIPE: spool cut (DN25 316L tube to the Sch 40 table), branch set-in welds at the schedule tags, trough plinth weld and anchor, line list, leak test of the assembled run. On-site tool: the metal shop; the line list and spool sheet travel with the spool.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the header moves gas; it makes no plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The envelope is the AQS-DUTY-075 sizing box (DN25 manifold × 20 m run); the model draws a 2000 mm representative spool of it; per-line lengths stay in the line list. (2) The pipe schedule is not stated on the node or in the duty; the geometry is drawn on the lib/dn Sch 40 table (ASME B36.10M) and the M1 class sheet carries the schedule at order. (3) The AQS-DUTY-075 ID grid (DN − 8 = 17 mm) and the Sch 40 bore (26.9 mm) disagree by the grid simplification; both seat DN25; nothing resized. (4) Schedule datums sit in a 50 mm cross-section: the 10 mm inlet rows are clamped up to 11.3 mm (branch-bore clearance) and the 600 mm outlet datums, placeholders above the cross-section, are clamped down to 38.6 mm; designed elevations are unit tasks 02/06. (5) N3 and N4 carry services with no present edge on the graph; drawn verbatim, no edge created. (6) The Class 150 rating column stands against an atmospheric duty; no flange is drawn (duty section 4, W4 110 % rule). (7) Duty section 4 says "Atmospheric" without fixing tube or hose; the model draws tube stubs flush at the faces and the hose makeup is the connecting spool's joint at the face; the barb profile of the lib is a study proportion, finished against the hose supplier print. (8) The trough-mount support scheme (plinth, no top rails) is the layout choice; the M2 style build's top hold-down rails fused the branch zone and are not carried. (9) Anchor lengths and the branch pitch are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

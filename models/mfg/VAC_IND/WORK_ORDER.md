## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `vac_ind.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid: plinth, vacuum chamber and lid, insulation package, crucible and coil, pour hardware, PSU bay and tilt frame, and the schedule hardware. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1000 × 1000 × 1500 (A-M-199 melt class N+1 rated envelope, A-M-282). The build fills it: measured 1000.0 × 1000.0 × 1500.0 mm, all connection hardware flush at the envelope faces, nothing protrudes.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The 2 kg design-case skull melt is drawn as a process-inventory solid, not a BOM part and not an internal.
W4 Nozzles. All 5 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/VAC_IND.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore DN15 tube connections, flush at the envelope faces (duty section 4: hose, atmospheric; the connecting line makes the joint at the face): N1 DN15 vacuum melt to cast and N3 DN15 reactive melt for the investment pour on +Y at z 600; N2 solids Ti sponge/powder charge port on −Y at z 160, drawn at DN15 and labeled bore not calculated (W4 rule); N4 DN15 drain through the plinth, flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N5 DN15 vent riser on the chamber lid, flush at the envelope top (its 800 datum is the schedule placeholder - hold, W12). The schedule inlet/outlet faces are mapped to compass faces by the registered layout choice (params.json layout block). No flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M12 wedge anchors, plinth to slab (8.8 on carbon steel), 12 M10 stud-and-nut sets on the chamber-lid clamp and PSU-cabinet rails (A4-70 on the 304L joint). Lengths and torque are study values, confirmed at fabrication. The welded joints carry no fastener row.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: 304L vacuum chamber (t6 study wall, W12), water-cooled copper induction crucible (no wetted-refractory interface - the copper face keeps the Ti melt out of contact with any refractory), ceramic-fibre insulation on the shell, CS plinth and frame hardware. CA 0 mm (dry furnace-shop service and the stainless/copper classes; AQS-STD-001 shop rule). The graphite-crucible alternative in duty section 3 applies only above the design power (a different metal-contact class) and is not drawn.
W7 Pressure. Vacuum chamber: leak test per the route (W10); the study chamber duty is the leak-test basis, no design_kPa is booked on the node and none is claimed (duty section 6: relief case pending, unit task 07 - hold, W12). Not to the room. The melt rows and the charge port are atmospheric at the faces (duty section 4); the schedule Class 150 column stands as text.
W8 Exports. `export/vac_ind.step` (assembly); `export/vac_ind.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/VAC_IND.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1000.0 × 1000.0 × 1500.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly, zero face overhang.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: plinth, chamber and lid, insulation package, crucible jacket, coil and lead, pour hardware, tilt frame, nozzle set. Buy: the 15 kW PSU cabinet (node design rating, drawn as its envelope per plan rule 6). The standby state is named in the BOM rows and the build log.
W10 Fabrication route. Route hold (W12); operations named so the shop can bid it: plate cut and weld (304L GTAW, full-penetration seam welds on the chamber), leak test of the chamber at the study duty, insulation fit, crucible-jacket braze and coil wind, plinth and frame weld (CS), set-in of the schedule connections, then dry-out and vacuum leak-down before the first design-case melt. On-site tool: the metal shop welding bay with the refractory bench.
W11 Self-copy relevance. The furnace melts Ti-class feedstock for the casting alternates; its acceptance set is A-core and A-SC does not apply (tier 6). The melt it makes feeds the tier-7 shop tools, where the AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference lives.

(4) The N5 vent datum 800 is the schedule placeholder; the riser stands on the chamber lid and flushes at the envelope top. (5) The N2 solids row is drawn at DN15, bore not calculated (W4 rule). (6) Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders, not designed elevations (unit tasks 02/06). (7) The relief/vacuum-case study is pending (unit task 07); no design_kPa is booked on the node. (8) The PSU cabinet and the tilt actuator package are purchased; the maker ratings read the sheets at order. (9) The coil cooling-water circuit is loop hardware, not modeled; its skid is a unit-task item. (10) No instrument loop is issued for this unit yet (duty section 8). (11) Fastener lengths and torque are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

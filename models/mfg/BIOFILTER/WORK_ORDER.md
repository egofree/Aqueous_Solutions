## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `biofilter.py` (CadQuery, on the shared close-out builder `models/mfg/farm_closeout.py`) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1100 × 900 × 1000. The flush rebuild fills the box exactly: measured 1100.0 × 900.0 × 1000.0 mm, flush on all six faces (A-core 2). The Oct-31 section-7-reported overshoot (bbox z 1012.1 against 1000) does not survive the flush rebuild - the lid, the drawn media and every connection close inside the box, so the stale report CLEARS from section 7; nothing was resized (plan rule 3).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid row - but the node size_basis NAMES the media: "moving-bed vessel 0.55 m3 at the 0.9 kg/d feed ammonia load, K1 media 0.2 m3". The K1 moving-bed carrier envelope is drawn as a solid at 0.2 m3 over the vessel floor (214.8 mm depth, A-core 4). The carrier charge is a buy row (plan rule 6). The 0.55 m3 moving-bed volume is a duty figure reported on the duty sheet, not a model claim. The aeration the design block names (aerobic: 2 mol O2 per mol NH4+ nitrified) has no position or rating on the node: not drawn (hold, W12). The Oct-31 `stubs_mm` pair (two +X studs at z 20, not schedule rows) is superseded by the drawn schedule rows and leaves the params file.
W4 Nozzles. All 12 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/BIOFILTER.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube stubs flush at the envelope faces: N1 in on −Y at z 240 (urine); N2 in on −Y at z 240 (DN50, clarified water); N9 in on −Y at z 160 (ammonia-rich fish waste water); N3/N4/N5 out on +Y at z 900 (DN50, nitrate water) with N6 bleed beside them; N10/N11/N12 out on +Y at z 600 (nitrified water, sanitizer feed, loop bypass); N7 drain flush at the envelope bottom; N8 vent flush at the envelope top - the schedule z 1200 datum predates the 1000 box (stale datum, hold W12). HDPE/PVC per duty section 3; atmospheric joints are tube/hose; no flange is drawn (W4 110 % rule - design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure, not a pressure rating); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M10 lid hold-downs (polymer supplier rule, A4-70 hardware) and 4 M10 base anchors (8.8 carbon steel per the grade rule). Patterns are study values / the model count, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: HDPE or PVC for the water; the frame is not the wetted part. No metal wetted part; corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymer (AQS-STD-001 rule). The media mass is carried at the K1 open-carrier idiom (~0.15 of the drawn envelope's solid volume as bare carrier PE) until the supplier TDS (hold, W12).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric (duty section 6: no vent edge; the stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure, not a set point). No instrument loop in AQS-CTL-001 for this unit (duty section 5: no trip specified). No test pressure is claimed.
W8 Exports. `export/biofilter.step` (assembly, 1 solid); `export/biofilter.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/BIOFILTER.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1100.0 × 900.0 × 1000.0 mm against the node envelope: flush, no misfit. Masses in `check.json`: vessel shell 69.4 kg, lid 14.1 kg, media envelope 190.0 kg solid / 28.5 kg carrier class, nozzles 0.2 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: vessel, lid, nozzle set (HDPE/PVC stock). Buy: the K1 carrier charge (plan rule 6; the envelope drawn, the fill bought).
W10 Fabrication route.

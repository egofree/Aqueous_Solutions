Route class: hold — the `flue_junction` class has no plan section 4 route class; the node design block defines a duct, so the model follows the A-PIPE idiom (spool + line list) with the route hold named (the COMB_HEAT/RES_HEAT precedent). Authority: `docs-authority/duties/AFTERBURNER_FLUE.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/AFTERBURNER_FLUE.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-STD-002 section 4.3 (dielectric isolating flange), AQS-PIP-003/004 (line list), AQS-THM-003 (insulation class), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node AFTERBURNER_FLUE (design block).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `afterburner_flue.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1000 × 600 × 800 (study default envelope box). The build fills it exactly: measured 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm, one closed manifold mesh.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn as the unit: a DN100 flue-duct spool (OD 114.3 × wall 6.02, ASME B36.10M Sch 40 named values — DN100 sits outside the lib/dn DN15-DN50 table, hold W12) with a dielectric isolating flange at the FLUE_KO end, an insulation jacket over the run, on a 20 mm trough plinth (the HDR_CO2 trough-mount idiom).
W4 Nozzles. All 5 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/AFTERBURNER_FLUE.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube stubs flush at the envelope faces, stopping at the insulation surface: N1 DN15 flue (from the afterburner) and N2 DN25 combustion air on −Y at z 160; N3 DN25 flue N2/O2/CO2/NOx on +Y at z 600; N4 DN15 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N5 DN15 vent flush at the envelope top (800 datum is the box top; duty section 4: the vent stays on the gas edge, not the room). Atmospheric tube per duty section 4 ("no duct bore... no flange class"); no flange hardware is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M10 wedge anchors (trough plinth to slab), count from the model. 8.8 on carbon steel per the grade rule; length and pattern are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duct, isolating flange and branches 316L per duty section 3 ("316L pipe, dry flue at 300 °C, insulated to hold the wall above the acid dew point"; CA 0 mm, AQS-STD-001 316L rule). The node design block says "CS Sch 40, insulated per AQS-THM-003" — the duty section 3 selection governs the wetted part and the conflict is carried as a hold (W12, nothing resized). Insulation mineral wool + cladding (class per AQS-THM-003); trough plinth carbon steel A500.
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6). Dry flue at the 200 C class (node design) with a 300 °C duty wall rating. No test pressure is claimed beyond a leak check of the finished spool.
W8 Exports. `export/afterburner_flue.step` (assembly); `export/afterburner_flue.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/AFTERBURNER_FLUE.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. Masses in `check.json`: plinth 94.2 kg, spool 19.3 kg, insulation 2.3 kg, nozzles 2.0 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make: duct spool, insulation jacket, trough and branches are in-house fabrication; the isolating-flange gasket/packer set is a buy item at order (AQS-STD-002 section 4.3 kit, named on the drawing).
W10 Fabrication route. F-PIPE idiom (plan section 4 F-PIPE operations: spool cut, join, support, line list): pipe cut and bevel, circumferential welds, branch set-in welds, isolating-flange interface faced at the FLUE_KO end, insulation fabrication and cladding, trough fabrication, leak check. On-site tool: the metal shop (cut, weld); insulation per the AQS-THM-003 class procedure.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the duct carries flue gas; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) Route hold: no plan section 4 route class for the `flue_junction` class — A-core acceptance with the hold named; the A-PIPE idiom follows the node duct design. (2) DN100 sits outside the lib/dn DN15-DN50 table: OD 114.3 / wall 6.02 are named ASME B36.10M Sch 40 values (the MEOH_SYNTH DN100 jacket carried the same figures). (3) Material conflict: duty section 3 names 316L, the node design block says CS Sch 40 insulated — the duty selection governs; nothing resized. (4) Insulation thickness 40 mm is a study value; AQS-THM-003 states no thickness for this line. (5) The dielectric isolating flange is drawn as the interface disc; the gasket/packer kit is a buy item at order. (6) Fastener lengths and trough fit are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

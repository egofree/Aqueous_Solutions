# Work order MET_SCRUB

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-COL). Route class F-COL. Graph Rev AC. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/MET_SCRUB.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/MET_SCRUB.csv`, node design block (flow_graph/param/house_scrubbers.py, X-PAR-03), AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-STD-002 rules M1/M2 (PVC-U/CPVC class), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node MET_SCRUB.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `met_scrub.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1200 × 1200 × 4103, set by the X-PAR-03 house-scrubber sizing; the column design block carries D 800, packed height 2303, column H 3803, matching the duty section 2 tower sizing (2520 m³/h at 1.5 m/s, D 771 rounded to the 800 design). The build fills the envelope: measured 1200.0 × 1200.0 × 4103.0 mm, one closed manifold solid.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The A-COL internals are drawn as envelope solids at recorded layout elevations: packing support grid (z 700), the 2303 mm packing in two beds with a redistribution disc (z 715..1866.5, 1881.5..3033), liquid distributor (z 3060), PP mesh demister (z 4043). The rings and the mesh are not modelled piece-wise. Bed elevations are the layout choice (holds, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 9 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/MET_SCRUB.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore: inlets on −Y (N1 DN50, N2 DN50, N3 DN15 at z 400; N8 DN15 at z 160), outlets on +Y (N4 DN15, N5 DN15 at z 1500; N9 DN300 at z 600), N6 drain flush at z 0 as a boss lapping the skirt ring, N7 vent as a riser on the top head. Atmospheric joints are hose barbs flush at the envelope faces on ray-set extension spools crossing to the shell; no flange is drawn (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. N9 (DN300) is outside the lib DN15–DN50 hardware table: drawn as a plain stub at the house DN300 outside diameter 323.9 with a study 6 mm wall (hold, W12).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M10 wedge anchors, plinth to slab (count from the model). Polymer joint to the supplier rule; A4-70 hardware per the observed house practice. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication. The welded shell and the bonded internals carry no fastener row.
W6 Materials. PVC-U shell, sump and packing support per duty section 3 (AQS-STD-002 M1/M2); the graph gives no gas temperature for this unit — above 60 °C it would be CPVC (hold, W12). Packing PP (in-house cut rings per the node design); demister PP mesh. No metal wetted part; corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymer. The node design block names PP-H — duty section 3 governs wetted materials (plan rule 5); the disagreement is a hold (W12).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6); dP 1171 Pa across the packing is the node design figure and is not a pressure rating. No hydrotest is claimed; leak test is a shop discretion (route W10).
W8 Exports. `export/met_scrub.step` (assembly); `export/met_scrub.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/MET_SCRUB.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1200.0 × 1200.0 × 4103.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. Masses in `check.json`: shell + skirt + plinth 147.3 kg, packing 1028.7 kg, distributor 10.2 kg, demister 17.8 kg, hardware 65.3 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make: shell, internals and hardware are in-house fabrication (PP rings cut in-house; the mesh pad is stocked PP mesh).
W10 Fabrication route. F-COL: PVC-U pipe/sheet cut, thermoplastic weld of shell, head, skirt and plinth, packing support and distributor fit, set-in ports, bed loading, leak test. On-site tool: the thermoplastic shop (AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 F-COL operations).
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the scrubber captures metal-aisle fume; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) Wall thickness: no authority states one; 6 mm PVC-U is a study value, confirmed against the service class at fabrication. (2) Gas temperature: the graph gives none; the PVC-U selection holds only below 60 °C, above it the duty names CPVC — the temperature fact is the sizing-module task. (3) The node design block material (PP-H) disagrees with duty section 3 (PVC-U/CPVC): the model draws duty section 3; the reconciliation is the sizing-module task. (4) Bed elevations, bed split (two beds + redistribution disc), distributor and demister elevations are layout choices; the design gives packed height 2303 only. (5) No stage or HTU count exists for this column anywhere (A-COL: it stays a hold where the duty says so — the duty states none). (6) N9 DN300 is outside the lib DN15–DN50 hardware table: stub drawn at the house OD series 323.9 with a study 6 mm wall; the lib table extension is a shared-library task. (7) N7's 2000 datum predates the sized column; the vent is drawn as a riser on the top head. (8) Schedule rating column (Class 150) against the atmospheric duty: no flange is drawn. (9) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

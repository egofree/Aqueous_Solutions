# Work order LITHO_DEV

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-MACH + A-SC). Route class F-MACH (the `wet_bench` class sits in the F-MACH row of plan section 4). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/LITHO_DEV.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/LITHO_DEV.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-MOD-002 (frame system), AQS-BOM-SC-001 (self-copy BOM), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node LITHO_DEV (design block and note; A-M-287).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `litho_dev.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1200 × 600 × 900 ("L x W x H mm, issued envelope (A-M-287)"). The build fills it: measured 1200.0 × 600.0 × 900.0 mm, one closed manifold mesh. The duty section 2 prose still carries the seed figure "Envelope 500 × 500 × 800 mm" — the node governs and the older figure is named (holds, W12; the WS_SOLV precedent). A four-post hold-down frame on the base plate pins the registry box so the X-PAR-04 envelope registry reads the build.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn per the node design block and its note ("Wet develop bench on the WET_BENCH chemistry class (TMAH/KOH developer fork, S38 resist-waste loop)"): the develop bowl is the wetted station (316L per duty section 3) with the puddle dispense point envelope above it (40 mL/wafer per the node design; A-M-287 basis).
W4 Nozzles. All 4 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/LITHO_DEV.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric tube stubs flush at the envelope faces: N1 DN15 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N2 DN15 vent flush at the envelope top — its 800 datum predates the 900 box and is carried as a placeholder (hold, W12); N3 DN15 (the `solids` row: spent resist and solvent out) on +Y at z 600 and N4 DN15 (the `solids` row: resist/solvent carry in from the coat bench) on −Y at z 160 — non-piped transfer rows drawn DN15, bore not calculated (plan W4). Atmospheric hose per duty section 4 ("DN is copied from the edge and is not recalculated"); no flange hardware is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M10 wedge anchors (base plate to slab, 2 per frame post line) and 4 M8 bowl-clip bolts (bowl base flange to the base plate, one per corner), counts from the model. 8.8 on carbon steel, A4-70 on the 316L assembly, per the grade rule; lengths and patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Develop bowl, dispense point and connection hardware 316L per duty section 3 ("316L where the stream is wet" — the TMAH developer stream is the wet service; CA 0 mm, AQS-STD-001 rule). Base plate and hold-down frame carbon steel A500 (AQS-MOD-002 dry service, dry structure outside the wetted station).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6: "Atmospheric. No vent edge. Not to the room."). No design_kPa is booked on the node and none is claimed. No pressure test; a fill-and-hold leak check of the welded bowl is the shop discretion of the route (W10).
W8 Exports. `export/litho_dev.step` (assembly); `export/litho_dev.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/LITHO_DEV.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1200.0 × 600.0 × 900.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. Masses in `check.json`: base 113.0 kg, frame 86.4 kg, bowl 15.6 kg, dispense point 0.5 kg, hardware 0.2 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make (shop-fabricable, flagged for AQS-BOM-SC-001): base plate, hold-down frame, develop bowl, dispense point envelope, connection hardware. No purchased item is named on the node or duty, so none is drawn; the A-MACH purchased-item criterion has nothing to match (the WS_SOLV precedent).
W10 Fabrication route. F-MACH applied to the wet bench: 316L sheet cut and welded for the bowl (GTAW per the 316L practice), dispense mount set, frame weldment, anchors drilled and set, fill-and-hold leak check. On-site tool: the metal shop.
W11 Self-copy relevance. A-SC applies (tier 7): the develop bench is a lithography line station for the plant's own wafers (AQS-BOM-SC-001 names LITHO-DEV-01), and every part of the unit — base, frame, bowl, dispense point, hardware — is shop-fabricable: flagged make in the BOM and cross-referenced to AQS-BOM-SC-001.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The duty section 2 prose carries the seed figure 500 × 500 × 800; the node 1200 × 600 × 900 governs and the older figure is named — nothing resized (plan rule 3). (2) The develop bowl size (inner 300 × 200 × 120, wall 10) is the layout choice; the puddle figure 40 mL/wafer is a node design figure. (3) The N2 vent datum 800 predates the 900 box — carried as a placeholder, flush at the top. (4) The N3/N4 solids rows are non-piped transfers drawn DN15, bore not calculated (plan W4). (5) No purchased item is named on the node or duty, so none is drawn (WS_SOLV precedent). (6) The developer is the TMAH 2.38 % class on the shared WET_BENCH chemistry fork (node design); the duty section 3 material (316L) governs the wetted path — carried, nothing resized. (7) Fastener lengths and patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core, route hold). Route-class hold: `store` has no section 4 class (W12). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/STOCK_OUT.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/STOCK_OUT.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-DUTY-072 (sizing study), AQS-MOD-002 (frame practice), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node STOCK_OUT (design block). LIVE unit - the foundry/mill stock staging rack at the 25.9 kg/d shop issue and 10.8 kg/d export.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `stock_out.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid: six uprights, three tier decks, back guard, issue-face edge stops and sill, and the schedule hardware. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `design` and `layout_note` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1200 × 800 × 1500 (AQS-DUTY-072 study box). The build fills it: measured 1200.0 × 800.0 × 1500.0 mm from the export STL - the flush-hardware build keeps every part inside the faces (check.json `connection_overhang_mm` 0/0/0/0 mm, A-core 2; the rack frame reads 1200 × 800 × 1480 with the vent stub pinning the top datum); the Oct-31 `external_parts` claim on the issue sill is corrected - the sill sits inside the +X face, so the in-envelope build solid is the whole rack.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine the design block names (three-tier bar/plate/ingot staging rack) is drawn as the unit per the duty sheet: six uprights 60 × 60, tier decks at z 160/600/1000 (the task-04 inlet datum and the issue datum), back guard plate, and the issue-face edge stops at +X. The stock itself is drawn as process-inventory solids (three tier loads at the 1.0 d hold), not BOM parts.
W4 Nozzles. All 10 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/STOCK_OUT.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore, flush at the envelope faces: N1..N7 stock inlets (solids rows - plate/sheet, rod/profile, wire, sintered stock, as-cast stock, powder stock, investment casting; bore not calculated per W4) landing on the tier decks at z 160 (the registered layout choice); N8 formed metal product issue (solids row, bore not calculated) at the +X face at z 600 over the issue sill; N9 DN15 nominal floor drain flush at the bottom (50 datum); N10 DN15 top vent at the envelope datum z 1500. Atmospheric joints; no flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06 - hold, W12).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M12 wedge anchors (uprights to slab), 12 M10 bolts (tier decks to the uprights); counts from the model. 8.8 on carbon steel per the grade rule (AQS-PLAN-006 W5); lengths, torque and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: carbon steel uprights, decks, guard, stops and sill. CA 0 mm - dry, non-hydrocarbon storage service (AQS-STD-001 shop rule).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric rack; the duty section 6 relief case is pending (unit task 07) and no set point is claimed. No instrument loop is issued (unit task 08). The N10 vent is the nominal rack-cover stub, not a process vent.
W8 Exports. `export/stock_out.step` (assembly); `export/stock_out.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/STOCK_OUT.stl` replaced from the in-envelope build solid (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 registry; `external_parts` empty - the flush build keeps everything inside). Bounding box 1200.0 × 800.0 × 1500.0 mm against the node envelope: filled exactly, nothing resized. Masses in `check.json`: uprights 250.9 kg, tier decks 678.2 kg, back guard 256.2 kg, edge stops 43.0 kg, sill 5.0 kg, hardware the remainder.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make (uprights, decks, guard, stops, sill, drain and vent hardware); there is no purchased row - the rack is a plain weldment with no drive or electrical rating on the node.
W10 Fabrication route. Route hold: the `store` class has no section 4 class (W12). The operations follow the F-MACH frame-weldment practice as the study route (named, not claimed): section cut (uprights, decks, guard), weld (upright set, deck supports), edge stops and sill fit, anchor set, then the test: rated stock load per tier at the 1.0 d hold (AQS-DUTY-072). On-site tool: the metal shop welding bay.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the rack stages the mill's own product, it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.

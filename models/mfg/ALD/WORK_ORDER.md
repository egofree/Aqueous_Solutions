# Work order ALD

Authority: AQS-DUTY-062 section 6 (X-MFG-31, A-M-157). In-house fabrication; welders and procedure qualified to ASME IX.

1. Cut DN15 Sch 40 316L to 600 mm; butt-weld the DN15 Class 150 weld-neck flanges (GTAW, back-purged).
2. Drill the blind flanges for the feed/product ports and thermowell.
3. Hydrostatic test at 1.3 x the Class 150 rating at temperature (UG-99).
4. Load inert balls, 8.3 g catalyst, inert balls between mesh retainers; close with spiral-wound gaskets.
5. Fit the band heater, vaporiser coil, insulation.
6. Inspect against models/mfg/ALD/export/ald.step; record on the traveller.

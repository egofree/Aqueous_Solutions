## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `litho_align.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1200 × 600 × 800 ("L x W x H mm, AQS-LITH-001 rev C frame 6061"). The build fills it: measured 1200.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm, one closed manifold mesh. The duty section 2 prose still carries the seed figure "Envelope 500 × 500 × 800 mm" — the node governs and the older figure is named (holds, W12; the WS_SOLV precedent). A four-post guard frame (6061) on the base plate pins the registry box so the X-PAR-04 envelope registry reads the build.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn per the node design block and its equipment authority AQS-LITH-001 rev C: aluminium stage plate, 4140 leadscrew 10 mm at 1 mm pitch (cut on the lathe), mask chrome on glass with the 100 µm pattern (cut on the mill), and the 365 nm LED array head (1 W optical) drawn as its purchased envelope per plan rule 6 — rating from the node, no invented internals.
W4 Nozzles. Both rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/LITHO_ALIGN.csv` are drawn at tag, face and bore as atmospheric tube stubs flush at the envelope faces: N1 DN15 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N2 DN15 vent flush at the envelope top (800 datum is the box top). Duty section 4: "No present edge. No nozzle." — the two rows are the envelope hardware rows, atmospheric hose per the duty. No flange hardware is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. AQS-LITH-001 section 4 names one SB350 hose barb as the machine service interface; it is not a schedule row and is not drawn (named, W12).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M8 socket screws (guard posts to base, 2 per post) and 4 M8 bench anchors (base to bench top, one per corner), counts from the model. No plan grade rule covers aluminium joints: A4-70 stainless selected against galvanic action with the 6061 frame; lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Frame and base plate aluminium 6061, stage aluminium, leadscrew steel 4140, mask glass with chrome coating — per AQS-LITH-001 rev C section 3 (the equipment authority the node names). Duty section 3 ("316L where the stream is wet") has nothing to match: mass in and out are zero and the machine is dry. CA 0 mm — dry machine (AQS-STD-001 shop rule).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6). No design_kPa is booked on the node and none is claimed. No hydrotest; the machine is dry.
W8 Exports. `export/litho_align.step` (assembly); `export/litho_align.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/LITHO_ALIGN.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry).

# Work order WET_BENCH

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-MACH + A-SC). Route class F-MACH (the `wet_bench` class sits in the F-MACH row of plan section 4). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/WET_BENCH.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/WET_BENCH.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-STD-002 rules M1/M2 (thermoplastic class rules), AQS-MOD-002 (frame system), AQS-BOM-SC-001 (self-copy BOM), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node WET_BENCH (equipment list).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `wet_bench.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1000 × 600 × 800 (study default envelope box; the duty section 2 states no envelope). The build fills it exactly: measured 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm, one closed manifold mesh. A four-post hold-down frame (40 × 40 posts + top rails) pins the registry box — the HX8 idiom, so the X-PAR-04 envelope registry reads the build.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn as the unit per the node equipment list (the RCA/etch bench load basis): SC1 bath 10 L at 80 °C and SC2 bath 10 L at 80 °C with immersion heater envelopes (2.479 kWh/d each, purchased per plan rule 6), DHF etch bath at ambient, and the DIW rinse bay. The node equipment rows name PP tanks; the wetted material is the duty section 3 selection (plan rule 5) — the tension is a named hold (W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 13 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/WET_BENCH.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube stubs flush at the envelope faces: inlets on −Y at z 160 — N1 DN15 HCl, N2 DN25 fab UPW > 18 MΩ·cm, N3 DN15 HF (aq), N4 DN15 H₂O₂, N9 DN15 NH₄OH / NH₃ (aq), N10 DN15 KOH, N11 DN15 HNO₃, N12 DN15 H₃PO₄, N13 DN15 ethanol and acetone; outlets on +Y at z 600 — N5 DN25 spent etch, N6 DN15 resist and solvent waste; N7 DN15 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N8 DN15 vent flush at the envelope top (800 datum is the box top). Atmospheric tube or hose per duty section 4 (nozzle DN15, no flange class); the stubs are drawn polymer per duty section 3 ("No metal wetted part"); no flange hardware is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M12 wedge anchors (plinth to slab, 2 per frame post line) and 8 M10 tank-clip bolts (2 per bath on the deck clip rail), counts from the model. 8.8 on carbon steel per the grade rule; polymer joints to the polymer supplier rule (A4-70 hardware observed house practice). Lengths and patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. SC1 and SC2 baths CPVC, DHF and rinse baths and the nozzle stubs PVC-U, per duty section 3 ("PVC-U wetted, per AQS-STD-002 rules M1 and M2. No metal wetted part. The graph gives no temperature for this unit; above 60 °C the thermoplastic is CPVC, never metal") applied at the bath temperatures the node equipment rows state (80 °C → CPVC; ambient → PVC-U). Bath wall 10 mm is a study value (W12) — the duty states no wall. Plinth, deck and frame carbon steel A500 (AQS-MOD-002 dry service, dry structure outside the wetted polymer).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6: "Atmospheric. No vent edge. Not to the room."). No test pressure is claimed beyond a fill-and-hold leak check of the welded baths.
W8 Exports. `export/wet_bench.step` (assembly); `export/wet_bench.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/WET_BENCH.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. Masses in `check.json`: plinth 94.2 kg, deck 73.3 kg, frame 76.4 kg, baths 63.1 kg total (SC1 20.2, SC2 20.2, DHF 12.0, rinse 12.0 approx per `bath_mass_kg`), heater envelopes 0.4 kg, nozzles 0.9 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make rows (shop-fabricable, flagged for AQS-BOM-SC-001): bench deck + plinth, hold-down/guard frame, the four baths, nozzle set. Buy rows (purchased per plan rule 6): immersion heater envelopes (energy basis from the node rows; no per-heater kW rating stated — hold, W12); N2 gun set (node equipment row, hand tools, not drawn).
W10 Fabrication route. F-MACH applied to the thermoplastic shop: PVC-U/CPVC sheet cut and welded (DVS 2207 class practice), bath floor and wall welds, nozzle set-in welds, bench and frame weldment, clip rail, anchors drilled and set, fill-and-hold leak check of the baths. On-site tool: the thermoplastic shop (baths) and the metal shop (bench, frame).
W11 Self-copy relevance. A-SC applies (tier 7): the bench supports the fab wet line the plant runs for its own wafers, and the parts of the unit the on-site shop can make — bench deck, plinth, frame, the thermoplastic baths and the connection hardware — are flagged make in the BOM and cross-referenced to AQS-BOM-SC-001. The immersion heaters and N2 guns are purchased.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The envelope is the study default registry box; neither the node nor the duty section 2 states a design envelope — the build fills the box via the hold-down frame (X-PAR-04); nothing resized (plan rule 3). (2) Bath arrangement, and the DHF and rinse bath sizes, are the layout choice — the node names "DHF etch bath, ambient" and "DIW rinse + N2 guns" with no size; the SC1/SC2 10 L figures are node figures, not choices. (3) Bath wall 10 mm is a study value; the duty states no wall. (4) The node equipment rows name PP tanks; duty section 3 selects PVC-U/CPVC — the duty governs (plan rule 5); nothing resized, the tension is carried. (5) No per-heater kW rating is stated on the node or duty: the heater rows carry 2.479 kWh/d each (heat-up + insulated hold); the rating is a purchase-line fact. (6) The N2 gun set is named from the node equipment row ("DIW rinse + N2 guns", 0.05 kWh/d) and is not drawn: hand tools, no rating stated. (7) The stub ODs follow the DN series (the steel pipe table); the polymer tube wall is the supplier rule, confirmed at fabrication. (8) Fastener lengths and clip pattern are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

# Work order AQUA_TANK

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-FARM). Route class F-FARM practice named as the study route (the `fish_tank` class has no section 4 row - hold, W12). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/AQUA_TANK.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/AQUA_TANK.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), A-M-415 (60 kg stock / 30 kg-per-m3 class), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node AQUA_TANK.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `aqua_tank.py` (CadQuery, on the shared close-out builder `models/mfg/farm_closeout.py`) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 2100 × 1100 × 1100 - "2.0 m3 working volume at the 60 kg stock / 30 kg-per-m3 class (TECHTREE tilapia glossary; A-M-415): 2000 x 1000 x 1000 ID + 50 walls, EPDM-lined HDPE (X-FARM sec3)". The rebuild fills the box exactly: measured 2100.0 × 1100.0 × 1100.0 mm, flush on all six faces (A-core 2; A-FARM: not shrunk to fit the block).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The parts the duty names are drawn beside the shell: the food-grade liner as a 5 mm sheet (floor + four walls, the GROW_BED ponded-zone idiom). The Oct-31 liner was a solid block filling the cavity (2230.4 kg); the rebuild draws the sheet the params name (`liner_mm` 5, duty section 3). The aeration the design block names (the air stones are the O2 source) has no position or rating on the node: not drawn (hold, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 8 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/AQUA_TANK.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore DN15 tube stubs flush at the envelope faces: N1-N3 out on +Y at z 600 (fish waste water, sludge to the larvae bin, harvest to the food store); N4-N6 in on -Y at z 160 (loop top-up, polished return, fresh duckweed ration); N7 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N8 vent flush at the envelope top - the schedule z 800 datum predates the 1100 box (stale datum, hold W12). Atmospheric joints are tube/hose per duty section 4; no flange is drawn (W4 110 % rule - no process pressure is stated); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M10 lid hold-downs (SS316 above the waterline per duty section 3, polymer supplier rule named) and 4 M10 base anchors (8.8 carbon steel per the grade rule). Counts are the study pattern / the model count; lengths confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: HDPE tank body, food-grade LDPE aquaculture liner sheet, SS316 hardware above the waterline, CPVC loop fittings. No metal wetted part; corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymers (AQS-STD-001 rule). The liner mass is carried at the HDPE table density (950 kg/m3) - the table has no LDPE row (hold, W12).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric; duty section 6 states the relief case is pending and no set point is claimed. Instruments AQUA_TANKPT-01/TE-01 are the duty section 5 loops (loop hardware, not modeled). No test pressure is claimed.
W8 Exports. `export/aqua_tank.step` (assembly); `export/aqua_tank.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/AQUA_TANK.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 2100.0 × 1100.0 × 1100.0 mm against the node envelope: flush, no misfit. Masses in `check.json`: shell 130.9 kg, lid 32.9 kg, liner sheet 41.9 kg, nozzles 0.1 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make: shell, lid and liner sheet are in-house fabrication; the CPVC nozzle set is fabricated from bought fittings stock.
W10 Fabrication route. F-FARM practice (plan section 4 F-FARM row: frame, liner, bed, drainage): HDPE sheet cut and thermoplastic weld (shell walls, floor, lid), liner sheet cut and field-fitted with the ponded-zone laps, nozzle stubs set in and welded, then a water-fill leak test at the loop line. On-site tool: the thermoplastic (polymer) bench with the farm fit-out crew.
W11 Self-copy relevance. The tank holds fish, not plant parts; A-SC does not apply (tier 9). The HDPE shell is itself a shop thermoplastic-weld product class.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The `fish_tank` class has no AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 route class; the F-FARM practice is the named study route. (2) The aeration (air stones) the design block names as the O2 source has no position or rating on the node - not drawn. (3) The liner class is LDPE per duty section 3; the material table has no LDPE row, so the mass is carried at the HDPE table density until the supplier TDS. (4) The lid thickness follows the shell wall (15 mm study). (5) The N8 vent datum (z 800) predates the 1100 box; drawn flush at the envelope top. (6) Fastener lengths and the lid clamp pattern are study values, confirmed at fabrication. (7) The design-block notes (ration 0.9 kg/d, ammonia and O2 basis A-M-415) are loop-duty facts reported on the duty sheet, not model claims.

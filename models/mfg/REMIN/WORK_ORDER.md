# Work order REMIN

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-VES). Route class F-VES. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/REMIN.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/REMIN.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-STD-002 rule M1, `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node REMIN (its design block names the machine, pumps, UV and chemistry).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `remin.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 600 × 600 × 600, set by `docs-authority/_storage_sizing.py` (AQS-DUTY-064). The build fills it: measured 600.0 × 600.0 × 600.0 mm, one closed manifold solid. The duty section 2 line "Envelope 600 × 400 × 800 mm is the shell" predates the storage-sizing box - the node governs and the older figure is named (holds, W12).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is a dosing skid: the node design block names 2 × metering diaphragm pumps (Ca slurry, MgCl2) and a UV unit after the dose (potable integrity); per plan rule 6 they are drawn as their envelope and interface only - their internals stay with the maker (holds, W12). Envelopes: pumps 110 × 90 × 130 each front-right, UV lantern OD 90 × 220 long front-left, on the tray floor zone that the tank footprint leaves clear.
W4 Nozzles. All 5 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/REMIN.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore: N1 DN15 UPW in on −Y at z 160; N2 DN15 potable and N5 DN15 remineralised makeup out on +Y (schedule datums 600 predates the box; clamped to 570.7 so the branch and riser stay inside - hold, W12); N3 drain, open bore through the tray floor flush at the bottom face (50 datum); N4 vent flush at the tank top head - its 800 datum predates the box (hold, W12). Side connections ride tray risers, flush at the envelope faces; atmospheric tube or hose per duty section 4 (hose makeup by the connecting line at the face); no flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. The along-face positions are the registered layout choice (per tag in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M10 wedge anchors (tray to slab, one per corner, count from the model) and 8 M8 hold-down bolts (4 per purchased envelope, count from the model). A4-70 hardware by the polymer-joint supplier rule. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication. The welded joints carry no fastener row.
W6 Materials. PVC-U drawn (duty section 3: "PVC-U or HDPE, per AQS-STD-002 rule M1"; the M1 audit list books the water-tank family). The unresolved HDPE alternative is a named hold (W12). Corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymer (AQS-STD-001). Tank wall 10 mm is the study fab-page value; no thickness is booked on the node (hold, W12). The purchased envelopes carry their maker class at order.
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6). Not to the room. Level and temperature are the duty section 5 instruments (high level trips the inlet; loop hardware, not modeled). No hydrotest pressure is claimed; the leak test is a shop discretion of the route (W10).
W8 Exports. `export/remin.step` (assembly); `export/remin.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/REMIN.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 600.0 × 600.0 × 600.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. A-VES: the working volume at the duty-basis level (z 520.4, 0.07 m³) is reported in `check.json`.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: tray, tank, connections. Buy: the two metering pumps and the UV unit (named with the rating from the node design block; drive and lamp ratings per the maker TDS at order).
W10 Fabrication route. F-VES: PVC-U sheet/pipe cut (saw), thermoplastic weld of tank shell, heads and tray, nozzle set-in welded, anchors drilled, leak test of the tank; the purchased envelopes mount on the tray. On-site tool: the thermoplastic shop (AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 F-VES operations).
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the skid doses potable water; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The envelope is the storage-sizing box; the duty section 2 shell line 600 × 400 × 800 is an older figure - the node governs, nothing resized. (2) The duty section 3 PVC-U-or-HDPE conditional is unresolved; PVC-U is drawn. (3) Tank wall 10 mm is the study fab-page value; no thickness is booked on the node. (4) Tank OD 460, axis at x 300 / y 350, the pump and UV envelopes and their floor zone are the registered layout choice. (5) The purchased pumps and UV are drawn as envelope and interface only; their wetted parts, drive and lamp ratings follow the maker TDS at order. (6) The N2/N5 outlet datums 600 and the N4 vent datum 800 predate the box; clamped as W4 records. (7) Schedule rating column (Class 150) against the atmospheric hose duty: no flange is drawn. (8) The A-VES working volume is the duty section 2 basis (7 days at the 10 L/d tap = 0.07 m³) applied as the model working level; no new residence-time claim. (9) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

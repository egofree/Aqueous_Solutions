# Work order ZNBR2

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-CELL). Route class F-CELL. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/ZNBR2.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/ZNBR2.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-STD-002 (halogen-class materials), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node ZNBR2 (purge_basis, cycling_thermal and design blocks; design written by `flow_graph/param/ess_tf.py`, X-MFG-43, A-M-456/457), AQS-BUS-001 (bus class method), A-M-179 (round-trip book), A-M-455 (cycling heat).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `znbr2.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` and the node design block; it rebuilds every solid. The `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks of `params.json` are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 7900 × 6050 × 1970 (set with the design by ess_tf, X-MFG-43). The build fills it: measured 7900 × 6050 × 1971 mm (the vent riser stands 0.5 mm proud of the lifting beams and the drain boss 0.5 mm below the plinth, both inside the 1 mm gate). The placement seed block for ZNBR2 stays the 1800 × 1200 × 1600 study box and under-books this machine - hold, W12(1).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine construction the design block defines is drawn: 47 module bays, each a bipolar plate field (bonded plate-pack envelope: 95 plates + 94 separator sheets at the 6 mm pitch, the drawn envelope carrying the booked active area 0.49789 m2 per plate; individual sheets are BOM rows 2/4) over its electrolyte reservoir (PVC-U walls, 529.4 L at 2 M ZnBr2), with the pump and cooler envelopes per plan rule 6. The drawn plates fill the booked cell-summed electrode area 2223.1 m2 (purge_basis) at the 20 mA/cm2 book: j·A = current closes at 99.6 A per stack, 172.9 V DC, 17.2 kW DC charge per module.
W4 Nozzles. All 12 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/ZNBR2.csv` are carried at tag, face, height and bore: the 11 piped rows draw as 150 mm atmospheric open-bore stubs flush at the hall faces (duty section 4: hose, atmospheric; DN copied from the edge, not recalculated; inlets −Y, outlets +Y; drain under the plinth; vent riser on the last bay lifting beam), and the `cable` row (N11, power terminal) is a text row with no stub. The schedule elevations (160/600/800) are the issued datums and draw as issued (hold, W12(7)). Per-tag positions in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: M16 tie rods with nuts, four per module stack (stack compression); M8 lug bolts at the per-stack bus terminals; M12 wedge anchors, plinth to slab. Lengths and torque values are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: no wetted alloy; conductors are copper; corrosion allowance does not apply (named per part in the BOM basis). Plates carbon-plastic composite (ZBFB class, A-M-456); reservoirs PVC-U sheet (AQS-STD-002 halogen class - the internals-register "PP electrolyte loops" wording predates that reading and is superseded, W12(4)); separators microporous polyolefin, material on coupon test with the hypobromite rating; bus copper C101; frame carbon steel (dry side).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric store: the purge loop holds the headspace at 25 % of the H2 LFL (ess_tf purge basis, NFPA 69); the electrolyte tanks are open-top, vented by the hall volume. The stored design_kPa idiom of duty section 6 is not a set point. The electrical charge path rides IA-ESS-ZNBR2-CHG at 23.5 kW per module (AQS-ELEC-008 distribution).
W8 Exports. `export/znbr2.step` (compound of 811 closed solids); `export/znbr2.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/ZNBR2.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 7900 × 6050 × 1971 mm against the node envelope: inside (1 mm gate). The electrolyte holdup 25 307 L (model, 47 × 538.2 L inside the PVC-U walls) against the design 24 882 L (47 × 529.4 L at 2 M, A-M-457) is reported in `check.json`; no residence time is derived (the duty section 2 claims no holdup; A-VES rule).
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: frame, plates (as the bonded pack), reservoirs, bus flats, connections. Buy: separator sheets, pumps, coolers, electrolyte inventory.
W10 Fabrication route. F-CELL: tank fabrication (PVC-U sheet, thermoplastic weld), plate shear and punch (carbon-plastic composite), bus cut and drill (copper), stack assembly under tie-rod compression, gasket cut. On-site tool: the metal shop plus the plastics bay; first-article stack per X-MFG-41 (AQS-TST-002) gates the performance class.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the store makes electricity; it makes no plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The placement seed block (1800 × 1200 × 1600 mm, duty section 4 size line) under-books the design-linked machine: the booked store occupies a 7900 × 6050 × 1970 rack hall plus the electrolyte farm - the floor-space reservation is the named consequence and needs the placement lane (nothing is resized here; the graph envelope follows the design per the X-MFG-43 lane). (2) The plate fields draw as bonded per-module pack envelopes (95 plates + 94 separator sheets at the 6 mm pitch); the individual sheets are BOM rows, not drawn solids - the drawn envelope carries the booked active area exactly (0.49789 m2 × 95 × 47 = 2223.09 against the booked 2223.1 m2). (3) The separator sheet material (microporous polyolefin class) stands on a coupon test with the hypobromite rating; the stack performance class (ASR, crossover, auxiliaries) is gated by the X-MFG-41 stack test (AQS-TST-002, A-M-179). (4) The reservoirs draw PVC-U per AQS-STD-002 (PP excluded for halogens); the internals-register "PP electrolyte loops" wording predates that reading and is superseded here. (5) The electrolyte basis (2.0 M ZnBr2, single mixed reservoir per module, 529.4 L) is a registered study assumption (A-M-457); volume scales 1/M. (6) The bus bar section follows the AQS-BUS-001 class method (one 20 × 6.3 copper flat per terminal at 79 A/cm2 on the 99.6 A stack current); POWER.md states no ZNBR2 bus section, so no POWER.md row is contradicted (A-CELL bus rule). (7) The schedule elevations (160/600/800) are the issued datums, drawn as issued; the connection hardware is the atmospheric hose joint at the face. (8) The cooler duty per module (3.1 kW) rides the cycling_thermal charge-window book (A-M-455); the pump rating (0.52 kW) the A-M-179 auxiliary share. (9) The duty section 2 states no plate geometry: the plate figures derive from the purge-basis electrode area and the registered plate class (A-M-456), and the duty section 2 render names them from the design block. (10) Fastener lengths, torque pattern and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication. (11) The electrolyte inventory (24.9 m3 at 2 M) is the reactant holdup; top-up on IA-ZNBR-MU, bleed on IA-ZNBR-BD, and the Br2 recovery loop to ZNBR2_BR2_REGEN runs on the purge air book (S69/S69B).

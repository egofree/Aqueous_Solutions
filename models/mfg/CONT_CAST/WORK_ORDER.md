Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-SC). Route hold: the node class `caster` has no row in the section 4 route table. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/CONT_CAST.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/CONT_CAST.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-DUTY-072 (sizing study), AQS-SIZE-FOUNDRY (mould basis), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node CONT_CAST (design block: feed_kg_d, billet_kg_d, ingot_kg_d, strands, mould).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `cont_cast.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` and rebuilds every solid: frame, mould housing and water jacket, withdrawal rolls, run-out table, billet bed, water manifold, and the schedule hardware. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `assumptions` and `design` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1500 × 800 × 1500 (A-M-199 forming class; hold-up of the continuous feed is below the class minimum). The build fills it: measured 1500.0 × 800.0 × 1500.0 mm, all connection hardware flush at the envelope faces, nothing protrudes.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The strands on the run-out are drawn as process-inventory solids, not BOM parts and not internals.
W4 Nozzles. All 5 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/CONT_CAST.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore connections (duty section 4; hose makeup by the connecting line at the face): N1 DN15 melt row (from IND_MELT N8) set in through the −X face at z 160 and carried to the mould inlet pass; N2/N3 solids rows (continuous billet/slab, as-cast stock) at the +X roll-off stop, z 600, drawn at DN15 and labeled bore not calculated (W4 rule); N4 DN15 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N5 DN15 vent on the table deck, flush at the envelope top (its 800 datum is the schedule placeholder - hold, W12). The schedule face words are mapped to compass faces by the registered layout choice (params.json layout_note). No flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated at the face (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. The mould-cooling water circuit is the issued Q3/Q4 M1 loop family (duty section 3) and is drawn as the manifold solid; its line list is loop hardware, not modeled (W12).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M10 stud-and-nut sets on the roll stands, 4 M12 anchors, frame to slab. Both rows grade 8.8 carbon steel by the W5 grade rule. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: water-cooled copper mould (the graph-design primary) in a steel jacket casing, graphite feed launder, demineralized-water jacket on the issued M1 loop family. CA 0 mm - dry foundry-floor service on the CS structure; the water side is the issued M1 family (AQS-STD-002 section 12.1; AQS-STD-001 shop rule).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric machine; the mould-water side carries the loop's issued classification, no new set point is claimed; the duty section 6 relief case is pending (unit task 07). Not to the room.
W8 Exports. `export/cont_cast.step` (assembly); `export/cont_cast.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/CONT_CAST.stl` replaced from the in-envelope build solid (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 registry; scope recorded in `check.json`). Bounding box 1500.0 × 800.0 × 1500.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly, zero face overhang.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: frame, mould housing and water jacket, withdrawal rolls, run-out rollers and bed, water manifold, melt inlet, stock stop, drain and vent. Buy: none - the machine structure is all in-house fabrication (the Cu moulds are shop-made per AQS-SIZE-FOUNDRY).
W10 Fabrication route. Route hold (W12); operations named so the shop can bid it: plate and section cut, frame weldment (CS fillet welds), Cu mould cavity machine and fit, jacket leak test, roll bore and press-fit, stub set-in, water manifold braze and pressure test, then dry strand pull and first cast from IND_MELT N8. On-site tool: the metal shop welding bay and the machine bay.
W11 Self-copy relevance. A-SC applies (tier 7): the caster makes the billet and ingot stock the shop rolls and fabricates from, which is the AQS-BOM-SC-001 feedstock path; the parts of the machine itself the on-site shop can make - frame, mould housing, water jacket, rolls, run-out and connection hardware - are flagged make in the BOM and cross-referenced to AQS-BOM-SC-001.
W12 Holds, named. (1) No AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 route class for class `caster`; the tracker carries the route hold and W10 names the operations anyway. (2) Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders, not designed elevations (duty section 4; unit tasks 02/06). (3) The N5 vent datum 800 is the schedule placeholder. (4) The two solids rows (N2, N3) are drawn at DN15, bore not calculated (W4 rule). (5) The mould-water circuit is loop hardware: its line list, pump and heat rejection live on the Q3/Q4 loop units, not modeled here. (6) The strand launders and tundish are the melt-side interface at the unit task. (7) The relief case is pending (unit task 07). (8) Fastener lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication. (9) No instrument loop is issued for this unit yet (duty section 8). (10) No measured rate is claimed; the duty section 1 rates are graph study values.

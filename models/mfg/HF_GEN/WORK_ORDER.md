# Work order HF_GEN

Authority: AQS-DUTY-073 fluoro train (A-M-283). LIVE unit - dock acid-spar landed.

1. Set the trunnion pedestals and drive; roll and load the D200 tube at 2 deg (4 rpm).
2. Build the indirect furnace chamber around the tube; cure the lining.
3. Fit the spar hopper/rotary valve and the acid day tank with metering pump.
4. Mount the discharge hood, cake bin and the DN110 gas offtake to the absorption tank.
5. Test: dry rotation and heat-up to 250 C, then a spar campaign at 0.33 kg/d.
6. Inspect against models/mfg/HF_GEN/export/hf_gen.step; record dimensions on the traveller.

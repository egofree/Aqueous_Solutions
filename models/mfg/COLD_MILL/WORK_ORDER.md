# Work order COLD_MILL

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-MACH + A-SC). Route class F-MACH (the `forming` class sits in the F-MACH row of plan section 4). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/COLD_MILL.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/COLD_MILL.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-MFG-010 (Table 2.1 tool row, rev C metering split), A-M-177 (metal-forming basis), AQS-MOD-002 (frame standard), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node COLD_MILL (continuous note).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `cold_mill.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1000 × 600 × 800 (the M0 study registry box). The build fills it: measured 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm from the export STL — the four-post hold-down/guard frame pins the box so the X-PAR-04 envelope registry reads the build.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn as the unit per the duty sheet and the AQS-MFG-010 tool row (Table 2.1, rev C metering split): two-high cold rolling mill, nameplate 2.2 kW, energy basis 250 MPa × strain 2.3 / 0.55 (DIETER1988) = 0.0324 kWh/kg + no-load 15 % × 1 h (A-M-177) — mill bedplate, two vertical housings (40 × 360, study) with crown tie, upper/lower work rolls OD 120 × 300 (5 mm gap at the strip class) with outboard journals, screw-down studs on the crown, entry/exit strip tables on the pass line (z 302.5), drive gearbox and motor as purchased envelopes (2.2 kW nameplate), control cabinet as a purchased envelope, roll-zone guard rail.
W4 Nozzles. All 4 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/COLD_MILL.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube stubs flush at the envelope faces: N1 strand in on −Y at z 160; N2 sheet/strip out on +Y at z 600 — the schedule datum is a placeholder above the pass line (hold, W12); N3 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N4 vent flush at the envelope top (800 datum is the box top). Duty section 4: DN15, no flange class, atmospheric tube or hose; no flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06 — hold, W12).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M12 wedge anchors (plinth to slab, 2 per frame post line) and 8 M16 journal-block bolts (2 per journal), counts from the model. 8.8 on carbon steel per the grade rule; lengths and patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: 316L where the stream is wet — the mill is a dry forming machine with no wetted stream on the graph, so CA 0 mm shop practice applies and the 316L row is carried for the connection stubs. Work rolls tool steel (AR400/D2 class, lib table; journals turned in the shop lathe). Frame, bedplate, housings, crown, tables, guard carbon steel A500 (AQS-MOD-002 dry service).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric machine; the duty section 6 relief case is pending (unit task 07) and no set point is claimed. Duty section 5: temperature or current with a high-temperature power trip — control hardware, sensor circuit not modeled (IND_MELT loop precedent — hold, W12).
W8 Exports. `export/cold_mill.step` (assembly, 6 solids); `export/cold_mill.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/COLD_MILL.stl` replaced from the in-envelope build solid (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 registry). Bounding box 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm against the node envelope: filled exactly.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make rows carry the AQS-BOM-SC-001 flag (A-SC): plinth, hold-down frame, bedplate/housings/crown, work rolls and journals (shop lathe), screw-downs, strip tables, guard rail, nozzle set. Buy rows (purchased per plan rule 6, ratings from the node): drive gearbox + motor (2.2 kW, AQS-MFG-010 Table 2.1), control cabinet (maker TDS mass governs).
W10 Fabrication route. F-MACH (plan section 4: frame weldment, guards, purchased drive or electrical by rating, anchors): plate cut and weld (bedplate, housings, crown, tables, guard), roll turning and journal bore (shop lathe), screw-down fit, guard assembly, drive set mounting and belt/coupling guard, anchors, then a no-load spin check and a pass-line trial strip. On-site tool: the metal shop (lathe, mill, welding bay).
W11 Self-copy relevance. The mill makes sheet/strip for the shop and goods lines (S59), not plant parts; A-SC applies to the shop-made hardware flagged in the BOM with the AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference (tier-7 set). The rolls themselves are the shop's own product class — the first-article roll is a self-copy candidate (X-MFG-37).
W12 Holds, named. (1) Housing, roll, journal, table and crown dimensions are study values sized to the strip class; no authority states a roll diameter or a gap beyond the 5 mm strip class drawn here. (2) The N2 schedule datum z 600 is a placeholder above the pass line; drawn verbatim at the face, the physical exit is the pass-line table. (3) Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06). (4) The drive-set and cabinet masses are table-density placeholders until the maker TDS quotes. (5) The high-temperature trip loop sensor circuit is not modeled. (6) The relief case is pending (unit task 07). (7) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication. (8) The duty section 8 open item stands: no machine envelope on the node beyond the M0 study box; the box is filled, nothing resized.

# Work order HOPPER

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-VES). Route class F-VES. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/HOPPER.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/HOPPER.csv`, AQS-DUTY-064 (storage sizing that sets the node `size_mm`), AQS-STD-001 (shop rule and corrosion allowance), AQS-STD-002 (material class rules), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node HOPPER.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `hopper.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 600 × 600 × 600, set by AQS-DUTY-064 (`docs-authority/_storage_sizing.py`). The duty sheet section 2 carries no size line and no holdup sentence - the node box is the claimed envelope and the build fills it: measured 600.0 × 600.0 × 600.0 mm, one closed manifold solid.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The body is the machine: bin section z 310..580, pyramidal frustum z 40..310 to a 210 mm square outlet box (196 mm bore) over a 190 mm base-plate discharge opening. No other internal is invented.
W4 Nozzles. All 11 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/HOPPER.csv` are scheduled at tag, face, height and bore; 10 are drawn: inlets N1/N2/N3/N10 on −Y at z 160, outlets N4/N5/N6/N9 on +Y, N7 drain flush at z 0 with the boss to its 50 datum, N8 vent flush at the top face. N11 is the dock-import make-up feed: a hand transfer, not piped - carried verbatim in `check.json`, never drawn (bore not calculated). Atmospheric joints are hose barbs (duty section 4: "no flange class. Atmospheric tube or hose"); no flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. The along-face x positions are the registered layout choice (per tag in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`). The z 160 roots are set in the frustum wall, which stands off the envelope face at that height; the z 600 outlet datums are clamped to 565..584.4 by the barb-radius rule, and the vent datum 800 predates the 600 box (W12 holds).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M10 wedge anchors, base plate to slab, one per corner (count from the model). Grade ISO 898-1 8.8 by the plan grade rule on carbon steel. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication. The welded joints carry no fastener row.
W6 Materials. Carbon steel plate throughout (duty section 3: hydrocarbon service, no aqueous chloride in this vessel). Corrosion allowance 1.5 mm (AQS-STD-001 rule). Wall thickness 4 mm is a study value - no authority states one (W12).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6). No hydrotest pressure is claimed; the leak test is a shop discretion (route W10).
W8 Exports. `export/hopper.step` (assembly); `export/hopper.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/HOPPER.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 600.0 × 600.0 × 600.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. Geometric holdup (bin + frustum + outlet, model) 0.1424 m³. Masses in `check.json`: body 36.5 kg, base plate 50.9 kg, rim frame 7.3 kg, hardware 3.3 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. No purchased rows: the hopper is an all-make weldment.
W10 Fabrication route. F-VES: carbon steel plate cut (laser or shear), pyramid break-form or plate weldment, fillet-welded seams, set-in nozzle stubs, anchors drilled, leak test of the discharge path. On-site tool: the MFG shop plate work (AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 F-VES operations).
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the hopper receives and discharges feedstock; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) Wall thickness: no authority states one; 4 mm plate is a study value, confirmed against the AQS-ASSAY-001 bounding lump load at fabrication. (2) No holdup basis for this class (duty sections 2 and 8); the model volume is reported, not turned into a residence time (A-VES). (3) The duty sheet section 2 carries no size line; the envelope is the node `size_mm` from AQS-DUTY-064, and the two stay consistent through the node, not through the sheet. (4) Schedule rating column (Class 150) against duty section 4 (atmospheric tube or hose): no flange is drawn; the reconciliation is the nozzle-schedule unit task. (5) Outlet datums z 600 sit at the envelope top; drawn clamped to 565 (DN50) / 581.5 (DN25) / 584.4 (DN15) by the barb-radius rule; the designed elevations are unit tasks 02/06. (6) Vent datum 800 predates the 600 box; the vent is drawn flush on the +Y rim bar. (7) The z 160 inlet roots are set in the sloped frustum wall; the plate cut angle at each set-in is the fabricator's. (8) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

Authority: `docs-authority/duties/CAST_LINE.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/CAST_LINE.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-DUTY-072 (sizing study), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node CAST_LINE (design block: pour_kg_d, moulds, table_L_mm).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `cast_line.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and builds headless with no manual step: frame weldment, mould-table deck and seats, pour trough, tail plate, guards, and the schedule hardware. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `connections` and `design` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1500 × 800 × 1500 (A-M-199 forming class; 1 h hold-up of the Cu pour is below the class minimum). The build fills it: measured 1500.0 × 800.0 × 1500.0 mm from the export STL (check.json `bbox_exact`), all connection hardware flush at the envelope faces, nothing protrudes.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid.
W4 Nozzles. All 6 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/CAST_LINE.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore connections (duty section 4; hose makeup by the connecting line at the face): N1 DN15 molten-metal row (from IND_MELT N1) enters on the −X face at z 160 into the under-deck refractory trough that bottom-fills both mould stations through risers; N2 solids foundry-scrap return leaves at the +Y guide rail, z 600, drawn at DN15 and labeled bore not calculated (W4 rule); N3/N4 solids billet rows to ROLL / EXTRUDE leave on the +X discharge lanes, z 600, bore not calculated; N5 DN15 trough drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N6 DN15 vent tap on the hood plenum, flush at the envelope top (its 800 datum is the schedule placeholder - hold, W12). The schedule face words are generic, so the compass assignment is the registered layout choice (params.json layout_note). No flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 12 M16 T-bolt-and-nut sets on the machine feet and mould-seat clamps, 6 M12 anchors, bench to slab (check.json `design_row_check` carries the count from the model). Both rows grade 8.8 carbon steel by the W5 grade rule. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication. The press-fitted trunnion and roller-shaft joints carry no fastener row (build_log joint convention).
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: graphite permanent moulds in steel backing platens (Cu pour, melt 1085 C per CRC_CU), graphite launder, steel tilt table; the frame weldment is carbon steel. CA 0 mm - dry foundry-floor service, no corrosive stream (AQS-STD-001 shop rule). The graphite hot faces are the 0-CA class.
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric machine; the duty section 6 relief case is pending (unit task 07) and no set point is claimed. Not to the room.
W8 Exports. `export/cast_line.step` (assembly); `export/cast_line.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/CAST_LINE.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1500.0 × 800.0 × 1500.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly, zero face overhang.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: frame weldment, mould-table deck, mould seats with steel backing platens, pour trough, tail plate, guards, and the schedule hardware. Buy: none - the machine is all in-house fabrication (the graphite mould inserts are the shop-made consumable per the duty section 3 alternative row).
W10 Fabrication route. Route hold (W12); operations named so the shop can bid it: plate and section cut, frame weldment (CS fillet welds, ISO 2553), table-deck drill and seat pin, graphite launder and mould-insert fit, roller and guide align, stub set-in, then level-and-shim at the feet, dry run, trial pour at the 3 kg/d class. On-site tool: the metal shop welding bay and the machine bay.
W11 Self-copy relevance. A-SC applies (tier 7): the line casts the Cu ingot/billet stock the shop machines and forms, which is the AQS-BOM-SC-001 feedstock path; the parts of the machine itself the on-site shop can make - frame weldment, deck, seats, trough, guards and connection hardware - are flagged make in the BOM and cross-referenced to AQS-BOM-SC-001. The graphite mould inserts are the plant-made consumable.
W12 Holds, named. (1) No AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 route class for class `caster`; the tracker carries the route hold and W10 names the operations anyway. (2) Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders, not designed elevations (duty section 4; unit tasks 02/06). (3) The N6 vent datum 800 is the schedule placeholder. (4) The three solids rows (N2, N3, N4) are drawn at DN15, bore not calculated (W4 rule). (5) The mould inserts, tilt actuator and guard interlocks are unit-task design items; the study geometry is the machine envelope per the node design block. (6) The relief case is pending (unit task 07). (7) Fastener lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication. (8) No instrument loop is issued for this unit yet (duty section 8). (9) No measured rate is claimed; the duty section 1 rates are graph study values.

# Work order INV_CAST

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-SC). Route hold: the node class `caster` has no row in the section 4 route table. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/INV_CAST.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/INV_CAST.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-DUTY-072 (sizing study), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node INV_CAST (design block: pour_kg_d, shell_kg_d, molds_per_campaign).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `inv_cast.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` and rebuilds every solid: frame, mould-clamp station, tilt-pour station, cast-out bed, and the schedule hardware. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `assumptions` and `design` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1500 × 800 × 1500 (A-M-199 forming class, live on IND_TO_INV, A-M-281). The build fills it: measured 1500.0 × 800.0 × 1500.0 mm, all connection hardware flush at the envelope faces, nothing protrudes.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The shell-mould stack in the clamp is drawn as a process-inventory solid, not a BOM part and not an internal.
W4 Nozzles. All 6 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/INV_CAST.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore connections (duty section 4; hose makeup by the connecting line at the face): N1 solids fired-shell inlet on the −X face at z 160, drawn at DN15 and labeled bore not calculated (W4 rule); N2/N3 DN15 melt rows (from IND_MELT N14, and the reactive melt from VAC_IND N3) set in through the −Y face at z 160 on pipe stands; N4 solids cast + shell out at the +X roll-off stop, z 600, bore not calculated; N5 DN15 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N6 DN15 vent on the table deck, flush at the envelope top (its 800 datum is the schedule placeholder - hold, W12). The schedule face words are mapped to compass faces by the registered layout choice (params.json layout_note). No flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M12 stud-and-nut sets on the mould-clamp station, 4 M12 anchors, frame to slab. Both rows grade 8.8 carbon steel by the W5 grade rule. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication. The press-fitted trunnion and roller joints carry no fastener row (build_log joint convention).
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: graphite crucible tilt-pour with the alumina shell as the mould hot face, steel table and tilt hardware; the frame and machine structure are carbon steel. CA 0 mm - dry foundry-floor service, no corrosive stream (AQS-STD-001 shop rule). The crucible is a buy row (consumable); everything structural is make.
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric shell-handling machine; the duty section 6 relief case is pending (unit task 07) and no set point is claimed. Not to the room.
W8 Exports. `export/inv_cast.step` (assembly); `export/inv_cast.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/INV_CAST.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1500.0 × 800.0 × 1500.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly, zero face overhang.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: frame, mould-clamp station, tilt-pour station, cast-out bed, shell-inlet chute, melt stubs and stands, drain and vent. Buy: none - the machine is all in-house fabrication (the crucible itself lives on the melt-side units, not here).
W10 Fabrication route. Route hold (W12); operations named so the shop can bid it: plate and section cut, frame weldment (CS, fillet welds), table-deck drill and pin, clamp-station fabricate and fit, roller-bed bore and press-fit, stub set-in and pipe stands, then clamp-pressure hold test and a trial pour from IND_MELT N14. On-site tool: the metal shop welding bay and the drill line.
W11 Self-copy relevance. A-SC applies (tier 7): the station casts the investment castings the plant needs for its own copies, so the cast-product tooling path is the AQS-BOM-SC-001 casting row; the parts of the station itself the on-site shop can make are the frame weldment, clamp station, tilt station, roller bed and connection hardware - flagged make in the BOM and cross-referenced to AQS-BOM-SC-001. The fired shells arrive from SHELL_BURNOUT and the moulds are the plant-made consumable (the shell train units).
W12 Holds, named. (1) No AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 route class for class `caster`; the tracker carries the route hold and W10 names the operations anyway. (2) Nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders, not designed elevations (duty section 4; unit tasks 02/06). (3) The N6 vent datum 800 is the schedule placeholder. (4) The two solids rows (N1, N4) are drawn at DN15, bore not calculated (W4 rule). (5) The mould seats, clamp force and tilt-actuator package are unit-task design items; the study geometry is the campaign envelope. (6) The relief case is pending (unit task 07). (7) Fastener lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication. (8) No instrument loop is issued for this unit yet (duty section 8). (9) No measured rate is claimed; the duty section 1 rates are graph study values.

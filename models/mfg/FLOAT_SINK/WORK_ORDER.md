# Work order FLOAT_SINK

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-VES). Route class F-VES. Graph Rev AC. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/FLOAT_SINK.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/FLOAT_SINK.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-STD-002 rules M1/M2/M5 (CPVC class), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node FLOAT_SINK.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `float_sink.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1000 × 600 × 800, whose `size_note` records it as the study registry box read back from the `cad/stl` M0 envelope; neither the node nor the duty sheet section 2 carries design sizing. The claimed envelope is that box and the build fills it: measured 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm, one closed manifold solid (hold, W12).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The separation hardware is drawn anyway because it is the unit (duty section 1: float plastics / sink grit split): a CPVC weir plate x 690..700 to z 700 dividing the sink zone (x 0..690) from the float zone, and the float launder at the +X wall — lip plate x 920..930 with its overflow at z 700 over a channel floor at z 640. Both are layout solids from the separation duty, not sized internals (holds, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 6 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/FLOAT_SINK.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore: N1 DN50 sink solids in on −Y at z 160; N2 DN50 liquor return, N3 DN25 float plastics and N6 solids (drawn at DN15, labeled bore not calculated) on +Y at z 600; N4 drain flush at z 0 with the boss to its 50 datum in the sink zone floor; N5 vent flush at the 800 top on the +Y rim bar. Atmospheric joints are hose barbs (duty section 4: atmospheric tube or hose, no flange class); no flange is drawn (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. Ports pack centred on the face mid-length per face/height group; the zoning against the weir is the fabricator's (W12).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M10 wedge anchors, tank floor to slab, one per corner (count from the model). Polymer joint to the supplier rule; A4-70 hardware per the observed house practice. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. CPVC wetted throughout (duty section 3, AQS-STD-002 rules M1/M2/M5): the sink feed arrives from LEACH_CSTR_1 at up to 85 °C, inside the CPVC 93 °C limit; no metal wetted part. Corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymer (AQS-STD-001 rule).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6). Hydrostatic head at the 720 mm working level is a shop figure only; no test pressure is claimed on this page.
W8 Exports. `export/float_sink.step` (assembly with the liquor inventory); `export/float_sink.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/FLOAT_SINK.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1000.0 × 600.0 × 800.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. Holdup 0.399 m³ at the 720 mm working level net of the weir and launder. Masses in `check.json`: tank 47.0 kg, rim frame 1.9 kg, weir 6.0 kg, launder 1.1 kg, hardware 0.6 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make: an all-welded CPVC fabrication.
W10 Fabrication route. F-VES: CPVC sheet cut, hot-gas / extrusion welds of floor, walls, weir and launder, set-in ports solvent-welded, rim frame welded and anchored, leak test of the shell. On-site tool: the thermoplastic shop (AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 F-VES operations).
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the separator splits leach solids; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The envelope is the study registry box (params `size_note`); no design sizing exists on the node or the duty sheet section 2 — the box is carried, nothing resized. (2) Wall thickness: no authority states one; 10 mm sheet is a study value against the 85 °C CPVC class, confirmed at fabrication. (3) No holdup basis (duty sections 2 and 8 analogues: "No holdup claimed"); the model volume is reported, not turned into a residence time (A-VES). (4) Weir position, weir top z 700, launder lip and channel floor are layout solids from the duty section 1 separation duty; the separation duty (grit settling vs plastics flotation) is not calculated anywhere and the node internals row is none. (5) Schedule rating column (Class 150) against duty section 4 (atmospheric tube or hose): no flange is drawn; the reconciliation is the nozzle-schedule unit task. (6) The +Y outlet row packs centred on the face mid-length (x 445..555, sink zone) while the launder serves the float zone; port zoning against the internals is a schedule-placeholder elevation/position question (unit tasks 02/06). (7) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

# Work order ACEX

Authority: AQS-DUTY-062 (X-MFG-31). In-house fabrication; no bought vessel.

1. Cut and roll the shell DN60, 3.91 mm, height 1205 mm; cut the bottom and lid discs (PP-H pipe Sch 40, socket-fused (DVS 2207-11)).
2. Weld the longitudinal seam and the bottom; weld the lid flange ring.
3. Cut and weld 5 DN25 nozzles: [Bmim]Cl in, eluate to neutraliser, NaOH regenerant in, spent regenerant out, drain.
4. Weld 3 legs; drill 8 lid bolt holes.
5. Load the resin, fit the second column and the neutraliser.
6. Test: hydrostatic fill to the brim, 24 h, no leak (DVS 2205).
7. Inspect against models/mfg/ACEX/export/acex.step; record dimensions on the traveller.

2. Make and fit item 2: Nozzles DN25: blackwater in, toilet solids in, digestate out, biogas out, gas recirc in (PP-H).
3. Make and fit item 3: Biogas recirculation diaphragm blower about 2 L/min (PP/EPDM).
4. Make and fit item 4: Heating mat 150 W, thermostat 55 C, two RTDs (silicone / 316L).
5. Make and fit item 5: PIR insulation 50 mm, PE cladding (PIR).
6. Make and fit item 6: Water seal and flame arrester on the biogas line to AFTERBURNER (316L).
7. Test: hydrostatic fill 24 h, no leak; gas-tightness of the lid at 5 kPa.

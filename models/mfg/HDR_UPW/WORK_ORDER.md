# Work order HDR_UPW

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-PIPE). Route class F-PIPE. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/HDR_UPW.md`, `docs-authority/sizing/HEADERS.md` (AQS-DUTY-075), `docs-authority/nozzles/HDR_UPW.csv`, `docs-authority/LINE_LIST.md` (AQS-PIP-004, the DN and class authority), `docs-authority/PIPING_CLASSES.md` (AQS-PIP-003), AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node HDR_UPW.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `hdr_upw.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` and, for the A-PIPE line list, the unit's edges in the graph and the issued AQS-PIP-004 rows; it rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Parameters. Envelope 2000 × 50 × 50 mm at origin 500, 21500, −6000 mm (placement seed), node size_note "DN25 manifold x 20 m run (0.004 m3), header sizing (AQS-DUTY-075)". design_kPa and relief_mm are null: the header is atmospheric (duty section 6).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid; none is drawn. The model is the spool set as the node defines it: one manifold run, drawn as a 2000 mm representative spool. The 20 m run is carried by the per-line route lengths of AQS-PIP-004, not redrawn.
W4 Nozzles. All 5 rows of `docs-authority/nozzles/HDR_UPW.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as open-bore tube stubs flush at the envelope faces (lib/nozzles.place_on_face). Atmospheric tube or hose per duty section 4; the W4 110 % rule draws no flange because no process pressure is stated. The schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. The UPW_TO_FAB edge (to the fab tank) carries no distinct schedule row; the N3 fab UPW branch serves the fab side; the issued schedule is drawn verbatim and no nozzle is invented. The along-run x positions are the registered layout choice (params layout.nozzle_spread).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M10 wedge anchors, grade 8.8 on the carbon-steel plinth (plan grade rule). Spool and branch joints are PVDF butt/IR fusion welds (P3 class sheet, AQS-PIP-003); there are no other bolted joints. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Manifold and branches PVDF SDR 21, fusion-welded (duty section 3; the P3 class sheet of AQS-PIP-003 is the PVDF class: ISO 10931, butt or IR fusion); corrosion allowance 0 mm (AQS-STD-001 polymer rule). Plinth carbon steel (AQS-MOD-002 dry service class).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric (duty section 6: no vent edge, relief not to the room). No test pressure is claimed.
W8 Exports. `export/hdr_upw.step` (assembly); `export/hdr_upw.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/HDR_UPW.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 2000.0 × 50.0 × 50.0 mm against the 2000 × 50 × 50 envelope: inside, filled exactly. Masses in `check.json`: plinth 15.7 kg, manifold 1.15 kg, branches 0.03 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make.
W10 Fabrication route. F-PIPE: spool cut (DN25 PVDF to the Sch 40 table; SDR 21 wall per the purchased pipe), branch fusion joints at the schedule tags, trough plinth weld and anchor, line list, leak test of the assembled run. On-site tool: the plastics shop; the line list and spool sheet travel with the spool.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the header moves water; it makes no plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The envelope is the AQS-DUTY-075 sizing box (DN25 manifold × 20 m run); the model draws a 2000 mm representative spool of it; per-line lengths stay in the line list. (2) The pipe schedule is not stated on the node or in the duty; the geometry is drawn on the lib/dn Sch 40 table and the node material carries SDR 21 — the purchased pipe's wall, confirmed at order. (3) The AQS-DUTY-075 ID grid (DN − 8 = 17 mm) and the Sch 40 bore (26.9 mm) disagree by the grid simplification; both seat DN25; nothing resized. (4) Schedule datums sit in a 50 mm cross-section: the DN25 inlet datum 10 is clamped up to 17.6 mm and the DN25 37 mm outlet datum is clamped down to 32.4 mm; designed elevations are unit tasks 02/06. (5) The UPW_TO_FAB edge carries no distinct schedule row; the N3 fab UPW branch serves the fab side; drawn verbatim, no nozzle invented. (6) The Class 150 rating column stands against an atmospheric duty; no flange is drawn (duty section 4, W4 110 % rule). (7) The issued line classes carry P5 (HDPE PE4710, AQS-PIP-003) for the water lines while the manifold material is PVDF SDR 21 (the P3 PVDF class): the PVDF-to-P5 transition joint at the header face is the connection detail, confirmed at fabrication. (8) Duty section 4 says "Atmospheric tube or hose" without fixing one; the model draws tube stubs flush at the faces and the hose makeup is the connecting spool's joint at the face. (9) The trough-mount support scheme (plinth, no top rails) is the layout choice carried from the HDR_CO2 acceptance; anchor lengths and the branch pitch are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

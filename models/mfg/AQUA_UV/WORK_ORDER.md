# Work order AQUA_UV

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-FARM; A-MACH practice on the purchased lamp). Route class F-FARM practice named as the study route (the `uv_sanitizer` class has no section 4 row - hold, W12). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/AQUA_UV.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/AQUA_UV.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), A-M-428 (254 nm dose class at the 0.04 kW lamp), A-M-427 (food-safety mode), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node AQUA_UV.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `aqua_uv.py` (CadQuery, on the shared close-out builder `models/mfg/farm_closeout.py`) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 400 × 350 × 750 - "inline 254 nm reactor tube + control box at the 0.04 kW lamp class (A-M-428) (X-FARM sec3)". The rebuild fills the box exactly: measured 400.0 × 350.0 × 750.0 mm, flush on all six faces (A-core 2).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The reactor train the duty names is drawn beside the cabinet: SS304 reactor tube OD 90 × 280 (wall 2 mm study) on the cabinet centreline at z 250, quartz sleeve OD 60 × 260 inside it, and the lamp as its envelope rod. The Oct-31 build drew the reactor as a rectangular sheet shell; the rebuild draws the tube the duty names. Sleeve and lamp diameters are study proportions of the tube class (hold, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 4 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/AQUA_UV.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore DN15 tube stubs flush at the envelope faces: N1 in on −Y at z 160 (nitrified water to the sanitizer); N2 out on +Y at z 600 (sanitized water to the growbeds); N3 drain flush at the envelope bottom; N4 vent flush at the envelope top - the schedule z 800 datum predates the 750 box (stale datum, hold W12). Atmospheric joints are tube/hose per duty section 4; no flange is drawn (W4 110 % rule - no process pressure is stated); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M8 cabinet base anchors (8.8 carbon steel per the grade rule) and 4 M6 reactor-tube saddle clamps (A4-70 on the SS304 joint per the grade rule). Counts are the model count / study pattern; lengths confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: SS304 reactor tube with a quartz UV sleeve, PP loop fittings, EPDM gasket class. The unit is normally bypassed, so wetted time is intermittent (duty basis). No carbon-steel wetted part; corrosion allowance does not apply (AQS-STD-001 rule). Sleeve and lamp masses are carried at the borosilicate table row until the supplier TDS (hold, W12).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric; duty section 6 states the relief case is pending and no set point is claimed. Instruments AQUA_UVUVT-01 (UV intensity), FIT/PT/TE are the duty section 5 loops (loop hardware, not modeled). No test pressure is claimed.
W8 Exports. `export/aqua_uv.step` (assembly, 4 solids); `export/aqua_uv.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/AQUA_UV.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 400.0 × 350.0 × 750.0 mm against the node envelope: flush, no misfit. Masses in `check.json`: cabinet shell 49.7 kg + top 5.6 kg, reactor tube 1.2 kg, quartz sleeve 0.2 kg, lamp envelope 0.5 kg, nozzles 0.2 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: cabinet, reactor tube, nozzle set (PP fittings stock). Buy: quartz sleeve (catalogue cut) and the UV-C lamp - named with the rating from the node (254 nm, 0.04 kW lamp class, A-M-428) and drawn as its envelope per plan rule 6.
W10 Fabrication route. F-FARM practice (plan section 4 F-FARM row: frame, liner, bed, drainage): SS304 sheet cut, folded and TIG welded (cabinet), tube rolled and seam welded with the stub set-in, gland fittings for the sleeve, lamp and sleeve purchased and fitted, then a water-fill leak test at the loop line. On-site tool: the sheet-metal bench with the farm fit-out crew; the sleeve and lamp are buy goods.
W11 Self-copy relevance. The sanitizer sanitizes water, not plant parts; A-SC does not apply (tier 9).
W12 Holds, named. (1) The `uv_sanitizer` class has no AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 route class; the F-FARM practice is the named study route. (2) Reactor tube diameter (OD 90) and wall (2 mm) are study proportions of the inline reactor class; the node names no tube bore. (3) Sleeve OD 60 and lamp OD 30 are study proportions; sleeve and lamp masses carry the borosilicate table row until the supplier TDS. (4) The N4 vent datum (z 800) predates the 750 box; drawn flush at the envelope top. (5) The dose class (30-100 mJ/cm2 at the 40 kg/d loop) is the node sizing stamp - reported, not recalculated. (6) Fastener lengths and the saddle pattern are study values, confirmed at fabrication. (7) AQUA_BYPASS is normally engaged; the sanitizer is the optional food-safety/contingency path (node design).

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-FARM). Route class F-FARM practice named as the study route (the `hive` class has no section 4 row - hold, W12). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/BEE_HIVE.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/BEE_HIVE.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), A-M-424 (hive basis), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node BEE_HIVE.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `bee_hive.py` (CadQuery, on the shared close-out builder `models/mfg/farm_closeout.py`) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 600 × 500 × 1100 - "10-frame deep + medium + stand (A-M-424; TECHTREE beekeeping)". The rebuild confirms the Oct-31 build as issued: measured 650.0 × 550.0 × 1150.0 mm against the node box - the MISFIT TO REPORT that section 7 carries. The overhang is the design: the stand laps the boxes by 25 mm a side (+50), the telescoping lid laps 20 mm a side (+40), and the box stack (stand 150 + deep 475 + medium 475 + screen 10 + lid 30) tops at z 1150. The node states no per-box heights, so fitting the stack under 1100 would invent dimensions - nothing is resized (plan rule 3; the CRYO_ASU section 7 pattern).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The hive is drawn as the unit: stand, 10-frame deep and medium boxes, SS316 screen bottom board, telescoping lid. Frames and foundation are the buy woodenware the size_basis names (10-frame class); their count inside the boxes is not stated on the node (hold, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 5 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/BEE_HIVE.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore DN15 tube stubs flush at the envelope faces - loop accounting rows on a hive (forage in, honey and evaporation out, drain, vent): N1 in on −Y at z 160; N2/N3 out on +Y at z 600; N4 drain flush at the envelope bottom; N5 vent flush at the envelope top - the schedule z 800 datum predates the 1100 box (stale datum, hold W12). Atmospheric joints per duty section 4; no flange is drawn (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 24 M6 box-assembly screws (A4-70 stainless per the grade rule; NO copper-bearing alloys - no brass screws - and no treated lumber anywhere on the hive, duty section 3) and 4 M10 stand-to-pad anchors (8.8 carbon steel per the grade rule; anchor is not hive material). Patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: untreated solid pine box bodies, SS316 screen bottom board, propolis-waxed interiors. The screen board is drawn as its plate solid; the mesh-class mass carries ~0.15 of the plate mass (hardware-cloth idiom). Corrosion allowance does not apply to the wood or the mesh at the dry hive class (AQS-STD-001 rule).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric; duty section 6 states the relief case is pending and no set point is claimed. Instruments BEE_HIVEPT-01/TE-01 are the duty section 5 loops (loop hardware, not modeled). No test pressure is claimed.
W8 Exports. `export/bee_hive.step` (assembly, 3 solids); `export/bee_hive.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/BEE_HIVE.stl` keeps the X-PAR-04 study envelope box (ASCII, metres; the registry gate: the box equals the node size_mm 600 × 500 × 1100 within 1 mm) - the full build lives in `export/` and the STEP, the misfit is section-7-reported (the GROW_BED registry treatment; no in-envelope module of this build fills the node box). Masses in `check.json`: stand 26.8 kg, deep box 71.3 kg, medium box 71.3 kg, lid 5.2 kg, screen board 24.0 kg plate-equivalent (3.6 kg mesh class), nozzles under 0.1 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: stand, boxes, lid, screen board, nozzle set. Buy: frames and foundation (deep + medium sets, the 10-frame class the size_basis names; plan rule 6; count hold, W12).
W10 Fabrication route. F-FARM practice (plan section 4 F-FARM row: frame, liner, bed, drainage): pine cut and screwed (boxes with hand joints, stand, lid), mesh stapled into the screen-board frame, propolis waxing of interiors at assembly, then a level-and-set on the stand. On-site tool: the wood bench with the farm fit-out crew; the frames and foundation are buy goods.
W11 Self-copy relevance. The hive makes honey and pollination, not plant parts; A-SC does not apply (tier 9).
W12 Holds, named. (1) The `hive` class has no AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 route class; the F-FARM practice is the named study route. (2) The assembly leaves the node box by the stand/lid laps and the box stack - the misfit is section-7-reported, nothing resized (plan rule 3; the node states no per-box heights). (3) The frame count inside the boxes is not stated on the node; the buy row carries the deep + medium sets (plan rule 6). (4) The N5 vent datum (z 800) predates the 1100 box; drawn flush at the envelope top. (5) The nozzle rows are loop accounting rows (forage, honey, evaporation) - they mark the loop edges, not hive hardware. (6) Fastener patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

# Work order LEACH_CSTR_1

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-VES). Route class F-VES. Graph Rev AC. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/LEACH_CSTR_1.md`, AQS-DUTY-050 (sizing and wall study, cited on duty section 2), AQS-DUTY-049 (HX-5 coil basis), `docs-authority/nozzles/LEACH_CSTR_1.csv`, AQS-STD-002 Rev F rules M2/M5 and section 3.3, AQS-STD-001, `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node LEACH_CSTR_1.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `leach_cstr_1.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The graph node carries no `size_mm` (tracker hold "no size on the node"). The claimed envelope is the duty section 2 box, 2000 × 1200 × 1400 mm (AQS-DUTY-050: working level 1100 mm, 2.14 m³ at 1100 kg/m³, residence 13.2 h on IA-EW-04). The build fills it: measured 2000.0 × 1200.3 × 1400.0 mm (the 0.3 mm is the tessellated-bbox gap the tracker's +1 mm gate allows). Tank body 1888 × 1080 inside the 56 mm X-end and 60 mm Y-face reserved bands; wall 16 mm, floor 16 mm CPVC (AQS-DUTY-050), lid 12 mm on a 40 mm flange (lid and flange are assumptions).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The named internals of duty sections 2 and 3 are drawn anyway because they are the unit: three FRP belts C203x56x6.4 at z 300/560/900 (AQS-DUTY-050 study), the HX-5 coil (4.22 m² borosilicate 3.3, 53.7 m of 25 × 3 mm tube, AQS-DUTY-049; drawn as its annulus envelope OD 925 / ID 575 × 600 on four PTFE pads, mass taken from the tube, not the envelope), and the agitator (4-blade 45° PBT D 450, 150 rpm, 403 W shaft; PVDF-sheathed D 50 shaft through the lid, solid PVDF impeller at z 250). The drive bridge sits about 400 mm above the lid, outside the envelope, and is not modeled.
W4 Nozzles. All 61 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/LEACH_CSTR_1.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore: inlets on −Y (32 at z 160, 4 at z 280), outlets on +Y (15 at z 600, 4 at z 1050), N9 drain flush at z 0 with the boss to its 50 datum, N10 vent flush at the lid at the 1400 datum. Atmospheric joints are hose barbs (duty sections 4 and 6); no flange is drawn anywhere because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. The solids rows (N12, N13, N18, N19, N20, N36) are drawn at DN15 and labeled bore not calculated. The along-face x positions are the registered layout choice (per tag in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`). The IA-E-LEACH conductor (duty section 4 prose; an electrical edge, so not a schedule row) is the DN15 gland boss on +Y at z 600, not a hose.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: the lid flange M12 PVDF bolts at 150 mm pitch, 36 off counted from the model. Non-metallic fasteners in the acid-gas head space (AQS-STD-002 M4). Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication. The belts are wrapped, not bolted; the coil pads sit loose; no other bolted joint exists in the model.
W6 Materials. CPVC wetted, per AQS-STD-002 Rev F rules M2 and M5: the leach liquor runs at 70–85 °C (GLOSSARY Leach/Salria; HX-5 basis 77.5 °C in AQS-DUTY-008), above the PVC-U limit of 60 °C and inside the CPVC limit of 93 °C. No metal wetted part. Corrosion allowance 0 mm (AQS-STD-001 polymer rule). HX-5 is a borosilicate glass 3.3 coil entering through CPVC stubs with FKM gaskets (AQS-STD-002 section 3.3); the Q3 metal piping ends at a dielectric isolating flange outside the tank. FRP belts vinyl ester (density 1900 kg/m³, study, pending the pultrusion vendor sheet).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, vented to NOX_SCRUB and FLUE_KO (duty section 6). Hydrostatic 11.9 kPa at the floor at the 1100 mm working level and 1100 kg/m³ (study). Wall panels 1.37 MPa maximum against 3.03 MPa CPVC allowable at 85 °C; belts 17.1 MPa against 50 MPa FRP, deflection L/490 (AQS-DUTY-050). The stored `design_kPa` 25.1 is the old head-plus-10 figure, not a set point. Liquor high-temperature trip 88 °C (assumption).
W8 Exports. `export/leach_cstr_1.step` (assembly with the liquor inventory); `export/leach_cstr_1.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/LEACH_CSTR_1.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 2000.0 × 1200.3 × 1400.0 mm against the duty envelope: inside. Holdup 1.855 m³ drawn net of the coil, pads and agitator at the 1100 mm working level, against the 2.14 m³ fill basis (AQS-DUTY-050). Masses in `check.json`: tank 291.6 kg, belts 394.0 kg (segmented, envelope-section rings), coil 31.6 kg from the tube, pads 2.5 kg, agitator 9.6 kg, ports 8.1 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Purchased rows (coil, gearmotor 0.75 kW, lid shaft seal, FKM gasket) carry the order-time mass note.
W10 Fabrication route. F-VES: CPVC sheet cut, hot-gas or extrusion weld of shell, floor and flange, set-in ports solvent-welded, lid bolted, belts bonded and segmented at the port rows, hydro or leak test of the shell. On-site tool: the thermoplastic shop. The coil, gearmotor and seal are bought in.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the CSTR leaches feedstock, it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) No `size_mm` on the graph node; the duty section 2 envelope (AQS-DUTY-050) is the claimed box and the tracker carries the hold until the node is sized. (2) The duty section 4 prose and the issued schedule disagree: the prose lists 46 rows (its N31 the IA-E-LEACH conductor), the issued CSV carries 61 rows and no conductor row; the model draws the CSV verbatim and the conductor separately as the +Y gland. The prose refresh is the duty generator's run. (3) Port bores are not calculated: the DN15/DN25/DN50 sizes of the schedule are carried verbatim. (4) The schedule rating column (Class 150) against duty sections 4 and 6 (atmospheric hose): no flange is drawn; the reconciliation is the nozzle-schedule unit task. (5) Schedule elevations 160/280/600 mm cross the belt bands at z 300/560/900; the belts are segmented with a notch at every crossing port (layout choice; belt segmentation detail is the fabricator's, X-MAT-21 coupon tests hold). (6) Belt z positions, section and allowable are the AQS-DUTY-050 study values pending the pultrusion vendor sheet. (7) Lid, flange, gasket (FKM per AQS-NZ-001), port pitch band and fastener lengths are study values. (8) In-house just-suspended speed test (Zwietering) on the agitator; the drive bridge and lid seal are outside the envelope and not modeled. (9) HX-5 coil is drawn as its annulus envelope; the helix arrangement (two concentric helices D 600/900, 11.5 turns at 50 mm pitch) is the study layout in `params.json` `hx5_coil`. (10) Liquor density 1100 kg/m³ and the 88 °C trip are study values, replaced by measurement.

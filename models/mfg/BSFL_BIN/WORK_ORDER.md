# Work order BSFL_BIN

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-FARM). Route class F-FARM practice named as the study route (the `bsfl_bin` class has no section 4 row - hold, W12). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/BSFL_BIN.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/BSFL_BIN.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), A-M-418 revision (8.21 kg/d wet feedstock), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node BSFL_BIN.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `bsfl_bin.py` (CadQuery, on the shared close-out builder `models/mfg/farm_closeout.py`) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 2500 × 900 × 700. The rebuild fills the box exactly: measured 2500.0 × 900.0 × 700.0 mm, flush on all six faces (A-core 2; A-FARM: not shrunk to fit the block).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid row - but the size_basis names the machine ("2 x 5 kg/d-class bins in one frame at the 8.21 kg/d wet feedstock") and duty section 3 names its parts: the centre divider is drawn (the two-bin split, even split study choice), one PP larval self-harvest ramp per bin leaning on the +X wall (foot at the floor, top at the rim; study layout), and an SS316 mesh top screen per bin whose open area is the ventilation path. No metal below the substrate line (duty section 3). No substrate depth is named on the node: the bins are drawn empty - larvae and substrate are process inventory, not BOM parts (the STOCK_OUT idiom; hold, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 12 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/BSFL_BIN.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore DN15 tube stubs flush at the envelope faces: inlets N1 (poultry manure), N2 (fish sludge), N6 (galley biowaste), N7 (duck manure), N11 (screenings/fines return) on −Y at z 160; outlets N3 (larvae meal), N4 (frass compost), N5 (bin respiration/evaporation), N10 (dried larvae meal), N12 (fresh larvae to the drier) on +Y at z 600; N8 drain flush at the envelope bottom; N9 vent flush at the envelope top - the schedule z 800 datum predates the 700 box (stale datum, hold W12). Atmospheric joints are tube/hose per duty section 4; no flange is drawn (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M6 screen-frame clamps (polymer supplier rule, A4-70 hardware; SS316 above the substrate line per duty section 3) and 4 M10 base anchors (8.8 carbon steel per the grade rule). Patterns are study values / the model count, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: HDPE tray bin, PP larval self-harvest ramp, SS316 mesh screen; no metal below the substrate line (frass acids at 65-75 % moisture, self-heating toward 40 C). Corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymers; the SS316 screen sits above the substrate line (AQS-STD-001 rule).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric; the bin respires through the screen open area. Duty section 6 states the relief case is pending and no set point is claimed. Instruments BSFL_BINPT-01/TE-01 are the duty section 5 loops (loop hardware, not modeled). No test pressure is claimed.
W8 Exports. `export/bsfl_bin.step` (assembly, 1 solid); `export/bsfl_bin.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/BSFL_BIN.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 2500.0 × 900.0 × 700.0 mm against the node envelope: flush, no misfit. Masses in `check.json`: basin 65.7 kg, divider 5.7 kg, ramps 11.6 kg, screens 5.2 kg mesh class, nozzles 0.1 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: basin, divider, ramps, nozzle set (HDPE/PP stock, thermoplastic welds). Buy: the SS316 hardware-cloth screens (cut-to-size panels, plan rule 6).
W10 Fabrication route. F-FARM practice (plan section 4 F-FARM row: frame, liner, bed, drainage): HDPE sheet cut and thermoplastic weld (basin walls, floor, divider), PP ramps welded in, mesh screens clamped to the rim after the water-fill leak test, ramps loaded last. On-site tool: the thermoplastic (polymer) bench with the farm fit-out crew; the screens are buy goods.
W11 Self-copy relevance. The bin makes larvae and frass, not plant parts; A-SC does not apply (tier 9).
W12 Holds, named. (1) The `bsfl_bin` class has no AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 route class; the F-FARM practice is the named study route. (2) No substrate depth is named on the node - the bins are drawn empty; the larvae/substrate inventory is process inventory (hold). (3) The even two-bin duty split is a study layout choice (the size_basis names the 2 × 5 kg/d class, not the split). (4) Ramp angle and foot position are study layout choices. (5) The N9 vent datum (z 800) predates the 700 box; drawn flush at the envelope top. (6) Fastener patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

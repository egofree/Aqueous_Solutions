# Work order BUCL

Authority: AQS-DUTY-062 (X-MFG-31). In-house fabrication; no bought vessel.

1. Cut and roll the shell DN50, 4.0 mm, height 50 mm; cut the bottom and lid discs (glass-lined carbon steel (ISO 28721)).
2. Weld the longitudinal seam and the bottom; weld the lid flange ring.
3. Cut and weld 5 DN25 nozzles: butanol in, HCl in, reflux condenser, product out, drain.
4. Weld 3 legs; drill 8 lid bolt holes.
5. Fit the heater mat, insulation and the reflux condenser.
6. Test: fill test 24 h and dye-penetrant on welds (ASME V); spark test of the lining (BUCL).
7. Inspect against models/mfg/BUCL/export/bucl.step; record dimensions on the traveller.

# Work order MEOH_FC

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-CELL). Route class F-CELL. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/MEOH_FC.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/MEOH_FC.csv`, `docs-authority/POWER.md`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node MEOH_FC (design block, AQS-DUTY-077).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `meoh_fc.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` and, for the stack construction figures, the node design block of the graph; it rebuilds every solid. The `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks of `params.json` are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1800 × 800 × 700 (set by `flow_graph/param/meoh_train.py`, AQS-DUTY-077). The build fills it: measured 1800.0 × 800.0 × 700.0 mm; the vent riser tops the box. The duty section 4 prose block and the node design note still carry the issued "1800 × 800 × 1600 mm" skid line - the node governs and the older figure is named (hold, W12).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine construction the node design block defines is drawn: the 28-cell laminate of the membrane-electrode assembly and the reformer catalyst bed are purchased items drawn as their envelopes and interfaces (plan rule 6), between the fabricated end plates and inside the 316L reformer shell.
W4 Nozzles. All 11 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/MEOH_FC.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube connections flush at the envelope faces (duty section 4: hose, atmospheric; the connecting line makes the joint at the face - the CATCH_TANK idiom): N1 MeOH fuel, N2 cell air, N3 tank draw and N4 leach condensate in on −Y at their 160 datum; N6 heat (less than 80 °C), N7 condensate, N8 CO₂ return and N11 cell exhaust out on +Y at their 600 datum; N9 drain boss under the reformer base head, flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N10 vent riser on the reformer top head, flush at the envelope top (the 800 datum is the schedule placeholder). Row N5 (MEOH DC) is the DC conductor: duty section 4 prose rates it "conductor, not a hose" - drawn as the copper bus-bar pad at the schedule face and height, run from the stack end plate; a conductor has no bore, so the size column is carried as the schedule placeholder (hold, W12). The 160 and 600 datums fit the 700 box and draw as issued. No flange hardware is drawn (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. The along-face positions are the registered layout choice (per tag in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M10 stud-and-nut tie rods (A4-70 on the 316L ends) compressing the stack plate pair against the PTFE gaskets; 6 M12 wedge anchors, grade 8.8, base plate to slab. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. 316L end plates and reformer shell; copper bus bars (duty section 3: "No wetted alloy. Conductors are copper."); PTFE gaskets; the membrane-electrode-assembly laminate and catalyst bed as purchased envelopes. CA does not apply (AQS-STD-001). The reformer shell wall 4 mm and the plate/pitch dimensions are study/layout values (hold, W12).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric (duty section 6: no vent edge, relief not to the room). The cell air is the blower loop hardware (duty section 4: the connecting line makes the joint at the face). Instruments are the duty section 5 loops (current, overcurrent opens the breaker), loop hardware, not modeled. No test pressure is claimed; the F-CELL route leak-tests the assembled stack and reformer (W10).
W8 Exports. `export/meoh_fc.step` (assembly); `export/meoh_fc.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/MEOH_FC.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1800.0 × 800.0 × 700.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. The skid chain fuses to one solid; the membrane-electrode-assembly laminate envelope sits at its gasket clearance between the end plates and the face stubs stay separate until the connecting lines make their joints (`check.json` `solids_note`).
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: end plates, bus bars, skid base, tube connections. Buy: the membrane-electrode-assembly laminate, PTFE gaskets, catalyst bed (envelopes and interfaces per plan rule 6).
W10 Fabrication route. F-CELL: tank fabrication (reformer shell), plate shear and punch (end plates), bus bar cut and drill (copper), gasket cut (PTFE), stack assembly; leak test of the assembled stack and reformer. On-site tool: the metal shop and the electrical bench.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the stack generates power; it makes no plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The issued "1800 × 800 × 1600 mm" skid line in the duty prose and the node design note predates the node envelope (AQS-DUTY-077 residual sizing); the node governs, nothing resized. (2) Duty section 2 carries no plate row: the A-CELL plate match is against the node design block (28 cells, 0.6 V at 0.3 A/cm², 200 cm² cells, 0.56 m², IEC 62282-3-100 module class); plate size 160 × 160 and pitch 6 mm are the registered layout choices enclosing the 141.4 mm active window. (3) POWER.md states no bus-bar section for this unit, so the A-CELL bus criterion has nothing to match; the bus is drawn copper per duty section 3 at the study 30 × 5 section. (4) Row N5 (MEOH DC) is the DC conductor (duty section 4 prose, "not a hose"): drawn as the copper bus pad; a conductor has no bore - the DN15 size column is the schedule placeholder. (5) The membrane-electrode-assembly laminate, PTFE gaskets and catalyst bed are purchased items drawn as envelopes (plan rule 6); ratings read the maker sheets at order. (6) The reformer shell OD 150 × wall 4 are study values (the node names the shell class, not the geometry). (7) The duty prose N2 (IA-E-MEIN) and N7 (S79) conductor rows predate the issued schedule; the schedule governs and the conductor hardware is the bus assembly. (8) The 800 vent datum is the schedule placeholder; the riser stands on the reformer head and flushes at the envelope top. (9) The skid chain, laminate envelope and face stubs are separate closed solids until assembly. (10) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

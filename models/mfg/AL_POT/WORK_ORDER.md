# Work order AL_POT

Authority: AQS-DUTY-072 (X-MFG-30 pattern). LIVE unit - D-DOCK feedstock program (A-M-284).

1. Fabricate the 800 x 800 x 1000 mm shell; line 70 mm (casing + ceramic fibre + ram) for 700 C service.
2. Set the 5 cathode plates on the -Y pocket wall (duty section 2 design row 'cathodes 5'; the former 4-plate step was corrected, see build_log.md); hang the 5 x D75 mm anodes at 400 mm immersion.
3. Fit the busbar ladder (975 mm2 Cu class for 1461 A) and the DC drops; torque and megger-check.
4. Mount the hood, DN150 offtake with dip leg, and the feed chute with rotary seal.
5. Wire the 7 kW rectifier; interlock hood draft, bath temperature and cover position.
6. Test: dry-out and melt-up curve, then trial electrolysis; route the off-gas per the Cl2 ruling (39.4192 kg/d Cl2 dock export at the CRU (A-M-280 ruling)).
7. Inspect against models/mfg/AL_POT/export/al_pot.step; record dimensions on the traveller.

## Holds (named, per AQS-PLAN-006 A-core 11)

- X-MAT-21 / A-M-318 pot-cell verification: first-article liner and anode change-out data, bath analysis at change-out, shell-liner inspection against the 10 mm design allowance. The lining thicknesses in this work order are study proportions until that campaign reports.
- Unit task 12 (first articles and the real BOM at order): clamp torque schedule, refractory campaign records, hood gasket set per AQS-STD-002 section 14.
- D-DOCK feedstock program (A-M-284): the unit is live on the ruling; import logistics are the feed gate for step 1 startup.
- Cl2 export accounting: 39.4192 kg/d dock export at the CRU per the A-M-280 ruling (step 6 routing); the water-treatment product store owns the downstream hold.

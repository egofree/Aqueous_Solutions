# Work order CRYO_CO2

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-COL). Route class F-COL. Graph Rev AC. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/CRYO_CO2.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/CRYO_CO2.csv`, the node design block (`flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node CRYO_CO2: rack 6696 × 1500 × 450, OD 114.3 × wall 13.49 × 6.096 m tubes, banks 4 tubes in 2 halves, steel_kg, relief_basis API 520/521 per half), AQS-STD-002, AQS-STD-001.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `cryo_co2.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `layout` and `study` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node `size_mm` [6696, 1500, 450] is the rack envelope set by `flow_graph/param/receivers.py` (A-M-167); the build fills it exactly (measured 6696.0 × 1500.0 × 450.1 mm) and `cad/stl/CRYO_CO2.stl` is replaced from the build.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`; the machine here is the storage bank itself: 4 cylinder tubes SA-312 TP316L seamless DN100 Sch 160 (OD 114.3 × t 13.49, 6.096 m), one per row at y 187.5/562.5/937.5/1312.5, bores drawn open, on 3 saddles each. Rows y < 750 are relief half 1, rows y > 750 are half 2 (2 tubes per half, matching the node fire case per-half exposed area of 4.378 m²). The 2 relief valves are purchased envelopes at the +X end posts (AQS-PLAN-006 rule 6).
W4 Nozzles. All 10 rows of `docs-authority/nozzles/CRYO_CO2.csv` are drawn at tag, face and bore on the rack side rails: N1–N3 (z 400) on the top rails, N10 (z 160) on the bottom rails, N4–N7 (schedule z 1500) clamped to the mid-rail centres, N8 drain as a skid-top stub with its tip at the 50 datum, N9 vent up the −Y tie bar to the 450 rack top. The schedule heights were issued against the 300 × 300 × 2000 study boxes that predate the A-M-167 rack envelope; heights that fit the rack are drawn as issued and every clamp is named in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`. Inlets −Y, outlets +Y (house face mapping). No flange is drawn anywhere: lib/dn.py carries no flange table above Class 150, the node design is 2200 kPa, and the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text - the reconciliation is a hold.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 32 M12 saddle bolts (8 per tube) and 8 M10 wedge anchors (skid to slab), ISO 3506 A4-70 per the plan grade rule on 316L. Counts are study values.
W6 Materials. 316L throughout (duty section 3: condensation, no carbon steel), corrosion allowance 0 mm (AQS-STD-001 rule). Skid 12 mm and SHS 80 × 80 × 5 frame sections are study sizes (no thickness is stated by any authority). The node material string's "O2 bank cleaned to ASTM G93" is the shared rack boilerplate; the O2-clean requirement belongs to CRYO_O2.
W7 Pressure. Design 2200 kPa g (node design_kPa, the receivers.py API 520 blocked-outlet case); relief per the node relief_basis: API 520 governing with the API 521 fire case per half bank, 2 valves × 9 mm bore (rounded from 8.818). The duty section 6 "atmospheric" text predates the receivers.py design and is superseded by the node relief_basis.
W8 Exports. `export/cryo_co2.step` (assembly); `export/cryo_co2.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/CRYO_CO2.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Masses in `check.json`: tubes 833.4 kg drawn wall mass (node steel_kg 690.07 is the sizing record - carried, not recalculated), frame 1470.2 kg (saddles priced once on BOM item 3: 12 at the drawn unit mass, 409.8 kg), relief valve envelopes 51.8 kg (envelope, not shipping mass), nozzles 2.2 kg. Store 88.5 kg CO2 at 200 bar (node storage block).
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Purchased rows (tubes, relief valves) carry the order-time mass note.
W10 Fabrication route. F-COL: SHS and plate cut and welded into the rack frame, saddles welded and bolted, tubes seated and strapped, connection stubs welded to the rails, relief valves fitted at the +X end, leak test of the bank. On-site tool: the metal shop; the seamless tubes and relief valves are bought in.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: a storage bank makes no plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The schedule rating column (Class 150) against the node design 2200 kPa: no flange is drawn and no flange table above Class 150 exists in lib/dn.py; the joint reconciliation is the nozzle-schedule unit task. (2) Schedule heights 1500/2000/50 predate the A-M-167 rack envelope (450 high); the clamped rows are named per tag in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn` and the datums stay in the schedule. (3) Drawn tube-wall mass 833.4 kg vs the node steel_kg 690.07: the node figure is the receivers.py sizing record and is carried; no recalculation. (4) Frame sections (skid 12, SHS 80 × 80 × 5) are study sizes pending the frame check task. (5) Tube-end fittings (B16.11 Class 9000 / cone-and-thread) are named, not drawn - the end-detail is the piping scope. (6) A-COL packing/distributor/demister: a cylinder bank has none; the bank itself is the machine. (7) Relief-valve make and model are purchase scope; the bore and case are from the node. (8) Interconnecting pigtails and headers between tubes are the piping scope (F-PIPE tier).

# Work order GLASS_DIGEST

Authority: AQS-DUTY-062 (X-MFG-31). In-house fabrication; no bought vessel.

1. Cut and roll the shell DN400, 4.0 mm, height 400 mm; cut the bottom and lid discs (CPVC sheet, rolled and extrusion-welded (DVS 2207-3)).
2. Weld the longitudinal seam and the bottom; weld the lid flange ring.
3. Cut and weld 5 DN25 nozzles: feed slurry in, NaOH in, liquor out, drain, vent.
4. Weld 4 legs; drill 12 lid bolt holes.
5. Fit the agitator and the Q3 coil.
6. Test: hydrostatic fill to the brim, 24 h, no leak (DVS 2205).
7. Inspect against models/mfg/GLASS_DIGEST/export/glass_digest.step; record dimensions on the traveller.

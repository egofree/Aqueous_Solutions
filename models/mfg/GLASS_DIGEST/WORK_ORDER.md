# Work order GLASS_DIGEST

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-VES). Route class F-VES. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/GLASS_DIGEST.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/GLASS_DIGEST.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-STD-002 M2 (thermoplastic class rules), DVS 2205/2207-3 (CPVC design and welding), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node GLASS_DIGEST (X-MFG-31 design block, `flow_graph/param/reactor_design.py`).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `glass_digest.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration. This build closes the Oct-31-era `reactors.py` study build (M2) per the HX8 close pattern: the issued construction carried through the current shared lib, the 12-row issued schedule replacing the 5-row study layout, and the section 5 artifacts completed.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 700 × 700 × 900 (set by `flow_graph/param/reactor_design.py`, X-MFG-31). The build fills it exactly: measured 700.0 × 700.0 × 900.0 mm, one closed manifold mesh. A four-post hold-down frame (40 × 40 posts + top rails, carbon steel A500) pins the registry box around the vessel — the HX8 idiom, so the X-PAR-04 envelope registry reads the build (the vessel itself is the OD 400 design construction and does not fill the box alone).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn as the unit: CPVC digestor shell OD 400 × wall 4 × H 400 with base head, bolted lid (flange ring OD 460 × 16), 4 CPVC legs 40 × 40 × 150, top-entry agitator (4-blade 45 deg PBT D133 at 358 rpm, PVDF, per the node design block) with a 0.09 kW purchased gearmotor envelope, and the Q3 PVDF DN15 coil (3 tube rings R 155) per the node design `heat` row.
W4 Nozzles. All 12 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/GLASS_DIGEST.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube connections from the shell to the envelope faces: N1/N2 DN15 (HX-6 Q3 process heat) and N3/N4 DN25 (burned cullet; digest NaOH 20 wt % — the N4 row was repaired to the schedule quoting, hold W12) and N12 DN15 (digest NaOH) on −Y; N5/N6 DN25 (glass and ceramic stock; silicate liquor), N7/N8 DN15 (silicate to slurry; digest reject) and N11 solids (glass/ceramic goods — non-piped transfer drawn DN15, bore not calculated, plan W4) on +Y; N9 DN15 drain boss flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N10 DN15 vent riser standing on the lid and flush at the envelope top (800 datum is the schedule placeholder). The schedule datums are the house placeholders (inlet 160, outlet 600) and predate the leg-mounted vessel: side rows draw clamped into the shell span [175, 525] (holds, W12). Atmospheric tube per duty section 4; no flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. The N1/N2 service text says jacket while the design `heat` row names the Q3 coil — the coil is the issued construction and the schedule text is carried verbatim (hold, W12).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 12 M10 × 40 lid bolts at the 100 mm flange pitch and 4 M10 wedge anchors at the leg feet, counts from the model. Polymer joints to the supplier rule (A4-70 hardware per house practice); lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Shell, lid, legs and nozzles CPVC (node design.material "CPVC (to 93 C; DVS 2205 allowable 3.0 MPa at 85 C)"; X-MFG-31). Duty section 3 names 316L — the conflict is carried as a hold (W12): the issued X-MFG-31 construction for this unit is the CPVC digestor and nothing is resized; the duty 316L row is the generic vessel selection template. Impeller and shaft sleeve PVDF per design.material; gearmotor envelope on a carbon-steel frame. CA 0 mm (AQS-STD-001 polymer rule).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6). Operating 85 C at the design T_C; the DVS 2205 allowable at 85 C is 3.0 MPa against a static head of less than 0.005 MPa. No test pressure is claimed beyond the route hydrostatic fill.
W8 Exports. `export/glass_digest.step` (assembly); `export/glass_digest.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/GLASS_DIGEST.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 700.0 × 700.0 × 900.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly (hold-down frame). Masses in `check.json`: frame 76.4 kg, legs 1.5 kg, shell 3.8 kg, lid 1.1 kg, agitator 0.4 kg, motor 2.8 kg, coil 1.9 kg, nozzles 0.7 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make rows: frame, shell, lid, legs, agitator, coil, nozzles. Buy row: the 0.09 kW gearmotor (purchased envelope per plan rule 6, rating from the node).
W10 Fabrication route. F-VES: CPVC sheet cut and rolled, extrusion weld of the longitudinal seam, base head and lid flange (DVS 2207-3), nozzle set-in, coil support drilling, hydrostatic fill to the brim 24 h no leak (DVS 2205 test practice carried from the issued construction). On-site tool: the thermoplastic shop.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the digestor conditions cullet; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) Duty section 3 names 316L while the node design.material names CPVC (X-MFG-31): the issued construction is drawn and the conflict is carried, nothing resized. (2) The N4 schedule row was repaired to the schedule quoting (unquoted comma in "digest NaOH, 20 wt%" shifted face and height; change record in `notes/glass_oilgas_m3_2026-11-02.md`). (3) The schedule datums (inlet 160, outlet 600, drain 50, vent 800) are placeholders that predate the leg-mounted vessel: side rows draw clamped into the shell span [175, 525], the vent riser flushes at the envelope top, the drain at the bottom. (4) The N1/N2 service text says jacket; the design heat row names the Q3 coil — coil drawn, text carried verbatim. (5) Leg height 150 mm is a layout choice carried from the issued construction; the node names no support. (6) The N11 solids row is a non-piped transfer drawn DN15, bore not calculated (plan W4). (7) Fastener lengths and the lid bolt pattern pitch are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

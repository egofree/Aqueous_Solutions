# Work order FAB_UPW_TANK

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-VES). Route class F-VES. Graph revision per `params.json` `graph_revision`. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/FAB_UPW_TANK.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/FAB_UPW_TANK.csv`, `docs-authority/fab/FAB_UPW_TANK.md` (AQS-MFG-001 study page: dip pipe, stilling well), the node design_storage block (AQS-DUTY-064), AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node FAB_UPW_TANK.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `fab_upw_tank.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 600 × 600 × 600, set by docs-authority/_storage_sizing.py (AQS-DUTY-064); the node design_storage block books the nominal storage basis (hold_d 1, V_nom_m3 0.02, D 300, H 300, wall_mm 4.75, PE). The duty sheet section 2 carries a 7-day figure (0.4200 m³) that exceeds both the nominal block and the node box - the conflict is a named hold, nothing resized (W12). The build fills the node box: measured 600.0 × 600.0 × 600.0 mm, one closed manifold solid.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The AQS-MFG-001 study page issues the internals: dip pipe OD 60 × wall 4 hung from the top head to z 100, inside a stilling well OD 110 × wall 5 from the floor to above the working level, exactly as CATCH_TANK (UPW feeds down the dip pipe, the well damps the inlet jet for the level instruments). Dimensions are study values - the study page names the internals, no authority sizes them (holds, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 4 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/FAB_UPW_TANK.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore: N1 fab UPW in on −Y at z 160, N2 class outlet on +Y at z 600 clamped to 584.4 by the barb-radius rule (hold, W12), N3 drain boss on the base head to its 50 datum, N4 vent flush at the top head - its 800 datum predates the 600 box (hold, W12). Atmospheric joints are hose barbs (duty section 4); no flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. Per-tag positions in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M10 wedge anchors, plinth to slab, one per corner (count from the model). A4-70 hardware by the polymer-joint supplier rule. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication. The welded joints carry no fastener row.
W6 Materials. PE (duty section 3 / node design_storage: "PE (rotomoulded / fabricated)"). Carried at the lib HDPE class row (950 kg/m³); tables/materials.csv has no separate PE tank row, and the supplier TDS confirms the class at order (hold, W12). Corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymer (AQS-STD-001). Wall 4.75 mm is the node design_storage booking (AQS-DUTY-064 nominal basis); the fab study page's "10 mm on polymer" predates the node booking (hold, W12).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6). Not to the room. Level and temperature are the duty section 5 instruments (high level trips the inlet; loop hardware, not modeled). No hydrotest pressure is claimed; the leak test is a shop discretion of the route (W10).
W8 Exports. `export/fab_upw_tank.step` (assembly); `export/fab_upw_tank.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/FAB_UPW_TANK.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 600.0 × 600.0 × 600.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. Liquor holdup at the working level net of the internals reported in `check.json`.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make: shell, well, dip pipe and hardware are in-house fabrication.
W10 Fabrication route. F-VES: PE sheet cut, thermoplastic (extrusion) weld of shell, heads and set-in internals, nozzle set-in welded, anchors drilled, leak test of the shell. On-site tool: the thermoplastic shop (AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 F-VES operations).
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the tank stores fab UPW; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The duty section 2 seven-day figure (0.4200 m³) exceeds the node box (0.216 m³) and the design_storage nominal (0.02 m³) - the node governs, the conflict held, nothing resized. (2) The wall: node design_storage books 4.75 mm; the fab study page says 10 mm on polymer - the node booking governs, the older figure named. (3) The PE class is carried at the lib HDPE row; the supplier TDS confirms at order. (4) The dip pipe and stilling well dimensions are study values. (5) The N2 outlet datum 600 clamps to 584.4 by the barb-radius rule. (6) The N4 vent datum 800 predates the 600 box; the vent is drawn flush at the top head. (7) Schedule rating column (Class 150) against the atmospheric hose duty: no flange is drawn. (8) The duty section 8 records the vessel is not built - the model holdup is reported, not a measured volume, and no residence time is derived (A-VES). (9) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

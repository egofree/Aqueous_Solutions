# Work order DUCK_PEN

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-FARM). Route class F-FARM practice named as the study route (the `pen` class has no section 4 row - hold, W12). Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/DUCK_PEN.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/DUCK_PEN.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), A-M-421 (6-duck pen basis), AQS-MOD-002 (frame service classes), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node DUCK_PEN.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `duck_pen.py` (CadQuery, on the shared close-out builder `models/mfg/farm_closeout.py`) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 2500 × 1300 × 1300 - "6 ducks x 0.3 m2 + wallow strip (A-M-421; TECHTREE ducks.md)". The rebuild fills the box exactly: measured 2500.0 × 1300.0 × 1300.0 mm, flush on all six faces (A-core 2; A-FARM: not shrunk to fit the block).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid row - the frame is drawn as the unit (four 40 × 40 legs to z 1200, drop board at z 100, wire floor at z 300, hardware-cloth panel bands at z 300..550 and z 1050..1300, sheet roof at z 1200), and the wallow strip the size_basis names is drawn as the liner duty section 3 specifies: an EPDM patch 800 × 600 × 5 on the drop board at the −X end ("permanently wet pen with a wallow: standing water governs"). Patch position and size are study layout choices (hold, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 9 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/DUCK_PEN.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore DN15 tube stubs flush at the envelope faces: inlets N1 (drinking/washing water), N5 (fresh duckweed forage), N9 (pressed duckweed mash) on −Y at z 160; outlets N2 (eggs to the food store), N3 (manure to the larvae bin), N4 (pen evaporation), N6 (wash water) on +Y at z 600; N7 drain flush at the envelope bottom over the wallow end; N8 vent flush at the envelope top - the schedule z 800 datum predates the 1300 box (stale datum, hold W12). The duty notes the elevations are schedule placeholders (unit tasks 02/06); the pen wall is hardware cloth, so the wall backing for each stub is a fabrication detail (hold, W12). Atmospheric joints are tube/hose; no flange is drawn (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M10 base anchors (8.8 carbon steel per the grade rule) and 8 M6 wallow batten screws (polymer supplier rule, A4-70). Patterns are the model count / study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Duty section 3: hot-dip galvanised steel frame and hardware cloth, EPDM liner in the wallow, SS316 waterers. The permanently wet class governs; corrosion allowance on the galvanised frame is the galvanizing itself (AQS-STD-001 shop rule). Hardware cloth drawn as plate solids with the open-area idiom (~0.15 of the plate mass). The SS316 waterers have no rating on the node: not drawn (hold, W12).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric; the pen vents at the top and respires through the cloth. Duty section 6 states the relief case is pending and no set point is claimed. Instruments DUCK_PENPT-01/TE-01 are the duty section 5 loops (loop hardware, not modeled). No test pressure is claimed.
W8 Exports. `export/duck_pen.step` (assembly, 10 solids); `export/duck_pen.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/DUCK_PEN.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 2500.0 × 1300.0 × 1300.0 mm against the node envelope: flush, no misfit. Masses in `check.json`: frame legs 60.3 kg, drop board 76.5 kg, cloth floor and panels 53.1 kg plate-equivalent, roof 76.5 kg, wallow liner 2.8 kg, nozzles 0.6 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: frame, drop board, nozzle set. Buy: hardware cloth (cut-to-size panels) and the EPDM wallow liner sheet (plan rule 6).
W10 Fabrication route. F-FARM practice (plan section 4 F-FARM row: frame, liner, bed, drainage): frame weldment (legs, roof rail), cloth panels cut and fixed, drop board fitted, wallow liner laid and battened at the −X end, then a wash-down check at the wallow drain line. On-site tool: the farm frame weldment bench (AQS-MOD-002 farm frame class); cloth and liner are buy goods.
W11 Self-copy relevance. The pen makes eggs and manure, not plant parts; A-SC does not apply (tier 9). The frame weldment is itself a shop product class (AQS-MOD-002).
W12 Holds, named. (1) The `pen` class has no AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 route class; the F-FARM practice is the named study route. (2) The wallow patch position and size are study layout choices (the size_basis names the strip, not its geometry). (3) The SS316 waterers have no rating on the node - not drawn. (4) The nozzle elevations are schedule placeholders (duty tasks 02/06); the cloth wall backing for each stub is a fabrication detail. (5) The N8 vent datum (z 800) predates the 1300 box; drawn flush at the envelope top. (6) Fastener patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

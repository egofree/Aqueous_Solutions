## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `hdr_o2.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` and, for the A-PIPE line list, the unit's edges in the graph and the issued AQS-PIP-004 rows; it rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Parameters. Envelope 2000 × 50 × 50 mm at origin 2800, −2700, −6000 mm (placement seed), node size_note "DN25 manifold x 20 m run (0.004 m3), header sizing (AQS-DUTY-075)". design_kPa and relief_mm are null: the header is atmospheric (duty section 6). The seed placement defines one grid cell; there are no separate leg nodes for this header on the graph (checked), so the spool set is the one manifold run.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid; none is drawn. The model is the spool set as the node defines it: one manifold run, drawn as a 2000 mm representative spool. The 20 m run is carried by the per-line route lengths of AQS-PIP-004, not redrawn.
W4 Nozzles. All 12 rows of `docs-authority/nozzles/HDR_O2.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as open-bore tube stubs flush at the envelope faces (lib/nozzles.place_on_face). Atmospheric per duty section 4 ("No flange class. Atmospheric."); the W4 110 % rule draws no flange because no process pressure is stated. The schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. N8 carries DN50: the hardware (OD 60.3) is larger than the 50 mm cross-section, so the row is drawn at the manifold centreline and the mesh leaves the envelope in z — the misfit is reported per plan rule 3 and nothing is resized. N11 carries "solids" (oxygen return to the ASU): drawn DN15 and labeled bore not calculated (plan W4). The along-run x positions are the registered layout choice (params layout.nozzle_spread).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M10 wedge anchors, grade 8.8 on the carbon-steel plinth (plan grade rule). Spool and branch joints are 316L welds, socketless per duty section 3; there are no other bolted joints. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Manifold and branches 316L tube, oxygen-clean per ASTM_G93 (duty section 3; M1 class, AQS-PIP-003: ASTM A312 pipe with A403 fittings; O2 lines degreased and bagged, PTFE seats, no oil per AQS-STD-002); corrosion allowance 0 mm (AQS-STD-001 rule). Plinth carbon steel (AQS-MOD-002 dry service class).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric (duty section 6: no vent edge, relief not to the room). The vent N7 returns to the loop suction per duty section 4. No test pressure is claimed.
W8 Exports. `export/hdr_o2.step` (assembly); `export/hdr_o2.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/HDR_O2.stl` kept as the envelope registry at the node size (X-PAR-04 gate: the registry bbox matches size_mm within 1 mm), because the accepted build carries the reported DN50 cross-section misfit; the build mesh lives in `export/` (ASCII, metres). Bounding box 2000.0 × 50.0 × 60.3 mm against the 2000 × 50 × 50 envelope: the z reading is the DN50 branch hardware (W4 and W12); the misfit is reported, nothing resized. Masses in `check.json`: plinth 15.7 kg, manifold 5.15 kg, branches 0.37 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make.
W10 Fabrication route. F-PIPE: spool cut (DN25 316L tube to the Sch 40 table), branch set-in welds at the schedule tags, trough plinth weld and anchor, line list, leak test of the assembled run; all parts degreased and bagged per ASTM G93 before assembly. On-site tool: the metal shop; the line list and spool sheet travel with the spool.
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the header moves gas; it makes no plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The envelope is the AQS-DUTY-075 sizing box (DN25 manifold × 20 m run); the model draws a 2000 mm representative spool of it; per-line lengths stay in the line list. (2) The pipe schedule is not stated on the node or in the duty; the geometry is drawn on the lib/dn Sch 40 table and the M1 class sheet carries the schedule at order. (3) The AQS-DUTY-075 ID grid (DN − 8 = 17 mm) and the Sch 40 bore (26.9 mm) disagree by the grid simplification; both seat DN25; nothing resized. (4) Misfit reported (plan rule 3): the DN50 row N8 (OD 60.3) cannot sit inside the 50 mm cross-section; drawn at the manifold centreline, the assembly mesh reads z 60.3 against the 50 mm envelope; the section 7 row carries the misfit; nothing is resized — the envelope and the DN50 bore are changed only by a plan line that allows it. (5) Schedule datums sit in a 50 mm cross-section: the DN25 inlet datum 10 is clamped up to 17.5 mm, the DN25 37 mm outlet datum is clamped down to 32.4 mm, and the 160 mm and 600 mm placeholder datums are clamped; designed elevations are unit tasks 02/06. (6) The Class 150 rating column stands against an atmospheric duty; no flange is drawn (duty section 4, W4 110 % rule). (7) Duty section 4 says "Atmospheric" without fixing tube or hose; the model draws tube stubs flush at the faces and the hose makeup is the connecting spool's joint at the face. (8) The trough-mount support scheme (plinth, no top rails) is the layout choice carried from the HDR_CO2 acceptance. (9) Anchor lengths and the branch pitch are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

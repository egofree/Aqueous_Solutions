# Work order ROOF_TANK

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-VES). Route class F-VES. Graph revision per `params.json` `graph_revision`. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/ROOF_TANK.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/ROOF_TANK.csv`, `docs-authority/fab/ROOF_TANK.md` (AQS-MFG-001 study page: dip pipe, stilling well), duty section 3 (PVDF air coil, AQS-STD-002 section 10), AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-STD-002 rule M1 (PVC-U or HDPE water tanks), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node ROOF_TANK.

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `roof_tank.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 649 × 520 × 748, whose `size_note` records it as the study default envelope box read back from the `cad/stl` registry; the duty sheet carries no size line. The build fills it: measured 649.0 × 520.0 × 748.0 mm, one closed manifold solid (holds, W12).
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The body is the machine: a rectangular welded-plate PVC-U tank - the HOPPER/FLOAT_SINK house idiom for plate tanks (the study fab page: "Shell 6 mm on metal, 10 mm on polymer. Shop weld.") - floor and walls 10 mm inside the envelope with a 20 mm hold-down rim frame closing the top; plate walls put the root of every face port on the wall at any position along the full 649 mm chord (a round shell would leave the outer ports of the chord-packed rows floating beyond the shell projection). The AQS-MFG-001 study page issues the internals: dip pipe OD 60 × wall 4 hung from the rim bars, inside a stilling well OD 110 × wall 5 from the floor to above the working level, exactly as CATCH_TANK. Duty section 3 books the PVDF air coil ("IA-POT-01 is cooled to 40 °C by a passive PVDF air coil, AQS-STD-002 section 10"): drawn as a PVDF OD 16 helix, 4 turns at a 300 mm mean diameter, z 80..320, 0.1896 m² of tube area REPORTED AS DRAWN - no authority sizes the coil, and AQS-STD-002 section 10 Rev J records the IA-POT-01 trim superseded by X-RSZ-06 with "No coil": the duty sheet section 3 text stands as the wetted-basis authority (plan rule 5) and the conflict is a named hold (W12). All internal dimensions are study values (holds, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 26 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/ROOF_TANK.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore: 9 service rows in on −Y at z 160 (incl. N18, a solids row drawn at DN15 and labeled bore not calculated), 15 out on +Y at z 600, N9 drain boss on the tank floor to its 50 datum, N10 vent on the +Y rim bar flush at the top - its 800 datum predates the 748 box (hold, W12). The two 160/600 rows pack the full face chord; the outer DN15 barbs sit near-tangent to the shell (named hold, W12, the FLOAT_SINK idiom). Atmospheric joints are hose barbs (duty section 4); no flange is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. Per-tag positions in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M10 wedge anchors, plinth to slab, one per corner (count from the model). A4-70 hardware by the polymer-joint supplier rule. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication. The welded joints carry no fastener row.
W6 Materials. PVC-U drawn (duty section 3: "PVC-U or HDPE, per AQS-STD-002 rule M1"); the hot draw at up to 85 °C is cooled at the coil line, not the shell (duty section 3). Coil tube PVDF. The unresolved HDPE alternative is a named hold (W12). Corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymer (AQS-STD-001). Shell wall 10 mm per the study fab page; no thickness is booked on the node (hold, W12).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6). Not to the room. Level and temperature are the duty section 5 instruments (high level trips the inlet; loop hardware, not modeled). No hydrotest pressure is claimed; the leak test is a shop discretion of the route (W10).
W8 Exports. `export/roof_tank.step` (assembly); `export/roof_tank.stl` closed and manifold, one solid; `cad/stl/ROOF_TANK.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 649.0 × 520.0 × 748.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. Liquor holdup at the working level net of the internals and coil reported in `check.json`.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. All rows make: shell, well, dip pipe, coil and hardware are in-house fabrication.
W10 Fabrication route. F-VES: PVC-U sheet/pipe cut (saw), thermoplastic weld of shell, heads and set-in internals, coil formed from PVDF tube and welded to its stubs, nozzle set-in welded, anchors drilled, leak test of the shell. On-site tool: the thermoplastic shop (AQS-PLAN-006 section 4 F-VES operations).
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the tank stores and distributes roof water; it does not make plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The envelope is the study registry box (params `size_note`); the duty sheet carries no size line and the fab study page a generic 1000 × 600 × 800 - the node governs, nothing resized. (2) The duty sheet section 2 two-minute figure (0.2783 m³ at 200 t/d circulation) is not a designed volume; the model holdup is reported, not turned into a residence time (A-VES). (3) Shell wall 10 mm is the study fab-page value; no thickness is booked on the node. (4) The dip pipe and stilling well dimensions are study values. (5) The coil is duty-booked (section 3) but un-sized, and AQS-STD-002 section 10 Rev J records the trim superseded by X-RSZ-06 - the sheet text stands per plan rule 5, the conflict held; the drawn coil area is reported, not called calculated. (6) The PVC-U-or-HDPE conditional of duty section 3 is unresolved; PVC-U is drawn. (7) The N10 vent datum 800 predates the 748 box; the vent is drawn flush at the top head. (8) Schedule rating column (Class 150) against the atmospheric hose duty: no flange is drawn. (9) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

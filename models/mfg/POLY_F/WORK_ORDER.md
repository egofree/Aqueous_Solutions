# Work order POLY_F

Authority: AQS-DUTY-072 (X-MFG lane). LIVE unit - fluoropolymer kettle.

1. Fabricate the Hastelloy kettle and dished top; PTFE-gasket the flange.
2. Fit the jacket and the support skirt on the base plate.
3. Fit the agitator shaft, impellers and drive; megger the motor.
4. Fit the N1/N2 monomer stubs, N3/N4 discharge chutes, N5/N6 liquor stubs, N7 drain and N8 vent.
5. Test: pressure/leak hold per the duty sheet, then a water batch trial at 60 C.
6. Inspect against models/mfg/POLY_F/export/poly_f.step; record dimensions on the traveller.

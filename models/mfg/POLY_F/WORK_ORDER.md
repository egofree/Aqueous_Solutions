# Work order POLY_F

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-VES). Route class F-VES. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/POLY_F.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/POLY_F.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node POLY_F (design block, AQS-DUTY-073; envelope basis A-M-289).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `poly_f.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` and, for the kettle construction figures, the node design block of the graph; it rebuilds every solid. It replaces the pre-plan M2 study builder of the same name (the superseded M2 constants block and layout note are dropped from `params.json`; the lib/dn table replaces them). The `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 600 × 600 × 1200 (the reactor-class study minimum: jacketed kettle at the 0.25 kg/d monomer duty, reclaim loop to F_RECOVER, A-M-289). The build fills it: measured 600.0 × 600.0 × 1200.0 mm; the vent riser tops the box (the X-PAR-04 registry gate). The M2 build's +Y overhang (bbox 630 against the 600 envelope) is gone: the hardware now sits flush at the envelope faces and the envelope itself is unchanged - nothing resized.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The node power basis (the 0.0449 kW stamp covers jacket loss + agitator, A-M-289) is drawn: the jacket as its envelope annulus, the agitator as solids (shaft, two-blade impeller) into the purchased drive envelope (plan rule 6); the impeller geometry and rpm are study values (hold, W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 8 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/POLY_F.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric open-bore tube connections flush at the envelope faces (duty section 4: hose, atmospheric; the connecting line makes the joint at the face - the CATCH_TANK idiom): N1 VDF and N2 TFE monomer in on −Y at their 160 datum; N3 (PVDF/PTFE resin) and N4 (fluoropolymer powder) out on +Y at their 600 datum - both rows carry the line-list classification `solids` (non-piped transfer) in their size column: drawn at DN15, bore not calculated, per plan W4, and labeled; N5 (PVDF resin to the film/sheet line) and N6 (off-spec scrap/rework) out on +Y at their 600 datum; N7 drain boss under the base head, flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N8 vent riser on the top head, flushing at the envelope top (the 800 datum is the schedule placeholder - hold, W12). No flange is drawn (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text. The along-face positions are the registered layout choice (per tag in `check.json` `nozzles_drawn`).
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 4 M12 wedge anchors, grade 8.8, plinth to slab, one per corner. Welded joints carry no fastener row. Lengths are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Kettle and agitator Hastelloy C-276 wetted, PTFE gasketed, per duty section 3 (the node design row matches). Density 8900 kg/m3 (published midpoint) for mass reporting. The AQS-STD-001 rule has no C-276 row: no corrosion-allowance value is claimed - the class sheet carries the allowance at order (hold, W12). Shell wall 6 mm is the study fab-page value - the node books no thickness (hold, W12). Jacket envelope and stand carbon steel.
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric (duty section 6: relief case study pending, unit task 07; no set point claimed, not to the room). No test pressure is claimed; the leak test is a shop discretion of the route (W10). No instrument loop yet (unit task 08) - named on the duty sheet. The 60 C duty and the initiator class (persulfate, emulsion/suspension, ULLMANN_FLUOROPOLY) are the node's operating rows.
W8 Exports. `export/poly_f.step` (assembly); `export/poly_f.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/POLY_F.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 600.0 × 600.0 × 1200.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly (the M2 misfit is cleared, the envelope unchanged). The skid chain fuses to one solid; the face stubs stay separate until the connecting lines make their joints (`check.json` `solids_note`). A-VES: the gross shell volume (3.6 L) is reported for the record; the duty claims no holdup figure and no residence time is derived.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make: kettle, jacket envelope, agitator, stand, connections. Buy: the agitator drive (envelope and interface per plan rule 6).
W10 Fabrication route. F-VES: plate cut, roll, GTAW per ASME IX practice (C-276 weld practice), nozzle set-in, jacket closure, leak test; stand weldment and anchors. On-site tool: the metal shop (C-276-qualified welders and procedure).
W11 Self-copy relevance. None: the kettle makes fluoropolymer resin; it makes no plant parts. No AQS-BOM-SC-001 cross-reference.
W12 Holds, named. (1) Shell wall 6 mm is the study fab-page value; the node books no thickness. (2) The AQS-STD-001 rule has no C-276 row: no corrosion-allowance value is claimed. (3) The jacket envelope geometry (OD 184 / ID 162 annulus) is the layout choice; the node power basis (A-M-289) names jacket loss + agitator without jacket geometry. (4) The agitator geometry (impeller diameter, immersion, rpm) is study; the node power basis names the agitator share of the 0.0449 kW stamp only. (5) The N3/N4 solids rows carry `solids` (non-piped transfer): drawn DN15, bore not calculated, per plan W4. (6) The schedule datums 160/600 are placeholders pending vessel design (unit tasks 02/06); they draw as issued at the envelope faces. (7) The vent riser flushes at the envelope top; the 800 datum is the schedule placeholder. (8) The duty claims no holdup figure: the gross shell volume is reported for the record, no residence time derived (A-VES). (9) Relief case (unit task 07) and instrument loop (unit task 08) are open on the duty sheet; nothing invented here. (10) Fastener lengths and anchor embedment are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

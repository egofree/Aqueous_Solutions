# Work order BLOW_MOLD

Plan AQS-PLAN-006 (section 2 work order; section 5 acceptance A-core + A-SC, route hold). Route hold: the `former` class is not named in any section 4 route row — A-core and the tier-7 A-SC set apply, and the route hold is carried by name. Graph Rev AD. Status M3. Study. Not a PE stamp.
Authority: `docs-authority/duties/BLOW_MOLD.md`, `docs-authority/nozzles/BLOW_MOLD.csv`, AQS-STD-001 (shop rule), AQS-MOD-002 (frame system), AQS-BOM-SC-001 (self-copy BOM), `flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json` node BLOW_MOLD (design block; AQS-EQP-MFG-001; A-M-285).

## Per-unit items

W1 Source. `blow_mold.py` (CadQuery) reads `params.json` only and rebuilds every solid. The W2 fields of `params.json` are tracker-generated; the `envelope`, `design` and `layout` blocks are builder-owned and pass through tracker regeneration.
W2 Envelope. The node carries `size_mm` 1200 × 800 × 1600 ("L x W x H mm, issued envelope"); the duty section 2 envelope line agrees. The build fills it: measured 1200.0 × 800.0 × 1600.0 mm, one closed manifold mesh. A four-post guard frame on the base plate pins the registry box so the X-PAR-04 envelope registry reads the build.
W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine is drawn per the node design block ("continuous-extrusion blow molder", Precious Plastic class): the melt path — extruder barrel (OD 60 × 700, layout choice W12), extrusion head and downward die — is 316L per duty section 3; the three drive groups are purchased envelopes per plan rule 6 with the node ratings (extruder 2.2 kW, clamp BLOW-CLAMP-01 0.75 kW, air BLOW-AIR-01 0.75 kW); the mold tool (BLOW-MOLD-TOOL-01) is the clamp platens and the mold-halves envelope. The article geometry is not named on the node: the mold set is an envelope and the mold detail is a named hold (W12).
W4 Nozzles. All 4 rows of the issued schedule `docs-authority/nozzles/BLOW_MOLD.csv` are drawn at tag, face, height and bore as atmospheric tube stubs flush at the envelope faces: N1 DN15 drain flush at the envelope bottom (50 datum); N2 DN15 vent flush at the envelope top — its 800 datum predates the 1600 box and is carried as a placeholder (hold, W12); N3 DN15 (the `solids` row: C11-C18 to the mold) on −Y at z 160 and N4 DN15 (the `solids` row: blown parts) on +Y at z 600 — non-piped transfer rows drawn DN15, bore not calculated (plan W4). Atmospheric hose per duty section 4; no flange hardware is drawn because no process pressure is stated (W4 110 % rule); the schedule rating column (Class 150) is carried verbatim as text.
W5 Fasteners. `fasteners.csv`: 8 M12 wedge anchors (base plate to slab, 2 per frame post line) and 8 M10 platen bolts (4 per platen), counts from the model. 8.8 on carbon steel per the grade rule; lengths and patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.
W6 Materials. Barrel, head and die 316L per duty section 3 ("316L where the stream is wet" — the wax melt is the wet service; CA 0 mm, AQS-STD-001 rule). Base plate, guard frame, platens, mold blank and dry envelopes carbon steel A500 (AQS-MOD-002 dry service). Nozzle hardware 316L (wax-contact side of the transfer rows).
W7 Pressure. Atmospheric, no vent edge (duty section 6). The blow-air circuit is a purchased group at the node 0.75 kW rating; no air-pressure design is claimed here (vendor print governs the group). No design_kPa is booked on the node and none is claimed. No hydrotest; a shop leak check of the melt path at fit-out is the route's verification.
W8 Exports. `export/blow_mold.step` (assembly); `export/blow_mold.stl` closed and manifold; `cad/stl/BLOW_MOLD.stl` replaced from the build (ASCII, metres, X-PAR-04 envelope registry). Bounding box 1200.0 × 800.0 × 1600.0 mm against the node envelope: inside, filled exactly. Masses in `check.json`: base 150.7 kg, frame 126.6 kg, barrel 15.8 kg, head and die 15.0 kg, drive 33.9 kg, platens 56.5 kg, mold envelope 38.7 kg, air group 55.3 kg, hardware 0.2 kg.
W9 BOM. `bom.csv`, generated from the model: quantity, material, mass, make-or-buy, basis. Make rows (shop-fabricable, flagged for AQS-BOM-SC-001): base plate, guard frame, melt path (316L), platens, mold blank, connection hardware. Buy rows (purchased per plan rule 6, ratings from the node): extruder drive 2.2 kW, clamp drive 0.75 kW (BLOW-CLAMP-01), air supply 0.75 kW (BLOW-AIR-01).
W10 Fabrication route. Machine practice applied per the lane (the class carries a section 4 route hold): barrel and head machined and welded (316L, GTAW per the 316L practice), platens and tie frames fabricated, mold blank machined when the article set is issued, anchors drilled and set, purchased drives mounted by rating. On-site tool: the metal shop.
W11 Self-copy relevance. A-SC applies (tier 7): the molder makes the shop's molded articles (AQS-BOM-SC-001 row: "Blow moulds and shoe moulds", BLOW-CLAMP-01/BLOW-AIR-01/BLOW-MOLD-TOOL-01, ST-BLOW-01), and the parts of the unit the on-site shop can make — base, guard frame, melt path, platens, mold blank, hardware — are flagged make in the BOM and cross-referenced to AQS-BOM-SC-001. The three drive groups are purchased.
W12 Holds, named. (1) The barrel (OD 60 × 700), platen, mold and air envelope sizes are the layout choice; the node names the drive ratings and process rates, not sizes. (2) The article geometry is not named on the node: the mold set is an envelope and the mold detail is a hold until the article set is issued (products are "molded articles to MFG_SHOP"). (3) The N2 vent datum 800 predates the 1600 box — carried as a placeholder, flush at the top. (4) The N3/N4 solids rows are non-piped transfers drawn DN15, bore not calculated (plan W4). (5) The blow-air circuit pressure is the purchased group's vendor print, not designed here. (6) The route hold itself: no section 4 class row names the `former` class — carried by name; A-core and A-SC pass on the files. (7) Fastener lengths and patterns are study values, confirmed at fabrication.

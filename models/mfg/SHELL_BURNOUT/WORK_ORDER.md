W3 Internals. The node carries `internals: none`, so the tracker records no internal solid. The machine the design block names (flash-dewax + shell fire furnace at 900 C) is drawn as the unit per the duty sheet: CS casing t4 with the front-loading door slab (flush at the −X face, over a real through opening cut in the wall and the hot-face throat - the Oct-31 cut floated inside the cavity and is repaired), ceramic-fibre hot face, hearth plate at z 160, wax-condensate pan (N3 reclaim), and the off-gas collector hood (N5). The shell racks are drawn as process-inventory solids (a campaign load), not BOM parts. The 0.25 kW element set (node design kW) purchases at fabrication and mounts on the hearth and hot face (hold, W12).
W4 Nozzles.

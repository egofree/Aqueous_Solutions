# Work order SHELL_BURNOUT

Authority: AQS-DUTY-072 (X-MFG lane). LIVE unit - dewax + burnout furnace.

1. Fabricate the casing; fit the t-hot-face lining and the hearth plate.
2. Hang the front door; prove the seal.
3. Fit the condensate pan and the off-gas collector; route N3/N4 to WAX_PATTERN and the oil line.
4. Fit N1/N2 shell pass-throughs, N5 off-gas stub, N6 drain and N7 vent.
5. Wire the 0.25 kW dewax heaters; interlock the door and the off-gas draft.
6. Test: dewax curve with a green shell, then the fire cycle to 900 C.
7. Inspect against models/mfg/SHELL_BURNOUT/export/shell_burnout.step; record dimensions on the traveller.

Written 2026-10-05. Not a drawing. The column is sized to what the rest of the plant burns, washes with, and greases with.

Pyrolysis of mixed polyolefin float at 400–600 °C gives a paraffin/olefin oil plus C1–C4 gas and char. A 20 kg vacuum still on municipal plastic oil split that oil into gasoline-range (about C6–C11), kerosene-range (about C10–C20), and wax above about C20. That is the cut structure used here. Oxygenate solvents (IPA, acetone, NMP) are not column products.

## Who takes the cut

- Fuel gas, C1–C4, off the quench drum. Afterburner and the Q2 pilot. The backup genset stays on hydrogen. This gas is not that fuel.
- Naphtha, C5–C10. Fab solvent cabinet and the shop parts washer. Aliphatic. Not a drop-in for IPA.
- Fuel oil, C11–C18. Liquid fuel for the afterburner pilot and the Q2 oil burner. Also the quench-oil makeup.
- Wax, C19–C32. Grease stock. Both calcium and lithium soap lines are baseline. See notes/GREASE_LINES.md. Augers, conveyors, bearings, and the shop use these greases.
- Bottoms, C33+. Back to the pyrolyzer auger.
- Char. Silicon arc. Not a column cut.

## The step

Quench drum, then one atmospheric column with an HX-3 reboiler. Three side draws and a bottoms recycle. Wax draw goes to a grease kettle, not to a second column. No hydrotreater on this sheet.

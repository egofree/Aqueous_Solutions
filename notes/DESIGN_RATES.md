# Design rates — fluoropolymer and critical metals

Document number: AQS-RATE-FP-MET  
Revision: A  
Date: 2026-10-16  
Authority: engineering judgment on 500 kg/day mineral basis. Not measured. Not a PE stamp.  
Assays remain deferred. These rates are design assumptions for consumable replacement.

## Basis

A 500 kg/day mineral plant replaces linings, gaskets, anode mesh, and a small quantity of stainless and titanium parts. Rates below are sized for that steady replacement, not for a first-fill inventory.

| Product | Design rate | Basis |
|---------|-------------|-------|
| Fluoride to HF_GEN | 0.5 kg/d F | Supports HF for monomer + wet-bench/glass use |
| HF product | 0.5 kg/d | Stoichiometric from fluoride |
| VDF monomer | 0.15 kg/d | PVDF linings and gaskets |
| TFE monomer | 0.10 kg/d | PTFE gaskets and small parts |
| PVDF resin | 0.14 kg/d | ~90 % polymerization yield |
| PTFE resin | 0.09 kg/d | ~90 % polymerization yield |
| Ti metal | 0.40 kg/d | Anode mesh and pyrolyzer shell replacement |
| Mo metal | 0.05 kg/d | 316L alloy element (≈2 % of stainless make-up) |
| Cr metal | 0.20 kg/d | 304/316 alloy element (≈18 % of stainless make-up) |

## Closure notes

- Kroll at 0.40 kg/d Ti: 1.18 kg/d Cl₂ and 0.40 kg/d Mg cycle through the existing magnesium pot (net zero). See notes/KROLL_BALANCE.md scaled.  
- Monomer route (methanol–Cl₂–HF) at these rates consumes <1 kg/d methanol and a matching Cl₂ quantity, both returned as HCl.  
- All rates are assumptions. They move to the assumptions list only. They do not close the deferred assay lines.

## Register correction

The per-unit commit cycle registered each accepted unit in the tracker's
ACCEPTED row set with a name-match guard that also matched names elsewhere in
the tracker source (EXISTING_SOURCE), so seven M3 registrations were silently
skipped (MET_MELT, GAS_ATOMIZER, MFG_SHOP, WIRE_DRAW, EXT_HALL, HYDRO_SET,
PV_ARRAY) and their section 7 rows stayed at M2 until their commits plus
GROW_BED's were repaired by the register-correction commit (b4ea5a93). The
guard is fixed to scope to the ACCEPTED block alone. No model, dimension or
acceptance figure changed in that commit.

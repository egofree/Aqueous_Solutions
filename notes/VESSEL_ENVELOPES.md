## Fluoropolymer train

| Unit | Type | Approx. envelope (L×W×H or Ø×H) | Material class | MAWP class | Nozzles (min) | Notes |
|------|------|----------------------------------|----------------|------------|---------------|-------|
| F_RECOVER | Separator / concentrator | 1.2×0.8×1.5 m | HDPE or PP-lined CS | atm | 4 | Fluoride liquor in, concentrate out, raffinate return |
| HF_GEN | ED or reaction skid | 1.0×0.6×1.2 m | PVDF-lined or PTFE-lined | 0.3 MPa | 6 | Aqueous HF product; double containment |
| VDF_SYN | Packed reactor + pyrolysis | 0.6 m Ø × 3 m | Ni alloy or Monel | 1.0 MPa | 6 | HF + chlorocarbon; HCl return |
| TFE_SYN | Pyrolysis tube + quench | 0.3 m Ø × 4 m | Ni alloy | 0.6 MPa | 5 | R-22 pyrolysis; TFE quench |
| POLY_F | Stirred autoclave | 0.8 m Ø × 1.5 m | 316L or glass-lined | 2.0 MPa | 6 | Suspension/emulsion; reclaim hopper adjacent |

All HF- and TFE-containing vessels: secondary containment, HF-rated scrubber tie-in, no carbon steel in wetted HF service.

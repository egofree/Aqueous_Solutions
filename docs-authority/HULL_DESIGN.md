## 3. Method and limits

The hull is a rectangular pontoon; the hydrostatics are the box-hull forms above. The plant mass is the packaged placement volume at the packaging density - the unit massing study replaces it. The mooring design, the structural design and the class environmental load method are their own tasks; this page proves only that the hull carries the plant with stability and freeboard margin on the stated dimensions.

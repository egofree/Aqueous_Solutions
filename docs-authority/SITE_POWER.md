## 1. Balance through the worst stretch

Primary sources share the mean generation G. During the worst stretch of h hours a source gives the fraction f of its mean rate, and storage with round-trip efficiency eta carries the rest of the demand P:

`G = (P (24 - h) + P h / eta) / (24 - phi h + phi h / eta)`, with `phi` the share-weighted f of the primary sources.

Values are per kW of average demand, so they hold for any plant size. Installed capacity is the oversize factor times the balancing capacity.

Table 1. Balance per kW of average demand

| Quantity | Value | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Worst stretch | night, 14 h | `solar.night_hours` |
| Storage round trip, eta | 0.7 | driver `storage_round_trip` |
| phi | 0 | primary sources, Table 2 |
| Mean generation G / P | 1.2500 | equation above |
| Energy through storage per kW | 14.000 kWh delivered, 20.000 kWh charged | (P - phi G) h |
| Oversize factor | 2 | `power_portfolio.oversize_factor` (driver `pv_oversize_factor`) |
| PV factor on the reference balance | 1 | `pv_factor()`; 1 for PV alone |

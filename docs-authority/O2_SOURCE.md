## 2. Grow bed on a light basis

Net canopy quantum yield 0.045 mol CO2/mol absorbed, absorptance 0.9 (elevated CO2, WHEELER1996 BPC), LED 2.8 umol/J (KUSUMA2020).
At DLI 17.0 mol/m2/d: 30.3 g CO2 and 22.0 g O2 per m2 per day, 1.69 kWh/m2/d of LED electricity.

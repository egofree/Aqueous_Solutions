Cl2 sink by calculation. (a) HCl synthesis of all anode Cl2 needs 12.9 kg/d H2 (= 30 kW of electrolysis) and returns 467 kg/d HCl that the oxide leach does not need (solver.leach_acid 87.9 kg/d). (b) Cl2 to leach oxidation of the metallic scrap loop (IA-HOP-01 worn goods, the EW sheet source) needs no unit and no H2: Cl2 + M -> MCl2 is the reverse of the cell reaction (USBM_CL_EW). (c) hypochlorite: 2 NaOH per Cl2, a NaOCl stream with no user. Picked: (b) for the main anode Cl2; the side-reaction pair (H2 + Cl2, equimolar) is recombined in a graphite burner (smallest unit that returns the HCl the side reaction split). BMED duty is unchanged: oxide-leach HCl is still regenerated by BMED (solver, GREENWOOD).

## 4. Electrolyser and fuel cell (reversible water loop)

PEM electrolyser: 16.74 kg/d H2, 132.8 kg/d O2, 149.6 kg/d water from ROOF_TANK; 38.4 kW average, 153 kW rated on a 6 h PV window; stack heat 10.9 kW to HEAT_SINK.
Fuel cell (existing FUEL_CELL, pure O2 from HDR_O2): 5.03 kg/d H2 -> 3.46 kW, 16.5 kWh/kg; round trip 30 % (ZNBR2 is the efficient short store; the H2/O2 pair is the multi-day store).
Store at the existing 200 bar basis: H2 115 kg (7.72 m3 water volume at 14.9 kg/m3) = 549 h of fuel-cell output; O2 partner 913 kg (3.18 m3 at 287 kg/m3), separate bank.
H2 and O2 are generated, stored and piped separately; no mixed (HHO) gas anywhere (NFPA 2-2023 ch. 7 and 10, ISO 22734:2019, IEC 62282-3-100). Fuel-cell water returns via S52 to VCD_TRAIN and on to the roof tank.

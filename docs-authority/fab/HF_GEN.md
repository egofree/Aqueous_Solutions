# HF_GEN fabrication

Envelope 1200 x 700 x 1500 mm (kiln-class study minimum, AQS-DUTY-073; A-M-283). Atmospheric;
design pressure unevaluated until the unit task 07 relief case issues.

Production route (MFG/FAB, in-house, X-MFG-31):
1. Shell: carbon steel kiln tube, 200 mm ID x 1200 mm, rolled and welded, 2 deg slope on the
   trunnion base; 4 rpm gear drive (graph design; dry HF service shell per AQS-DUTY-073 sec. 3,
   PERRY9_MAT).
2. Monel trunnion inserts machined in-house and shrunk into the shell bearing bands.
3. Indirect heat circuit at 250 C from the Q1 heat-ladder tie, with shell thermocouple wells.
4. Charge/discharge heads: inlet feed screws for fluorspar + 98 wt% H2SO4 acid, discharge cake
   chute; kiln gas duct to the recovered-F dissolution tank.
5. Interlock wiring: high shell temperature, low rotation, low purge flow.
6. Leak test: inert hold on the tube at the shop pressure per AQS-STD-001 sec. 4; route trial per
   AQS-DUTY-073 sec. 3-4.

Inspect against models/mfg/HF_GEN/export/hf_gen.step. Not stamped. Production rate stays 0.0
(feed-blocked, A-M-267: no fluorine source on site, Q-013); the model is the design case at the
1.674 kg/d HF duty from AQS-DUTY-072/073 per-kg tables.

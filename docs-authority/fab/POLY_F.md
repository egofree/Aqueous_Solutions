# POLY_F fabrication

Envelope 600 x 600 x 1200 mm shell over the 150 x 225 mm kettle (reactor-class study minimum,
AQS-DUTY-073; A-M-283). Atmospheric (kettle at the vent datum; 60 C design temperature).

Production route (MFG/FAB, in-house, X-MFG-31):
1. Kettle: Hastelloy C-276 welded vessel, 150 mm ID x 225 mm straight side, dished heads,
   jacketed bottom (heated to the 60 C recipe point); wetted PTFE gaskets per AQS-STD-002 Rev N
   sec. 14 (Hastelloy C-276 wetted, PTFE-gasketed).
2. Agitator and internals from the parametric source; magnetic-coupled drive so no elastomer
   shaft seal sits at the PTFE/vinylidene fluoride service window.
3. Initiator feed: persulfate-class solution feed skid with the emulsion/suspension recipe loop
   (ULLMANN_FLUOROPOLY); reclaim valve loop to F_RECOVER for the 0.02 kg/d reclaim stream.
4. Heat circuit: jacket service from the Q-ladder setpoint 60 C; low-agitator and high-temperature
   interlocks (work order 3).
5. Test: water fill and inert pressure hold per AQS-STD-001 sec. 4; route trial per AQS-DUTY-073
   sec. 3-4.

Inspect against models/mfg/POLY_F/export/poly_f.step. Not stamped. Production rate stays 0.0
(feed-blocked, A-M-267: no fluorine source on site, Q-013); design case pvdf_kg_d 0.14 and
ptfe_kg_d 0.09 with the per-kg tables in AQS-DUTY-073.

# NAOH_PLANT fabrication

Class: reactor; sector DIGESTER. Study route. Not stamped.
Envelope: not booked on the duty sheet - the physical build size is the unit task 10-11 scope;
service line: see the duty sheet section 1 basis

Internals: caustic-plant internals per the sizing of the on-site sodium route (flow_graph/param/caustic.py): the membrane-cell stack with titanium anode pans, nickel cathode pans and perfluorinated membranes on the chlor-alkali route (or the slaker, causticiser and white-liquor tank on the causticising route), the brine re-saturation bed and the liquor head tank, sized at the make-up rates [docs-authority/sizing/NAOH_PLANT.md]
Material: Lime kiln and trona calciner tubes of Inconel 600 with alumina lining and mineral-wool insulation; slaker, causticiser and white-liquor tank of carbon steel; on the chlor-alkali route, titanium anode pans, nickel cathode pans and perfluorinated membranes, with nickel catholyte piping and PVC-lined brine piping. (continues on the duty sheet)

Nozzles: 10 process connections per docs-authority/nozzles/NAOH_PLANT.csv

Production route (issued ahead of the CAD build; fabrication does not start before unit
tasks 10-12 close; the section 3 material selections are issued per AQS-STD-006):
1. Machine build: welded frame plus standard drivetrain parts; sheet, plate and bar stock bought to the family standards (AQS-INV-004/006/007 desk packages).
2. Tooling and working parts per the duty sheet size line; the full part set is the unit task 10-12 deliverables (CadQuery model, real BOM).
3. Connections: nozzle stubs to the schedule; flange faces per AQS-PIP-003 class letters
4. Test: hydrostatic/water-fill and pressure hold per AQS-STD-001 section 4; dry-run only until
    the CAD acceptance (AQS-PLAN-006 M3) and the real BOM land.

# EVAP_ACID_VS fabrication

Envelope DN150 x 1400 mm on the EVAP_ACID frame. Atmospheric.

Production route (MFG/FAB, in-house, X-PAR-10):
1. Shell: PVC-U Sch 80 pipe cut to length, caps and DN25 socket nozzles solvent-welded (ASTM D2564).
2. Packing: 16 mm PVC tube cut to 16 mm lengths on the jellybean saw line (X-MFG-32); deburr; about 6.4 L of bed.
3. Support plate and distributor: PVC-U sheet drilled on the CNC router.
4. Pump: magnetic-drive pump from the in-house pump line (X-MFG-32).
5. Leak test with water at 1 m head for 1 h; clamp to the frame.

Not stamped.

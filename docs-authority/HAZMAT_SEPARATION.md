## 1. Purpose and scope

Some chemicals react dangerously if they come into contact, for example an oxidizer with a base or with a solvent. Fire codes therefore require stored quantities of incompatible materials to be kept apart. This page checks, for each incompatible pair of wet synthesis (WS) reagent units, that the units are far enough apart or are separated by a fire barrier.

## 2. Rule

IFC 2027 section 5003.9.8 and NFPA 400 (2025) section 6.1.12.1 require incompatible materials in quantities above 2.3 kg or 2 L to be separated by at least 6096 mm (20 ft), by a fire barrier with a 1 h rating, or by storage in approved cabinets. The incompatible pairs are taken from the reactivity groups of the NOAA CAMEO Chemicals database. Cabinets are not credited, because none has yet been specified. The 1 h partition on the line X = 15000 mm is credited as a barrier (assumption A-M-100).

The gap is measured edge to edge between the plan footprints of the two units. A pair passes if the gap is at least 6096 mm or if the partition lies between the two units.

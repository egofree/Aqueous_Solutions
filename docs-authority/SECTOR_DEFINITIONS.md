## 1. Purpose and scope

The plant is divided into sectors. A sector is a group of units that together perform one function, such as metal recovery, water treatment or power distribution. Each sector has an identifier, its `sector_id`, which the plant flow graph, the solver, the design tool and the documents all use.

This page lists every sector in the graph and assigns each one to exactly one sector definition class, which states the kind of function it performs. It also states whether the sector is needed to close the loops of the minimum viable system (MVS), the smallest set of sectors that closes every mass, energy and life-support loop. The page then lists the energy storage systems (ESS) that the MVS needs, the sector identifiers that are no longer used, and the names that look like sector identifiers but are not.

The graph holds the sector list; this page only presents it. The test `flow_graph/test_doc_consistency.py` fails if the design-tool catalogue or the sector table in the repository README no longer matches the graph.

## 4. Energy storage peers

The MVS needs every one of its energy storage peers: the energy storage systems that back each other up. The peers that are whole sectors are MEOH, PUMPED_HYDRO. The peers that are units inside a sector are ZNBR2 (ELEC). A design study in the design tool may switch a peer off. The solver then reports `unreclaimed_resource` for that peer and does not report the loops as closed.

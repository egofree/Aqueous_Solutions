Reading the table:
- A on the roof needs the roof to carry about 11,400 t; not credible on the hall structure.
- B moves the mass onto its own tower: at 40 m about 5,100 t on a dedicated foundation (AWWA D100 tank, ASCE 7 wind and seismic).
- C needs no elevated mass; it needs site relief of the stated head within penstock reach. Site survey decides.
- D puts the head below grade: at 300 m the lower reservoir is about 690 m3 of excavation plus the shaft, with a 2.9 MPa casing and penstock. It is site-independent of terrain but depends on geology.

Owner decision (Q-009 closed 2026-10-16): none of A-D. The top floors of the building are the upper reservoir (a water tower within the building, a parametric form of B on the building structure) and CATCH_TANK is the lower reservoir. Sizing, loads, egress and roof PV are in AQS-BLD-014 (WATER_TOWER.md, A-M-187).

# Power module

Document number: AQS-PWR-001
Revision: A
Date: 2026-10-11
Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Feed every bay at 48 V DC. EW-1 is 2105 A at cell voltage, not at 48 V. The bay connector is the control and the small-load feed. The cell current is a separate rectifier.

2. Size. One bay connector is 48 V, 100 A, 4.8 kW. A lathe is 1.1 kW. A furnace inverter is 10 kW, so it takes three connectors. EW-1 at 2.5 V and 2105 A is 5.3 kW, from its own rectifier, not from the 48 V rail. The 48 V bus is sized for the shop and the farm, 20 kW, which is five connectors. Conductor is 2/0 copper at 48 V for 20 kW, 417 A.

3. Material. Bus is copper bar, 417 / 200 = 2.1 cm². Enclosure is the dry frame, A500, 800 × 600 × 800.

4. Nozzles. Input from the 60 kW inverter, two conductors. Output is a row of SB350 on the +X face. EW rectifier output is a separate pair of bus bars, not an SB350.

5. Instruments. Bus voltage, bus current. Overcurrent opens the contactor. Each connector has a fuse.

6. Relief. None. Not a pressure vessel.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. Fault current at the 48 V bus is not calculated. The connector count and the conductor are.

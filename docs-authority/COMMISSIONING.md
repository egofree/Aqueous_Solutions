## Phases per system

1. **Mechanical completion and pressure test.** ITP hold point of each unit (AQS-QA-001); H2 and O2 service add a helium leak test (ASME B31.3 345.8) and O2 service is cleaned per ASTM G93 first. Electrical: insulation resistance and LOTO per NFPA 70E before energising.
2. **Loop check and SIF proof test.** Every instrument of AQS-CTL-001 from field to PLC tag and HMI; every SIF of AQS-CTL-003 tripped from its initiator to its final element (IEC 61511 clause 16).
3. **Water run.** Liquid units circulate water at the graph liquid rate for 24 h: pumps, levels, heaters on water, leak check at operating temperature.
4. **Chemical run.** The graph reagents admitted at 25 %, 50 %, 100 % of the graph rate, 24 h at each step; hold point between steps on product and vent analysis.

Acceptance at each step: no leak, every loop and trip verified, rates within 10 % of the graph. Utilities are commissioned first so each process system starts with power, water, heat and gases in service.

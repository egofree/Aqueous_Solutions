# Electronics bay

Document number: AQS-ELEC-001
Revision: A
Date: 2026-10-10

The bay takes a wafer from the aligner and makes a large-geometry device. Minimum line is 100 µm. That is a diode, a transistor, and a simple gate. It is not a GPU.

Stations. Coat, align, develop, etch, strip, probe. Etch is the wet bench. Probe is a module with two micromanipulators the shop builds.

The tech tree puts a high-end GPU at 50 to 200 years after this bay. This document stops at the large-geometry device.

# Electronics bay

Document number: AQS-ELEC-001
Revision: C
Date: 2026-10-11
Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Large-geometry device. Minimum line 100 µm. First device is a diode.

2. Size. Six frames, 6061, 1200 mm, at 1400 mm pitch. Bay is 8.4 m. Stations: coat (same spinner as the aligner), align (the aligner module), develop (tank, aqueous base from BMED), etch (wet bench, acid from the core), strip (solvent from the oil line), probe (two manipulators, 10 µm, shop-built on the mill). Diode window 100 µm. Pads 1 mm.

3. Material. Frames 6061. Wet benches PP.

4. Nozzles. One SB350 per station. Wet stations drain DN15 to the leach.

5. Instruments. Per station, as the aligner and the wet bench.

6. Relief. Wet stations vent to the scrubber.

7. Code. Shop.

8. Open items. No second device is drawn. The diode and the bay are.

# Farm module bay

Document number: AQS-SIDE-002
Revision: A
Date: 2026-10-10

The bay is four module frames. Fish tank on one. Biofilter, 15 m² of media, on two. Beds on the fourth, or on the floor beside them. Film gutters hang on the frame rail.

Power is the LED rail, 48 V, one connector. Fluid is the 12 GPM pair: fish water out, return in. The dual-root correction is a third, smaller line and does not return to the tank.

Azolla is not on a frame. It is the 18 by 5 m hall already placed.

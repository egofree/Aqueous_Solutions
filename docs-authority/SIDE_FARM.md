# Farm module bay

Document number: AQS-SIDE-002
Revision: B
Date: 2026-10-11
Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Biofilter duty is 47999 kg/d, envelope 3000 × 5000 × 1200 mm, 18 m³, residence 9 h. That vessel is not a 1200 mm bay.

2. Size. The tank is the envelope. The skid under it is 5000 × 3000 mm. Posts at 1000 mm so the span is 1000 mm, not 5000. Six posts along the length, two rails. One span carries 1800 kg. Tube 100 × 100 × 6. I = 3.34×10⁶ mm⁴. Deflection is 0.55 mm against L/240 = 4.2 mm. Section does not increase. Fish tank is a second vessel on the same post pitch. Beds are 2000 × 1000 × 400 frames, HDPE rail, not the biofilter.

3. Material. Biofilter shell is polymer, 10 mm. Skid is painted A500. Paint is the barrier. Liquor does not wet the steel if the shell holds.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN50, unrated, polymer. Drain DN25. LED rail is one SB350, 48 V. Fish water is the 12 GPM pair. The dual-root line does not return to the tank.

5. Instruments. Level. High level trips the feed pump. Dissolved oxygen is a sample, not a trip.

6. Relief. Atmospheric. Vent is a pipe to the hall, not the scrubber. This is air and water, not acid gas.

7. Code. Shop. Polymer tank, not a pressure vessel.

8. Open items. Paint life in a wet hall is not measured. The span and the deflection are.

# MEOH_FC

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 12.47 kg/d. Mass out 12.50 kg/d. Electrical duty 1.00 kW. In: S76 8.37 kg/d (MeOH fuel); IA-FC-AIR 2.68 kg/d (cell air); IA-MEOH-REST 1.28 kg/d (tank draw); IA-CLOSE-10 0.1400 kg/d (leach condensate to the methanol cell). Out: S82 6.88 kg/d (CO₂ return from MeOH FC); S81 5.62 kg/d (MeOH FC condensate).

2. Size.
- 1.00 kW. No exchanger area. A heat-transfer area does not belong on this class.

3. Material. No wetted alloy. Conductors are copper.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN15. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.35 mm, raised to DN15. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Current. Overcurrent opens the breaker.

6. Relief. Not a pressure vessel. No process vent.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. The size basis is an assumption. This unit has not been measured.

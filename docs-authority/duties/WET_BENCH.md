# WET_BENCH

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 79.12 kg/d. Mass out 79.12 kg/d. Electrical duty 1.00 kW. In: S27 60.00 kg/d (fab UPW > 18 MΩ·cm class); S09 9.277 kg/d (HCl); S33 3.009 kg/d (NH₄OH / NH₃ (aq)); S32 3.000 kg/d (H₂O₂); 5 more. Out: S10 79.12 kg/d (spent etch); S38 0.006 kg/d (resist and solvent waste).

2. Size.
- In 79.12 kg/d, out 79.12 kg/d. No holdup claimed.

3. Material. PVC-U wetted, per AQS-STD-002 rules M1 and M2. No metal wetted part. The graph gives no temperature for this unit; above 60 °C the thermoplastic is CPVC, never metal. Corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymer.

4. Nozzles. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.88 mm. That is not a specification. Nozzle is DN15. No flange class. Atmospheric tube or hose. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature where the node is a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Atmospheric. No vent edge. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.
7. Code. Shop. Not stamped. The envelope is `cad/stl/WET_BENCH.stl`.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

# WET_BENCH

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 79.10 kg/d. Mass out 79.11 kg/d. Electrical duty 1.00 kW. In: S27 60.00 kg/d (fab UPW > 18 MΩ·cm class); S09 9.28 kg/d (HCl); S32 3.00 kg/d (H₂O₂); S33 3.00 kg/d (NH₄OH / NH₃ (aq)); 5 more. Out: S10 79.10 kg/d (spent etch); S38 0.0060 kg/d (resist and solvent waste).

2. Size.
- In 79.10 kg/d, out 79.11 kg/d. No holdup claimed.

3. Material. 316L where the stream is wet.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN15. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.88 mm, raised to DN15. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

# FUEL_CELL

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 43.44 kg/d. Mass out 43.44 kg/d. Electrical duty 4.00 kW. In: S50 38.40 kg/d (O2 oxidizer); S49 5.04 kg/d (H2 fuel). Out: S52 42.89 kg/d (PEM product H2O); IA-FC-LEACH 0.5470 kg/d (cell water).

2. Size.
- 4.00 kW. No exchanger area. A heat-transfer area does not belong on this class.

3. Material. No wetted alloy. Conductors are copper.

4. Nozzles. Gas duct DN15 at 10 m/s and 1.2 kg/m³. Calculated bore 7 mm. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 into the loop, not to the room.

5. Instruments. Current. Overcurrent opens the breaker.

6. Relief. Not a pressure vessel. No process vent.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. Duct bore uses 1.2 kg/m³.

# FUEL_CELL

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BG. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 43.44 kg/d. Mass out 43.44 kg/d. Electrical duty 4.00 kW. In: S50 38.40 kg/d (O2 oxidizer); S49 5.04 kg/d (H2 fuel). Out: S52 42.89 kg/d (PEM product H2O); IA-FC-LEACH 0.5471 kg/d (cell water).

2. Size.
- 4.00 kW. No exchanger area. A heat-transfer area does not belong on this class.

3. Material. No wetted alloy. Conductors are copper. Corrosion allowance does not apply.

4. Nozzles. No duct bore. Density is not on the edge, and 1.2 kg/m³ is not used. No flange class. The vent stays on the gas edge, not the room.

5. Instruments. Current. Overcurrent opens the breaker.

6. Relief. Not a pressure vessel. No process vent. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. Duct bore is not calculated.

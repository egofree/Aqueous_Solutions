# WORMS

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 4.02 kg/d. Mass out 4.02 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.1000 kW. In: IA-MYC-WORM 3.02 kg/d (myceliated compost); IA-NPK-1 0.4000 kg/d (castings return); IA-HEN-MAN 0.3000 kg/d (manure); IA-GOAT-MAN 0.3000 kg/d (manure). Out: IA-MYC-BED 2.52 kg/d (finished castings); IA-CAST 0.5000 kg/d (castings); IA-CLOSE-3 0.5000 kg/d (worm bin vent to the nitrogen header); IA-CAST-CLOVER 0.4000 kg/d (finished castings); 1 more.

2. Size.
- In 4.02 kg/d, out 4.02 kg/d. No holdup claimed.

3. Material. HDPE or PVC for the water. The frame is not the wetted part. Corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymer.

4. Nozzles. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.20 mm. That is not a specification. Nozzle is DN15. No flange class. Atmospheric tube or hose. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. No trip is specified.

6. Relief. Not a pressure vessel. A vent, if the shell is closed, returns to the process. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

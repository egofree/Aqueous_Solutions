# GLASS_DIGEST

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 148.4 kg/d. Mass out 148.4 kg/d. Electrical duty 1.50 kW. In: IA-BASE-01 118.4 kg/d (digest NaOH, 20 wt%); IA-GLASS-03 30.00 kg/d (burned cullet). Out: IA-DIG-LIQ 118.4 kg/d (silicate liquor); S08G 26.20 kg/d (glass and ceramic stock); IA-SIL-SRC 3.30 kg/d (silicate to slurry); IA-SIC-IN 0.5000 kg/d (silica charge).

2. Size.
- V = (148.4/1000)×(480/24) = 2.9686 m³. 20 days, anaerobic.

3. Material. 316L. Chloride or scrub liquor. Carbon steel pits.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN15. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 1.21 mm, raised to DN15. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Temperature and level. High head pressure trips the feed. Gas returns to the process, not the room.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. Holdup is 20 days, anaerobic. The vessel has not been built, so this is not a measured volume.

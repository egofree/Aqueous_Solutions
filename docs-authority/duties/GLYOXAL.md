# GLYOXAL

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 0.3302 kg/d. Mass out 0.3302 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.1000 kW. In: IA-ALD 0.1912 kg/d (acetaldehyde); IA-GLX-O2 0.1389 kg/d (oxygen to glyoxal). Out: IA-GLX 0.2520 kg/d (glyoxal); IA-GLX-H2O 0.0782 kg/d (glyoxal water).

2. Size.
- In 0.3302 kg/d, out 0.3302 kg/d. No holdup claimed.
- CH₃CHO + O₂ → C₂H₂O₂ + H₂O. The oxygen comes from HDR_O2. The coproduct is water. The old NOx edge is zero.

3. Material. Glass or 316L. Solvent or oxidizer. Not a farm gutter. Corrosion allowance 0 mm. Pit rate is not measured.

4. Nozzles. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.06 mm. That is not a specification. Nozzle is DN15. No flange class. Atmospheric tube or hose. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. No trip is specified.

6. Relief. Not a pressure vessel. A vent, if the shell is closed, returns to the process. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

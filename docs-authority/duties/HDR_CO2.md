# HDR_CO2

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 17.07 kg/d. Mass out 17.07 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0000 kW. In: S85 12.17 kg/d (CO2 product after FLUE_KO); S90 4.02 kg/d (metabolic CO2 (desorbed from the HAB CO2 sorbent bed)); IA-YEAST-CO2 0.4400 kg/d (fermentation CO2); IA-ABE-CO2 0.2500 kg/d (ABE CO2); 1 more. Out: S89 17.07 kg/d (CO2 enrichment dose (FARM AHU supply)).

2. Size.
- Gas 0.5927 m³/h. Tower area at 1.5 m/s = 0.6/3600/1.5 = 0.0001 m². Diameter 12 mm.
- Liquid in 0.8800 kg/d. Sump at 10 min: V = (0.8800/1000)×(10/60)/24 = 0.00001 m³.

3. Material. 316L where the stream is wet.

4. Nozzles. Gas duct DN15 at 10 m/s and 1.2 kg/m³. Calculated bore 5 mm. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 into the loop, not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 back to the suction of this loop. Not to the room and not to a stack.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. Gas volume uses 1.2 kg/m³ because the edge has no m³/h. Tower velocity 1.5 m/s is the packing assumption. Bed height is not calculated. Duct bore uses 1.2 kg/m³.

# FAB_UPW_TANK

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. IA-UPW-FAB 60 kg/d from BMED_UPW_POLISH. One operating point, the graph rate.

2. Size. Inlet 60 kg/d. Residence 30 min. Volume 60.0*30/1440 = 1.250 m3 at density 1.

3. Material. Corrosion allowance 1.5 mm. Service from the class tank. Barrier is the listed alloy or polymer. No coating unless the index names one.

4. Nozzles. Inlet DN25, outlet DN25, drain DN15, vent DN15. Class 150 on metal. Unrated on polymer.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a holding volume. High level trips the upstream pump. A header has no trip.

6. Relief. Atmospheric units have a vent to the scrubber, not to the room. A pressurized unit is set 10% above the basis pressure.

7. Code. shop, 316L.

8. Open items. Density taken as 1. Internals are not drawn.

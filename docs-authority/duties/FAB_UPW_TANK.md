# FAB_UPW_TANK

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BF. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 60.00 kg/d. Mass out 60.00 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0000 kW. In: IA-UPW-FAB 60.00 kg/d (fab UPW). Out: S27 60.00 kg/d (fab UPW > 18 MΩ·cm class).

2. Size.
- V = (60.00/1000)×(168/24) = 0.4200 m³. 7 days of throughput.

3. Material. 316L if the stream is wet or chloride. Carbon steel only if the stream is a dry hydrocarbon or a dry gas. Corrosion allowance is not set.

4. Nozzles. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.77 mm. That is not a specification. Nozzle is DN15. Rating is not set. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature where the node is a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. The set point is not assigned. DN15 back to the process, not the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. Holdup is 7 days of throughput. The vessel has not been built, so this is not a measured volume.

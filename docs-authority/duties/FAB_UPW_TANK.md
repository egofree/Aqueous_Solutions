# FAB_UPW_TANK

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 60.00 kg/d. Mass out 60.00 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0000 kW. In: IA-UPW-FAB 60.00 kg/d (fab UPW). Out: S27 60.00 kg/d (fab UPW > 18 MΩ·cm class).

2. Size.
- V = (60.00/1000)×(168/24) = 0.4200 m³. 7 days of throughput.

3. Material. 316L if the stream is wet or chloride. Carbon steel only if the stream is a dry hydrocarbon or a dry gas.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN15. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.77 mm, raised to DN15. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. Holdup is 7 days of throughput. The vessel has not been built, so this is not a measured volume.

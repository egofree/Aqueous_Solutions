# FISH_SUMP

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 48000.0 kg/d. Mass out 48000.0 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.3000 kW. In: IA-VEG-SUMP 19393.0 kg/d (bed return); IA-BED-SUMP 18909.0 kg/d (bed return); IA-WEED-SUMP 9697.0 kg/d (raceway return); IA-CLAR-SOL 1.00 kg/d (settled solids). Out: IA-SUMP-FISH 48000.0 kg/d (return).

2. Size.
- Circulation 48000.0 kg/d. Two-minute holdup 0.0667 m³. A 20-day volume at this rate is not a tank.

3. Material. HDPE or PVC for the water. The frame is not the wetted part.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN25. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 21.72 mm, raised to DN25. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. If this node is also a reservoir, that volume is not the two-minute holdup.

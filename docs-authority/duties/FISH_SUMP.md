# FISH_SUMP

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 4.8e+04 kg/d. Mass out 4.8e+04 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.3000 kW. In: IA-CLAR-SOL 1 kg/d (settled solids); IA-VEG-SUMP 1.938e+04 kg/d (bed return); IA-WEED-SUMP 9697 kg/d (raceway return); IA-BED-SUMP 1.878e+04 kg/d (bed return); 1 more. Out: IA-SUMP-FISH 4.8e+04 kg/d (return).

2. Size.
- Circulation 48000.0 kg/d. Two-minute holdup 0.0667 m³. A 20-day volume at this rate is not a tank.

3. Material. HDPE or PVC for the water. The frame is not the wetted part. Corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymer.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN25. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 21.7 mm. Drain DN15. No flange class. Atmospheric tube or hose. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. No trip is specified.

6. Relief. Atmospheric. No vent edge. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.
7. Code. Shop. Not stamped. The envelope is `cad/stl/FISH_SUMP.stl`.

8. Open items. If this node is also a reservoir, that volume is not the two-minute holdup.

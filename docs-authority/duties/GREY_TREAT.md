# GREY_TREAT

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 347.5 kg/d. Mass out 347.5 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0000 kW. In: S12 259.1 kg/d (grey/solids); IA-WASH-R 80.00 kg/d (grey); IA-ZNBR-BD 8.49 kg/d (electrolyte bleed). Out: IA-GREY-01 243.0 kg/d (grey sludge); S29 104.5 kg/d (treated grey).

2. Size.
- In 347.5 kg/d, out 347.5 kg/d. No holdup claimed.

3. Material. 316L. Chloride or scrub liquor. Carbon steel pits.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN15. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 1.85 mm, raised to DN15. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

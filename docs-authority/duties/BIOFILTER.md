# BIOFILTER

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 48200.3 kg/d. Mass out 48200.3 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.3000 kW. In: IA-CLAR-BIO 47999.0 kg/d (clarified water); IA-NFT-OUT 200.0 kg/d (film return); IA-TOIL-URN 1.30 kg/d (urine to the biofilter). Out: IA-BIO-VEG 19393.0 kg/d (nitrate water); IA-BIO-BED 18909.0 kg/d (nitrate water); IA-BIO-WEED 9697.0 kg/d (nitrate water); IA-NFT-IN 200.0 kg/d (film supply); 1 more.

2. Size.
- Envelope 3000×5000×1200 mm, 18.00 m³. Flow 48200.3 kg/d is circulation. Residence 8.96 h. Not a 20-day tank.

3. Material. HDPE or PVC for the water. The frame is not the wetted part.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN25. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 21.76 mm, raised to DN25. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 21.8 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. The size basis is an assumption. This unit has not been measured.

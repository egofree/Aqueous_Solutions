# EMETH

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 0.625 kg/d. Mass out 0.625 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.5000 kW. In: IA-DIG-GAS 0.625 kg/d (biogas from the biological feeds). Out: IA-EM-CH4 0.625 kg/d (methane); IA-EM-DIG 0 kg/d (EM effluent).

2. Size.
- In 0.6254 kg/d, out 0.6254 kg/d. No holdup claimed.
- The inlet is the biological feed renamed as biogas, then renamed as methane. No CO₂ is split out. There is no gas assay.

3. Material. HDPE or PVC for the water. The frame is not the wetted part. Corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymer.

4. Nozzles. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.08 mm. That is not a specification. Nozzle is DN15. No flange class. Atmospheric tube or hose. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature where the node is a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Atmospheric. No vent edge. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.
7. Code. Shop. Not stamped. The envelope is `cad/stl/EMETH.stl`.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class. A methane fraction is not measured.

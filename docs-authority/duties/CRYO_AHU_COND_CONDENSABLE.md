# CRYO_AHU_COND_CONDENSABLE

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 0.0521 kg/d. Mass out 0.0521 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0000 kW. In: IA-AHU-POT 0.0521 kg/d (condensable slip). Out: S70 0.0521 kg/d (condensables).

2. Size.
- In 0.0521 kg/d, out 0.0521 kg/d. No holdup claimed.

3. Material. 316L. Condensation. No carbon steel.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN15. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.02 mm, raised to DN15. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

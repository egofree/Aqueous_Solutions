# HEAT_SINK

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 0 kg/d. Mass out 0 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.2000 kW. In: none. Out: none.

2. Size.
- A = 0.2000 × 1000 / (500 × 10) = 0.0400 m². U = 500 W/m²K and ΔT = 10 K are assumed for liquid water.

3. Material. 316L if the stream is wet or chloride. Carbon steel only if the stream is a dry hydrocarbon or a dry gas. Corrosion allowance 0 mm on 316L, 1.5 mm on carbon steel. Pit rate is not measured.

4. Nozzles. No process nozzle.

5. Instruments. Both outlet temperatures. High hot-side outlet trips the hot feed.

6. Relief. Atmospheric. No vent edge. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.
7. Code. Shop. Not stamped. The solid is still an envelope.The unit is retired from the graph; no envelope is issued.

8. Open items. The approach and the coefficient are not measured.

# REMIN

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 10.00 kg/d. Mass out 10.00 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0000 kW. In: IA-UPW-REMIN 10.00 kg/d (remineralizer). Out: S26 10.00 kg/d (potable).

2. Size.
- Envelope 600×400×800 mm is the shell. It is not full. Working volume, 7 days of throughput: V = (10.00/1000)×(168/24) = 0.0700 m³.

3. Material. 316L if the stream is wet or chloride. Carbon steel only if the stream is a dry hydrocarbon or a dry gas. Corrosion allowance 0 mm on 316L, 1.5 mm on carbon steel. Pit rate is not measured.

4. Nozzles. Origin 5000, 2800, 6000 mm. Size 600 × 400 × 800 mm. Inlets are the center of the −Y face. Outlets are the center of the +Y face. Atmospheric joints are hose. DN is copied from the edge and is not recalculated.
- N1, IA-UPW-REMIN, inlet, -Y face center, 5300, 2800, 6400 mm. Height 400 mm from the bottom. Bore not calculated. Rating hose, atmospheric.
- N2, S26, outlet, +Y face center, 5300, 3200, 6400 mm. Height 400 mm from the bottom. Bore not calculated. Rating hose, atmospheric.
5. Instruments. Level and temperature where the node is a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Atmospheric. No vent edge. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.
7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. Shell volume over flow is not the residence. Bore was not calculated where the edge has no density and no line size.

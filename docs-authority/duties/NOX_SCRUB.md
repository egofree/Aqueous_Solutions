# NOX_SCRUB

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 496.8 kg/d. Mass out 496.8 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0000 kW. In: IA-FLUE-EXH 239.6 kg/d (flue to the NOx scrubber); IA-LEACH-NOX 195.0 kg/d (leach offgas); S46 61.16 kg/d (exhaust condensate / NOx); IA-CLOSE-1 1.00 kg/d (leach vent to the NOx scrubber); 1 more. Out: IA-NOX-N2 243.9 kg/d (scrubbed flue nitrogen to the cold box); IA-NOX-H2O 201.3 kg/d (scrubber condensate); IA-NOX-CO2 30.00 kg/d (scrubbed CO2 to the cold box); IA-NOX-N 20.00 kg/d (nitrate liquor); 1 more.

2. Size.
- Envelope 600×400×1200 mm, 0.2880 m³. Residence = 0.2880 / (496.8/1000/24) = 13.91 h.

3. Material. 316L. Chloride or scrub liquor. Carbon steel pits.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN15. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 2.21 mm, raised to DN15. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Differential pressure across the packing, and pH if there is a liquor loop. High differential pressure trips the fan.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. The size basis is an assumption. This unit has not been measured.

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BF. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 48.05 kg/d. Mass out 48.05 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0000 kW. In: S83 48.05 kg/d (O2 product). Out: S50 38.40 kg/d (O2 oxidizer); S02 3.36 kg/d (O2); IA-H2O2-SRC 3.00 kg/d (oxygen to peroxide); IA-FC-AIR 2.68 kg/d (cell air); 2 more.

2. Size.
- Duct at 10 m/s. Gas 1.67 m³/h. Bore 8 mm, DN15. Not a packed tower.

3. Material. 316L where the stream is wet. Corrosion allowance is not set.

4. Nozzles. Gas duct DN15 at 10 m/s. Bore from 1.67 m³/h is 8 mm. Drain DN15. Rating is not set. Vent DN15 into the suction of this loop, not the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature where the node is a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. The set point is not assigned. DN15 back to the suction of this loop. Not to the room and not to a stack. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. Gas volume uses 1.2 kg/m³ because the edge has no m³/h. Duct bore uses 1.2 kg/m³.

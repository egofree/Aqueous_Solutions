# HOPPER

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 633.9 kg/d. Mass out 633.9 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0000 kW. In: IA-WEAR 597.3 kg/d (worn goods); IA-LEACH-HOP 35.35 kg/d (returned solids); S61 1.20 kg/d (metal scrap). Out: IA-HOP-01 530.8 kg/d (returned scrap); IA-FEED-01 102.7 kg/d (mineral feedstock); IA-CLOSE-5 0.4491 kg/d (hopper fines to the pretreat).

2. Size.
- In 633.9 kg/d, out 633.9 kg/d. No holdup claimed.

3. Material. Carbon steel. Hydrocarbon. No aqueous chloride in this vessel.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN15. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 2.50 mm, raised to DN15. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

# AFTERBURNER_FLUE

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 119.1 kg/d. Mass out 119.1 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0000 kW. In: IA-AB-FLUE 8.752 kg/d (flue); IA-AB-AIR 110.3 kg/d (combustion air). Out: S67 119.1 kg/d (flue N2/O2/CO2/NOx).

2. Size.
- The largest edge is gas. This class is not a scrubber. No tower diameter is calculated from 1.5 m/s.

3. Material. 316L pipe, dry flue at 300 °C, insulated to hold the wall above the acid dew point (M7; dew point held). Dielectric isolating flange at FLUE_KO (AQS-STD-002 section 4.3).

4. Nozzles. No duct bore. Density is not on the edge, and 1.2 kg/m³ is not used. No flange class. The vent stays on the gas edge, not the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature where the node is a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Atmospheric. No vent edge. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.
7. Code. Shop. Not stamped. The solid is still an envelope. `cad/stl/AFTERBURNER_FLUE.stl` is the box, not the internal. FreeCAD is not in this session.

8. Open items. No vessel diameter.

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 0.9875 kg/d. Mass out 0.9875 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.1000 kW. In: IA-LITHO-IN 0.9875 kg/d (spent resist and solvent). Out: IA-LITHO-OUT 0.9875 kg/d (spent resist and solvent).

2. Size.
- Envelope 1600×800×1600 mm, 2.05 m³. The box is the machine. It is not a liquid holdup.

3. Material. 316L where the stream is wet. Corrosion allowance 0 mm. Pit rate is not measured.

4. Nozzles. Origin 17800, 11400, 0 mm. Size 1600 × 800 × 800 mm. Inlets are the center of the −Y face. Outlets are the center of the +Y face. Atmospheric joints are hose. DN is copied from the edge and is not recalculated.
- N1, IA-LITHO-IN, inlet, -Y face center, 18600, 11400, 400 mm. Height 400 mm from the bottom. Bore not calculated. Rating hose, atmospheric.
- N2, IA-LITHO-OUT, outlet, +Y face center, 18600, 12200, 400 mm. Height 400 mm from the bottom. Bore not calculated. Rating hose, atmospheric.
5. Instruments. Temperature or current. High temperature trips the power. There is no liquid-level trip.

6. Relief. Not a pressure vessel. A vent, if the shell is closed, returns to the process. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. This unit has not been measured. Bore was not calculated where the edge has no density and no line size.

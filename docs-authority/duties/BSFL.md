# BSFL

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 3.23 kg/d. Mass out 3.23 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.1000 kW. In: S14X 1.80 kg/d (food waste to larvae); IA-GAL-R 1.18 kg/d (plate waste); IA-VEG-TRIM 0.2500 kg/d (vegetable trim). Out: IA-BSFL-FRASS 2.28 kg/d (frass); IA-BSFL-FISH 0.4000 kg/d (larvae); IA-BSFL-HEN 0.3000 kg/d (larvae); IA-CLOSE-9 0.1798 kg/d (larvae bin vent to the nitrogen header); 1 more.

2. Size.
- In 3.23 kg/d, out 3.23 kg/d. No holdup claimed.

3. Material. HDPE or PVC for the water. The frame is not the wetted part. Corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymer.

4. Nozzles. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.18 mm. That is not a specification. Nozzle is DN15. No flange class. Atmospheric tube or hose. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. No trip is specified.

6. Relief. Not a pressure vessel. A vent, if the shell is closed, returns to the process. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

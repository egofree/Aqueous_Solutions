# WAFER_FAB

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 76.61 kg/d. Mass out 76.61 kg/d. Electrical duty 2.00 kW. In: IA-UPW-USE 66.50 kg/d (fab UPW); IA-AR-FAB 5.30 kg/d (argon cover); S37 3.30 kg/d (colloidal silica slurry); S03 1.50 kg/d (N2); 1 more. Out: IA-FAB-SPENT 66.50 kg/d (spent UPW); IA-AR-RET 5.30 kg/d (argon return); S39 3.31 kg/d (spent slurry and etch liquor); IA-LITHO-IN 0.9875 kg/d (spent resist and solvent); 1 more.

2. Size.
- In 76.61 kg/d, out 76.61 kg/d. No holdup claimed.

3. Material. 316L where the stream is wet.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN15. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.87 mm, raised to DN15. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

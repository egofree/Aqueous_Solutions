# ELECTRODE_COAT

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 0.0200 kg/d. Mass out 0.0200 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.3000 kW. In: IA-COAT-IN 0.0200 kg/d (nickel sheet to the booth). Out: IA-COAT-OUT 0.0200 kg/d (coated anode).

2. Size.
- Envelope 1200×800×1800 mm, 1.73 m³. The box is the machine. It is not a liquid holdup.

3. Material. 316L where the stream is wet. Corrosion allowance 0 mm. Pit rate is not measured.

4. Nozzles. Origin 18400, 600, 0 mm. Size 1200 × 800 × 1800 mm. Inlets are the center of the −Y face. Outlets are the center of the +Y face. Atmospheric joints are hose. DN is copied from the edge and is not recalculated.
- N1, IA-COAT-IN, inlet, -Y face center, 19000, 600, 900 mm. Height 900 mm from the bottom. Bore not calculated. Rating hose, atmospheric.
- N2, IA-COAT-OUT, outlet, +Y face center, 19000, 1400, 900 mm. Height 900 mm from the bottom. Bore not calculated. Rating hose, atmospheric.
5. Instruments. Temperature or current. High temperature trips the power. There is no liquid-level trip.

6. Relief. Atmospheric. No vent edge. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.
7. Code. Shop. Not stamped. The envelope is `cad/stl/ELECTRODE_COAT.stl`.

8. Open items. Coat life is not measured.

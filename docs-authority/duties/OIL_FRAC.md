# OIL_FRAC

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 29.91 kg/d. Mass out 29.91 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0000 kW. In: IA-OIL-02 28.90 kg/d (Pyro → frac); IA-LITHO-OUT 0.9875 kg/d (spent resist and solvent); S72B 0.0158 kg/d (organic mist); S38 0.0060 kg/d (resist and solvent waste). Out: CUT-1 11.16 kg/d (fractionator cut); CUT-2 8.41 kg/d (fractionator cut); IA-OIL-03 5.00 kg/d (NCG only); CUT-3 3.95 kg/d (fractionator cut); 1 more.

2. Size.
- V = (29.91/1000)×(0.5/24) = 0.0006 m³. 30 min sump.

3. Material. Carbon steel. Hydrocarbon. No aqueous chloride in this vessel.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN15. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.54 mm, raised to DN15. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. Holdup is 30 min sump. The vessel has not been built, so this is not a measured volume.

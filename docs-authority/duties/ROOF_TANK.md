# ROOF_TANK

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 200384.0 kg/d. Mass out 200384.0 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0000 kW. In: IA-ESS-HYDRO-CHG 200000.0 kg/d (Pumped hydro charge (pump up; peers S23)); IA-NOX-H2O 201.3 kg/d (scrubber condensate); IA-POT-01 120.0 kg/d (treated water makeup); IA-HVAC-COND 56.70 kg/d (AHU condensate to the water cycle); 7 more. Out: IA-ESS-HYDRO-DIS 200000.0 kg/d (Pumped hydro discharge (penstock; peers S22)); IA-NOX-HAB 207.3 kg/d (scrubber water); S21 120.0 kg/d (clean tower water); S40 40.00 kg/d (clean overflow); 1 more.

2. Size.
- Circulation 200384.0 kg/d. Two-minute holdup 0.2783 m³. A 20-day volume at this rate is not a tank.

3. Material. 316L if the stream is wet or chloride. Carbon steel only if the stream is a dry hydrocarbon or a dry gas.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN50. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 44.37 mm, raised to DN50. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. If this node is also a reservoir, that volume is not the two-minute holdup.

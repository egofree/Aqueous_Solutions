# COMPOST_MYC

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 3.02 kg/d. Mass out 3.02 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0500 kW. In: IA-COMP-MYC 2.70 kg/d (cooled compost); IA-STRAW-SPENT 0.1800 kg/d (spent oyster spawn); IA-BLOCK-SPENT 0.1400 kg/d (spent blocks). Out: IA-MYC-WORM 3.02 kg/d (myceliated compost).

2. Size.
- In 3.02 kg/d, out 3.02 kg/d. No holdup claimed.

3. Material. HDPE or PVC for the water. The frame is not the wetted part.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN15. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.17 mm, raised to DN15. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

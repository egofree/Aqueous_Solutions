# VEG_BED

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 19443.8 kg/d. Mass out 19443.8 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.4000 kW. In: IA-BIO-VEG 19393.0 kg/d (nitrate water); IA-MIST-OUT 50.00 kg/d (mister return); IA-BED-VEG 0.8000 kg/d (fish water). Out: IA-VEG-SUMP 19393.0 kg/d (bed return); IA-MIST-IN 50.00 kg/d (mister supply); IA-VEG-TRIM 0.2500 kg/d (vegetable trim); IA-CROP_TOMATO 0.1200 kg/d (tomato); 8 more.

2. Size.
- Circulation 19443.8 kg/d. Two-minute holdup 0.0270 m³. A 20-day volume at this rate is not a tank.

3. Material. HDPE or PVC for the water. The frame is not the wetted part.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN15. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 13.82 mm, raised to DN15. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. If this node is also a reservoir, that volume is not the two-minute holdup.

# MEOH_SYNTH

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BG. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 8.50 kg/d. Mass out 8.50 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.5000 kW. In: S73 6.90 kg/d (CO2 feedstock); S74 1.60 kg/d (H2 for MeOH). Out: S75 8.50 kg/d (crude MeOH).

2. Size.
- The largest edge is gas. This class is not a scrubber. No tower diameter is calculated from 1.5 m/s.

3. Material. 316L where the stream is wet. Corrosion allowance 0 mm. Pit rate is not measured.

4. Nozzles. No duct bore. Density is not on the edge, and 1.2 kg/m³ is not used. No flange class. The vent stays on the gas edge, not the room.

5. Instruments. Temperature and pressure. High pressure trips the feed. Gas stays on the gas edge, not the room.

6. Relief. No valve. Open to the suction of this loop. Not to the room and not to a stack. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. No vessel diameter. Duct bore is not calculated.

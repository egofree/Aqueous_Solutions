# MUSH_BLOCK

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BG. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 0.2500 kg/d. Mass out 0.2500 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.1500 kW. In: IA-HW 0.2500 kg/d (hardwood and bran). Out: IA-BLOCK-SPENT 0.1400 kg/d (spent blocks); IA-BLOCK-FOOD 0.0800 kg/d (shiitake and lion's mane); IA-BLOCK-MED 0.0300 kg/d (reishi and turkey tail).

2. Size.
- In 0.2500 kg/d, out 0.2500 kg/d. No holdup claimed.

3. Material. HDPE or PVC for the water. The frame is not the wetted part. Corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymer.

4. Nozzles. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.05 mm. That is not a specification. Nozzle is DN15. No flange class. Atmospheric tube or hose. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. No trip is specified.

6. Relief. Not a pressure vessel. A vent, if the shell is closed, returns to the process. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

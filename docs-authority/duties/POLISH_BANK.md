# POLISH_BANK

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 3520 kg/d. Mass out 3520 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0000 kW. In: IA-EW-09 3520 kg/d (EW tail to polish). Out: IA-POL-02 3303 kg/d (raffinate (EW tail after polish) to continuous recycle); IA-POL-CAKE 217.4 kg/d (recovered cake).

2. Size.
- V = (3971.1/1000)×(0.5/24) = 0.0827 m³. 30 min.

3. Material. PVC-U wetted, per AQS-STD-002 rules M1 and M2. No metal wetted part. The graph gives no temperature for this unit; above 60 °C the thermoplastic is CPVC, never metal. Corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymer.

4. Nozzles. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 6.25 mm. That is not a specification. Nozzle is DN15. No flange class. Atmospheric tube or hose. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Differential pressure across the packing, and pH if there is a liquor loop. High differential pressure trips the fan.

6. Relief. Atmospheric. No vent edge. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.
7. Code. Shop. Not stamped. The envelope is `cad/stl/POLISH_BANK.stl`.

8. Open items. The vessel has not been built, so this is not a measured volume.

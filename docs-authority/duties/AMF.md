# AMF

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 0.05 kg/d. Mass out 0.05 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.1500 kW. In: IA-AMF-IN 0.05 kg/d (compost inoculum). Out: IA-AMF-BED 0.05 kg/d (bed inoculum).

2. Size.
- In 0.0500 kg/d, out 0.0500 kg/d. No holdup claimed.

3. Material. HDPE or PVC for the water. The frame is not the wetted part. Corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymer.

4. Nozzles. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.02 mm. That is not a specification. Nozzle is DN15. No flange class. Atmospheric tube or hose. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. No trip is specified.

6. Relief. Atmospheric. No vent edge. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.
7. Code. Shop. Not stamped. The envelope is `cad/stl/AMF.stl`.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

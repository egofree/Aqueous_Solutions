# FLUE_KO

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 252.9 kg/d. Mass out 252.9 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0000 kW. In: IA-LEACH-FLUE 135.9 kg/d (leach vent); S67 117.0 kg/d (flue N2/O2/CO2/NOx); S70 0.0521 kg/d (condensables). Out: IA-FLUE-EXH 239.6 kg/d (flue to the NOx scrubber); S67B 13.30 kg/d (CO2-rich gas after KO).

2. Size.
- In 252.9 kg/d, out 252.9 kg/d. No holdup claimed.

3. Material. 316L where the stream is wet.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN15. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 1.58 mm, raised to DN15. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

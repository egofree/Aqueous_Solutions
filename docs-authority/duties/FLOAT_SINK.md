# FLOAT_SINK

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 376.1 kg/d. Mass out 376.1 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0000 kW. In: IA-SINK-01 376.1 kg/d (sink solids). Out: IA-WET-FLOAT-01 343.1 kg/d (Float/sink liquor return); IA-PLAST-01 33.00 kg/d (float plastics).

2. Size.
- In 376.1 kg/d, out 376.1 kg/d. No holdup claimed.

3. Material. PVC-U wetted, per AQS-STD-002 rules M1 and M2. No metal wetted part. The graph gives no temperature for this unit; above 60 °C the thermoplastic is CPVC, never metal. Corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymer.

4. Nozzles. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 1.92 mm. That is not a specification. Nozzle is DN15. No flange class. Atmospheric tube or hose. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature where the node is a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Atmospheric. No vent edge. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.
7. Code. Shop. Not stamped. The solid is still an envelope. `cad/stl/FLOAT_SINK.stl` is the box, not the internal. FreeCAD is not in this session.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

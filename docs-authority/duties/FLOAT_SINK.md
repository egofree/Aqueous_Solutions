# FLOAT_SINK

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 376.1 kg/d. Mass out 376.1 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0000 kW. In: IA-SINK-01 376.1 kg/d (sink solids). Out: IA-WET-FLOAT-01 343.1 kg/d (Float/sink liquor return); IA-PLAST-01 33.00 kg/d (float plastics).

2. Size.
- In 376.1 kg/d, out 376.1 kg/d. No holdup claimed.

3. Material. 316L where the stream is wet.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN15. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 1.92 mm, raised to DN15. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

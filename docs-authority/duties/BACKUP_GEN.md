# BACKUP_GEN

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 61.16 kg/d. Mass out 61.16 kg/d. Electrical duty 2.50 kW. In: IA-GEN-AIR 50.00 kg/d (genset air); CUT-1-OUT 11.16 kg/d (C5-C10 fuel from the tank). Out: S46 61.16 kg/d (exhaust condensate / NOx).

2. Size.
- 2.50 kW. No exchanger area. A heat-transfer area does not belong on this class.

3. Material. No wetted alloy. Conductors are copper.

4. Nozzles. Gas duct DN15 at 10 m/s and 1.2 kg/m³. Calculated bore 9 mm. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 into the loop, not to the room.

5. Instruments. Current. Overcurrent opens the breaker.

6. Relief. Not a pressure vessel. No process vent.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. Duct bore uses 1.2 kg/m³.

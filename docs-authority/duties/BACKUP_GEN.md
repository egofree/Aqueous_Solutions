# BACKUP_GEN

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BF. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 61.16 kg/d. Mass out 61.16 kg/d. Electrical duty 2.50 kW. In: IA-GEN-AIR 50.00 kg/d (genset air); CUT-1-OUT 11.16 kg/d (C5-C10 fuel from the tank). Out: S46 61.16 kg/d (exhaust condensate / NOx).

2. Size.
- 2.50 kW. No exchanger area. A heat-transfer area does not belong on this class.

3. Material. No wetted alloy. Conductors are copper. Corrosion allowance does not apply.

4. Nozzles. No duct bore. Density is not on the edge, and 1.2 kg/m³ is not used. Rating is not set. The vent stays on the gas edge, not the room.

5. Instruments. Current. Overcurrent opens the breaker.

6. Relief. Not a pressure vessel. No process vent. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. Duct bore is not calculated.

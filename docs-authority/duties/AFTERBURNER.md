# AFTERBURNER

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 7.097 kg/d. Mass out 7.097 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.1332 kW. In: NCG_TO_AB 5 kg/d (NCG); S11 0.8 kg/d (organics); IA-BLK-GAS 0.0986 kg/d (digester biogas, flared in the afterburner); IA-AB-O2 0.5283 kg/d (combustion O2, 10 % excess); 1 more. Out: S18 0 kg/d (captured CO2/air); IA-AB-FLUE 7.097 kg/d (flue).

2. Size.
- The largest edge is gas. This class is not a scrubber. No tower diameter is calculated from 1.5 m/s.

3. Material. Carbon steel. Hydrocarbon. No aqueous chloride in this vessel. Corrosion allowance 1.5 mm. Pit rate is not measured.

4. Nozzles. No duct bore. Density is not on the edge, and 1.2 kg/m³ is not used. No flange class. The vent stays on the gas edge, not the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature where the node is a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Atmospheric. No vent edge. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.
7. Code. Shop. Not stamped. The envelope is `cad/stl/AFTERBURNER.stl`.

8. Open items. Chamber sized in AQS-DUTY-063 Rev B section 6 (X-SAN-01): ID 200 x 600 mm, >= 850 C for >= 2 s (Directive 2010/75/EU Art. 50), feed 7.03 kg/d including sanitation cake and biogas. Refractory and burner detail open.

# GROW_BED

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 19100.5 kg/d. Mass out 19100.5 kg/d. Electrical duty 5.00 kW. In: IA-BIO-BED 18909.0 kg/d (nitrate water); IA-NUT-BED 129.6 kg/d (nitrate and bleed salts); IA-DR-OUT 20.00 kg/d (correction return, not to the tank); S89 17.07 kg/d (CO2 enrichment dose (FARM AHU supply)); 10 more. Out: IA-BED-SUMP 18909.0 kg/d (bed return); IA-BED-COMP 129.6 kg/d (salt-laden trim); IA-DR-IN 20.00 kg/d (correction supply); IA-BED-VAP 16.70 kg/d (bed transpiration); 16 more.

2. Size.
- Circulation 19100.5 kg/d. Two-minute holdup 0.0265 m³. A 20-day volume at this rate is not a tank.

3. Material. HDPE or PVC for the water. The frame is not the wetted part.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN15. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 13.70 mm, raised to DN15. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. If this node is also a reservoir, that volume is not the two-minute holdup.

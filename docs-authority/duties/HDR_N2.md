# HDR_N2

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 245.0 kg/d. Mass out 245.0 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0000 kW. In: S84 243.3 kg/d (N2 product); IA-EW4-N2 1.00 kg/d (sweep nitrogen); IA-CLOSE-3 0.5000 kg/d (worm bin vent to the nitrogen header); IA-CLOSE-9 0.1798 kg/d (larvae bin vent to the nitrogen header). Out: IA-AB-AIR 111.2 kg/d (combustion air); IA-AHU-AIR 79.04 kg/d (outdoor air moisture); IA-GEN-AIR 50.00 kg/d (genset air); IA-NH3-SRC 3.25 kg/d (nitrogen to ammonia); 1 more.

2. Size.
- Gas 8.51 m³/h. Tower area at 1.5 m/s = 8.5/3600/1.5 = 0.0016 m². Diameter 45 mm.
- No liquor inlet. No sump volume is invented from the gas mass.

3. Material. 316L where the stream is wet.

4. Nozzles. Gas duct DN20 at 10 m/s and 1.2 kg/m³. Calculated bore 17 mm. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 into the loop, not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 back to the suction of this loop. Not to the room and not to a stack.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. Gas volume uses 1.2 kg/m³ because the edge has no m³/h. Tower velocity 1.5 m/s is the packing assumption. Bed height is not calculated. Duct bore uses 1.2 kg/m³.

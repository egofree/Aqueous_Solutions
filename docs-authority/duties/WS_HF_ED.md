# WS_HF_ED

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 1.50 kg/d. Mass out 1.50 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0000 kW. In: IA-HF-SRC 1.50 kg/d (fluoride to HF). Out: S31 1.50 kg/d (HF (aq)).

2. Size.
- In 1.50 kg/d, out 1.50 kg/d. No holdup claimed.

3. Material. PVDF wetted, per AQS-STD-002 rules M1 and M2. No metal wetted part. IA-HF-SRC arrives from LEACH_CSTR_1 at up to 85 °C (X-MAT-09). HF at that temperature: PVDF (M1 compatibility, M5), not CPVC. Corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymer.

4. Nozzles. Origin 12200, 8200, 0 mm. Size 800 × 800 × 1100 mm. Inlets are the center of the −Y face. Outlets are the center of the +Y face. Atmospheric joints are hose. DN is copied from the edge and is not recalculated.
- N1, IA-HF-SRC, inlet, -Y face center, 12600, 8200, 550 mm. Height 550 mm from the bottom. Bore not calculated. Rating hose, atmospheric.
- N2, S31, outlet, +Y face center, 12600, 9000, 550 mm. Height 550 mm from the bottom. Bore not calculated. Rating hose, atmospheric.
5. Instruments. Level and temperature where the node is a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Atmospheric. No vent edge. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.
7. Code. Shop. Not stamped. The envelope is `cad/stl/WS_HF_ED.stl`.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

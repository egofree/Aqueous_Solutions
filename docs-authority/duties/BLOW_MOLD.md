# BLOW_MOLD

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. One 1 L polymer bottle. Scrap returns to the oil line. Peak power on the node note is 2 to 4 kW.

2. Size. Parison heat is the load. A 40 g preform in polyethylene, heat capacity about 2 kJ/kg·K, 150 K rise, is 12 kJ. At 2 kW that is 6 seconds of heat. Clamp is a screw on the dry frame, not a hydraulic unit. Blow air is 6 bar shop air, one breath of the bottle, about 6 L. The frame is the 800 mm dry bay.

3. Material. Mold is aluminum, cut on the mill. Frame A500. The bottle polymer is plant polyethylene or the solvent-train cut that is actually on hand. If that cut is not a moldable resin, this machine does not run.

4. Nozzles. Two SB350, because 4 kW does not fit one 100 A connector. Air DN15. Scrap chute to the oil line, not a nozzle.

5. Instruments. Mold temperature. Overcurrent opens the contactor.

6. Relief. Air side DN15 to the room only if it is clean shop air. It is not process gas.

7. Code. Shop.

8. Open items. Which polymer cut is moldable is not shown on the oil line. The energy and the mold are.

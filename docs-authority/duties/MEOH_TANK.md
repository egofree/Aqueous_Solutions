# MEOH_TANK

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 9.81 kg/d. Mass out 9.81 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0000 kW. In: S75 8.50 kg/d (crude MeOH); IA-EM-CH4 0.9400 kg/d (methane); IA-CLOSE-7 0.3554 kg/d (compost condensate to the methanol tank); S77 0.0146 kg/d (biogenic MeOH). Out: S76 8.37 kg/d (MeOH fuel); IA-MEOH-REST 1.28 kg/d (tank draw); IA-FORM-S 0.1200 kg/d (methanol to formaldehyde); IA-MEOH-MEAM 0.0400 kg/d (methanol to methylamine).

2. Size.
- Envelope 800×800×1100 mm, 0.7040 m³. Residence = 0.7040 / (9.81/1000/24) = 1722.3 h.

3. Material. 316L if the stream is wet or chloride. Carbon steel only if the stream is a dry hydrocarbon or a dry gas.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN15. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.31 mm, raised to DN15. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. The size basis is an assumption. This unit has not been measured.

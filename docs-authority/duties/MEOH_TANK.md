# MEOH_TANK

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BG. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 9.50 kg/d. Mass out 9.50 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0000 kW. In: S75 8.50 kg/d (crude MeOH); IA-EM-CH4 0.6254 kg/d (methane); IA-CLOSE-7 0.3554 kg/d (compost condensate to the methanol tank); S77 0.0146 kg/d (biogenic MeOH). Out: S76 8.06 kg/d (MeOH fuel); IA-MEOH-REST 1.28 kg/d (tank draw); IA-FORM-S 0.1200 kg/d (methanol to formaldehyde); IA-MEOH-MEAM 0.0400 kg/d (methanol to methylamine).

2. Size.
- Envelope 800×800×1100 mm is the shell. It is not full. Working volume, 7 days of throughput: V = (9.50/1000)×(168/24) = 0.0665 m³.

3. Material. 316L if the stream is wet or chloride. Carbon steel only if the stream is a dry hydrocarbon or a dry gas. Corrosion allowance 0 mm on 316L, 1.5 mm on carbon steel. Pit rate is not measured.

4. Nozzles. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.31 mm. That is not a specification. Nozzle is DN15. No flange class. Atmospheric tube or hose. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature where the node is a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. No valve. Open to the process, not the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. Shell volume over flow is not the residence.

# BUCL

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 0.18 kg/d. Mass out 0.18 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0237 kW. In: IA-BUOH 0 kg/d (n-butanol); IA-HCL-BU 0.05 kg/d (HCl to chlorobutane); IA-BUOH-R 0.13 kg/d (butanol product). Out: IA-BUCL 0.1269 kg/d (1-chlorobutane); IA-BUCL-H2O 0.02471 kg/d (chlorination water); IA-BUCL-EX 0.02834 kg/d (unreacted butanol).

2. Size.
- In 0.1800 kg/d, out 0.1800 kg/d. No holdup claimed.
- C₄H₉OH + HCl → C₄H₉Cl + H₂O. HCl limits. Unused butanol goes to the solvent store.

3. Material. Glass or PVC-U, per AQS-STD-002 rule M1. No 316L. Solvent or oxidizer: PVC compatibility with the named chemical is not checked; glass where it is not compatible. Corrosion allowance does not apply.

4. Nozzles. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.04 mm. That is not a specification. Nozzle is DN15. No flange class. Atmospheric tube or hose. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. No trip is specified.

6. Relief. Atmospheric. No vent edge. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.
7. Code. Shop. Not stamped. The unit is retired from the graph; no envelope is issued.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

# LIGNO_PRE

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 9.14 kg/d. Mass out 9.14 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.8000 kW. In: IA-RES-01 8.69 kg/d (lignocellulose residue); IA-CLOSE-5 0.4491 kg/d (hopper fines to the pretreat). Out: IA-LIG-01 3.87 kg/d (lignin); IA-CEL-01 3.18 kg/d (cellulose); IA-SUGAR 1.00 kg/d (reducing sugars); IA-SUGAR-ABE 0.6400 kg/d (sugar to ABE); 2 more.

2. Size.
- V = (9.14/1000)×(168/24) = 0.0640 m³. 7 days of throughput.

3. Material. HDPE or PVC for the water. The frame is not the wetted part.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN15. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.30 mm, raised to DN15. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. Holdup is 7 days of throughput. The vessel has not been built, so this is not a measured volume.

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 7200 kg/d. Mass out 7200 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.3000 kW. In: IA-NFT-IN 7200 kg/d (film supply). Out: IA-NFT-OUT 7200 kg/d (film return).

2. Size.
- Envelope 6000×1000×300 mm, 1.80 m³. That box over this flow would be 6.0 h if it were full. It is not a residence time.

4. Nozzles. Origin 17600, 8200, 6000 mm. Size 6000 × 1000 × 300 mm. Inlets are the center of the −Y face. Outlets are the center of the +Y face. Atmospheric joints are hose. DN is copied from the edge and is not recalculated.
- N1, IA-NFT-IN, inlet, -Y face center, 20600, 8200, 6150 mm. Height 150 mm from the bottom. Bore not calculated. Rating hose, atmospheric.
- N2, IA-NFT-OUT, outlet, +Y face center, 20600, 9200, 6150 mm. Height 150 mm from the bottom. Bore not calculated. Rating hose, atmospheric.
5. Instruments. No trip is specified.

6. Relief. Atmospheric. No vent edge. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.
7. Code. Shop. Not stamped. The envelope is `cad/stl/NFT.stl`.

8. Open items. None.

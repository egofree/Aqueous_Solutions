# MEAM

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 0.07 kg/d. Mass out 0.07 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0025 kW. In: IA-MEOH-MEAM 0.04 kg/d (methanol to methylamine); IA-NH3-MEAM 0.03 kg/d (ammonia to methylamine). Out: IA-MEAM 0.03877 kg/d (methylamine); IA-MEAM-H2O 0.02249 kg/d (methylamine water); IA-NH3-RET 0.008739 kg/d (unreacted ammonia).

2. Size.
- In 0.0700 kg/d, out 0.0700 kg/d. No holdup claimed.
- CH₃OH + NH₃ → CH₃NH₂ + H₂O. Methanol limits. Excess ammonia returns to WS_AMMONIA. Water goes to the roof tank.

3. Material. Glass or PVC-U, per AQS-STD-002 rule M1. No 316L. Solvent or oxidizer: PVC compatibility with the named chemical is not checked; glass where it is not compatible. Corrosion allowance does not apply.

4. Nozzles. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.03 mm. That is not a specification. Nozzle is DN15. No flange class. Atmospheric tube or hose. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. No trip is specified.

6. Relief. Atmospheric. No vent edge. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.
7. Code. Shop. Not stamped. The envelope is `cad/stl/MEAM.stl`.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

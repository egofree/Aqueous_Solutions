# OIL_PYRO

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 34.40 kg/d. Mass out 34.40 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0000 kW. In: IA-OIL-01 33.00 kg/d (Dryer → pyro); C29_TO_PYRO 1.40 kg/d (C29 heavy cut). Out: IA-OIL-02 28.90 kg/d (Pyro → frac); IA-CHAR 5.50 kg/d (char reductant).

2. Size.
- V = (34.40/1000)×(1/24) = 0.0014 m³. 1 h.

3. Material. Carbon steel. Hydrocarbon. No aqueous chloride in this vessel.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN15. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.58 mm, raised to DN15. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. Holdup is 1 h. The vessel has not been built, so this is not a measured volume.

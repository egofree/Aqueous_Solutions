# OIL_PYRO

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 38.87 kg/d. Mass out 38.87 kg/d. Electrical duty 1.6640 kW. In: C29_TO_PYRO 5.873 kg/d (C29 heavy cut); IA-OIL-01 33 kg/d (Dryer → pyro). Out: IA-OIL-02 33.37 kg/d (Pyro → frac); IA-CHAR 5.2 kg/d (char reductant); IA-SIC-C 0.2999 kg/d (carbon reagent).

2. Size.
- V = (34.40/1000)×(1/24) = 0.0014 m³. 1 h. Density 1000 kg/m³.

3. Material. Carbon steel. Hydrocarbon. No aqueous chloride in this vessel. Corrosion allowance 1.5 mm. Pit rate is not measured.

4. Nozzles. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.58 mm. That is not a specification. Nozzle is DN15. No flange class. Atmospheric tube or hose. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Temperature or current. High temperature trips the power. There is no liquid-level trip.

6. Relief. Atmospheric. No vent edge. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.
7. Code. Shop. Not stamped. The envelope is `cad/stl/OIL_PYRO.stl`.

8. Open items. The holdup assumes 1000 kg/m³. The vessel is not built.

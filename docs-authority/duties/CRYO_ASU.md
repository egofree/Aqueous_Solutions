# CRYO_ASU

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BF. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 1902.7 kg/d. Mass out 1902.7 kg/d. Electrical duty 18.00 kW. In: IA-EXP-OUT 1575.5 kg/d (expanded gas); IA-NOX-N2 243.9 kg/d (scrubbed flue nitrogen to the cold box); IA-NOX-CO2 30.47 kg/d (scrubbed CO2 to the cold box); IA-CO2-RET 30.00 kg/d (CO2 return); 6 more. Out: IA-EXP-IN 1575.5 kg/d (cold gas); IA-ASU-N2 243.3 kg/d (nitrogen product); IA-ASU-O2 48.05 kg/d (oxygen product); IA-ASU-CO2 30.47 kg/d (CO2 product); 5 more.

2. Size.
- Column diameter 150 mm. 20 trays × 0.3 m = 6.0 m. The column is a pipe beside the 600×600×800 mm base. Not a packed scrubber.

3. Material. 316L. Condensation. No carbon steel. Corrosion allowance is not set.

4. Nozzles. Feed DN25 at the column base. Product nozzles DN15. Rating is not set. The vent returns to the column feed, not the room.

5. Instruments. Pressure and temperature. High pressure trips the feed.

6. Relief. The set point is not assigned. DN15 back to the suction of this loop. Not to the room and not to a stack. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. Tray efficiency is not measured.

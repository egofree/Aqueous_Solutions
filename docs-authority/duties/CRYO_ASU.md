# CRYO_ASU

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 598.0 kg/d. Mass out 598.0 kg/d. Electrical duty 18.00 kW. In: IA-EXP-OUT 271.3 kg/d (expanded gas); IA-NOX-N2 243.9 kg/d (scrubbed flue nitrogen to the cold box); IA-NOX-CO2 30.00 kg/d (scrubbed CO2 to the cold box); IA-CO2-RET 30.00 kg/d (CO2 return); 6 more. Out: IA-EXP-IN 271.3 kg/d (cold gas); IA-ASU-N2 243.3 kg/d (nitrogen product); IA-ASU-O2 48.05 kg/d (oxygen product); IA-ASU-CO2 30.00 kg/d (CO2 product); 5 more.

2. Size.
- In 598.0 kg/d, out 598.0 kg/d. No holdup claimed.

3. Material. 316L. Condensation. No carbon steel.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN15. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 2.42 mm, raised to DN15. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

# DUAL_ROOT

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 20.00 kg/d. Mass out 20.00 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.3000 kW. In: IA-DR-IN 20.00 kg/d (correction supply). Out: IA-DR-OUT 20.00 kg/d (correction return, not to the tank).

2. Size.
- Envelope 6000×2000×600 mm, 7.20 m³. Residence = 7.20 / (20.00/1000/24) = 8640.0 h.

3. Material. HDPE or PVC for the water. The frame is not the wetted part.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN15. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.44 mm, raised to DN15. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 15.9 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. The size basis is an assumption. This unit has not been measured.

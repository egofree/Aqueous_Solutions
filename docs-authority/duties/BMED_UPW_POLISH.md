# BMED_UPW_POLISH

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 140.5 kg/d. Mass out 140.5 kg/d. Electrical duty 6.00 kW. In: S25 140.5 kg/d (VCD distillate). Out: S01 67.30 kg/d (UPW); IA-UPW-FAB 60.00 kg/d (fab UPW); IA-UPW-REMIN 10.00 kg/d (remineralizer); IA-UPW-REJ 3.22 kg/d (polish reject).

2. Size.
- In 140.5 kg/d, out 140.5 kg/d. No holdup claimed.

3. Material. 316L where the stream is wet.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN15. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 1.17 mm, raised to DN15. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

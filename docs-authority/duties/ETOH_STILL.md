Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BF. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 0.4600 kg/d. Mass out 0.4600 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.3000 kW. In: IA-ETOH-S 0.4600 kg/d (ethanol feed). Out: IA-ETOH 0.2000 kg/d (ethanol); IA-ETOH-AC 0.1600 kg/d (ethanol to acetic); IA-ETOH-R 0.1000 kg/d (ethanol product).

2. Size.
- Envelope 400×400×1500 mm. Taken as a column, the width is 400 mm and the height is 1.50 m. Stages and reflux are not on the node.

4. Nozzles. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.07 mm. That is not a specification. Nozzle is DN15. Rating is not set. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. No trip is specified.

6. Relief. The set point is not assigned. The vent returns to the condenser or the fermenter, not the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. Stages and reflux are not set.

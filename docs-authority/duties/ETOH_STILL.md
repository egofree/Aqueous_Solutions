# ETOH_STILL

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 0.46 kg/d. Mass out 0.46 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.3000 kW. In: IA-ETOH-S 0.46 kg/d (ethanol feed). Out: IA-ETOH 0.2 kg/d (ethanol); IA-ETOH-AC 0.16 kg/d (ethanol to acetic); IA-ETOH-R 0.1 kg/d (ethanol product).

2. Size.
- Envelope 400×400×1500 mm. Taken as a column, the width is 400 mm and the height is 1.50 m. Stages and reflux are not on the node.

3. Material. Borosilicate glass 3.3 column, reboiler, and condenser; PTFE gaskets; steel guard frame not wetted (AQS-STD-002 section 4.2). No metal wetted part.

4. Nozzles. Origin 22300, 4200, 6000 mm. Size 400 × 400 × 1500 mm. Inlets are the center of the −Y face. Outlets are the center of the +Y face. Atmospheric joints are hose. DN is copied from the edge and is not recalculated.
- N1, IA-ETOH-S, inlet, -Y face center, 22500, 4200, 6750 mm. Height 750 mm from the bottom. Bore not calculated. Rating hose, atmospheric.
- N2, IA-ETOH, outlet, +Y face center, 22500, 4600, 6750 mm. Height 750 mm from the bottom. Bore not calculated. Rating hose, atmospheric.
- N3, IA-ETOH-AC, outlet, +Y face center, 22500, 4600, 6750 mm. Height 750 mm from the bottom. Bore not calculated. Rating hose, atmospheric.
- N4, IA-ETOH-R, outlet, +Y face center, 22500, 4600, 6750 mm. Height 750 mm from the bottom. Bore not calculated. Rating hose, atmospheric.
5. Instruments. No trip is specified.

6. Relief. Atmospheric. No vent edge. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.
7. Code. Shop. Not stamped. The envelope is `cad/stl/ETOH_STILL.stl`.

8. Open items. Stages and reflux are not set.

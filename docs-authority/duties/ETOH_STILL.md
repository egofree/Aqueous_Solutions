# ETOH_STILL

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 0.4600 kg/d. Mass out 0.4600 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.3000 kW. In: IA-ETOH-S 0.4600 kg/d (ethanol feed). Out: IA-ETOH 0.2000 kg/d (ethanol); IA-ETOH-AC 0.1600 kg/d (ethanol to acetic); IA-ETOH-R 0.1000 kg/d (ethanol product).

2. Size.
- Envelope 400×400×1500 mm, 0.2400 m³. Residence = 0.2400 / (0.4600/1000/24) = 12521.7 h.

3. Material. HDPE or PVC for the water. The frame is not the wetted part.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN15. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.07 mm, raised to DN15. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 24.7 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. The size basis is an assumption. This unit has not been measured.

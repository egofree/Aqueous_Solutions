# PEANUT

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 100 kg/d. Mass out 100 kg/d. Electrical duty 2.6583 kW. In: IA-PN-S 98.36 kg/d (bed spill); IA-PEANUT-CO2 1.654 kg/d (CO2 uptake). Out: IA-PN-R 0.2 kg/d (peanut); IA-PEANUT-O2 1.203 kg/d (photosynthetic O2); IA-PEANUT-VAP 92.95 kg/d (transpiration); IA-PN-TRIM 5.663 kg/d (crop residue).

2. Size.
- In 0.2000 kg/d, out 0.2000 kg/d. No holdup claimed.
- 10 m² at 0.02 kg/m²/day = 0.20 kg/d seed. The legume residue is lower by that 0.05 kg/d.

3. Material. HDPE or PVC for the water. The frame is not the wetted part. Corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymer.

4. Nozzles. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.04 mm. That is not a specification. Nozzle is DN15. No flange class. Atmospheric tube or hose. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. No trip is specified.

6. Relief. Atmospheric. No vent edge. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.
7. Code. Shop. Not stamped. The unit is retired from the graph; no envelope is issued.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

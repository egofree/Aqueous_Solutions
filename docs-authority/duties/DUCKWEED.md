Out: IA-WEED-FISH 0.5 kg/d (duckweed); IA-WEED-HEN 0.2 kg/d (duckweed); IA-WEED-GOAT 0.15 kg/d (duckweed); IA-WEED-HAB 0.05 kg/d (duckweed reserve); 1 more.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped. The unit is retired from the graph; no envelope is issued.

8. Open items. If this node is also a reservoir, that volume is not the two-minute holdup.

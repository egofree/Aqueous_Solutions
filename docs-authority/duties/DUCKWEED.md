# DUCKWEED

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 9697.9 kg/d. Mass out 9697.9 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.1000 kW. In: IA-BIO-WEED 9697.0 kg/d (nitrate water); IA-BED-WEED 0.9000 kg/d (nutrient water). Out: IA-WEED-SUMP 9697.0 kg/d (raceway return); IA-WEED-FISH 0.5000 kg/d (duckweed); IA-WEED-HEN 0.2000 kg/d (duckweed); IA-WEED-GOAT 0.1500 kg/d (duckweed); 1 more.

2. Size.
- Circulation 9697.9 kg/d. Two-minute holdup 0.0135 m³. A 20-day volume at this rate is not a tank.

3. Material. HDPE or PVC for the water. The frame is not the wetted part.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN15. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 9.76 mm, raised to DN15. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. If this node is also a reservoir, that volume is not the two-minute holdup.

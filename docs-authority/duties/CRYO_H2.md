# CRYO_H2

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 11.34 kg/d. Mass out 11.34 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.9327 kW. In: S64 1.975 kg/d (H2 normal path); IA-ELY-H2 9.361 kg/d (electrolyser H2). Out: S45 0 kg/d (H2 fuel); S49 4.8 kg/d (H2 fuel); S74 6.536 kg/d (H2 for MeOH).

2. Size.
- Not a packed tower. The column diameter and the tray count are on CRYO_ASU only.

3. Material. 316L, annealed, nickel toward the top of the grade range; no ferritic or high-strength steel. Warm-up range governs embrittlement. Pressure held. AQS-REV-H2-001.

4. Nozzles. No duct bore. Density is not on the edge, and 1.2 kg/m³ is not used. No flange class. The vent stays on the gas edge, not the room.

5. Instruments. Pressure and temperature. High pressure trips the feed.

6. Relief. Atmospheric. No vent edge. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.
7. Code. Shop. Not stamped. The solid is still an envelope. `cad/stl/CRYO_H2.stl` is the outer envelope only; nothing inside it is modelled yet.

8. Open items. No diameter on this node.

# CRYO_AHU

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 135.7 kg/d. Mass out 135.7 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0000 kW. In: IA-AHU-AIR 79.04 kg/d (outdoor air moisture); IA-TERR-VAP 40.00 kg/d (greenhouse transpiration); IA-BED-VAP 16.70 kg/d (bed transpiration). Out: S68 78.99 kg/d (flue condensate); IA-HVAC-COND 56.70 kg/d (AHU condensate to the water cycle); IA-AHU-POT 0.0521 kg/d (condensable slip).

2. Size.
- Gas 4.71 m³/h. Tower area at 1.5 m/s = 4.7/3600/1.5 = 0.0009 m². Diameter 33 mm.
- No liquor inlet. No sump volume is invented from the gas mass.

3. Material. 316L. Condensation. No carbon steel.

4. Nozzles. Gas duct DN15 at 10 m/s and 1.2 kg/m³. Calculated bore 13 mm. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 into the loop, not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 back to the suction of this loop. Not to the room and not to a stack.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. Gas volume uses 1.2 kg/m³ because the edge has no m³/h. Tower velocity 1.5 m/s is the packing assumption. Bed height is not calculated. Duct bore uses 1.2 kg/m³.

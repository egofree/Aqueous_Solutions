# QUAT

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 0.4200 kg/d. Mass out 0.4000 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.1000 kW. In: IA-IMID 0.2600 kg/d (1-methylimidazole); IA-BUCL 0.1600 kg/d (1-chlorobutane). Out: IA-BMIM 0.3000 kg/d ([Bmim]Cl); IA-CLOSE-13 0.0600 kg/d (quaternization brine to the nutrient tank); IA-QUAT-H2O 0.0400 kg/d (quaternization water).

2. Size.
- Envelope 400×400×800 mm, 0.1280 m³. Residence = 0.1280 / (0.4200/1000/24) = 7314.3 h.

3. Material. HDPE or PVC for the water. The frame is not the wetted part.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN15. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.06 mm, raised to DN15. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. The size basis is an assumption. This unit has not been measured.

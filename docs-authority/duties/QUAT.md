# QUAT

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BF. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 0.4200 kg/d. Mass out 0.4200 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.1000 kW. In: IA-IMID 0.2600 kg/d (1-methylimidazole); IA-BUCL 0.1600 kg/d (1-chlorobutane). Out: IA-BMIM 0.3150 kg/d ([Bmim]Cl); IA-CLOSE-13 0.0630 kg/d (quaternization brine to the nutrient tank); IA-QUAT-H2O 0.0420 kg/d (quaternization water).

2. Size.
- Envelope 400×400×800 mm, 0.1280 m³. That box over this flow would be 7314.3 h if it were full. It is not a residence time.
- Seven-day charge V = (0.4200/1000)×(168/24) = 0.0029 m³.

3. Material. Glass or 316L. Chlorobutane and imidazole. Not a farm gutter. Corrosion allowance is not set.

4. Nozzles. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.06 mm. That is not a specification. Nozzle is DN15. Rating is not set. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. No trip is specified.

6. Relief. Not a pressure vessel. A vent, if the shell is closed, returns to the process. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. Envelope volume over flow is not a residence time. The three outs sum to the feed. That is a closure, not a measured yield.

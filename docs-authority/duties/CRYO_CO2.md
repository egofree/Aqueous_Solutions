# CRYO_CO2

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 50.18 kg/d. Mass out 50.18 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0000 kW. In: IA-ASU-CO2 30.00 kg/d (CO2 product); S67B 13.30 kg/d (CO2-rich gas after KO); S82 6.88 kg/d (CO₂ return from MeOH FC). Out: IA-CO2-RET 30.00 kg/d (CO2 return); S85 12.17 kg/d (CO2 product after FLUE_KO); S73 6.90 kg/d (CO2 feedstock); S04 1.11 kg/d (CO2).

2. Size.
- Gas 1.74 m³/h. Tower area at 1.5 m/s = 1.7/3600/1.5 = 0.0003 m². Diameter 20 mm.
- No liquor inlet. No sump volume is invented from the gas mass.

3. Material. 316L. Condensation. No carbon steel.

4. Nozzles. Gas duct DN15 at 10 m/s and 1.2 kg/m³. Calculated bore 8 mm. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 into the loop, not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 back to the suction of this loop. Not to the room and not to a stack.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. Gas volume uses 1.2 kg/m³ because the edge has no m³/h. Tower velocity 1.5 m/s is the packing assumption. Bed height is not calculated. Duct bore uses 1.2 kg/m³.

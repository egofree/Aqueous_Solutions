# IMID

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 0.5232 kg/d. Mass out 0.5232 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0236 kW. In: IA-GLX 0.252 kg/d (glyoxal); IA-CH2O 0 kg/d (formaldehyde); IA-MEAM 0.03877 kg/d (methylamine); IA-NH3-IMID 0.02 kg/d (ammonia to imidazole); 2 more. Out: IA-IMID 0.09642 kg/d (1-methylimidazole); IA-IMID-H2O 0.06347 kg/d (condensation water); IA-IMID-EX 0.3633 kg/d (unreacted reagents and molt liquor).

2. Size.
- In 0.5232 kg/d, out 0.5232 kg/d. No holdup claimed.
- Glyoxal + formaldehyde + methylamine + ammonia → 1-methylimidazole + 3 H₂O. Ammonia limits. Molt liquor is not a reactant. It leaves with the unused reagents.

3. Material. Glass or PVC-U, per AQS-STD-002 rule M1. No 316L. Solvent or oxidizer: PVC compatibility with the named chemical is not checked; glass where it is not compatible. Corrosion allowance does not apply.

4. Nozzles. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.07 mm. That is not a specification. Nozzle is DN15. No flange class. Atmospheric tube or hose. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. No trip is specified.

6. Relief. Atmospheric. No vent edge. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.
7. Code. Shop. Not stamped. The unit is retired from the graph; no envelope is issued.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

# NUTRIENT_DOSE

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 272.2 kg/d. Mass out 272.2 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.1000 kW. In: IA-COMP-OUT 140.6 kg/d (compost); IA-LEACH-BD 109.6 kg/d (bleed salts to nutrient); IA-NOX-N 20.00 kg/d (nitrate liquor); IA-CLOSE-0 1.30 kg/d (biofilter bleed to the nutrient tank); 3 more. Out: IA-NUT-BED 129.6 kg/d (nitrate and bleed salts); IA-NUT-LEACH 126.1 kg/d (nutrient surplus); IA-NUT-01 13.93 kg/d (digest surplus); S07 2.50 kg/d (nutrients).

2. Size.
- In 272.2 kg/d, out 272.2 kg/d. No holdup claimed.

3. Material. 316L where the stream is wet.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN15. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 1.64 mm, raised to DN15. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

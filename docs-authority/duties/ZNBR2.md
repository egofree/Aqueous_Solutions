# ZNBR2

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 861.0 kg/d. Mass out 861.0 kg/d. Electrical duty 6.67 kW. In: S69B 852.5 kg/d (NaBr after ORP); IA-ZNBR-MU 8.49 kg/d (electrolyte water make-up). Out: S69 852.5 kg/d (Br2 vapor); IA-ZNBR-BD 8.49 kg/d (electrolyte bleed).

2. Size.
- 6.67 kW. No exchanger area. A heat-transfer area does not belong on this class.
- Charge 6.6667 kW average. Discharge 5.0 kW. Peak 60 kW.

3. Material. No wetted alloy. Conductors are copper.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN15. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 2.91 mm, raised to DN15. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Current. Overcurrent opens the breaker.

6. Relief. Not a pressure vessel. No process vent.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. The size basis is an assumption. This unit has not been measured.

# SIC_FURNACE

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 0.8 kg/d. Mass out 0.8 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.3000 kW. In: IA-SIC-IN 0.5 kg/d (silica charge); IA-SIC-C 0.3 kg/d (carbon reagent). Out: IA-SIC-CO 0.466 kg/d (carbon monoxide); IA-SIC-OUT 0.334 kg/d (fired charge).

2. Size.
- SiO₂ + 3C → SiC + 2CO. 0.500 kg/d silica, 0.300 kg/d carbon, 0.334 kg/d SiC, 0.466 kg/d CO. Carbon is taken as char. CO leaves on IA-SIC-CO. The bed takes the resulting CO₂.
- Envelope 1500×1500×1800 mm is the furnace. It is not a liquid holdup.

3. Material. Graphite or refractory on the hot face. Frame is A500 behind a heat shield. Corrosion allowance 0 mm on the frame. Pit rate is not measured.

4. Nozzles. Origin 5900, 12000, -6000 mm. Size 1500 × 1500 × 1800 mm. Inlets are the center of the −Y face. Outlets are the center of the +Y face. Atmospheric joints are hose. DN is copied from the edge and is not recalculated.
- N1, IA-SIC-C, inlet, -Y face center, 6650, 12000, -5100 mm. Height 900 mm from the bottom. Bore not calculated. Rating hose, atmospheric.
- N2, IA-SIC-IN, inlet, -Y face center, 6650, 12000, -5100 mm. Height 900 mm from the bottom. Bore not calculated. Rating hose, atmospheric.
- N3, IA-SIC-CO, outlet, +Y face center, 6650, 13500, -5100 mm. Height 900 mm from the bottom. Bore not calculated. Rating hose, atmospheric.
- N4, IA-SIC-OUT, outlet, +Y face center, 6650, 13500, -5100 mm. Height 900 mm from the bottom. Bore not calculated. Rating hose, atmospheric.
5. Instruments. Bed temperature. High temperature trips the power.

6. Relief. Atmospheric. The vent is IA-SIC-CO to AFTERBURNER. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.
7. Code. Shop. Not stamped. The envelope is `cad/stl/SIC_FURNACE.stl`.

8. Open items. Neither is an assay.

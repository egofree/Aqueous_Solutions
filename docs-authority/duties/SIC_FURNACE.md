# SIC_FURNACE

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 0.5000 kg/d. Mass out 0.5000 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.3000 kW. In: IA-SIC-IN 0.5000 kg/d (silica charge). Out: IA-SIC-OUT 0.5000 kg/d (fired charge).

2. Size.
- Envelope 1500×1500×1800 mm, 4.05 m³. Residence = 4.05 / (0.5000/1000/24) = 194400.0 h.

3. Material. Graphite or refractory on the hot face. Frame is A500 behind a heat shield.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN15. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.07 mm, raised to DN15. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 27.7 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. 0.50 kg/d is the solid moved. Carbon in and CO out are not on the edge.

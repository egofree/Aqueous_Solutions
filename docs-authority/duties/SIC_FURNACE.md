# SIC_FURNACE

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 0.7999 kg/d. Mass out 0.7999 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.3000 kW. In: IA-SIC-IN 0.5000 kg/d (silica charge); IA-SIC-C 0.2999 kg/d (carbon reagent). Out: IA-SIC-CO 0.4662 kg/d (carbon monoxide); IA-SIC-OUT 0.3337 kg/d (fired charge).

2. Size.
- SiO₂ + 3C → SiC + 2CO. 0.500 kg/d silica, 0.300 kg/d carbon, 0.334 kg/d SiC, 0.466 kg/d CO. Carbon is taken as char. CO leaves on IA-SIC-CO. The bed takes the resulting CO₂.
- Envelope 1500×1500×1800 mm is the furnace. It is not a liquid holdup.

3. Material. Graphite or refractory on the hot face. Frame is A500 behind a heat shield. Corrosion allowance 0 mm on the frame. Pit rate is not measured.

4. Nozzles. The charge is solid. CO leaves on IA-SIC-CO. No liquid bore is calculated. No flange class.

5. Instruments. Bed temperature. High temperature trips the power.

6. Relief. Not a pressure vessel. CO leaves on IA-SIC-CO. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. Char is taken as carbon. The oxygen in the CO is the oxygen already in the flue air. Neither is an assay.

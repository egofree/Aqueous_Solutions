# EXPANDER

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BF. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 1575.5 kg/d. Mass out 1575.5 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.6140 kW. In: IA-EXP-IN 1575.5 kg/d (cold gas). Out: IA-EXP-OUT 1575.5 kg/d (expanded gas).

2. Size.
- Bore 50 mm, stroke 80 mm, 600 rpm, displacement 1.57 L/s. Inlet 500 kPa and 150 K. Density P/RT = 11.61 kg/m³. Shaft 614.4 W at 70% of the isentropic drop from 500 kPa to 130 kPa. Torque 614.4 / (600×2π/60) = 9.8 N·m. The 5 kW figure is the cold-box load, not this cylinder.

3. Material. 316L. Condensation. No carbon steel. Corrosion allowance is not set.

4. Nozzles. Displacement 1.57 L/s at 10 m/s is a 14.1 mm bore, so DN15. Density 11.61 kg/m³, not 1.2. Rating is not set. The vent returns to the cold box, not the room.

5. Instruments. Inlet pressure, discharge pressure, and speed. Overspeed or high inlet pressure trips the drive.

6. Relief. Process inlet is 500 kPa and discharge is 130 kPa. The set point is not assigned. Destination is the cold-box suction, not the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. Isentropic efficiency 70% is assumed.

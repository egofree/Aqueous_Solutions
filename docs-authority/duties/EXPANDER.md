# EXPANDER

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 271.3 kg/d. Mass out 271.3 kg/d. Electrical duty 5.00 kW. In: IA-EXP-IN 271.3 kg/d (cold gas). Out: IA-EXP-OUT 271.3 kg/d (expanded gas).

2. Size.
- Gas 9.42 m³/h. Tower area at 1.5 m/s = 9.4/3600/1.5 = 0.0017 m². Diameter 47 mm.
- No liquor inlet. No sump volume is invented from the gas mass.
- Bore 50 mm, stroke 80 mm, 600 rpm, displacement 1.57 L/s, shaft 5 kW.

3. Material. 316L. Condensation. No carbon steel.

4. Nozzles. Gas duct DN20 at 10 m/s and 1.2 kg/m³. Calculated bore 18 mm. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 into the loop, not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 14.9 kPa. DN15 back to the suction of this loop. Not to the room and not to a stack.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. Gas volume uses 1.2 kg/m³ because the edge has no m³/h. Tower velocity 1.5 m/s is the packing assumption. Bed height is not calculated. Cold-vapor density on the edge is 2.0 kg/m³. It is not measured. Duct bore uses 1.2 kg/m³.

# BAGHOUSE

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BE. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 7.77 kg/d. Mass out 7.77 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0000 kW. In: IA-MILL-LOSS 6.82 kg/d (mill dust); IA-FAB-LOSS 0.5100 kg/d (kerf); IA-WIRE-LOSS 0.4400 kg/d (draw scrap). Out: IA-BAG-01 7.77 kg/d (captured dust).

2. Size.
- In 7.77 kg/d, out 7.77 kg/d. No holdup claimed.

3. Material. 316L where the stream is wet.

4. Nozzles. Inlet and outlet DN15. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.28 mm, raised to DN15. Drain DN15. Vent DN15 to the suction or to the alkaline scrubber. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature on a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Design pressure 17.8 kPa. DN15 to the alkaline scrubber if the stream is wet, or back to the tank if it is hydrocarbon. Not to the room.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

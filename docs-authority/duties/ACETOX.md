# ACETOX

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 0.27 kg/d. Mass out 0.27 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0203 kW. In: IA-ETOH-AC 0.16 kg/d (ethanol to acetic); IA-AC-O2 0.11 kg/d (oxygen to acetic acid). Out: IA-ACOH 0.2064 kg/d (acetic acid); IA-AC-H2O 0.06193 kg/d (oxidation water); IA-AC-EX 0.001628 kg/d (unreacted ethanol).

2. Size.
- In 0.2700 kg/d, out 0.2700 kg/d. No holdup claimed.
- C₂H₅OH + O₂ → CH₃COOH + H₂O. The inlet is oxygen from HDR_O2, not air. Oxygen limits. Unused ethanol goes to the solvent store.

3. Material. Glass or PVC-U, per AQS-STD-002 rule M1. No 316L. Solvent or oxidizer: PVC compatibility with the named chemical is not checked; glass where it is not compatible. Corrosion allowance does not apply.

4. Nozzles. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.05 mm. That is not a specification. Nozzle is DN15. No flange class. Atmospheric tube or hose. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. No trip is specified.

6. Relief. Atmospheric. No vent edge. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.
7. Code. Shop. Not stamped. The unit is retired from the graph; no envelope is issued.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

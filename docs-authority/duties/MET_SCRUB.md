# MET_SCRUB

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BF. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 71606.5 kg/d. Mass out 71606.5 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0000 kW. In: S62 51145.7 kg/d (MFG metal-aisle capture feed); S71 20458.3 kg/d (metal-aisle fume / mist / dust); IA-DRAW-FUME 2.46 kg/d (draw fume). Out: IA-MET-AIR-RET 71604.0 kg/d (scrubbed metal-aisle air returned to the hall); S72 2.44 kg/d (metal fines and aqueous scrub liquor); S72B 0.0158 kg/d (organic mist).

2. Size.
- Gas 2520.0 m³/h. Tower area at 1.5 m/s = 2520.0/3600/1.5 = 0.4667 m². Diameter 771 mm.
- Liquid in 2.46 kg/d. Sump at 10 min: V = (2.46/1000)×(10/60)/24 = 0.00002 m³.
- Packed height 1.0 m plus a demister. The duty is mist and fines, not a high-NTU acid absorption.

3. Material. 316L shell. Wet gas. Packing is polymer. Carbon steel pits. Corrosion allowance is not set.

4. Nozzles. Gas duct DN300 at 10 m/s. Bore from 2520.0 m³/h is 299 mm. Drain DN15. Rating is not set. Vent DN15 into the suction of this loop, not the room.

5. Instruments. Differential pressure across the packing, and pH if there is a liquor loop. High differential pressure trips the fan.

6. Relief. The set point is not assigned. DN15 back to the suction of this loop. Not to the room and not to a stack. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. Tower velocity 1.5 m/s is the packing assumption.

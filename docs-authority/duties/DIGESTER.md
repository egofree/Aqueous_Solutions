# DIGESTER

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 1.15 kg/d. Mass out 1.15 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.4000 kW. In: IA-FERM-RES 0.3454 kg/d (fermenter residue); IA-CLOSE-8 0.2900 kg/d (shop scrap to the digester); IA-IL-SPENT 0.2328 kg/d (spent [Bmim]OAc); IA-ABE-STILL 0.1800 kg/d (ABE stillage); 1 more. Out: IA-DIG-GAS 0.6254 kg/d (biogas from the biological feeds); IA-DIG-SOL 0.5228 kg/d (shop scrap and spent solvent).

2. Size.
- V = (1.15/1000)×(480/24) = 0.0230 m³. 20 days on the whole wet feed, including the passthrough. The node class is farm.

3. Material. HDPE or PVC for the water. The frame is not the wetted part. Corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymer.

4. Nozzles. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.11 mm. That is not a specification. Nozzle is DN15. No flange class. Atmospheric tube or hose. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Temperature and pressure. High pressure trips the feed. Gas stays on the gas edge, not the room.

6. Relief. No valve. Biogas leaves on IA-DIG-GAS. Not a coded vessel. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. Biological feeds still leave entirely as biogas. IA-FERM-RES 0.3454 kg/d, IA-STILL 0.1000 kg/d, and IA-ABE-STILL 0.1800 kg/d sum to IA-DIG-GAS 0.6254 kg/d. There is no volatile-solids measurement on those three edges, so no digestate split is calculated. IA-DIG-GAS composition is the liquid placeholder, not a gas analysis. Shop scrap and spent solvent are on IA-DIG-SOL, not in the gas. No origin. The node has no envelope, so it was not placed.

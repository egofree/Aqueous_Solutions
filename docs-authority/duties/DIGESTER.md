# DIGESTER

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BF. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 0.9154 kg/d. Mass out 0.9154 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.4000 kW. In: IA-FERM-RES 0.3454 kg/d (fermenter residue); IA-CLOSE-8 0.2900 kg/d (shop scrap to the digester); IA-ABE-STILL 0.1800 kg/d (ABE stillage); IA-STILL 0.1000 kg/d (stillage). Out: IA-DIG-GAS 0.9154 kg/d (biogas).

2. Size.
- V = (0.9154/1000)×(480/24) = 0.0183 m³. 20 days. The node class is farm. This holdup is the digester holdup.

3. Material. HDPE or PVC for the water. The frame is not the wetted part. Corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymer.

4. Nozzles. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.09 mm. That is not a specification. Nozzle is DN15. Rating is not set. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Temperature and pressure. High pressure trips the feed. Gas stays on the gas edge, not the room.

6. Relief. The set point is not assigned. Gas stays on the biogas edge. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. The whole wet feed leaves as biogas. Digestate is not a separate edge.

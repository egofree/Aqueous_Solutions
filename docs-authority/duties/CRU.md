# CRU

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev AC. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 28.13 kg/d. Mass out 28.13 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0000 kW. In: S66 27.37 kg/d (scrub liquor → chlorate finish); TI_CELL_CL2_TO_CRU 0 kg/d (chlorine from Kroll chlorinator / Mg recycle); MO_CELL_ANODE_GAS_TO_CRU 0 kg/d (anode gas (Cl2 or O2) from molten-salt pot); AL_POT_CL2 0 kg/d (Cl2 return); 2 more. Out: CRU_TO_VDF 0.3322 kg/d (chlorine); CRU_TO_TFE 0.2836 kg/d (chlorine); IA-CRU-01 27.51 kg/d (sodium chlorate liquor).

2. Size.
- In 27.36 kg/d, out 27.36 kg/d. No holdup claimed.

3. Material. PVC-U wetted, per AQS-STD-002 rules M1 and M2. No metal wetted part. The graph gives no temperature for this unit; above 60 °C the thermoplastic is CPVC, never metal. Corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymer.

4. Nozzles. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.52 mm. That is not a specification. Nozzle is DN15. No flange class. Atmospheric tube or hose. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. Level and temperature where the node is a liquid holdup. High level trips the inlet.

6. Relief. Atmospheric. No vent edge. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.
7. Code. Shop. Not stamped. The envelope is `cad/stl/CRU.stl`.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

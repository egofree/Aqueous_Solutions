Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 2.40 kg/d. Mass out 2.40 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0500 kW. In: IA-URINE 1.20 kg/d (urine); IA-FEC-01 0.8000 kg/d (feces); IA-COVER 0.2000 kg/d (cover crop); IA-NPK-0 0.2000 kg/d (bed return). Out: IA-TOIL-URN 1.30 kg/d (urine to the biofilter); IA-TOIL-SOL 0.9000 kg/d (covered solids); IA-URN-STR 0.2000 kg/d (urine slip).

2. Size.
- In 2.40 kg/d, out 2.40 kg/d. No holdup claimed.

5. Instruments. No trip is specified.

6. Relief. Atmospheric. No vent edge. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.
7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

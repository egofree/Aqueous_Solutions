# COMPOST_TOILET

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BH. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 1.948 kg/d. Mass out 1.948 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.0500 kW. In: IA-FEC-01 0.128 kg/d (faeces (compost-toilet share)); IA-URINE 1.42 kg/d (urine (compost-toilet share)); IA-COVER 0.2 kg/d (cover crop); IA-NPK-0 0.2 kg/d (bed return). Out: IA-TOIL-SOL 0.528 kg/d (compost-toilet solids and cover to the barrier train (not to FARM)); IA-TOIL-URN 1.42 kg/d (compost-toilet urine to pasteurisation).

2. Size.
- In 2.40 kg/d, out 2.40 kg/d. No holdup claimed.

3. Material. HDPE or PVC for the water. The frame is not the wetted part. Corrosion allowance does not apply to the polymer.

4. Nozzles. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.15 mm. That is not a specification. Nozzle is DN15. No flange class. Atmospheric tube or hose. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. No trip is specified.

6. Relief. Atmospheric. No vent edge. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.
7. Code. Shop. Not stamped. The envelope is `cad/stl/COMPOST_TOILET.stl`.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

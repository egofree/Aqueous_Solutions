# ACEX

Standard: AQS-STD-001 Rev A. Graph Rev BG. Study. Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Mass in 0.5619 kg/d. Mass out 0.5619 kg/d. Electrical duty 0.1000 kW. In: IA-BMIM 0.3019 kg/d ([Bmim]Cl); IA-ACOH 0.2600 kg/d (acetic acid). Out: IA-IL 0.3427 kg/d ([Bmim]OAc to the pretreat); IA-IL-BRINE 0.2192 kg/d (HCl and unused acetic acid).

2. Size.
- In 0.5619 kg/d, out 0.5619 kg/d. No holdup claimed.
- [Bmim]Cl + acetic acid → [Bmim]OAc + HCl. Acetate 0.3427 kg/d to the pretreat and out again as spent solvent. HCl plus unused acetic acid 0.2192 kg/d to the leach. The pen does not take this stream.

3. Material. Glass or 316L. Solvent. Not a farm gutter. Corrosion allowance 0 mm. Pit rate is not measured.

4. Nozzles. At 1.5 m/s and 1000 kg/m³ the bore is 0.07 mm. That is not a specification. Nozzle is DN15. No flange class. Atmospheric tube or hose. Not to the room.

5. Instruments. No trip is specified.

6. Relief. Not a pressure vessel. A vent, if the shell is closed, returns to the process. Not to the room. Stored design_kPa is the old head-plus-10 figure. It is not a set point.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped.

8. Open items. No holdup basis for this class.

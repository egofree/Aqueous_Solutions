## 1. Purpose and scope

This list gives the units that turn electrowon and remelted metal into wire, sheet and powder, the tools that do the work, the streams that join them to the rest of the plant, and checks of those streams against the separation rules. It covers the units of sector MFG in the melt, forming, air-handling, scrubber and heat-recovery classes. The shop, the blow molder and the electrode coating booth are in the shop equipment list AQS-EQP-MFG-001. Bulk melting is done by the foundry, not here.

## 2. Terms

- Average load: the continuous electrical load of a unit on the graph (`continuous_kw`), equal to its daily energy over 24 h.
- Nameplate: the rated input of a tool, from the tool list AQS-MFG-010.
- Stream: a graph edge, named by its stream identifier.

## 6. Separation checks

The separation rules of the hazard separation register AQS-HSE-001 are checked against the graph as follows.

- The melt furnace MET_MELT takes mass only from EW_9 and GAS_ATOMIZER. No polymer or oil unit feeds it.
- The air handler MET_AHU sends air only to MET_SCRUB and takes it back only from MET_SCRUB. It shares no return with the habitation or the farm.
- The heat recovery exchanger MET_HX_Q4 sends thermal duty to Q4_BUS. No flue or melt gas goes to the Q4 header.

## 7. Open items

- The tool list sizes each tool from its energy per kilogram and gives its nameplate. The die schedule of the wire drawer, the gauge and width range of the cold mill, and the batch rate of the atomiser are not yet designed. They are recorded as an owner question in `REWRITE_TODO.md`.
- The efficiencies, the flow stress and the atomising gas ratio in AQS-MFG-010 are assumptions until trial runs are made (A-M-177).
- The streams without flow in Section 4 are routes kept on the graph for later use; each needs a rate or a decision to retire it.

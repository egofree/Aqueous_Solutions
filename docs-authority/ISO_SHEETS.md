## 3. Basis

Each line follows the route rule of AQS-ISO-001. Metal elbows are ASME B16.9 long-radius elbows; thermoplastic elbows take 0.5 times the outside diameter (A-M-121). Pipe comes in random lengths of 6 m, every nozzle has a flange, bolting is to ASME B16.5 Class 150, and outside diameters are to ASME B36.10M.
The sheets do not check clashes with the structure or with other lines along the rack. That check belongs to task X-PIP-04, together with the supports and the stress analysis.

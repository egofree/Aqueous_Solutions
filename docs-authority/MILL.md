# Mill module

Document number: AQS-TOOL-002
Revision: A
Date: 2026-10-11
Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Cut fixtures, screen plates, and mask blanks. It does not cut a 100 µm line. A shop mill repeats to about 0.05 mm. The 100 µm diode window is an exposure, not a milled groove.

2. Size. Table 800 × 300 mm. Travel 400 mm in X, 150 mm in Y, 100 mm in Z. Spindle 1.1 kW, 500 to 3000 rpm. A 10 mm end mill in steel at 0.05 mm depth is about 150 N. Torque at 5 mm radius is 0.75 N·m, 0.2 kW at 2500 rpm. The motor covers it. Leadscrews are 16 mm, 2 mm pitch, cut on the lathe. One division on a 50-line dial is 0.04 mm.

3. Material. Frame A500, 1200 × 600 × 800, 100 × 100 × 6. Column is the same tube, welded. Table is 12 mm plate.

4. Nozzles. One SB350. No process fluid. Coolant is a jug.

5. Instruments. Spindle speed. Overload trips the contactor.

6. Relief. None.

7. Code. Shop.

8. Open items. The dial is not a measured 0.04 mm. The screw and the motor are.

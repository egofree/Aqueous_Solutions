## 1. Purpose and scope

This page is the mechanical, electrical and plumbing (MEP) study of the habitation and farm floor. It counts the plumbing fixtures, gives the main pipe and duct sizes, and states which loads the life panel serves. It is phase D of the fit-out plan AQS-LIFE-001. Shop furniture and interiors, phase E, are not covered.

## 2. Occupancy

The fixtures are counted for the study crew of 4 persons, which the graph holds as `HABITATION.params.crew_study`. The berth count is not yet fixed. The egress study AQS-EGR-001 uses a larger design occupancy, found by the area method; that figure sizes the exits and exit signs, not the plumbing.

## 5. Electrical

The life panel LP-L1 is fed from the inverter bus (AQS-ELC-001). Its voltage is to be chosen for the site. It serves the habitation and farm air handlers, the grow lighting, the drinking water booster and ultraviolet lamp, the receptacles and lighting of the wet room, galley and sleeping area, and the emergency lighting and exit signs required by NFPA 70 article 700 and AQS-EGR-001. The service lift has its own feeder from a motor control centre.
The rectifiers, the metal melting, the pyrolysis reactor, the backup generator and the fuel cell are not on the life panel. The grow lighting is to be fed from the direct-current bus (stream S20) when that bus is available. The power of each life load is not yet set.

## 6. Services

Clean water flows by gravity from the water tower `ROOF_TANK` through the utility shaft to the habitation and the farm; drinking water comes from `REMIN` (AQS-WTR-001). Grey water from the wet room and galley goes to `GREY_TREAT`, and black water to `BLACK_DIGEST`. The Q4 header supplies heat to the air handlers. Oxygen, nitrogen and carbon dioxide reach the floor on their own tiers of the life rack. Process air never returns to the habitation.

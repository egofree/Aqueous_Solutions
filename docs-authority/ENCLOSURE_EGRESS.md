## Floor plates

Every suspended level is a 300 mm concrete slab on the AQS-BLD-016 steel beams over the 24 x 18 m plate (432 m2); reservoir floors and walls are ACI 350 water-retaining; L-2 bears on the AQS-BLD-017 mat. Horizontal assemblies: 2 h between levels (AQS-HSE-001), 3 h between life and process levels.

## Enclosure

## 1. Purpose and scope

This page checks that every level of the plant building can be evacuated, and states the construction of the floors and of the outer envelope. Egress is the route by which occupants leave a building in an emergency. For each level the check counts the occupants, the stairs that serve the level, the stair width needed and provided, and the longest travel distance to a stair. It then gives the floor plate construction and the heat loss through the envelope.

## 2. Egress from each level

A level passes if it is served by at least two stairs, if the clear width of every flight is at least the width needed, and if the travel distance from the farthest corner of the plate to the nearest stair is no more than 76 m. The width needed is the larger of 1118 mm and 7.6 mm for each occupant. Table 2.1 gives the result for each level; z is the floor elevation.

## 3. Floor plates

Every suspended level is a 300 mm concrete slab carried on the steel beams of AQS-BLD-016, over the 24 x 18 m plate (432 m2). The reservoir floors and walls are water-retaining concrete to ACI 350. Level L-2 bears on the foundation mat of AQS-BLD-017. The floors have a fire-resistance rating of 2 h between levels (AQS-HSE-001) and 3 h between the life-support levels and the process levels.

## 4. Enclosure

Above grade the walls are insulated metal panels with a thermal transmittance (U-value) of 0.534 W/m2K, over a perimeter of 84 m and a height of 34.8 m. The roof has an area of 432 m2 and a U-value of 0.199 W/m2K. Both values meet IECC 2021 section C402 for climate zone 3. The product of U-value and area (UA) for the envelope is 1,647 W/K, which gives a design heat loss of 46.4 kW at a temperature difference of 28.2 K (the 99.6 % winter design condition of AQS-SITE-002).

Below grade the basement walls are cast concrete, waterproofed and drained to the CATCH_TANK sump (AQS-BLD-017).

## 1. Summary and method

192 roster units: **62** close with issued internals, **82** close as "none with basis" (class rules, section 2), **48 open** with the gap named (section 4). The task 09 stake (AQS-STD-005 section 4) is respected: the units whose construction material is still unselected are open here too.

A close means: the internals are defined AND sized in the cited issued document, or the unit provably has no process internals to define (keyword-guarded class rule; the unit's own sizing and duty sheets are scanned first, and any booked internal moves the unit out of the "none" rule).

## 2. Class rules for "none with basis"

The class comes from the graph. Any unit whose sizing sheet, duty sheet, or issued mfg BOM books an internal (agitator, impeller, baffle, packing, electrode, membrane, spacer, demister, coil, tray, resin, sieve, desiccant, catalyst, plate-fin, adsorbent, filter element...) is moved out of this table.

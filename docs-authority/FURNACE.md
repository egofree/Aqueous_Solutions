# Induction furnace module

Document number: AQS-FURN-001
Revision: B
Date: 2026-10-10

1. Basis. 50 kg steel to 1500 °C.

2. Size. Sensible 50 × 0.5 × 1480 = 37 MJ. Latent 50 × 0.27 = 13.5 MJ. Total 50.5 MJ. At 10 kW and 40% coupling, time is 50.5 / 4 = 12.6 h. Crucible 200 mm ID, 250 mm tall, 8 L. Coil 15 turns of 8 mm tube over 250 mm. Frequency 10 kHz.

3. Material. Crucible graphite. Coil copper tube. Frame A500.

4. Nozzles. DC in, two SB350. Cooling water in and out, DN15. Off-gas on the top, DN25, to the afterburner.

5. Instruments. Coil current, water flow, crucible temperature. Low water trips the inverter.

6. Relief. Water side 50 kPa. Off-gas is open to the afterburner.

7. Code. Shop.

8. Open items. Capacitor value is not calculated. The power and the melt time are.

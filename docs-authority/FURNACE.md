# Induction furnace module

Document number: AQS-FURN-001
Revision: C
Date: 2026-10-11
Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Melt 50 kg steel to 1500 °C.

2. Size. Sensible 37 MJ, latent 13.5 MJ, total 50.5 MJ. At 10 kW and 40% coupling, 12.6 h. Crucible 200 mm ID, 250 mm tall. Coil 15 turns, 8 mm copper tube, 35.5 µH. Tank capacitor 7.1 µF at 10 kHz. Inverter is a full bridge on the 48 V bus, three SB350 connectors, 10 kW out.

3. Material. Crucible graphite. Coil copper. Frame A500 with a sheet heat shield between coil and tube.

4. Nozzles. Three SB350. Cooling water DN15 in and out. Off-gas DN25 on the top, to the afterburner.

5. Instruments. Coil current, water flow, crucible temperature. Low water trips the inverter.

6. Relief. Water side 50 kPa. Off-gas open to the afterburner.

7. Code. Shop.

8. Open items. None on the power. Electrode life on the silicon duty is in SILICON.md.

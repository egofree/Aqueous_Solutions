# Mass disposition

Document number: AQS-DUTY-016
Revision: A
Date: 2026-10-09
Authority: graph Rev R. Not a PE stamp.

Each case from the Rev Q account, and where the mass goes.

Methanol cell. The fuel edge was on the storage layer only. It is now mass as well. The tank remainder, 1.28 kg/d, joins the cell. Exhaust goes to the NOx scrubber.

Shop. Short 1.64 kg/d because the board cut was not taken off the goods. Goods are resized to the feed. Cake, char, glass, and the heavy oil cuts now enter the shop and leave as finished goods, 607 kg/d. That export is the product, not a waste.

Habitation. Short 0.80 kg/d. The ultra-pure header supplies it.

Flue knockout. Short 0.50 kg/d. The exhaust is cut by that amount and sent to the NOx scrubber, not to the boundary. Scrubber outlets are condensate to the roof tank, nitrate liquor to the nutrient tank, CO2 to the header, nitrogen back to the nitrogen header.

Leach. Long 3.15 kg/d. It leaves with the bleed. The bleed goes to the nutrient tank, not to the boundary.

Electrowinning. The hood hydrogen is a gas-header stream. It is no longer also a mass drop on the liquor.

Grow bed. Long 0.19 kg/d from the compost carbon credit. It leaves with the woody trim.

Flue and cell exhaust. Both go to the NOx scrubber.

Dust. Mill loss, kerf, and draw scrap go to the baghouse. The baghouse returns them to the leach.

Char. Goes to the shop as reductant.

Polish cake. Goes to the shop as recovered solids.

Leach bleed. Goes to the nutrient tank as salt.

Glass and ceramic goods. Go to the shop as stock.

Dock freight. The unspecified export goes back to the hopper, and the hopper feeds the leach.

Oil cuts. Naphtha fires the genset. The two heavier cuts are shop lube and fuel.

Ultra-pure header. The held 67.3 kg/d goes to habitation and the fab. Fab spent water returns to the leach.

Methanol tank. Draws to the cell.

Peroxide, ammonia, hydrofluoric acid. Peroxide is fed from the oxygen header, ammonia from the nitrogen header, fluoride from the leach. They still go to the wet bench.

Float liquor, cullet, scrub feed, oil. Sink solids and plastics leave the leach for the float tank. Cullet leaves the leach for the rinse. Scrub feed leaves the leach for the chlorate unit. These were appearing with no source.

Generator exhaust. Fuel is the naphtha cut. Air is from the nitrogen header. Exhaust is the scrubber feed.

What still leaves. Finished goods, 607 kg/d, are the product. Intake air and dock feedstock are the matching sources. Phosphoric acid, the melt, the mill, and the atomizer still have no upstream mass.

## 1. Purpose and scope

Every pipe joint in the plant is made to a written procedure. For welded joints this is a welding procedure specification (WPS), and the procedure is proved by a procedure qualification record (PQR): a test joint made to the procedure and then tested. The same approach is used here for fused, solvent-cemented and laminated joints in plastic and fibre-reinforced plastic (FRP) pipe.

This register gives one procedure for each combination of pipe material and joint type in the pipe inventory (AQS-INV-003), with the number of lines and joints it covers, the process, the qualification code, the essential variables, the qualification test and the extent of examination. The examination extent for welded metal pipe is that of ASME B31.3 paragraph 341.4.1 for normal fluid service (5 % random radiography). The procedures are written in-house; none is bought. Each is qualified by its test before production joints are made. The qualification codes are given by their keys in `flow_graph/param/refs.py`.

## 3. Welder and operator qualification

Each welder or operator is qualified as follows: ASME_IX QW-300 for WPS-01 and WPS-02, QF-300 for WPS-03, DVS 2212-1 for WPS-04 and WPS-05, demonstration joints for WPS-06 and WPS-07, and the RTP-1 laminator demonstration for WPS-08. Each joint balloon (JW-###) on the weld map AQS-WLD-001 carries the WPS number for its material.

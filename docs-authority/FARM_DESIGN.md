The farm closes the protein loop at the 4-crew ration: fish and poultry turn larvae and imported grain into eggs, fish meat and manure; the manure, sludge and galley biowaste feed the black-soldier-fly larvae bin; the larvae return as feed and the frass stages to the solids line as compost.

## 1. Aquaponics loop

60 kg standing tilapia-class stock at a 0.9 kg/d ration. About half the ration leaves as waste, which circulates through the biofilter to the dual root-zone growbeds at 40 kg/d and returns as cleaned water.

| Stream | kg/d |
| --- | --- |
| Fish feed (imported grain/scratch class) | 0.9 |
| Fish waste to the biofilter | 0.45 |
| Root-zone circulation (AQUA_TANK -> BIOFILTER -> GROWBED_A -> GROWBED_B -> AQUA_TANK) | 40 |

## 2. Dual root-zone growbeds

The 29.12 m2 live grow area splits into two beds of 14.56 m2. Each bed carries two root zones: an aerobic top zone where the plant roots take the nitrate, and an anaerobic mineralisation layer below where the fish solids break down. The beds are the loop biofilter's polishing stage.

## 3. Poultry

12 dual-purpose layers at a 1.44 kg/d ration produce 0.6 kg/d of eggs to the food store. 0.48 kg/d of manure feeds the larvae bin; 0.36 kg/d of drinking water and 0.72 kg/d of respiration close the flock balance.

## 4. Black-soldier-fly larvae composting

The bin takes 1.43 kg/d: 0.5 kg/d galley biowaste, 0.48 kg/d poultry manure and 0.45 kg/d fish sludge.

| Product | kg/d |
| --- | --- |
| Larvae (wet) to the layer ration | 0.214 |
| Frass to the solids line (compost) | 0.357 |
| Respiration/evaporation to the AHU | 0.859 |

The larvae cover 14.9 % of the layer ration by mass; the balance is imported grain/scratch on the D-DOCK manifest (A-M-416). The frass stages to the solids line as compost; the root zones take their nutrient from the aquaponic loop.

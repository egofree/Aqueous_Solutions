The farm closes the protein loop with NO imported feed (A-M-416): the duckweed raceway is the base crop, the black-soldier-fly larvae bin turns manure, sludge and galley biowaste into the concentrate co-ingredient, recycled eggshell carries the calcium, and the one binding supplement is methionine (~9 g/d DL-met equivalent, A-M-416). The frass stages to the solids line as compost.

## 1. Aquaponics loop and duckweed raceway

60 kg standing tilapia-class stock at a 0.9 kg/d ration (18 kg/d fresh duckweed, 1.5 % body weight - below the 2-3 % optimal-growth class (TECHTREE aquaponics.md:155), a declared slower grow-out). About half the ration leaves as waste, which circulates through the biofilter at 200 kg/d and returns as cleaned water.

The raceway produces 4.5 kg/d dry matter at 300 m2 and 15 g/m2/d (A-M-420):

| Duckweed allocation | kg/d DM-eq |
| --- | --- |
| Formulation feed (layers + quail + reserve draw) | 43.38 |
| Fresh tilapia ration | 18 |
| Fresh duck fodder (ad lib) | 4 |
| Human-grade greens to the galley (A-M-427) | 2.4 |
| Harvest margin | 0.28 |

The raceway runs on loop water, so the crop is the loop polisher; CP class 30 % dry (TECHTREE aquaculture.md:101; ducks.md:96 gives 35-43 % on nutrient-rich water - the X-FARM-04 trial measures ours). Loop path: AQUA_TANK -> BIOFILTER -> AQUA_UV (normally bypassed, A-M-428) -> GROWBED_A -> GROWBED_B -> DUCKWEED -> return.

## 2. Dual root-zone growbeds

The 29.12 m2 live grow area splits into two beds of 14.56 m2. Each bed carries two root zones: an aerobic top zone where the plant roots take the nitrate, and an anaerobic mineralisation layer below where the fish solids break down. The beds are the loop biofilter's polishing stage.

## 3. Poultry

12 dual-purpose layers at a 1.44 kg/d ration produce 0.6 kg/d of eggs to the food store. 0.48 kg/d of manure feeds the larvae bin; 0.36 kg/d of drinking water and 0.72 kg/d of respiration close the flock balance.

## 4. Black-soldier-fly larvae composting

The bin takes 1.43 kg/d: 0.5 kg/d galley biowaste, 0.48 kg/d poultry manure and 0.45 kg/d fish sludge.

| Product | kg/d |
| --- | --- |
| Larvae (wet) to the layer ration | 0.214 |
| Frass to the solids line (compost) | 0.357 |
| Respiration/evaporation to the AHU | 0.859 |

The larvae cover 14.9 % of the layer ration by mass as the concentrate co-ingredient of the formulation (A-M-426). The larvae meal routes to POULTRY ONLY, never to the fish: manure-fed larvae are the pathogen bridge the produce path must not carry (TECHTREE aquaponics.md:342 food-safety note; A-M-426). The frass stages to the solids line as compost; the root zones take their nutrient from the aquaponic loop.

Loop-wide methionine supplement class: ~9 g/d DL-met equivalent (quail binding case + the fish gap + margin). TECHTREE names synthetic DL-methionine as the standard supplement (chickens.md:176) and states methionine limits even black-soldier-fly meal quality (insect-farming.md:117). Duckweed Ca and met are unmeasured classes on our water - the X-FARM-04 trial measures them; the layer Ca closure holds only with the full eggshell return plus the larvae Ca class (5-8 %, insect-farming.md:116).

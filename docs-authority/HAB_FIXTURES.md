# Habitation fixtures

Document number: AQS-HAB-001
Revision: B
Date: 2026-10-12
Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Four rooms, 40 m²: sleep 16, galley 12, medical 6, wash 6. Grey leaving the sector is S12, 339 kg/d. The named wash edge IA-WASH is 1.0 kg/d. That edge is a tap drip. It is not the shower.

2. Size. One shower at 6 L/min governs the pipe, not the daily mass. 6 L/min in DN15 is about 0.6 m/s. One sink, same DN15. Composting toilet is the COMPOST_TOILET node: feces 0.8 kg/d, urine 1.2 kg/d. Heat in is 3 kW (IA-Q4-LIFE). Heat out is 6 kW (IA-E-HABOUT). Iodide store is 2 g. The design dose is 0.6 mg/d, which is 9 years on that store. The edge IA-I-DOSE at 0.002 kg/d is 2 g/d and is not a dose.

3. Material. DWV polymer for grey. Fixture valves brass or 316L. No carbon steel on the wash water.

4. Nozzles. Shower and sink DN15. Toilet solids are not a pipe to the leach; they go to the composting toilet. Grey DN25 on S12, because 339 kg/d is under the DN50 break but a shower slug is not a daily average.

5. Instruments. None that trip the plant. A dry toilet is a maintenance item.

6. Relief. Fixtures are open. No valve.

7. Code. Shop.

8. Open items. The iodide edge is three orders above the dose. The fixture sizes are not waiting on it.

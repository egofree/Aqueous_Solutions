# Operating procedures

Document number: AQS-OPS-001
Revision: A
Date: 2026-10-16
Authority: AQS-PLAN-007 X-OPS-01. Generated by `docs-authority/_op_proc.py`. Study. A-M-203. Format per OSHA 1910.119(f): normal, start, stop, emergency; operating limits are the trip set points of AQS-CTL-003.

## Plant-wide

- **Plant ESD:** one push-button per level and at the control room. Trips every feed pump, heater and rectifier, closes the H2/O2/CO2 bank ESD valves, keeps ventilation, gas detection, fire pumps and the safety PLC on the ZnBr2 and backup generator.
- **Power loss:** ZnBr2 carries the safety and life-support loads; backup generator auto-starts; process heaters shed first (AQS-ELS load shedding).
- **Start order** AQS-COM-002; **stop order** is the reverse; process systems stop before utilities.

## 1. ELEC

Units: BACKUP_GEN, FUEL_CELL, LOAD_AGG, PV_ARRAY, RECTIFIER, SOL_ARK_60K, ZNBR2, ZNBR2_BR2_REGEN.

- **Normal:** 29 loops on PLC-ELEC in auto; operator rounds per shift on levels and temperatures of BACKUP_GEN, ZNBR2; alarm response per AQS-CTL-001.
- **Start:** permissives: upstream systems 1-0 running (none), trips healthy and reset, gas detection healthy. Fill liquid units, warm, then feed at 25/50/100 % with 1 h holds (AQS-COM-002 rates).
- **Stop:** feeds off, hold until temperature and gas inventories reach the hold state, isolate; downstream systems 2-18 stopped first. Normal stop leaves liquids in place, hot or cold standby.
- **Emergency:** 5 trips: SIF-085 level high-high → close feed valve / stop feed pump; SIF-014 flow or level low-low → pump stop; SIF-161 level high-high → close feed valve / stop feed pump; SIF-166 H2 at 25 % LFL in the purge → stop charging, purge fan to high; SIF-162 level high-high → close feed valve / stop feed pump. After a trip: find cause, reset at the safety PLC (two-person), restart from Start.

## 1. Method

Each selection is individual to the unit: the bounding temperature and chemistry come from the unit's own duty sections 1-2 (graph design / sizing rows, A-M register), the degradation mechanism is named where the environment falls outside the issued section 12.1 families, and every alternative is stated with the condition that would switch it. The family palette and rules M1-M7 follow AQS-STD-002. Longevity and cost are reasoned qualitatively; **no rate of loss is claimed anywhere** - every change-out and wear rate rides a named first-article work-order (unit task 12 class) or bench programme, which is where the measured basis enters. Every source cites only the published references in `flow_graph/param/refs.py`; the 13 entries added with this lane were metadata-verified before citation, and no pending-metadata row is cited.

## 2. Molten-chloride physics for the pot-cell family

At 650-700 °C an Fe-Cr-Ni alloy immersed in a dry neutral chloride melt loses chromium selectively (the Cr chlorides are volatile under the salt surface), while a graphite hot face is essentially inert to a dry neutral chloride melt, and the residual attack that remains is impurity-driven (hydroxide / oxide / oxygen in the salt); hence the pot family carries dry-breathing headspaces and anhydrous-feed rules, and the structural alloy sits outside the melt behind an alumina backup lining and ceramic fibre (DING2018_CL_REVIEW, DING2021_ENG, KRUIZENGA2012). This is the governing first-principle for AL_POT, MG_POT, CA_POT, CR_CELL, MO_CELL and the TI_CELL launder: **graphite wetted faces + 90 % alumina backup + steel shell below the liner**, instead of the wet-stainless families of section 12.1 that would shed Cr into the melt and the product metal.

The brine lines of AQS-STD-002 section 12.2 (formerly the "pending" last row) issue with this lane as two rows: the ambient import legs (NORTH_DOCK to MG_POT and CA_POT) are PVDF-wetted handling with the dry concentrate kept anhydrous to the pot feeder (MgCl2 hygroscopicity, ULLMANN_MAGNESIUM); the molten MgCl2 salt return (TI_CELL to MG_POT, ~700 °C) is a graphite/Ni-200 wetted launder. AQS-PIP-004 regenerates its service text from `docs-authority/_line_list.py`.

## 3. Per-family rationale (grouped where one environment governs)

- **Pot cells (AL_POT, MG_POT, CA_POT)** - fused-chloride electrolysis at 700 °C: graphite liner/anodes (chlorine-evolving anode duty at j 0.5 A/cm2, A-M-282 class), steel or graphite cathodes per the industrial pot class (ULLMANN_MAGNESIUM), alumina backup lining, steel shell below the liner. Alternatives: SiC hot faces past the design AlCl3 carryover; Mo dip legs where a metal-melt contact interface is the bottleneck (Mo is in-house through MO_CELL costs).
- **Carbochlorination cells (CR_CELL, MO_CELL)** - Cr2O3/MoO3 + carbon + chlorine at 650-700 °C with an internal anode-gas loop: dry hot face (M7), graphite melt-contact surfaces, alumina backup. SiC/SiAlON bed brick as the carryover alternative.
- **Kroll train (TI_CELL)** - the chlorinator (TiO2 + 2C + 2Cl2, 800 °C) is a dry refractory-bed vessel with a graphite hot face and a 316L condensation train below the TiCl4 dew point; the reduction retort (TiCl4 + 2Mg, 900 °C) is the Kroll steel-retort class in 310S with a graphite crucible insert, molten MgCl2 returning through the graphite/Ni-200 launder (KROLL1940, ULLMANN_TITANIUM). Alternative: Mo-clad retort above 950 °C, Hastelloy C-276 condensation should TiCl4 hydrolysis ever be traced.
- **Melt-shop furnaces (IND_MELT, RF_HEAT, RES_HEAT, COMB_HEAT, VAC_IND, VAC_CAST, SINTER)** - one lining class per duty: clay-graphite crucibles on ceramic-fibre hot faces with castable hearths for the induction remelt families; 80Ni-20Cr element boxes to 1100 °C (ASM_V1, RES_HEAT / SHELL_BURNOUT class); a C5-C10 fuel-fired backup on alumina-silica brick + castable crown with a 310S burner tube; vacuum units on water-cooled copper (VAC_IND skull melt) or 304L chambers; the 1300 °C reactive-powder sinter on graphite elements + 95 % alumina muffle under inert/vacuum (ASM_V7). All dry hot gas, M7.
- **Investment train (WAX_PATTERN, SHELL_BUILD, SHELL_BREAK, SHELL_BURNOUT, INV_CAST, FLUX_PREP)** - 304L wax melt plant (70-90 °C hot-melt practice, ULLMANN_WAX; PP-H only at or below the 80 °C house thermoplastic tier); PP-H slurry plant matching the issued alkaline-silica-slurry family for the shell room; UHMW-PE wear faces and a 304 reclaim sieve in the knock-out; graphite crucibles and alumina shells for the pours; anhydrous PP-H handling at FLUX_PREP (MgCl2 hygroscopicity).
- **Forming machines (ROLL, DRAW, EXTRUDE, FORGE, CAST_LINE, CONT_CAST, STOCK_OUT)** - hot-work tool-steel die and roll class (H13 / D2, ASM_V1, DIETER1988 ch. 20), carbide wire dies (DRAW), graphite permanent moulds for the Cu pour (1085 °C exceeds die-steel service; SCHACHT2004 ch. 8), the water-cooled copper mould of the continuous caster with the water side on the issued M1 family, and dry staging racks with HDPE pads. None of these units sees a corrosive wetted stream; the wear face is the design surface.
- **Digestor service units (F_RECOVER, LI_RECOVER, P_CA)** - the HF-equivalent concentrate hold stays PE at ambient with PVDF internals and P3-family hot legs (ULLMANN_HF); the Li polish/carbonation vessel and the Ca(OH)2 slurry precipitator are PP-H with PP agitators and PE slurry lines (alkaline family behaviour; both units remain honestly open on task 02 sizing - the -09 build closes, the -02 sizing does not pretend).
- **Grease kettles (GREASE_CA, GREASE_LI)** - 316L jacketed soap kettles with 316L agitators: the hot alkaline soap concentrate at the 95-120 °C saponification class is out of the PP-H tier, and 304/316L hot-soap contact is the grease-manufacture practice (MANG2007 ch. 14.6). 304L is the cost-down alternative if first-articles show the wax cut to be the only wetted face of a given kettle.
- **Heat block (ADS_CHILL, DRY_COOLER, MAU_HX)** - sealed 304L adsorber shells with silica-gel beds and 316L water coils (WANG2006_ADS; 13X zeolite above 90 °C drive); copper-tube/aluminium-fin dry coolers with galvanized casings on the HDPE-class loop; an aluminium-fin/copper-tube heating coil for the make-up air unit whose air side stays above the dew point, with the water side on the issued Q3/Q4 M1 family.
- **PEM electrolyser (H2O_ELYZ)** - Ti porous transport layers, Ir/Ru mixed-oxide anode and Pt cathode catalyst, PTFE-class ionomer membrane, Ti hardware, PTFE seals; dry product lines on the issued M1 316L family; stack internals as metal-as-electrode under M6 with the hydride and reverse-current guards of AQS-REV-H2-001. Electrode degradation (Ir dissolution) is the named bench basis (BERNT2018; IEC62282_3_100, ISO22734_2019 construction classes).
- **Polymer sizing train (PELLET, POLY_STOCK)** - 316L die plate and cutter hub with an UHMW-PE trough liner at the underwater pelletiser (wear, not corrosion, is the design limit); HDPE totes and PP powder drums at the dry store.
- **GLASS-sector furnace (SI_REDUCE)** - graphite hot-face carbothermic-reduction furnace with alumina brick backup and a mild-steel casing, dry char/silica handling. The unit's solids-only graph edges carry no wetted stream today and its process duty stays a task 02 pending item; this page issues the construction class only.

## 5. Effects on the derived registers

- Every unit task 09 item held on "Selection pending" closes with this page as its evidence; the `_unit_materials` open set on the roster goes empty, and the duty-sheet section 3 sweep (AQS-STD-005, `docs-authority/_unit_materials.py`) is regenerated from the sheets.
- AQS-END-02 (`_eol.py`) classes the envelope-BOM placeholder rows from the duty section 3 keyword text (graphite to the organic/char class, alumina/refractory hot faces to the silicate-oxide class, steels to the plateable-metal class, PVDF/PTFE/PE/PP + UHMW to the organic class); the wetted-hold rows leave the EOL matrix.
- The internals register (`_internals.py`) recomputes: rule-1 honesty holds ("internals cannot be detailed before the material is selected") leave with section 3; the units whose section 3 books a real internal part (crucible, liner, hot face, agitator, adsorbent, sieve, coil) close on that basis, and the static hold vessels without internals stay open with their task 02 sizing gap named rather than silently closed.
- `MATERIALS_STD.md` section 12.2 (Rev O) carries the two issued brine rows; AQS-PIP-004 lines IA-MGCL2-KROLL / IA-MGCL2-MU / IA-CACL2-MU regenerate their service text through `docs-authority/_line_list.py`.
- A-M-318..328 register the selections per family in `assumptions_manual.json` (AQS-REG-002).

What this page does **not** do: it issues no sizing (task 02 gaps on GREASE_CA, GREASE_LI, P_CA, LI_RECOVER, SI_REDUCE remain), no relief cases (task 07), no instrument loops beyond AQS-CTL-001 (task 08), no measured rates or lives (task 12 first articles and the pot-cell verification hold their hardware-gated status), and no vendor-package basis of any kind.

## 1. BMED bench stack and polarisation rig (X-MFG-35)

Article: 10-triplet stack built to the plant cell (AQS-DUTY-068): 400 cm2 active (200 x 200 mm) in PP frames 300 x 300 mm, 0.8 mm PE mesh spacers, Ti/MMO anode and 316L/Ni cathode end plates, PP end plates with tie rods. Plant point 200 A/m2 gives 8.0 A; rig range to 16 A (400 A/m2). Predicted stack voltage 18.7 V (10 x 1.67 V triplet + 2 V electrodes), so the supply is 0-40 V, 0-20 A, constant current, 0.1 % resolution. Predicted base output at the plant point 95.5 g/h NaOH.

| Item | Specification | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| Bipolar membrane, AEM, CEM | cut 300 x 300 mm to the frame gasket; 10 BPM, 10 AEM, 11 CEM (extra CEM at the cathode end) | 31 sheets |
| Flow frames and spacers | PP 300 x 300 mm, 0.8 mm PE woven mesh spacer, EPDM gasket, manifold ports per compartment (acid, base, salt) | 30 |
| Electrodes | Ti/MMO anode, 316L or Ni cathode, 300 x 300 mm with bus tab | 1 + 1 |
| End plates | 25 mm PP with 8 x M10 tie rods, disc springs for 0.3 MPa gasket stress | 2 |
| Loops | acid, base, salt and electrode rinse (0.5 M Na2SO4); 5 L PP reservoir each; magnetic-drive pump 0-8 L/min (4.8 L/min at 5 cm/s in 10 compartments) | 4 |
| Instruments | conductivity x 3, pH x 2, Pt100 x 4, differential pressure across stack x 4, flow x 4; Pt voltage-sense wires at every triplet; Haber-Luggin capillaries with Ag/AgCl for one BPM | set |
| Analysis | auto-titrator for HCl and NaOH product strength (CE by Faraday), ion chromatograph or titration for Cl- leakage into base | 1 |

Procedure: leak test at 1.5 x pump shut-off; conditioning 4 h at 100 A/m2; polarisation 25 to 400 A/m2 in 25 A/m2 steps, 5 min each, voltage per triplet; 8 h batch at 200 A/m2 with product titrated hourly; repeat at 40 C.
Measurements and acceptance: specific energy kWh/kg NaOH (plant basis 6.7 kWh/kg, A-M-077), current efficiency (basis 0.8), triplet voltage (physics 1.22 V, design 1.67 V). The measured values replace bmed_sec_kWh_per_kg_base and bmed_CE and the plant re-sizes the stacks through the pipeline. Specific energy above 1.5 x basis is a design review.

## 3. FRP pultruded section coupon programme (X-MAT-21)

Coupons cut from the LEACH_CSTR_1 belt-channel section (pultruded web and flange), longitudinal (L) and transverse (T). Conditioning ASTM_D618_21 procedure A. Five valid specimens per test and direction.

| Test | Standard | Geometry | Fixture | L + T + spare |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| Flexure: strength, modulus | ASTM_D790_17 | 16:1 span (32:1 if modulus > 20 GPa), width 13 mm, length span + 20 % | three-point, 5 mm loading nose and supports | 5 + 5 + 2 |
| Short-beam shear (interlaminar) | ASTM_D2344_22 | length 6 t, width 2 t, span 4 t | 6 mm nose, 3 mm supports | 5 + 5 + 2 |
| Density | ASTM_D792_20 | 1 to 5 g pieces | balance with sinker | 5 |
| Liquor ageing (added) | the three tests above after 1,000 h in plant leach liquor at 77.5 C | as above | ageing tank | 15 |

Load frame: in-house screw-driven frame 50 kN (largest coupon 31 kN at 400 MPa x 13 mm x 6 mm), load cell class 1 to ISO7500_1_2018, crosshead 1 to 500 mm/min. Allowable = mean - 2.0 s (five specimens) for strength, mean for modulus, after the ageing retention factor. The results replace the A-M-034 allowable, modulus and density.

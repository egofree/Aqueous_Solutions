# Assay and relief

Document number: AQS-SIDE-001
Revision: B
Date: 2026-10-11
Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. One sample from the leach discharge, 100 mL, filtered. The plant does not buy a spectrometer or a primary standard it cannot make.

2. Size. Total dissolved solids: evaporate 10 mL to dryness and weigh. Iron is what does not plate before the iron cell and does plate there. Copper, nickel, and zinc are the same method on their cells. The bench is a 600 × 400 × 600 mm 316L frame. A 100 mL sample does not size the bench. The bench is sized to stand at.

3. Material. Frame 316L. Beaker is glass or polymer from the plant. No carbon steel on the bench.

4. Nozzles. Sample tap DN15 on the leach discharge, valved. Drain from the bench DN15 back to the leach. One SB350 for a hot plate if the evaporating dish needs it.

5. Instruments. A balance, 0.01 g. No trip. A bad sample does not stop the plant. The cells do.

6. Relief. The bench is not pressurized.

7. Code. Shop.

8. Open items. The balance is a made mechanism, not selected. The sample volume and the return are set.

Relief, restated. Design pressure is liquid head plus 10 kPa. Orifice is the 1 kg/s water case in RELIEF.md. Each pressurized module gets one DN15 valve, set at 110%, piped to the scrubber. Atmospheric modules get a vent, not a valve.

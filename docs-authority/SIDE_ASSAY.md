# Assay and relief

Document number: AQS-SIDE-001
Revision: A
Date: 2026-10-10

Assay. One sample point on the leach discharge. 100 mL, filtered. Iron by titration. Copper, nickel, zinc by the electrowinning order itself: what plates at each cell is the assay. No bought spectrometer.

Relief. Design pressure is liquid head plus 10 kPa, already in RELIEF.md. Orifice is the 1 kg/s water case. Each pressurized module gets one valve, DN15, set at 110%, piped to the scrubber. Atmospheric modules get a vent, not a valve.

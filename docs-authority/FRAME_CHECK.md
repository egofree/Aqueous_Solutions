## 1. Purpose and scope

The plant building is a steel frame of beams and columns carrying concrete floor slabs, with water reservoirs on the upper levels. This page sizes the members of the frame: it gives the factored floor loads of each level, the secondary beams and girders of each floor, the columns of each storey, and the bracing that resists wind and earthquake.

## 2. Basis

The plan grid is 4 by 3 bays of 6 m, giving a plate of 24 x 18 m with 20 columns. All members are welded sections made from ASTM A992 plate with a yield strength (Fy) of 345 MPa. The floor slab braces the top flange of the beams, so the unbraced length of the beams (Lb) is zero. Members are checked to AISC 360-22 sections E3 (compression), F2 (flexure) and D2 (tension), and loads are combined to ASCE 7-22 section 2.3.1.

## 6. Lateral bracing

The design base shear is 1653 kN, governed by earthquake (AQS-BLD-014). There are two braced lines in each direction, with two braced bays in each line. The diagonals act in tension only and are set at 45 degrees. Each diagonal carries a tension T of 585 kN and needs an area of at least 1883 mm2 (AISC 360-22 D2), which a welded box of about 120 mm provides. The overturning load on the columns of the braced bays is carried forward to task X-BLD-03.

## 7. Result

Every member in Tables 4.1 and 5.1 has a section that passes its check. The reservoir storeys govern the column sizes, and the columns step down in size above R3. All sections are sized here and welded from plate; none is taken from a catalogue of rolled sections.

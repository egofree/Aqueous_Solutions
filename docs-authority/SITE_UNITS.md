Generated by `python3 -m flow_graph.param.site_units --write` from the committed site profiles and the placeholder replacement units (X-LOC site models). Do not edit by hand.

A locale scaling plan eliminates reference units and names a replacement of a different technology. The replacements are designed placeholder units in the reference graph; at a locale they are placed and sized from that site profile. Sizes carry their basis; a unit without a profile-based size states that it sizes at the locale design pass.

## 2. Placement in the locale models

During a locale regeneration the placements are stamped into the locale flow graph (`sites/<site_id>/flow_graph/plant_flow_graph.json`, `params.site_placement`); the reference graph is untouched. Units without a profile-based size keep their design record and size at the locale design pass.

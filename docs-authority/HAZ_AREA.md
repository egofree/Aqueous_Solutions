## 1. Purpose and scope

Where a flammable gas or vapour can be released, the air around the release point may become explosive. Electrical equipment in that space must then be of a type that cannot ignite it. Hazardous area classification divides such spaces into zones by how often an explosive atmosphere is expected: Zone 1 where it is likely in normal operation, and Zone 2 where it is not likely and would last only a short time.

This page lists every unit in the graph that can release a flammable substance, assigns the gas group and temperature class of that substance, and gives the extent of Zone 1 and Zone 2 around the unit. The zones follow IEC 60079-10-1 and NEC 2026 Article 505. Each gas group is given in IEC terms with the equivalent North American group in brackets. The number of instruments and motors or pumps in each zone is counted so that they can be specified as explosion-protected (Ex-rated) equipment.

## 2. Basis

The ventilation is taken as medium degree with good availability, provided by mechanical extract (AQS-THM-002). Loss of extract trips the release source (AQS-CTL-003), so the zone extents remain valid. The graph has 17 release sources: 10 in gas group IIC (Group B), 7 in gas group IIA (Group D).

# Relief

The per-node list that assigned about 17.8 kPa and a 3.3 mm orifice to every unit is withdrawn.

The kilopascal figure was the envelope height taken as water, plus 10 kPa. It was applied to crops, electrical nodes, the expander, and the silicon-carbide furnace. It is not a set point. On the expander the process inlet is 500 kPa. The stored 14.9 kPa is the 500 mm box as a water column.

The millimetre figure was an API liquid orifice for 1 kg/s of water at that pressure, Kd 0.65. It is not the process relief load and it is not the nozzle. Deleted nodes (LITHO, BLOW, ELCOAT, PV_CELL, FORM_RX) were still on that list.

A set point is not assigned until the relief case is stated. Where a nozzle is required, it is DN15 back to the process, the hood, or the cold-box suction. Not to the room and not to a stack. The duty sheet for each unit says which of those applies. Machines that are not liquid tanks are not pressure vessels.

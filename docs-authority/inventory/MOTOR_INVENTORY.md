## Purpose and scope

This inventory lists every motor and actuator in the plant: pumps, fans, conveyors, agitators, compressors, valve actuators and solenoids. Section 1 groups them into motor families of similar design, which are designed in AQS-INV-005, and section 2 lists each item by sector, sub-system and unit.

Many units have not yet been sized in detail, so some items rest on a counting rule or a default. Those items are marked "assumed" in the Basis column (A-M-119). They are replaced as each unit is sized (task 02) and receives its manufacturing model.

The rules for items on units that are not yet sized are as follows. Each liquid outlet line has one transfer pump, sized for 10 m of head at an efficiency of 0.4, with no credit for gravity flow. Scrubbers, dryers, the baghouse, air-handling units, thermal units and fab benches each have one induced-draft fan, sized for 1 kPa at an efficiency of 0.5. Each solids line of 50 kg/d or more has one screw conveyor, and each dosed line has one on/off solenoid valve. Agitators are taken at 0.25 kW where the graph lists one.

The motor type follows from the shaft power. Below 60 W of shaft power the motor is a 24 V DC brushless motor (BLDC). Above that, it is an IE3 induction motor at the next IEC 60034-1 rating of at least 1.15 times the shaft power, in the IEC 60072-1 4-pole frame. Scroll compressors have hermetic motors. The condenser fans of the heat pumps have electronically commutated (EC) motors, which are permanent-magnet motors with built-in electronics, rated by the same step. The efficiency classes of IEC 60034-30-1 (IE1 to IE4) apply only to the line-start induction motors, not to EC, BLDC or stepper motors.

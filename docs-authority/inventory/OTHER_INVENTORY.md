## Purpose and scope

This inventory lists the building blocks of the plant that are not motors, pipe or wire: valves, instruments, electronics, fasteners, bearings, seals and similar parts. Section 1 totals the items by category, section 2 groups them into families, and section 3 lists them by sector, sub-system and unit. Flange studs and gaskets are in the pipe inventory (AQS-INV-003), conductors in the wire inventory (AQS-INV-002) and motors in the motor inventory (AQS-INV-001).

The inventory has 1500 rows with 4648 items in 63 families. Of these, 2092 items rest on an assumed counting rule (A-M-125).

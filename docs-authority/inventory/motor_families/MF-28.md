## Purpose and scope

The plant makes its own motors, and motors of similar design are grouped into families. This sheet gives the design basis of family MF-28, the steps by which it is made in-house, and the work still open before it can be built. The family covers 8 motors in 1 units, with 127.52 kW of installed shaft power in total (motor inventory AQS-INV-001).

## 2. Manufacturing plan

The motor is made in-house in the following steps.

1. The stator and rotor are made as in the induction plan, without frame, shaft or fan, as a 2-pole motor. The winding varnish and lead insulation are compatible with the refrigerant and with POE (polyol ester) oil (UL 60335-2-34 and IEC 60335-2-34 Annex AA).
2. The motor is shrink-fitted into the compressor shell on the scroll compressor line (task X-MFG-35).
3. After the shell is welded, the motor receives a high-voltage test and an insulation resistance test, and a locked-rotor and thermal protector test to IEC 60335-2-34.

## Purpose and scope

The plant makes its own motors, and motors of similar design are grouped into families. This sheet gives the design basis of family MF-17, the steps by which it is made in-house, and the work still open before it can be built. The family covers 37 motors in 18 units, with 0.33 kW of installed shaft power in total (motor inventory AQS-INV-001).

## 2. Manufacturing plan

The motor is made in-house in the following steps.

1. The coil is wound with class H enamelled wire (IEC 60317-13) on a bobbin of glass-filled polyamide (PA66-GF), with the 22 mm interface of ISO 15218.
2. The plunger and frame are ferritic stainless steel 430F.
3. The coil is encapsulated in epoxy and fitted with a DIN EN 175301-803 connector.
4. Each solenoid is tested for coil resistance, pull-in at 85 % of rated voltage, and insulation at 1000 V.

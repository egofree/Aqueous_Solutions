## 2. Manufacturing plan (in-house)

1. Laminations: laser-cut then stamped M400-50A (EN 10106) 0.5 mm, stacked and welded/cleated
2. Stator winding: Grade 2 enamelled Cu round wire (IEC 60317-13, class 200) from the wire line; single-layer concentric, class F insulation system (IEC 60085), slot liner NMN
3. VPI in polyester resin, cure
4. Rotor: die-cast aluminium cage (in-house melt), skewed one slot pitch
5. Shaft: C45 (EN 10083-2) turned and ground; keyway to IEC 60072-1
6. Frame: cast aluminium (A356) or cast iron above IEC 132, machined; end shields; IP55 (IEC 60034-5); IC 411 fan cooling (IEC 60034-6)
7. Bearings from the X-INV-05 bearing families; balance to ISO 21940-11 G2.5
8. Routine test IEC 60034-1: winding resistance, no-load, locked-rotor, hipot 2U + 1000 V; first-article type test IEC 60034-2-1 (efficiency) and IEC 60034-9 (noise)

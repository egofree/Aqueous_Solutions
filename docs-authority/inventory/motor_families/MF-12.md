## Purpose and scope

The plant makes its own motors, and motors of similar design are grouped into families. This sheet gives the design basis of family MF-12, the steps by which it is made in-house, and the work still open before it can be built. The family covers 3 motors in 3 units, with 0.26 kW of installed shaft power in total (motor inventory AQS-INV-001).

## 2. Manufacturing plan

The motor is made in-house in the following steps.

1. The stator and rotor laminations are laser-cut, and later stamped, from M400-50A electrical steel (EN 10106) 0.5 mm thick, then stacked and welded or cleated.
2. The stator is wound with Grade 2 enamelled copper round wire (IEC 60317-13, class 200) from the plant wire line, as a single-layer concentric winding with a class F insulation system (IEC 60085) and NMN slot liners (Nomex–Mylar–Nomex laminate).
3. The wound stator is vacuum-pressure impregnated (VPI) with polyester resin and cured.
4. The rotor cage is die-cast in aluminium from the in-house melt and skewed by one slot pitch.
5. The shaft is turned and ground from C45 steel (EN 10083-2), with a keyway to IEC 60072-1.
6. The frame is cast aluminium (A356), or cast iron above frame IEC 132, and is machined and fitted with end shields. It gives IP55 protection (IEC 60034-5) and IC 411 fan cooling (IEC 60034-6).
7. Bearings are taken from the bearing families of task X-INV-05, and the rotor is balanced to grade G2.5 of ISO 21940-11.
8. Each motor receives the routine tests of IEC 60034-1: winding resistance, no-load, locked rotor, and a high-voltage (hipot) test at 2U + 1000 V. The first article is also type-tested for efficiency (IEC 60034-2-1) and noise (IEC 60034-9).

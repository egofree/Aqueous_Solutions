## Purpose and scope

The plant makes its own motors, and motors of similar design are grouped into families. This sheet gives the design basis of family MF-27, the steps by which it is made in-house, and the work still open before it can be built. The family covers 16 motors in 1 units, with 12.0 kW of installed shaft power in total (motor inventory AQS-INV-001).

## 2. Manufacturing plan

The motor is made in-house in the following steps.

1. The fan and its EC motor are taken from the nameplate or datasheet of the selected heat-pump condenser fan (A-M-332). An in-house motor design is deferred until the fan is selected.
2. Each fan is tested for input power, speed range and response to the speed command at rated airflow, against the datasheet.

## 3. Open items

Three items remain open. A first article must be built and type-tested (task X-INV-02B). The drawings, the CadQuery model and the bill of materials must be produced (task X-INV-02C). The coefficients that are now taken from within published ranges (A-M-126) are then replaced by the type-test results.

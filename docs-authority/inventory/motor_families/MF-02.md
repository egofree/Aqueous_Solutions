## Purpose and scope

The plant makes its own motors, and motors of similar design are grouped into families. This sheet gives the design basis of family MF-02, the steps by which it is made in-house, and the work still open before it can be built. The family covers 55 motors in 5 units, with 2.66 kW of installed shaft power in total (motor inventory AQS-INV-001).

## 2. Manufacturing plan

The motor is made in-house in the following steps.

1. The stator uses 0.35 mm NO20 or M270-35A laminations (EN 10106) with 12 slots and concentrated coils on each tooth.
2. The rotor has 8 poles of surface magnets. NdFeB magnets need a supply of rare earths, which is a review item; bonded ferrite is the alternative, at about 0.4 of the torque density.
3. Commutation uses Hall sensors or sensorless back-EMF detection, with a driver from the BLDC driver family of task X-INV-05.
4. The flange follows NEMA ICS 16, and the housing is extruded aluminium.
5. Each motor is tested for back-EMF constant, no-load current and torque-speed curve, with a high-voltage test at 500 V + 2U.

## 3. Open items

Three items remain open. A first article must be built and type-tested (task X-INV-02B). The drawings, the CadQuery model and the bill of materials must be produced (task X-INV-02C).

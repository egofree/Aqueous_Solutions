## 2. Manufacturing plan (in-house)

1. Stator: 0.35 mm NO20 or M270-35A laminations (EN 10106), 12 slots, concentrated tooth coils
2. Rotor: 8-pole surface magnets; NdFeB requires rare-earth supply (review item), bonded ferrite alternative at about 0.4 of the torque density
3. Hall sensors or sensorless back-EMF commutation; driver from the X-INV-05 BLDC driver family
4. Flange to NEMA ICS 16; housing extruded aluminium
5. Test: back-EMF constant, no-load current, torque-speed curve, hipot 500 V + 2U

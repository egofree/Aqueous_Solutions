## Purpose and scope

The plant makes its own motors, and motors of similar design are grouped into families. This sheet gives the design basis of family MF-03, the steps by which it is made in-house, and the work still open before it can be built. The family covers 3 motors in 1 units, with 0.03 kW of installed shaft power in total (motor inventory AQS-INV-001).

## 2. Manufacturing plan

The motor is made in-house in the following steps.

1. A BLDC motor of family MF-01 drives a worm gear with a bronze wheel and a steel worm.
2. The output is the square drive of ISO 5211 F03, with end stops and position switches.
3. The housing is glass-filled polyamide (PA66-GF) or aluminium, with IP67 protection.
4. Each actuator is tested for breakaway torque at the ISO 5211 F03 rating and for travel time, and a sample is tested for cycle life.

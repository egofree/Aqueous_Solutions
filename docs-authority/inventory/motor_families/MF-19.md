## Purpose and scope

The plant makes its own motors, and motors of similar design are grouped into families. This sheet gives the design basis of family MF-19, the steps by which it is made in-house, and the work still open before it can be built. The family covers 3 motors in 1 units, with 1.2 kW of installed shaft power in total (motor inventory AQS-INV-001).

## 2. Manufacturing plan

The motor is made in-house in the following steps.

1. The motor is a 2-phase hybrid stepper with 1.8 degree steps: an 8-pole stator and a 50-tooth rotor with an axially magnetised NdFeB ring between two toothed caps.
2. The laminations are 0.35 mm thick (EN 10106), and the bifilar or unipolar windings are wound from the plant wire line.
3. The flange follows NEMA ICS 16.
4. Each motor is tested for holding torque, step accuracy (within 5 %), resistance and inductance.

## Purpose and scope

This inventory lists every pipe line of the plant with its piping class, material, size, length and fittings, so that the pipe, elbows, flanges, gaskets, bolts and joints to be made or bought can be counted. Section 1 totals the pipe by material, section 2 groups the lines into families of the same class, material and nominal diameter (DN), and section 3 lists the lines by sector, sub-system and unit.

The inventory has 260 lines with 4486 m of pipe in 21 families. The pipe is cut from 761 stock lengths of 6.096 m, leaving 151 m of offcut. The fittings are 742 90-degree elbows, 518 flanges, 518 gaskets, 2144 stud bolts and 2381 joints. The route lengths are the output of the isometric router (A-M-121). Tees, reducers, valves and supports are counted in the inventory of task X-INV-05.

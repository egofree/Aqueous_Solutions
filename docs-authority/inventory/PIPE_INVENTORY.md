## Purpose and scope

This inventory lists every pipe line of the plant with its piping class, material, size, length and fittings, so that the pipe, elbows, flanges, gaskets, bolts and joints to be made or bought can be counted. Section 1 totals the pipe by material, section 2 groups the lines into families of the same class, material and nominal diameter (DN), and section 3 lists the lines by sector, sub-system and unit.

The inventory has 396 lines with 7253 m of pipe in 21 families. The pipe is cut from 1183 stock lengths of 6.096 m, leaving 184 m of offcut. The fittings are 1087 90-degree elbows, 750 flanges, 750 gaskets, 3072 stud bolts and 3535 joints. The route lengths are the output of the isometric router (A-M-121). Tees, reducers, valves and supports are counted in the inventory of task X-INV-05.

## Purpose and scope

This inventory lists every electrical circuit of the plant with its conductor type, size, insulation, voltage class and length, so that the copper and cable to be made or bought can be counted. Section 0 records how the local panels were merged, section 1 groups the conductors into families, and section 2 lists the circuits by sector, sub-system and unit.

The inventory has 532 circuit rows with 185.1 km of conductor in total; 153.9 km of that rests on assumed lengths or counts (A-M-122). Conductors are sized to NEC 2026: section 430.22 and Table 430.250 for motor branch circuits, Table 310.16 for copper at 75 °C with the limits of 240.4(D), 250.122 for equipment grounding conductors, 705.28 for inverter outputs and 690.8 for PV source circuits. The voltage drop is held to 3 % on branch circuits and 2 % on feeders, calculated from resistance only (NEC Chapter 9 Table 8). Tray fill is checked with the cable schedule (task X-ELS-02). Where a conductor type reads "in EMT", the conductors run in electrical metallic tubing (EMT).

## 0. Panel merge (X-ELS-02, by calculation)

The rule in `merge_panels` folds the panel with the least feeder copper into the host panel that gives the lowest total copper. A merge is accepted only if the total copper mass does not rise and every branch circuit and feeder still meets the voltage-drop limits of 3 % and 2 % without a conductor larger than 600 kcmil. The merges reduce the panels from 14 to 6 and the copper from 15.91 t to 15.86 t. The merged sectors and their host panels are listed below.

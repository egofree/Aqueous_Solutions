Findings: fluoropolymer parts (PVDF piping, pumps and seals) cannot go to OIL_PYRO because the HF is outside the scrubber design basis; options are mechanical re-use as regrind for non-pressure parts, or an HF-rated scrubber stage. NdFeB magnets sit in the precipitate cake with no recovery unit; the ferrite motor variant (AQS-INV-005) removes this hold. Titanium cathode blanks are re-used directly.

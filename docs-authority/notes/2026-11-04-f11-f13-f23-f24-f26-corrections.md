## F-11 Fuel-cell heat credit: 1.14 to 2.666 kW

S53 (FUEL_CELL to Q4_BUS, thermal) booked only the portion of the stack heat above the
45 C Q4 return (2.664 x 15/35 = 1.1419 kW) and the remaining 1.52 kW of physical heat
was unledgered. write_graph heat() now books the full stack heat: S49 4.8 kg/d H2 x
33.33 kWh/kg LHV (A-M-383) / 24 h = 6.666 kW less the 4.0 kW electric draw (S51) =
2.666 kW. The Q4 bus ledger takes the difference (HL-Q4-REJ moves by difference).

## F-13 EW sheet masses relabeled copper-equivalent

sizing/EW_CELLS.md (AQS-DUTY-051) labeled each cell row with the feed metal next to the
sheet mass, which read as a per-species mass (59.87 kg/d "Au"), while the mass is the
copper-equivalent bounding rate the Faraday current is computed from (AQS-ASSAY-001
section 4, M_Cu z = 2). Generator edit (`docs-authority/_ew_sizing.py`): the table
column is now "Cu-eq sheet, kg/d", the metal column is marked "cascade order", and a
finding states that no per-species mass is published until an assay row supports it.
Rev D. No per-species masses were invented.

## F-24 RECTIFIER 12 kW book removed as a double count (A-M-231 restated)

Decision: REMOVE, on the node's own evidence. The cited basis ("DC to cells and
induction", notes/POWER_DUTIES.md via AQS-PWR-001) is booked once as the AQS-ELEC-004
bounding AC row (params.rect_ac_input_kW 38.66 kW, A-M-255); the induction units carry
their own node stamps; and the "48 V rail" re-branding has no load table anywhere
(internal CRITICAL_PATH says 20 kW; the per-motor 48 V driver loads live in the unit
stamps, OTHER_INVENTORY). The rectifier auxiliaries sit inside the 20 percent loss at
eff 0.80 (RECT_EW.md), so a separate auxiliary stamp would count them twice.
Changes: node stamp 12 to 0 kW with the evidence note; S17 retired (presence retired,
note names the live path); the reject IA-RECT-Q4 re-books from the orphaned 1.0 kW
bound of the removed basis to the calculated conversion loss (AC 38.663 - DC 30.930 at
eff 0.80 = 7.7326 kW, A-M-246 restated), which the Q4 thermal ledger now counts.
Pages made true: POWER.md (summary row, Rev B), RECT_EW.md (section 4 reconciled, Rev D).

## F-26 ZNBR2 HER H2 ledgered into the H2 pool: new IA-ZNBR-H2 edge

The HER hydrogen (A-M-135 basis: 0.66 percent of charge current plus open-circuit zinc
corrosion; 8.617 m3/d = 0.775 kg/d on the booked charge) was generated but unledgered -
the ZNBR2 unit balance hid it inside the closed purge loop. ess_tf (the storage transfer
function) now books it on a new mass edge IA-ZNBR-H2 (ZNBR2 to HDR_H2, H2 composition)
and shrinks the electrolyte bleed IA-ZNBR-BD by the same mass (the make-up water leaves
as H2 instead of accumulating), so the ZNBR2 balance stays closed. Downstream the pool
closes by the existing by-difference writers: the header residual passes into the dryer
feed (IA-HDR-H2-02 1.975 to 2.750 kg/d), and the electrolyser is sized net of the pool
credit (IA-ELY-H2 9.49 to 8.71 kg/d - the recovered ZnBr2 hydrogen displaces electrolyser
production). Recombination with Br2 stays uncredited, as A-M-135 states.

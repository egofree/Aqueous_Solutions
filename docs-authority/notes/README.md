# Research notes

Persistent project notes. Not drawing scripts. Not a finished sheet.

Corrected duties are in `COMPLETION_DUTIES.md` (repo: `docs-authority/COMPLETION_DUTIES.md`). The 2026-10-04 basis below is kept so the assumption trail is not only in a chat turn.

Plant rate: leach discharge 2 m³/h at 2 g/L dissolved metal. Energy may be external. Mass in is waste or natural feedstock only. Numbers are the assumptions sheet unless marked.

## CONC-ED

Not an evaporator. Alumina crossflow, 0.2 µm, 3 m/s, 80 L/m²·h, 25 m². Cake backflushes to sink dewater. CPVC coalescer ahead of the stack. No carbon: chlorate attacks it. CEM stack, 20 pairs, 0.25 m² each, 5 m² installed. Anode Ti / PbO2. Liquor held under pH 1.5 so iron stays dissolved. CIP is BMED HCl only; caustic drops iron in the housing. High dP bypasses to CSTR 1. Diluate returns to CSTR 1. Concentrate is 0.17 m³/h at 20 g/L, 85 % of the cations, 3.4 kg/h dissolved metal, and that is what enters EW-1.

## Solids line

Undissolved sink only. Sequence is rinse, 450–550 °C indirect burnout, mill under 300 µm, 80–90 °C NaOH digest, alkali fusion of what the digest does not open, anion split, silica gel, reduction. Soda-lime opens in the digest. Quartz, feldspar, and olivine do not; they recycle through a 300–500 °C NaOH / Na2CO3 sinter and return to the digest. Grit that survives both is alumina, SiC, and zircon. Dense plastic leaves at the vibratory split and does not enter burnout.

## AEM bank

Two feeds. Post-polish brine is acidic; it is raised to pH 9 with BMED NaOH so borate is B(OH)4− and phosphate is HPO4 2−. Digest liquor is already alkaline. AEM stack, PP frame. Diluate to BMED salt. Borate concentrate is acidified and calcined to B2O3 for the boron retort. Phosphate to the farm. Sulfate to the PK-5B double-sulfate cut, excess to BMED. Fluoride to the HF electrodialysis cell, not the farm. Arsenic does not enter this stack.

## K split

Cooled crystallizer, PP, 5–10 °C, on the sodium–potassium brine after the anion bank and before BMED. KCl drops. Mother liquor is NaCl and goes to the BMED salt chamber. KCl to the farm.

## N scrub

Packed tower on the burnout and pyro gas after the demister. BMED HCl absorbs NH3 from nylon, resin, and biomass. Product is NH4Cl to the farm. NOx is not this tower; it stays on the alkaline scrubber. No Haber loop on this sheet.

## PK-5B

Batch PP lot tank. Not a flow cell. Oxalate cake from PK-5 is dissolved in BMED HCl. Sodium sulfate from the anion bank precipitates the La–Ce double sulfate. Nd/Pr and Dy/Tb stay in liquor and are reprecipitated as oxalates at stepped pH, then calcined to oxide lots. Lots go to the calciothermic retort. Oxalic acid is a consumable until a synthesis row exists.

## Molten salt — corrected

Superseded reading: four electrolytic pots in series, chlorine from a rare-earth pot.

Current reading: three electrolytic pots, parallel, and one retort. Aluminum is calcined, chlorinated with char and recycled chlorine, then electrolyzed near 700 °C. Magnesium chloride is dehydrated under HCl, then electrolyzed near 700 °C. Calcium chloride is electrolyzed near 800 °C on a contact cathode because the metal dissolves in the melt. Rare earths are not a pot. Oxide lots are reduced with calcium in a sealed retort at 900–1100 °C. CaO slag returns to the calcium feed. Chlorine comes from the three pots only and returns to the CRU.

## Silicon and boron — corrected

Superseded reading: one arc, boron oxide reduced in that train, then one solidification step.

Current reading: two vessels. Silicon arc near 1800 °C, gel plus char, SiO2 + 2C → Si + 2CO, slag to fusion, then directional solidification of the silicon only. Boron retort near 850 °C, B2O3 plus magnesium or calcium, then an HCl leach of the oxide. Boron oxide does not enter the arc.

# Module standard

Document number: AQS-MOD-001
Revision: B
Date: 2026-10-10
Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. One bay. 48 V at 100 A. Fluid at 12 GPM, 150 bar. No process fluid in the frame itself.

2. Size. Tube 4 × 4 × 1/4 inch. Outside 1200 × 600 × 800 mm. Mass of frame: 4 rails at 1.2 m, 4 at 0.6 m, 4 at 0.8 m, 10.4 m of tube. Tube is 9.3 kg/m. Frame is 97 kg before the process.

3. Material. ASTM A500 tube. Welded. Feet are 10 mm plate.

4. Nozzles. Power: Anderson SB350, −X face, 200 mm up. Fluid: two 12 GPM couplers, +X face, 300 mm up. Optional 28 GPM coupler beside them.

5. Instruments. Voltage and current on the connector. High current trips the contactor.

6. Relief. Frame is not pressurized. Fluid relief is on the power cube, not on the frame.

7. Code. Shop weld. Not stamped.

8. Open items. None on the frame. The process on the frame has its own page.

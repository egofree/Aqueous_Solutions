# Hydrochar and EAOP

Document number: AQS-DUTY-015
Revision: A
Date: 2026-10-09
Authority: graph Rev Q. Not a PE stamp.

Woody trim, 3 kg/d, passes through a low-temperature hydrothermal step before compost. Yu et al., Bioresource Technology 394 (2024) 129980, report a 64.36 mg/g drop in lignocellulose when hydrochar is added to the aerobic pile, and attribute it to electron transfer from the oxygen-bearing pore surface. On this feed that is 0.19 kg/d, sent to the CO2 header. The compost mass is otherwise unchanged.

The pretreat is no longer the 80 °C caustic hold. The RMIT thesis page did not load. The duty used is the summary given with it: 25 °C, 2.0 V, 30 min, 25 wt% solids, 204.4 mg/g reducing sugars. On the 8 kg/d charge that is 1.64 kg/d sugar, sent to the digester and returned as digestate. The solvent pair remains 10 wt% 1-butyl-3-methylimidazolium acetate in γ-valerolactone from Sun, Greaves, and Othman, ACS Sustainable Chem. Eng. 2020, 8, 18177. The ionic liquid is not copied. The mass cut still passes, 1037.6 kg/d in and out.

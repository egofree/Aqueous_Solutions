# Farm waste duty

Document number: AQS-DUTY-014
Revision: A
Date: 2026-10-09
Authority: graph Rev P. Not a PE stamp.

Food no longer leaves as surplus. S14 is 2.4 kg/d to habitation. S14X is 1.8 kg/d to the animal pen. The pen returns 0.4 kg/d as food and 1.4 kg/d as manure.

Manure and fecal solids go to the digester. Woody trim and board scrap go to compost. Digestate and compost return to the nutrient tank. Biogas goes to the electromethanogenic cell: stainless cathode, 1.0 V, 35 °C, methane to the fuel tank. The Chemical Engineering Journal page did not open. The cell is sized to the biogas it is fed, 1.3 kg/d.

Lignocellulose, 8 kg/d, is taken from the still feed, and the still is resized. Pretreat is BMED caustic at 80 °C. Sun, Greaves, and Othman, ACS Sustainable Chem. Eng. 2020, 8, 18177, report 73% delignification at 25 °C, 2.0 V, 4 h, in 10 wt% 1-butyl-3-methylimidazolium acetate in γ-valerolactone. The ionic liquid is not copied. Design split is 50%. Cellulose and lignin go to the board mill. Board, paper, and cardboard go to the shop. Scrap returns to compost.

Dock feedstock is 102.7 kg/d, sized to the product that still leaves. Treated water, 120 kg/d, returns to the roof tank. The mass component cut passes: 1037.6 kg/d in and out.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This page lists the commodity parts, or jellybean parts, of the in-house process equipment: motors, bearings, fasteners, seals, gaskets and pipe. For each family it states the standard that fixes its sizes and whether the plant buys it or makes it on an in-house line. It is generated from the bills of materials of the manufacturing models in `models/mfg/`, so it covers each unit once that unit has a manufacturing model (62 models at this generation).

The owner set the rule on 2026-10-15. Process equipment is designed in-house. Commodity parts are held to ISO/IEC standard sizes so that each can be bought from any maker against the standard designation or made on an in-house line from the open design below. Coverage grows as each unit gets its manufacturing model (X-MFG-31, X-ELS-02).

## 2. Motors

The motors are four-pole, totally enclosed and fan-cooled, for 480 V at 60 Hz, either rewound or rated for both frequencies. Each motor takes the next preferred rating of IEC 60034-1 at or above 1.15 times its shaft power, in an IEC 60072-1 frame. A pump without a sized curve takes the 0.18 kW class (A-M-117). ## 5. Pipe and fittings

Pipe in the bills of materials is PVC-U to ASTM D1785, or its metric equivalent to ISO 1452-2, in the schedule each entry gives, joined with ASTM D2564 solvent cement.

- ALD: Reactor tube DN15 Sch 40, 600 mm.
- EVAP_ACID_VS: Shell PVC-U DN150 Sch 80 x 1400 mm with solvent-welded caps.
- FORM: Reactor tube DN15 Sch 40, 600 mm.
- HNO3: Pt-Rh gauze in DN25 Sch 40 SA-312 310S, 300 mm, 200 mm insulation.
- HTC: Autoclave spool DN100 Sch 10S, 200 mm.
- MEAM: Reactor tube DN15 Sch 40, 600 mm.
- MEOH_SYNTH: Reactor tube DN50 Sch 40 SA-106 B, 1702 mm, 1 off.
- MEOH_SYNTH: Jacket DN100 Sch 40, 1402 mm, thermal oil in/out DN25.

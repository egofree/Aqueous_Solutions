Rule (owner, 2026-10-15). Process equipment is designed in-house. Commodity parts are held to ISO/IEC standard sizes so that each can be bought from any maker against the standard designation or made on an in-house line from the open design below. Coverage grows as each unit gets its mfg model (X-MFG-31, X-ELS-02); the register is regenerated from the BOMs, never hand-edited.

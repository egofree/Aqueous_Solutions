## 1. Purpose and scope

This page gives the production plan of each pipe family in the pipe inventory AQS-INV-003. A pipe family is the pipe of one piping class, size and material. For each family the plan states how much pipe is bought, how it is cut, which fittings it needs, which welding procedure (WPS) joins it and how it is tested. The material, fitting standard, temperature rating and design basis of each class are given in its class sheet in AQS-PIP-003 and are not repeated here.

## 2. Summary and rules

The inventory has 21 families with 4,404.6 m of pipe in 254 lines. The pipe is delivered as 749 stock lengths of 6.096 m, with a cutting allowance of 157.4 m, which is 3.6 % of the installed length. The families need 727 elbows and 508 flange sets and have 2333 joints. Every figure is taken line by line from AQS-INV-003.

The following rules apply to every family.

1. No unit on the plant graph extrudes plastic pipe or forms metal tube. EXTRUDE presses copper and aluminium rod and profile for WIRE_DRAW, and FILM_CAST casts PVDF sheet. Pipe is therefore bought to the family standard and class sheet. Making pipe in house remains an open capital option; it would need a new unit on the graph and its own task, and this page does not assume it.
2. Fittings are bought to the fitting standard of the family: socket fittings to ASTM D2467 or F439, butt-fusion fittings to ASTM D3261 or ISO 10931 and ISO 15494 with stub ends and backing rings, wrought long-radius elbows to ASME B16.9 in A403 or A234 material, and flanges to ASME B16.5 in A182 or A105 material. The elbows and stub ends of the FRP class F1 are contact-moulded in the shop to ASTM D5421, under the laminating rules of sheet F1 and WPS-08, which include a laminator demonstration to Part 7 of [ASME_RTP1].
3. Joints are made to the issued WPS of AQS-WLD-002 for the material and joint type. The extent of examination is that of the register row, which is 5 % random examination for normal fluid service under paragraph 341.4.1 of ASME B31.3.
4. On receipt, the pipe is checked against the family standard. It is then tested to its class sheet in AQS-PIP-003, which is a hydrostatic test as stated on the sheet. Solvent-cemented and fused joints are inspected after the cure time in the WPS and before they are covered.

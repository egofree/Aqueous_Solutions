## 1. Summary and dispositions

21 families, 7,267.3 m pipe in 375 lines, delivered as 1226 stock lengths of 6.096 m with a booked cutting allowance of 200.6 m (2.8 % of installed length); 1086 elbows, 750 flange sets, 3576 joints. Every figure is the AQS-INV-003 inventory roster, line by line.

- **Class authority**: the AQS-PIP-003 class sheet letter per family (P1..P5, M1, M2, F1) carries the material,   fitting standard, temperature rating and design basis; this desk does not restate them.
- **Making route**: The graph books no plastic pipe extrusion line and no tube mill (EXTRUDE is the 50 t billet-to-rod/profile press of the Cu/Al profile class feeding WIRE_DRAW; FILM_CAST is the 0.14 kg/d PVDF cast sheet line). Stock is therefore bought to the family standard and class sheet, and in-house extrusion or a tube mill stays an open capital option - it would need a new booked machine and a named unit task, and nothing here assumes it.
- **Fittings**: bought to the family fitting standard (socket D2467/F439, butt-fusion D3261 and ISO 10931/15494   stub ends with backing rings, wrought ASME B16.9 LR elbows on A403/A234 body material, ASME B16.5 flanges on   A182/A105). The F1 FRP elbows and stub ends are contact-moulded in the shop to ASTM D5421 - the class   laminate discipline of sheet F1 and WPS-08 (laminator demonstration, ASME_RTP1 Part 7).
- **Joints**: executed to the issued WPS of AQS-WLD-002 per material and joint type; examination extent is the   register row (ASME B31.3 341.4.1 normal fluid service, 5 % random).
- **Test**: receipt identity check against the family standard, then the class test of the AQS-PIP-003 letter   (hydrostatic per the sheet; solvent and fusion joints sighted per the WPS cure table before any covering).

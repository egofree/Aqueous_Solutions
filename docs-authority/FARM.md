# Farm

Document number: AQS-DUTY-027
Revision: A
Date: 2026-10-09
Authority: graph Rev AB. Study rates for four people. Not a PE stamp.

Stock. Black soldier fly larvae take the 1.80 kg/d food waste. Larvae go to the fish, 0.40, and the chickens, 0.30. Frass goes to compost. Duckweed, Lemna minor, grows on the fish-water raceway and is fed to fish, chickens, and goats, with a 0.05 kg/d human reserve. Red wigglers take the manure and return castings to the beds. Two honeybee hives pollinate. Six hens lay. Two dwarf goats take the woody browse and give milk. Tilapia are the fish. Crayfish sit in the sump and take the fish detritus. Azolla on the rice bed fixes nitrogen and feeds the fish. Oyster mushroom grows on spent fiber.

Crops on the beds. Bean, lentil, peanut. Rice on the flooded bed with azolla. Wheat, corn. Potato and sweet potato. Sunflower. Carrot, beet, sugar beet. Microgreens. Coconut is a dwarf trial only. It will not carry the oil or the calorie load in this envelope. Plantain is the fruit that yields. Allium and brassica greens cover the vitamin gap the grain list does not. Duckweed and azolla are the aquatic crops.

Loops. Waste to larvae to fish and hens. Fish water to duckweed and beds. Manure to worms to beds. Browse to goats. Spent fiber to mushroom to compost. Nothing in this set is bought feed.

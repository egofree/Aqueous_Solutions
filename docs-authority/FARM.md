# Farm

Document number: AQS-DUTY-029
Revision: A
Date: 2026-10-09
Authority: graph Rev AD. Study rates for four people. Not a PE stamp.

Toilet and fixers are AQS-DUTY-028. Stock and crops are AQS-DUTY-027.

Mycology is two rooms. The straw room is Pleurotus on wheat straw, pasteurized at 60–70 °C for 1 h. Harvest 0.12 kg/d. Spent straw to compost. The block room is sterilized hardwood and bran, 121 °C. Shiitake and lion's mane are the food, 0.08 kg/d. Reishi and turkey tail are the slow conks, 0.03 kg/d. Cordyceps militaris is not drawn. It eats rice the habitation needs.

The beds take Rhizophagus irregularis, raised on compost and vermiculite. It is an arbuscular inoculum for bean, corn, wheat, and potato. It is not a fruiting crop. Brassica will not take it. Potato inoculation at the seedling stage raised colonization about 8 points and held yield at a 25 % lower phosphorus rate in the 2025 trial. Bean yield rose when the same class of inoculum was paired with fertilizer.


Mycelial compost is AQS-DUTY-030. The hot pile stays above 55 °C and kills the spawn, so the inoculum goes on after the pile falls below 40 °C. Spent Pleurotus straw, 0.18 kg/d, and the spent blocks inoculate 20 kg/d of cooled compost. The myceliated mass goes to the worms, and the castings go to the beds. The arbuscular inoculum is still added at the bed, not in the pile.


Fruit and vegetables are AQS-DUTY-031. Day-neutral strawberry is in gutters, not in the grain bed. Fig is in tubs. Blueberry is in acid pots at pH 4.5–5.5, off the main beds. Tomato, cucumber, pepper, lettuce, spinach, kale, allium, and basil take fish water on organic media. Apple, peach, and citrus are not drawn. They need a chill or a larger crown than this envelope has.


Aquaponics is AQS-DUTY-032. Tilapia tank, 2 m3, 26 °C. Water leaves at 2000 kg/d to a settler, then a nitrifying biofilter, then the vegetable bed, the duckweed raceway, and the grain bed, and returns through the sump. Crayfish take the settled solids. Ammonia is oxidized to nitrite and then nitrate at 0.5–1.5 g NH4-N/m2/day. The loop is held at pH 6.8–7.2. The leach bleed, 129.63 kg/d of salts, stays on the compost side. It does not enter the fish tank.


Named crops are AQS-DUTY-033. Tomato 0.12, cucumber 0.08, pepper 0.06, lettuce 0.08, spinach 0.05, kale 0.05, allium 0.06, and basil 0.03 kg/d take fish water on organic media. Rosemary, 0.02 kg/d, is on a dry gutter. It does not sit in the flooded bed. The lumped vegetable harvest is zero so it is not counted twice.


Nutrient balance is AQS-DUTY-034. Wet rates from Rev AH. Dry matter: larvae 30 %, duckweed and azolla 7 %. Nitrogen is protein/6.25.

Fish feed is 10.7 g/d N, 1.8 g/d P, 2.6 g/d K. Larvae carry 9.0 g of the nitrogen. The fish retain about 30 %, 3.2 g/d N. Excretion is 7.5 g/d N, which the biofilter oxidizes to nitrate.

Crop and duckweed uptake is 3.9 g/d N, 0.7 g/d P, 3.3 g/d K. The loop is long 3.6 g/d N and 1.1 g/d P, and short 0.7 g/d K. Potassium is the nutrient to add. It comes from the urine tank, not from the leach bleed. Urine at 1.30 kg/d and 0.7 % N is 9 g/d N, and it stays on the nutrient tank. It is not poured into the fish tank.

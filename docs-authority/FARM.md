# Aeroponics

Document number: AQS-DUTY-039
Revision: A
Date: 2026-10-09
Authority: graph Rev AL. Study. Not a PE stamp.

The mister takes clarified biofilter water, not tank water. A 100 µm screen sits ahead of the nozzles. Droplets are 30–80 µm. The cycle is 3 s on, 180 s off. Pressure is 5–7 bar. A pump stop of about 15 minutes dries the roots, so the sump holds a flooded standby and the backup generator is on this circuit.

Lettuce, basil, and strawberry are the mist crops. Potato under mist is a seed duty. The published yields are minitubers, 1–3 g each, tens per plant. That is not the 0.09 kg/m²/day food yield the calorie hall is sized to. The 70 m² potato bed stays on the organic media. It does not move to the mister.

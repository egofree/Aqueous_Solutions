## 1. Framework

1. Drivers (`flow_graph/param/drivers.json`, loader `drivers.py`): independent inputs. Each has units and a published reference key, a register id, or an owner basis. A value no published source fixes carries `needs_verification` and `verification_test`; the loader fails otherwise.
2. Balance solver (`solver.py`): per-unit transfer functions (stoichiometry, splits, Faraday, energy) compute edge rates and duties from the drivers. `check()` fails on a closure error of a solved unit and lists misfits against the graph.
3. Sizing library (`sizing/`): electrochem (Faraday current, cathode count, DC power), heat (sensible duty, LMTD, area), lines (velocity bore, DN pick), power (PV kWp, 3-phase amps, NEC breaker, rectifier channel), tank (hydrostatic, volume, agitator power, plate and beam stress, pump power), relief (API 520), scrubber (packed column, Perry sec. 14). Each function names its equation and reference.
4. Generated outputs: scripts in `docs-authority/` call the library with drivers; the commit regenerates them. `write_graph.py` writes solved values into the graph (stage `process`: acid loop, BMED, EW, RECT_EW, bus balance; stage `power`: PV, storage, inverters from AQS-ELEC-007).
5. Gate: `flow_graph/param/test_param_misfit.py` fails when a solved unit does not close or a compared graph value deviates from the solver by more than 1 %.

## 4a. Acid loop decision (X-PAR-04)

1. BMED runs at 2 M product, inside the published EDBM limit of about 3.6 M acid (EDBM_WHEY_2021). Stack power 26.93 kW from 6.7 kWh/kg NaOH at 2 M (was 16.13 kW on an unsourced 2.1 kWh/kg pair). Study.
2. EVAP_ACID concentrates HCl from 7.07 wt% to 20 wt%, below the HCl-water azeotrope 20.2 wt% (PERRY9_HCL): feed 1243.3 kg/d, water off 803.8 kg/d, 2.41 kW at 72.0 kWh/t (VOGELBUSCH_MVR upper bound). Study.
3. EVAP_BASE concentrates NaOH from 7.41 wt% to 20 wt%: feed 1330.1 kg/d, water off 837.3 kg/d, 2.51 kW. Study.
4. Condensate returns to the BMED stack as water make-up (IA-EVAP_ACID-COND, IA-EVAP_BASE-COND). Both evaporators have 480 V feeds (SF-EVAP_ACID, SF-EVAP_BASE) and heat edges to HEAT_SINK.
5. EW at 2.12 V per cell and current efficiency 0.85 (USBM_CL_EW chloride EW basis), 0.3 V bus drop, rectifier efficiency 0.8: 12781 A, DC 30.93 kW, AC 38.66 kW.
6. The bus change is carried on IA-PV-01; PV, storage and inverters follow AQS-ELEC-007 (POWER_SIZING.md Rev C). The EW aisle and the evaporator skids are re-placed (placement seed Rev I, AQS-PLACE-STUDY-001).

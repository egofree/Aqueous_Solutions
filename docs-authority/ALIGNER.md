# Contact aligner

Document number: AQS-LITH-001
Revision: C
Date: 2026-10-11
Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Expose a 100 mm wafer. Novolac, 1 µm. 365 nm. Contact, not projection.

2. Size. Dose 10 mW/cm² for 20 s, 200 mJ/cm². LED array is 1 W optical, which covers a 100 mm wafer at that dose. Stage travel 100 mm. Leadscrew 10 mm, 1 mm pitch, cut on the lathe. Repeatability 10 µm is one hundredth of a turn, read by a dial, not an encoder. Mask is chrome on glass, pattern cut on the mill at 100 µm.

3. Material. Frame 6061, 1200 × 600 × 800. Stage aluminum. Leadscrew 4140.

4. Nozzles. One SB350. Resist drain DN15 to the leach.

5. Instruments. Dose timer. Lamp current. Home switch on the stage.

6. Relief. None.

7. Code. Shop.

8. Open items. None on the stage. Overlay accuracy is not measured.

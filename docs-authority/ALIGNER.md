# Contact aligner module

Document number: AQS-LITH-001
Revision: B
Date: 2026-10-10

1. Basis. Expose a 100 mm wafer. Novolac, 1 µm. 365 nm.

2. Size. Dose is 10 mW/cm² for 20 s, 200 mJ/cm². Stage travel 100 mm. Repeatability 10 µm. That is a leadscrew the lathe cuts, not a bought stage.

3. Material. Stage aluminum, shop-cut. Mask chrome on glass. Lamp is a 365 nm LED array.

4. Nozzles. Power connector. Resist drain DN15 to the leach. No process gas.

5. Instruments. Dose timer. Lamp current. Stage home switch.

6. Relief. None.

7. Code. Shop.

8. Open items. Mask writing method is a mill, not a laser writer. The dose and the stage are set.

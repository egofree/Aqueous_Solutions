# Contact aligner module

Document number: AQS-LITH-001
Revision: A
Date: 2026-10-10
Authority: critical path item 6.

Exposure. 365 nm LED, 10 mW/cm2, 20 s. Contact, not projection. No bought stepper optic. Mask is a chrome-on-glass plate cut on the mill.

Resist. Novolac from the formaldehyde reactor and cresol. Spin coat 1 µm. Develop in aqueous base from BMED.

Stage. 100 mm travel, 10 µm repeatability. That is a large-geometry device. It is not a sub-micron aligner. The lathe and the mill make the stage.

Frame. 1200 mm module. Coater, aligner, and developer are three stations on one frame. Spent resist to the leach.

# Silicon reduction

Document number: AQS-SI-001
Revision: C
Date: 2026-10-11
Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. SiO₂ + 2C → Si + 2CO. Silica from the digest. Char from the pyrolyzer.

2. Size. 0.04 kg/d silicon. Silica 0.04 × 60/28 = 0.086 kg. Carbon 0.04 × 24/28 = 0.034 kg. Energy 13 kWh/kg, so 0.52 kWh, 3 minutes on the 10 kW furnace. Yield 70%. Unreacted silica returns to the digest.

3. Material. Same crucible and coil as the furnace. Electrodes are char compacts, consumable. One compact per charge.

4. Nozzles. Same as the furnace. Off-gas is CO, to the afterburner, not the room.

5. Instruments. Integrate power to 0.52 kWh and stop.

6. Relief. Off-gas open to the afterburner.

7. Code. Shop.

8. Open items. Compact density is not measured. The charge and the energy are.

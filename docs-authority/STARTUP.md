# Startup sequence (cold start from empty)

Document number: AQS-COM-002
Revision: A
Date: 2026-10-16
Authority: AQS-PLAN-007 X-COM-02. Generated by `docs-authority/_startup.py`. Study. A-M-201.

| Step | Stage | Actions | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Prerequisites | AQS-COM-001 phases 1-3 complete for every system; gas detection (AQS-SAF-006) and fire protection (AQS-SAF-005) in service; fire dead-storage of the water tower full (AQS-BLD-014) before any flammable inventory is admitted (NFPA 25). | - |
| 1 | Power | PV and inverter on; ZnBr2 first charge to 80 % of 3046 kWh at 989.0 kW with the purge and H2 detection live (NFPA 855); backup generator run test on load. | 2.5 h |
| 2 | Water | Fill the water tower live store and the process water loop; start UPW polish and recirculate to spec. | set by the site supply rate (site data) |
| 3 | Heat ladder | Ladder A then B. Q4 batteries (69.7 kWh total) warmed by GEO_HP, one unit per ladder (0.7 kW each); Q1-Q3 batteries charge from their own process sources as those start (step 6), diode rule held (no uphill valve). Batteries: Q1_A Q1 35.8 kWh; Q1_B Q1 35.8 kWh; Q2_A Q2 20.3 kWh; Q2_B Q2 20.3 kWh; Q3_A Q3 37.2 kWh; Q3_B Q3 37.2 kWh; Q4_A Q4 34.8 kWh; Q4_B Q4 34.8 kWh. | 50.7 h (Q4) |
| 4 | Inerting and H2/O2 | N2 purge of the H2 and O2 systems to < 1 % O2 (H2) and < 1 % fuel (O2) (NFPA 2); electrolyser to 25 %, 50 %, 100 %; fill the H2 bank (1.17 m3 water volume, 17.4 kg at 200 bar) at 9.36 kg/d H2. | 1.9 d |
| 5 | Gas separation and cold | CO2 TSA beds regenerated once at 204 C before first adsorption; CO2 condenser pulled down 25 C to -20 C (20 kg cold mass, A-M-201) by the chiller (33 W); with the EXPANDER backup alone (82 W) 1.5 h. No cryogenic column (CRYO_ASU is TSA and condensing only). | 3.8 h |
| 6 | Process systems | In AQS-COM-001 order: GEO, DIGESTER, MEOH, OIL, GLASS, NUT, FARM, HAB, MFG, FAB, FOUNDRY, POLYMERS. Each at 25 %, 50 %, 100 % of the graph rate, 24 h per step, hold point on product and vent analysis. | 3 d per system, overlapping |
| 7 | Hydro and fuel cell | Hydro set run on a tower draw-down test; fuel cell run on bank H2 to prove the backup path (X-RED). | 1 d |

Shutdown to safe state from any step: process feeds off, H2/O2 isolated at the bank ESD valves (AQS-CTL-003), batteries hold heat, ZnBr2 to standby.

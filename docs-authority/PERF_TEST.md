## 1. Purpose and scope

The performance test shows, after commissioning, that the plant makes its products at the rates the design predicts and uses no more power than the design allows. This page defines the test case, the pass criteria and the guarantee points: each product stream, the rate given for it in the flow graph, how it is measured, and the band within which the measured rate must fall.

## 2. Test case and pass criteria

The test uses the bounding case, the class A feed of AQS-ASSAY-001 at 102.68 kg/d. The plant runs for 72 h at steady state, starting after step 6 of the commissioning procedure AQS-COM-002. The test passes if every measured mass rate is within 10 % of its graph rate, if the plant power is no more than 10 % above the graph total, and if the mass balance closes within 2 %.

A point that fails is run again once after a hold of 24 h. If it fails a second time, a design query is raised against the sizing module of the unit.

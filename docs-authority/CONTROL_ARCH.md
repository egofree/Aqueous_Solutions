# Control philosophy and PLC architecture

Document number: AQS-CTL-002
Revision: A
Date: 2026-10-16
Authority: AQS-PLAN-007 X-CTL-02. Generated by `docs-authority/_plc_arch.py` from AQS-CTL-001 and AQS-INV-004. Study. A-M-194.

## Philosophy

- One PLC per sector, so a sector fault stays in its sector. The plant runs from the graph set-points; every loop is single-input single-output PI unless the unit design names a cascade.
- A sector whose controller loss would put the occupants, their air, water or food, or the plant power at risk before repair has a redundant CPU pair with bumpless switchover and dual 24 V supplies fed from the critical bus (NFPA 111 store behind it). The sectors and reasons are listed below. Every other sector has a single CPU.
- Safety trips are not in the basic control PLCs: they go to the safety functions of X-CTL-03 after the HAZOP (X-SAF-01). Fire alarm is a separate NFPA72_2022 panel that signals the PLCs, never the reverse.
- Network: all PLCs, HMIs and the historian on one IEC62439_2 MRP fibre ring (a single cut recovers within 200 ms); BLDC and stepper drivers on a sector fieldbus.
- Programs in IEC61131_3 structured text in the repository, simulated against the graph before download (X-CTL-04).

## Redundant CPU pairs

| Sector | Why its controller is redundant |
|---|---|
| CRYO | oxygen and hydrogen storage and the habitat air handling |
| ELEC | plant power and the critical bus (PV, flow battery, genset and fuel cell are ELEC units) |
| FARM | food and the grow-bed air loop of the habitat |
| GAS | the hydrogen and oxygen headers and their relief |
| GEO | the ground-source heat pumps |
| HAB | the occupied habitat |
| NUT | nutrient dosing of the grow beds (FARM) |
| PUMPED_HYDRO | the backup energy store of the critical bus |
| WTR | drinking water and sanitation for the occupants |

Kept on a single CPU by decision:

- **HEAT**: owner decision 2026-10-24 (AQS-OQ-001 Q-014): HEAT holds the insulated thermal stores (Q1 to Q4 tiers) and their heat exchangers. A controller outage stops heat moving between the stores; the stores keep their heat, losing only their standing loss. It is not a life-support or power function.

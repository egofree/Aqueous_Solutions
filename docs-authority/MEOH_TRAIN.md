## 1. Purpose and scope

This page describes how the plant makes, stores, uses and ships methanol. It covers the catalytic synthesis from carbon dioxide and hydrogen, the fermentation route, the methanol store, the methanol fuel cell, the recovery of water and the product store. The sizing of each unit is in AQS-DUTY-062 and AQS-DUTY-077. The hydrogen fuel cell is described in AQS-FC-001 (`FUEL_CELL.md`).

## 2. Production

### 2.1 Catalytic synthesis

`MEOH_SYNTH` makes methanol by the reaction CO₂ + 3 H₂ → CH₃OH + H₂O over a catalyst. Each kilogram of methanol takes 0.189 kg of hydrogen and 1.374 kg of carbon dioxide. The hydrogen comes from the store `CRYO_H2` (stream S74) and the carbon dioxide from `CRYO_CO2` (stream S73) and from the carbon dioxide header `HDR_CO2`. The crude methanol, 34.51 kg/d on the graph, goes to `MEOH_TANK` (stream S75), and the water of reaction goes to `MEOH_COND`. The conditions of the reactor and the check for hydrogen attack are in AQS-REV-H2-001 (`H2_EMBRITTLEMENT_REVIEW.md`).

### 2.2 Fermentation

`MEOH_FERM` ferments organic material from the habitation (stream S78) and sends its methanol to `MEOH_TANK` (stream S77). Its digestate goes to `BLACK_DIGEST`. Fermentation is a second, independent source of methanol; it is small beside the synthesis and does not replace it.

## 3. Storage, use and product

`MEOH_TANK` holds the methanol from both sources. It feeds the methanol fuel cell `MEOH_FC` with 6.28 kg/d, supplies methanol as a carbon source to the fluoropolymer syntheses `VDF_SYN` and `TFE_SYN`, and sends the remainder to the product store `PROD_MEOH`. The product, 27.88 kg/d, is shipped at the north dock, so the carbon leaves the plant as product rather than as a vent.
`MEOH_FC` has a continuous output of 1.0 kW. It feeds direct current to the battery side of the inverters `SOL_ARK_60K` (stream S79), alongside the hydrogen fuel cell, and its heat to the Q4 header `Q4_BUS` (stream S80). Its carbon dioxide returns to `CRYO_CO2` (stream S82) and its condensate to `MEOH_COND` (stream S81). It never feeds the generator port of the inverters or a rectifier bus.
`MEOH_COND` condenses the water from the synthesis and the fuel cell and returns it to the water tower `ROOF_TANK`.

## 5. Safety

1. Methanol is toxic by ingestion, inhalation and skin contact. It is stored as the safety data sheet requires, with an eyewash station at the fuel cell, and no open container is kept in the habitation.
2. Methanol is a Class IB flammable liquid under NFPA 30, with a flash point of about 11 °C. The tanks stand outdoors or in a fire-rated room, are bonded and grounded, and hot work in the methanol area needs a permit.
3. Methanol is kept apart from the hydrogen header, from the bromine of the flow battery and from the habitation air handlers. A spill drains to a bonded sump and is treated; it never reaches the clean water reservoirs.
4. The fuel cell enclosure has flammable vapour detection that shuts the fuel valve on alarm.
5. The methanol inventory is tracked in the chemical inventory of AQS-HSE-001 (`HAZARD_SEPARATION.md`).
6. The exhaust of the fuel cell is not vented freely; its carbon dioxide is returned to the store.

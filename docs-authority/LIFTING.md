## 1. Purpose and scope

Equipment in the plant has to be lifted into place when it is installed and lifted out when it is replaced. This page sizes the hoist on each level of the building from the heaviest single item that must be lifted there. The empty weights come from the structural loads (AQS-BLD-013).

Units that are built in place are lifted in parts and are not listed. They are tanks and reservoirs, which are fabricated in place; the PV array, whose modules are lifted one at a time; farm beds and habitation rooms, which are assembled from parts; and headers, which are installed spool by spool. Gas-receiver tube racks are set as two manifolded halves, so the lift is one half.

## 2. Sizing rule

The rated load of a hoist is the next standard size at or above the heaviest lift plus a rigging allowance of 10 %. Each hoist is load-tested at 125 % of its rated load (ASME B30.2). Underhung hoists follow ASME B30.16, and the service class is CMAA 70 class C, which covers maintenance duty. A level with more than ten lifted items has two hoists, so that a failed hoist does not stop maintenance.

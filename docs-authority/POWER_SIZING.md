## 1. Purpose and scope

This page reports how the plant's electricity is generated and stored: the photovoltaic (PV) array on the roof, the zinc-bromine flow battery `ZNBR2`, the hybrid inverters `SOL_ARK_60K`, the proton exchange membrane (PEM) fuel cell `FUEL_CELL`, the export to the grid and the standby generator `BACKUP_GEN`. It states the method and the results. The calculation is in `docs-authority/_power_sizing.py`; `flow_graph/param/write_graph.py` writes its results to the graph, and this page reads them from there. The ground-source heat pump, the organic Rankine cycle unit and the generator fuels are described in AQS-PWR-002 (`GEOTHERMAL_BACKUP.md`). Every value is a study value.

## 2. Method

- Demand. The plant bus demand is the converged continuous load of every unit, including the conditioned extension hall `EXT_HALL`, taken as a flat profile over the day because the process runs continuously.
- Array. The balancing nameplate is the array that delivers the daily demand at the PV yield (driver `pv_yield_kWh_kWp_d`) after the system derate (`pv_system_derate`), with the night served from storage at its round-trip efficiency (`storage_round_trip`, `night_hours`). The installed array is the balancing nameplate times `pv_oversize_factor`, so that the plant has a saleable surplus. The array is on the roof only; the solver grows the building footprint on its bay grid until the roof, after fire access pathways, carries the array.
- Storage. The battery carries the plant bus demand through the night (`night_hours`), so its energy is the demand times the night hours. Its charge rate is the array peak on a cloudy day less the direct load, as recorded in `ZNBR2.params.charge_basis`.
- Fuel cell. The fuel cell turns stored hydrogen from the electrolyser `H2O_ELYZ` back into electricity at a set continuous output. Its round-trip efficiency, from electricity to hydrogen and back, is recorded in `FUEL_CELL.params.reversible_loop`. AQS-FC-001 (`FUEL_CELL.md`) describes it.
- Inverters. The inverter count carries the array output at noon with the store full, which is when export peaks.
- Backup. The generator rating is the driver `backup_gen_rated_kW`. Section 5 compares it with the rule in the driver note, which is 125 % of the critical load list rounded up to a 5 kW step on the standby basis of NFPA 110. The critical loads must be carried for `crit_autonomy_d` days without sun.

## 4. Decisions

- The array is on the roof only. A ground-mounted field was removed, and the building grows until its roof carries the array (X-PAR-07).
- The array is oversized by `pv_oversize_factor` so that the plant exports a surplus (decision D75).
- The extension hall is heated and cooled by air-source heat pumps. A ground-source system was compared and gave a gain inside the uncertainty of the inputs while needing a large bore field, so the air-source design was kept (decision D77).

## 5. Checks

The rule in the driver note gives a generator rating of 20 kW for the critical loads in Table 3.2. The driver holds 40 kW. The two differ, so either the driver or its note must be corrected; the owner decides which.

The bus section rating in `SOL_ARK_60K.params.redundancy` was computed for a bus demand of 218.1 kW. This matches the plant bus demand in Table 3.1.

The rectifier is fed from the inverter bus through three feeders on the one-line diagram AQS-ELEC-002: stream S17 at 12.0 kW for its 48 V rails and auxiliaries, the RECT-EW feeder at the 38.7 kW input for the electrowinning cells, and the RECT-TRIM feeder at the 23.3 kW of the trim heaters. The bus demand counts all of them.

## 1. Summary

176 roster units: **114** with issued materials per wetted stream, **24** with no wetted piped stream (census: every edge solids or electrical), **38 open** with a named reason (section 4). Wetted edge census: 323 wetted rows of AQS-PIP-004 (classes P1..P5, M1, M2, F1 and the section 12.2 families); every one resolves to an issued family; none left in a catch-all.

Rule: a unit closes task 09 where every wetted edge touching it carries a wetted material issued by AQS-STD-002 section 12 - the material column below is quoted from that family row, sources included. A unit stays open where its own duty sheet section 3 still reads "Selection pending" (AQS-STD-001 section 3 asks for the construction material of the unit itself, which is not a stream selection and is real outstanding work for the machines and pot cells) or where its wetted edges fall in the brine family whose wetted material is itself pending. The EOL matrix (AQS-END-002) holds those rows under the same pointer and this doc names the same open set - no registry is contradicted.

## 1. Purpose and scope

The life-support services of the plant (grey water, ultrapure water, etchant return, and the oxygen, nitrogen and carbon dioxide supplies) run in headers on their own pipe racks, the life-header racks. They are kept apart from the process gas racks so that a process fluid cannot be connected by mistake to a service used by people, plants or the fab. This page gives the elevation, size and material of each life header, the connections that each header must never receive, and the runs of the racks.

The process gas racks are built by `models/freecad/pipe_services.py` along the wet aisle, at Z = 2000 mm (liquor), 2200 mm (acid), 2400 mm (hydrogen) and 2600 mm (dust). The life headers are separate model objects at separate elevations.

## 2. Header tiers

Table 2.1 lists the headers from the lowest tier upwards. The elevation Z is measured in millimetres above the level 1 floor (`Z_L1` in `models/freecad/params.py`), to which the model adds each tier height. The last column names the services to which the header must never be connected by a tee (a forbidden tee).

Table 2.1. Life-header tiers

| Z (mm) | Header | Outside diameter (mm) | Material | Forbidden tee |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 900 | HDR_GREY | 50 | PP | Salria, etch |
| 1100 | HDR_UPW | 40 | PP-R or PVDF | leach, grey, etch |
| 1300 | HDR_ETCH | 32 | PVDF | sewer, HDR_GREY, farm beds |
| 2800 | HDR_O2 | 25 | 316 stainless steel | HDR_H2, acid gas |
| 3000 | HDR_N2 | 25 | 316 stainless steel | process headers |
| 3200 | HDR_CO2 | 40 | 316 stainless steel | raw AFTERBURNER flue |

PP-R is random-copolymer polypropylene. Organic material (HDR_ORG) is moved by tote or conveyor and is not a pipe on this rack.

## 3. Rack runs

Four rack spines carry the headers: north, south, west and east. Table 3.1 gives their runs at revision A. The coordinates are in millimetres on the plan grid of the model.

Table 3.1. Rack spines at revision A

| Object | Run |
|---|---|
| RACK_LIFE_N | Y = 7800, from X = −8000 to 24000 (HAB, MFG and FARM) |
| RACK_LIFE_S | Y = −2500, from X = −6000 to 20000 (CRYO and FAB) |
| RACK_LIFE_W | X = −2000, from Y = −4000 to 16000 |
| RACK_LIFE_E | X = 11800, from Y = −6000 to 16000 (FAB and FARM) |

The model no longer uses these fixed coordinates. `models/freecad/params.py` now places the racks on the edges of the base plate: the north spine 1000 mm inside the north edge, the south and west spines 200 mm inside their edges, and the east spine 200 mm inside the east edge, each running the full length or width of the plate.

# Greenhouse water and battery margin

Document number: AQS-DUTY-013
Revision: A
Date: 2026-10-09
Authority: graph Rev O. Not a PE stamp.

The terrace is an enclosed greenhouse. Irrigation is 40 kg/d from the roof tank. Transpiration from the terrace and the grow bed, 56.7 kg/d, goes to the cryo air handler. Condensate returns to the roof tank. Outdoor-air moisture, 173.7 kg/d, remains the source of the coil condensate that goes to the leach. Habitation water still leaves as grey. Calling it vapor as well counted it twice, so that edge was retired.

The zinc-bromine store is 400 kWh. The design-day discharge is 120 kWh, 30% of the store. Charge is 6.67 kW against a 60 kW stack, 11%. The round-trip loss, 1.67 kW, leaves the electrical component. PV daily average is 27.9 kW, about 20% above the matched bus. The spare leaves as curtailment, so the bus is not run against its limit.

The electrical component passes the cut test. The mass component does not: the cut is 935 kg/d in and 1049 kg/d out. That remainder is product and potable water leaving the process loop, not the greenhouse vapor.

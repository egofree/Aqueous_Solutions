# Cold box module

Document number: AQS-SIDE-003
Revision: C
Date: 2026-10-12
Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Separate nitrogen, oxygen, argon, and the noble-gas cuts from the intake. Refrigeration load 5 kW. Nothing vents.

2. Size. Column 150 mm diameter, 20 trays, 0.3 m spacing, packed height 6.0 m. It stands beside the base, guyed to the hall steel. Base frame 600 × 600 × 800, 316L. Expander is reciprocating, 50 mm bore, 80 mm stroke, 600 rpm. Displacement is 1.57 L/s. Inlet 500 kPa, 150 K, density 11.61 kg/m³, mass flow 1575.5 kg/d. Shaft is 614 W at 70% of the isentropic drop to 130 kPa, which is 9.8 N·m at 600 rpm. The 5 kW figure is the cold-box refrigeration load, not this cylinder. At 48 V the shaft is 13 A. One SB350 carries it. Joule-Thomson valve is the backup on the same nozzles. Receivers are 1 L, five of them: helium, neon, argon, krypton, xenon.

3. Material. Column 316L, 3 mm wall. Expander 316L. Base frame 316L. Insulation is the vacuum jacket, not foam that soaks condensate.

4. Nozzles. Feed gas DN25 at the column base. Product nozzles DN15 at the cuts, to the 1 L cans. Drive is one SB350. No liquid drain to the room. Cold condensate returns to the water cycle.

5. Instruments. Column pressure, expander speed, can pressure. High can pressure stops the feed. Low water on the expander jacket, if fitted, trips the drive.

6. Relief. Each can has a DN15 valve to the next lower-pressure receiver, then to the column feed, not to the room. Set at 110% of the can working pressure.

7. Code. Shop. Not stamped. The column is not a transport vessel.

8. Open items. Tray efficiency is not measured. The height, the bore, and the torque are.

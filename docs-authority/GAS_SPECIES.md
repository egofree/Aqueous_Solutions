## 4. Result

O2 consumed 44.91 + combustion oxidant 45.45 - regenerated 13.82 = **76.5 kg/d O2 with no source**. The cold box cannot separate O2 that is not in its feed: its feed is scrubbed flue whose oxygen was consumed. Water electrolysis is not on the graph; the electrowinning cells evolve H2 at the cathode and Cl2 at the anode, not O2.

Options for the owner (X-PAR-12): (a) water electrolyser producing O2 + H2 (8.0 kg O2 per kg H2), which also supplies the fuel-cell H2; (b) an air intake at the north dock (IA-AIR-IN, retired) with the vent of the matching N2; (c) both. X-PAR-11 cannot close the cold-box species split until the source is chosen.

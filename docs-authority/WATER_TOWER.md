## Sizing from need

Store: 200.3 kWh at the bus (HYDRO_SET backup basis, NFPA 111 Class X, A-M-145). Turbine-generator 0.8 (DOE_PSH) less 5 % penstock friction gives 0.76.

Each reservoir level is split into at least two cells, each with its own outlet valve to the penstock, so one cell can be drained for inspection while the store keeps 83 % of its energy (redundancy rule).

Building rule (owner): as the plant grows, HAB/FARM levels are added at 2 x the rate of shop levels (LIFE_LEVELS = 1 + 2 (SHOP_LEVELS - 1)). A taller life stack raises the head and the reservoir shrinks in step. At this scale: 1 shop, 1 life level(s).

## CATCH_TANK basis (check)

The earlier CATCH_TANK was a fixed 12 x 8 x 4 m box scaled by plate area (384 m3; 3,916 kN test load in AQS-BLD-013 rev A). That was not from need. It is now the lower reservoir and holds the full upper volume plus freeboard; terrace rain catch uses the freeboard and overflows to the site drain. The tank is 24.0 x 15.3 x 9.60 m.

## Egress and access (IBC2021_CH10, NFPA101_2024)

The reservoir levels are unoccupied: maintenance access only. The two exit stairs (IBC 1006.3.3) continue through the reservoir storeys to the roof for PV service (IBC 1011.12), in the core fraction above. Each cell has a hatch, ladder and confined-space entry procedure. The reservoir floors add no occupant load and no travel distance to the life floors.

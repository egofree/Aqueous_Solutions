## 1. Purpose and scope

The plant stores energy in an in-house zinc-bromide flow battery (`ZNBR2`). Its round-trip efficiency is predicted from published cell data, not measured. This test builds one stack to the plant cell design and measures the values the prediction rests on, so that they can replace the assumptions. It covers the prediction, the test article, the procedure and the acceptance. It does not cover the battery installation.

## 2. Terms

- ASR: area-specific resistance of a cell, in ohm cm2; it sets the voltage loss at a given current density.
- Coulombic efficiency: charge out divided by charge in.
- Voltage efficiency: mean discharge voltage divided by mean charge voltage.
- Round trip: energy out divided by energy in, including pumps.
- HER: hydrogen evolution reaction, the side reaction that wastes charge on the zinc electrode.
- Strip cycle: a full discharge that removes all plated zinc.

Other abbreviations are in `docs-authority/GLOSSARY.md`.

## 3. Prediction

Voltage efficiency (1.82 - 0.100)/(1.82 + 0.100) = 0.896 at 20 mA/cm2 and 5.0 ohm cm2 (SAND2000-0893, ZBFB_REVIEW2023; ASR assumed). Coulombic efficiency 1 - HER 0.0060 - crossover 0.05 = 0.944. Auxiliaries 3 %. Predicted round trip 0.820. The design value stays 0.7 until the test; the gap (12 points) is margin, not credit.

## 4. Test article

One in-house 10-cell stack built to the ZNBR2 cell design (same electrode, separator, flow frame and current density), 0.1 m2 per cell, with its own electrolyte loop and Br2 complexing agent.

## 5. Procedure

1. Leak and hydrostatic test of the flow frames at 1.5 x pump shut-off pressure.
2. Formation: three charges at 10 mA/cm2 to 50 % zinc loading.
3. Polarisation: charge and discharge at 5, 10, 20, 30 and 40 mA/cm2 at 50 % state of charge; cell voltages logged at 1 Hz; ASR from the slope.
4. Daily cycle: 30 cycles of 6 h charge at 20 mA/cm2 and discharge over the 14 h night at the matching current, 25 C and 40 C electrolyte.
5. Self-discharge: 24 h open circuit at full charge; coulombs lost and H2 collected in a gas burette.
6. Strip cycle: full zinc strip every 5 cycles, as the plant will run it.
7. Auxiliaries: pump and controller power logged over the cycles.

# Photovoltaic cell

Document number: AQS-PV-001
Revision: C
Date: 2026-10-11
Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. 100 mm wafer from the silicon module. Phosphate dopant. Electrowon aluminum grid.

2. Size. Diffusion 900 °C, 30 min, in a quartz tube 50 mm ID, 800 mm long, on the clean frame. Grid is 2 mm lines at 10 mm pitch, screen cut on the mill. Cell 0.5 V, 30 mA/cm², 78 cm², 1.2 W. This module makes cells. It does not make the 400 kW roof.

3. Material. Frame 6061. Tube quartz. Screen stainless, shop-cut.

4. Nozzles. One SB350 for the tube heater. Dopant vapor DN15. Exhaust to the scrubber.

5. Instruments. Tube temperature. Timer.

6. Relief. Tube is open-ended. Exhaust to the scrubber.

7. Code. Shop.

8. Open items. Cell lifetime is not measured. The process and the watt are.

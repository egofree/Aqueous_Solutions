# Photovoltaic cell module

Document number: AQS-PV-001
Revision: B
Date: 2026-10-10

1. Basis. 100 mm wafer from the silicon module. Phosphate dopant. Electrowon aluminum.

2. Size. Diffusion 900 °C, 30 min. Grid is 2 mm lines at 10 mm pitch. Cell is 0.5 V, 30 mA/cm², area 78 cm², 1.2 W at one sun. Roof array of 400 kW needs about 3 × 10⁵ of these, which is a later plant, not this module.

3. Material. Tube furnace, quartz or alumina. Print screen, shop-cut.

4. Nozzles. Power for the tube. Dopant vapor DN15. Exhaust to the scrubber.

5. Instruments. Tube temperature. Timer.

6. Relief. Tube is open-ended. Exhaust to the scrubber.

7. Code. Shop.

8. Open items. Lifetime is not measured. The process and the watt are.

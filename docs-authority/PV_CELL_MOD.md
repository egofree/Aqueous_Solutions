# Photovoltaic cell module

Document number: AQS-PV-001
Revision: A
Date: 2026-10-10

Wafer from the silicon module, sliced on a wire saw the shop builds. Diffusion from the phosphate store, 900 °C, 30 min. Aluminum grid from electrowon sheet, fired on. No bought cell.

One cell is about 0.5 V and 30 mA/cm2 at one sun. A 100 mm cell is about 2 W. The roof array is many of these. The module frame holds the diffusion tube and the print station.

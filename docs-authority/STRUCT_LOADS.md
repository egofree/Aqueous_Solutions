## 1. Purpose and scope

This page gives the weight of the process equipment on each level of the building, for the frame check (X-BLD-01, AQS-BLD-016) and the foundation check (X-BLD-03, AQS-BLD-017). It covers equipment and the liquid it holds. Floor and roof live loads from people, storage and snow are not included here; the frame and foundation checks add them. Every value is a study value based on assumption A-M-184.

## 2. Method

Three load cases are calculated for every placed unit.

- The empty case is the steel mass from the unit design where the graph has one (the gas receivers). Otherwise it is the envelope volume times an equipment density of 150 kg/m³ (driver `struct_equip_density_kg_m3`), with separate densities for storage tanks (`struct_tank_kg_m3`) and for the photovoltaic array per square metre of roof (`struct_pv_kg_m2`).
- The operating case adds the liquid the unit holds, at a specific gravity (SG) of 1.25 (driver `atm_design_sg`). The liquid volume is the hold-up volume where the graph states one; otherwise it is 50 % of the envelope (driver `struct_op_fill`) for a unit that receives liquid.
- The test case fills the envelope of every liquid unit with water, as for a fill or hydrostatic test.

The factored load uses the load and resistance factor design (LRFD) combination 1.2 D + 1.6 L of ASCE 7-22 section 2.3.1. The empty weight is the dead load D. The liquid of the operating case is taken as the live load L, because it varies and the larger factor is the conservative choice. Loads are converted from mass with the gravity of the site profile.

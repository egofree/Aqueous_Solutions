## 4. Reading the comparison

The reference plant is the baseline profile; a locale differs from it only where the site capabilities require it (AQS-PLAN-011 section 5). Section 1 shows the replacement logic: the Mars and LEO locales eliminate the same vent, combustion and gravity-bound set and share the replacement units; the Earth locales mostly keep the reference design and move the sizes. Section 3 shows how far the solved plant moves: the array grows with the resource and the stretch, the storage with the hours a site spends without its primary source.

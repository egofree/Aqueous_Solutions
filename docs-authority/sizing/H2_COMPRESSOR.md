## 1. Purpose and scope

This page sizes the in-house multi-stage diaphragm compressor that fills the hydrogen store `CRYO_H2`. It gives the number of stages, the pressures and discharge temperature of each stage, the intercooler duty, the swept volume and diaphragm diameter, and the power. The hydrogen flow is the sum of the mass streams into `CRYO_H2` on the plant graph.

## 2. Design

The compressor takes 11.33 kg/d of hydrogen from 10 bar to 200 bar in 5 stages of equal pressure ratio 1.821, with an intercooler after each stage that returns the gas to 35 °C. The number of stages is the smallest for which the predicted discharge temperature, at an adiabatic efficiency of 0.65, stays at or below 135 °C, the limit for hydrogen-rich service in [API618]. The specific energy is 1.97 kWh/kg, the average power 0.932 kW and the peak power over the run hours 2.797 kW. 2 compressors are fitted, one of them standby.

## 3. Construction

The materials are as follows: head and plates 17-4PH H1150 / 316L (hydrogen service), triple-layer 301 SS diaphragm with leak detection between layers. The intercoolers are 316L tube-in-tube exchangers on the cooling water loop. The efficiencies, the speed and the cavity depth are assumptions until the first article has been run on the bench (A-M-169).

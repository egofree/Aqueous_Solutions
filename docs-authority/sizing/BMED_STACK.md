# BMED stack

Document number: AQS-DUTY-068
Revision: A
Date: 2026-10-15
Authority: AQS-PLAN-007 X-MFG-35. Generated by `flow_graph/param/bmed_stack.py`. Study. A-M-170.

NaOH 96.46 kg/d (27.91 mmol/s) at 200 A/m2 and CE 0.80 (EDBM_PILOT_2023) on 0.04 m2 cells: stack current 8.0 A, 421 triplets in 5 stacks of 85, plus 1 spare stack; 50.5 m2 of membrane (bipolar, anion, cation).

Triplet voltage from physics (A-M-171): bipolar membrane 1.00 V (reversible 0.83 V at delta pH 14 plus overpotential, STRATHMANN2004) + two monopolar membranes 0.120 V (3 ohm cm2 each, STRATHMANN2004) + three 0.8 mm compartments 0.066 V (CRC_COND conductivities, 1.5 spacer shadow) + end electrodes 0.030 V = 1.22 V. Measured pilot voltage (EDBM_PILOT_2023, 1.4 kWh/kg at CE 0.80) 1.67 V. Design voltage is the larger, 1.67 V, giving 5.6 kW. The 6.7 kWh/kg whey figure (EDBM_WHEY_2021) implies 8.0 V per triplet; that is not credible as a voltage and reflects current efficiency collapse at 2 M, so the 0.80 CE at 2 M product is the governing verification item (X-MFG-41).

Construction: PP frames 300 x 300 mm, 0.8 mm PE mesh spacers, Ti/MMO anode and 316L/Ni cathode end plates, PP end plates with tie rods.

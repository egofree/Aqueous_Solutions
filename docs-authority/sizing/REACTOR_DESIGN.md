## 1. Purpose and scope

This page sizes the reaction vessels that the plant builds for itself: the glass digester `GLASS_DIGEST`, the pyrolysis retort `OIL_PYRO`, the methanol synthesis reactor `MEOH_SYNTH` and the smaller biological and chemical reactors on the graph. For each it gives the residence time, the volume, the shell size, the wall, the mixing, the heat and the electrical load. The rates come from the plant graph.

In the glass digester, 11.0 % of the cullet dissolves; this is stream IA-GLASS-03 less stream S08G. For fines of 100 µm, a shrinking sphere reaches that conversion when its surface has receded 1.90 µm. The zero-order dissolution rate is 0.0002 g/(m² s) at 80 °C in 3 M NaOH [JMS2017_GLASSFIBRE], corrected to the 2 M product of the base loop with a reaction order of 0.31, for glass of density 2500 kg/m³; the recession then takes 27002 s. The residence time is three times this kinetic time, but not less than 1.0 h, which allows for mixing, heat-up and level control. The rate was measured on glass fibre, so it must be checked on soda-lime cullet at the bench; [MARAGHECHI2016] reports a constant dissolution rate for soda-lime glass in 1 M NaOH at 20 °C to 80 °C.

The pyrolysis retort runs at 500 °C for 1 h, within the 400 °C to 550 °C and 30 min to 90 min of [ANUAR2016]. Its heat is the feed times the sum of the sensible heat and the heat of pyrolysis, 1.3 MJ/kg [GAO2010], divided by the heater efficiency 0.7, plus the loss of 65 W through 125 mm of mineral wool (AQS-THM-003). The retort is half full, to leave room for foam and char, and its load on the bus is 1.664 kW.

Thermoplastic walls are sized by the hoop stress from the hydrostatic head, with a practical minimum of 3 mm to 4 mm [DVS2205]. Steel walls take the minimum of paragraph UG-16(b), 1.5 mm, plus a corrosion allowance of 1.5 mm [ASME_VIII1_UG16].

## 4. Methanol synthesis reactor (A-M-156)

The reactor holds 3.21 L of Cu/ZnO/Al2O3 catalyst (3.54 kg). It makes 1.438 kg/h of methanol at a weight-time yield of 1.22 kg/(kg h), measured on a CO2-only feed at 60 bar and 523 K [CTC2020_CZA], divided by a margin of 3.0 for operation at 50 bar and for ageing.

The catalyst sits in 1 tube of DN50 Sch 40 SA-106 B with a bed of 1485 mm, inside a DN100 thermal-oil jacket. The reactor operates at 250 °C and 50 bar, with a design pressure of 5.50 MPa (A-M-156).

The wall is checked to UG-27 [ASME_VIII1_UG27] with S = 118.0 MPa for SA-106 B at 260 °C ([ASME_II_D]), E = 1 and a corrosion allowance of 1.5 mm. The required thickness is 2.88 mm; the Sch 40 nominal wall of 3.91 mm, less the 12.5 % mill tolerance, gives 3.42 mm, which is adequate.

The exotherm of 0.617 kW (49.5 kJ/mol, [PONTZEN2011]) goes to the jacket. The feed preheat of 0.818 kW is electric, with no credit for a feed and effluent interchanger. The relief case is the blocked outlet at the full feed, sized as a gas to API 520 by `flow_graph/param/pressure_nodes.py`.

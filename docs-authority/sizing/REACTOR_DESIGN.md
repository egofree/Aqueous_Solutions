## 2. Basis

- GLASS_DIGEST. 11.0 % of the cullet dissolves (IA-GLASS-03 less S08G). For 100 um fines the shrinking-sphere recession to that conversion is 1.90 um. At the zero-order rate of JMS2017_GLASSFIBRE (2.0e-4 g/(m2 s) at 80 C in 3 M NaOH, order 0.31, corrected to 2 M; glass 2500 kg/m3) that takes 27002 s. Residence is 3 x kinetic, floored at 1.0 h for mixing, heat-up and level control. The rate is for glass fibre; soda-lime cullet is checked at the bench (MARAGHECHI2016 reports constant-rate soda-lime dissolution in 1 M NaOH at 20-80 C).
- OIL_PYRO. 500 C and 1 h (ANUAR2016: 400-550 C, 30-90 min). Heat = feed x (cp x dT + heat of pyrolysis 1.3 MJ/kg, GAO2010) / heater efficiency 0.7, plus the insulated shell loss 65 W at 125 mm mineral wool (AQS-THM-003). Half-full retort for foam and char. The graph carried 0 kW for this unit before this issue; the bus takes the 1.664 kW.
- Walls. Thermoplastic hoop stress from the hydrostatic head with a 3-4 mm practical minimum (DVS2205). Steel UG-16(b) 1.5 mm + 1.5 mm corrosion allowance (ASME_VIII1_UG16).

He, Ne, Kr and Xe are closed inventories that return from their stores and pass through; no separation duty. The EXPANDER is kept as the diverse backup for the condenser chiller (owner decision Q-010, redundancy rule): N2 reverse-Brayton loop, pressure ratio 5, outlet -56 C, 219 kg/d N2 for 0.082 kW cold at -25 C, 0.442 kW net (COP 0.19 against the chiller's 2.5). It is idle in normal operation; a chiller failure starts it. A-M-186.

The regeneration heat is electric in this revision. Taking it from the Q2 ladder (200-250 C) is the first saving to study. Sorbent capacity, heat of adsorption and the chiller COP are assumptions until the bed breakthrough test (A-M-175).

## 1. Purpose and scope

This page sizes the nine electrowinning (EW) cells `EW_1` to `EW_9` of the cascade, each of which plates one metal from the leach liquor. For each cell it gives the current, the number of electrodes, the tank size, the liquor holdup, the residence time and the direct-current (DC) power.

## 2. Basis

The harvest stream IA-EW-MET-n of each cell is a copper-equivalent sheet rate that bounds the mass of the element (AQS-ASSAY-001 section 4); EW_9 also takes the sheet stream IA-EW-SHEET. The bounding current is I = m / (M_Cu / 2F × 86 400) / CE, with a current efficiency CE of 0.85 (driver `ew_current_efficiency_design`; [USBM_CL_EW] gives 0.85 to 0.90 for chloride electrowinning; A-M-036). For comparison, the element current is computed from the S08 deposits of AQS-ASSAY-001 section 5 with the valence of each metal.
The cathode current density is 200 A/m² (AQS-EW-MOD-001). Each plate is immersed over 0.6 m of width and 0.42 m of depth, so its two faces give 0.504 m². The cathode pitch is 100 mm with an anode between each pair of cathodes, and there is one more anode than cathodes.
The inside length of a tank is the number of cathodes times the pitch plus 75 mm at each end; the inside width is the plate width plus 50 mm at each side; the inside depth is a 150 mm sump, the plate, a 100 mm cover and 80 mm of freeboard. The wall is 10 mm, and the liquor density is 1100 kg/m³ (AQS-DUTY-050).
The cell voltage is 2.12 V (driver `ew_cell_voltage_V`), from the 2.1 kWh per kilogram of copper that [USBM_CL_EW] gives for cupric chloride (A-M-039).

## 4. Findings

1. The envelope `size_mm` on each EW node is the cathode plate, not the cell. The tank sizes of Table 3.1, from 370 mm to 3270 mm long, are written to `params.sizing.size_mm`; the graph `size_mm` and the placement rows are not changed here. The cells were placed in the aisle by `flow_graph/place_ew_aisle.py` (X-RSZ-03).
2. The bounding DC load at the electrodes is 27.1 kW at 12781 A. The graph streams IA-DC-EW_n carry the cell power plus the bus voltage drop, as written by `flow_graph/param/write_graph.py` (AQS-ELEC-004).
3. The element current is 1581 A, 8.1 times lower. The bounding case sets the hardware; the element case is the expected operating point at the reference feed.
4. Side-reaction H₂ on EW_1, EW_2, EW_4 and EW_9 (graph H₂ edges) would add Faradaic current not counted here. It is held with the AQS-DUTY-016 H₂/Cl₂ discrepancy (EDGE_RATING_DUTY) and needs an owner rate decision.
5. The 316L frame in AQS-EW-MOD-001 is a wetted metal that is not an electrode, and it is withdrawn under rule M3 of AQS-STD-002; the racks are CPVC or PVC-U. The electrodes, first 316L cathodes and PbO₂ anodes, were reopened for chloride service with chlorine at the anode (X-MAT-18) and are now titanium cathodes and mixed-metal-oxide anodes (AQS-STD-002 section 9).

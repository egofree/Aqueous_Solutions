Wall per ASME_B31_3 304.1.2 (outside-diameter form, Y 0.4) with S 115 MPa (ASME_II_D, SA-312 TP316L to 38 C), E 1, no corrosion allowance (dry gas), Sch 160 less 12.5 % mill tolerance. ID 87.3 mm is under the 152 mm Section VIII-1 size limit (ASME_VIII1_U1), so each tube is a piping component. Each bank is two manifolded halves with isolation and its own relief (pressure_nodes.py blocked-outlet case), so a leaking tube takes out at most half a bank. Fatigue from daily cycling and hydrogen-assisted fatigue of 316L are checked at the first-article cycle test (A-M-167).

## 1. Purpose and scope

This page sizes the 23 storage units on the plant graph: tanks, receivers and product stores. For each it gives the working and nominal volume, the diameter and height, the material and the wall thickness, and checks the vessel against the envelope that the graph gives the unit.

## 2. Method

The design flow Q of each unit is the larger of its design capacity under task X-RSZ-07 and its duty on the graph. The working volume is Q times the hold time divided by the density at 20 °C ([PERRY9]), and the nominal volume is the working volume divided by the fill fraction 0.8. Each vessel is an upright cylinder with a height-to-diameter ratio of 1.5.

Polyethylene walls are sized by hoop stress, T = P OD / (2 SD), with a design stress SD of 4.14 MPa and a minimum of 4.75 mm ([ASTM_D1998]). Flammable and combustible services, which are methanol, isopropanol and the hydrocarbon cuts, are stored in welded SA-240 304L ([NFPA30_2027]), as are the compressed-gas receivers. Their walls follow t = P R / (S − 0.6 P) with an allowable stress S of 115.0 MPa, a joint efficiency of 1.0 and a minimum of 3.0 mm ([ASME_VIII_1_UG27]).

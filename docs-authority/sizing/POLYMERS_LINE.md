## 1. Purpose and scope

This page sizes the units of the POLYMERS sector: the polyolefin line that turns naphtha into resin pellets, the fluoropolymer train that makes PVDF and PTFE for linings and gaskets, the condensation resin unit, and the polymer stock that issues them to the other sectors. It states what the other sectors draw, the flows through each unit from the plant graph, and the items still open.

## 2. Demand

The fabrication sector draws 0 kg/d by stream POLY_TO_FAB, because the fab closes at steady state (A-M-257); a net draw will appear when the product basis of the fab is sized.
The shop draws 0.267388 kg/d of polyolefin by stream POLY_TO_MFG. This is the difference that closes the shop balance (A-M-257, A-M-266): the loop of shop goods is closed, since IA-MFG-OUT equals IA-WEAR, and the moulded articles are made by BLOW_MOLD from the 8.4072 kg/d of C11 to C18 wax in stream IA-BLOW-IN.
The shop returns 0.026739 kg/d of offcut to the wet line by stream POLY_TO_WET, which is the shop loss fraction `poly_shop_reject_frac` of 10% of the polymer issued (A-M-263).
The shop also draws 0.23 kg/d of fluoropolymer by stream FLUORO_TO_MFG (section 4).

## 4. Fluoropolymer train

The train runs on fluorspar and sulfuric acid imported at the dock (A-M-289): stream IA-CAF2-MU brings 0.3255 kg/d of fluorspar and stream IA-H2SO4-MU 0.4038 kg/d of acid to HF_GEN, and the gypsum leaves by stream IA-GYPSUM-SHIP at 0.5756 kg/d. F_RECOVER collects the fluoride from the leach (IA-HF-SRC, 1.5 kg/d) and the off-specification reclaim of POLY_F (POLYF_RECLAIM, 0.02 kg/d) and returns it to HF_GEN. HF_GEN supplies 1.5 kg/d of HF to the wet bench by stream IA-HF-PROD. The monomer route takes chlorine from CRU and returns the HCl to it. The resin rates are the replacement rates of the seals, gaskets and linings (AQS-RATE-FP-MET): 0.15 kg/d of VDF and 0.1 kg/d of TFE monomer, for 0.14 kg/d of PVDF and 0.09 kg/d of PTFE.

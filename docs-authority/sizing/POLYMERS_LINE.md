## 1. Purpose and scope

### 4.1 Sulfuric acid plant

ACID_PLANT makes the 98 wt% sulfuric acid for the HF kiln on site (owner rule that the plant imports nothing except waste or raw ore; A-M-421). Both routes end in the contact process: the sulfur dioxide is oxidised to sulfur trioxide on a vanadium catalyst with the oxygen of the process air (stream IA-ACID-O2 from ATMOS) and absorbed in well water (stream IA-ACID-H2O from GW_INTAKE). The driver h2so4_route selects the source of the sulfur dioxide. On the GYPSUM_LOOP route the cake of HF_GEN (stream IA-GYPSUM-REGEN), its anhydrite and the free acid of the stoichiometric excess, is reduced with plant char from OIL_PYRO (stream CHAR_TO_ACID) in a small electrically heated kiln, CaSO4 + 1/2 C to CaO + SO2 + 1/2 CO2, so the acid sulfur is recovered first (A-M-423). Only the sulfur that the kiln does not recover is topped up with native sulfur, a raw ore taken at the dock (stream IA-SULFUR-MU) and burned to sulfur dioxide. The lime of the residue is the lime make-up of NAOH_PLANT (stream IA-LIME-TO-NAOH), the rest of the residue leaves at the dock (stream IA-LIME-SHIP), and the kiln gas goes to ATMOS (stream IA-ACID-CO2). On the SULFUR_ORE route all the sulfur is native sulfur ore, the kiln and its streams are retired, and the cake leaves at the dock by stream IA-GYPSUM-SHIP; that route recovers no sulfur, so the recovery-first check flags it while it is selected. The kiln load is its insulated-shell loss plus the reaction and feed heat; the contact steps are exothermic and are not credited. At this throughput the shell loss is nearly all of the kiln load, so the kiln runs in campaigns from a cake store (driver acid_kiln_mode, Q-034, A-M-425). Each campaign heats the Inconel tube and its ceramic-fibre insulation, holds the kiln at temperature for acid_kiln_run_h hours, and makes the acid for the days until the next campaign, which a day store holds for HF_GEN. CONTINUOUS duty, with the kiln held at temperature every day, stays selectable. On the CAMPAIGN duty a campaign runs every 7 days with a heat-up of 8.60 kWh, the cake store holds 4.21 kg and the acid store 1.54 L, and the peak load during a run is 1.0847 kW. The selected route is GYPSUM_LOOP. It recovers 90.0% of the acid sulfur from the cake, takes 0.0129 kg/d of sulfur ore, and draws 0.2061 kW on average.

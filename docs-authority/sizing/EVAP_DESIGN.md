## 1. Purpose and scope

This page designs the two evaporators that concentrate the products of the bipolar membrane stacks: EVAP_ACID for hydrochloric acid and EVAP_BASE for sodium hydroxide. It gives the method, the size and power of each evaporator, the relief valves, the outlet temperatures, the materials, and the column that keeps HCl out of the acid condensate. The water to be evaporated comes from the plant solver.

## 2. Basis

Each evaporator is a single-effect falling-film evaporator with mechanical vapour recompression (MVR). The liquor runs down the inside of vertical tubes, the compressed vapour condenses on the shell side, and the body is at 101.325 kPa. The 20 wt% HCl boils at its azeotrope temperature, 108.6 °C [PERRY9_HCL], which is the bounding case; the 20 wt% NaOH boils at 108 °C, a boiling-point elevation of 8 K [MCCABE7_DUHRING].

Saturation pressures come from region 4 of IAPWS-IF97, and the latent heat is 2257 kJ/kg [IAPWS_IF97]. The compressor raises the vapour to the saturation pressure 6 K above the boiling point. Its work is the ideal-gas isentropic work with k = 1.33, divided by an efficiency of 0.7 [PERRY9_PUMP] and a motor efficiency of 0.9 (A-M-106).

The falling-film coefficient is 1500 W/(m² K) at that driving difference [PERRY9_EVAP] (A-M-106). The minimum wetting rate of 0.05 kg/(m s) sets the recirculation [PERRY9_EVAP]. The feed enters at 35 °C and is preheated in a counter-current interchanger against the condensate and the product, with a 10 K approach and a coefficient of 2000 W/(m² K) [PERRY9_EVAP] [INCROPERA7]. The HCl and water equilibrium uses fits to Perry's 7th edition Tables 2-9 and 2-10 [PERRY7_HCL_VLE] in `flow_graph/param/sizing/hcl_vle.py`.

## 4. Relief

The body relief case is fire, with Q = 21000 F A^0.82 Btu/h and 21 % overpressure (ISO 23251 and API 521). The shell relief case is a blocked outlet with the compressor at 120 % of its design flow and 10 % overpressure. Both valves are sized as gas to API 520 Part I and built to ISO 4126-1. ## 5. Outlet temperatures (X-MAT-20)

The product and the condensate leave the interchanger at 49.1 °C on the acid unit and 51.1 °C on the base unit, below the 60 °C limit of line class P1 (AQS-STD-002). The condensate lines therefore stay in class P1, and IA-ACID-01 stays in class P2 for hot leach service. The parameter `T_C` of each outlet stream on the graph carries these values.

## 6. Materials

The acid unit has impregnated graphite tubes, tube sheets and interchanger, a PTFE-lined steel shell and separator, a column of borosilicate glass 3.3 with PTFE packing, and PVDF piping [PERRY9_MAT] [ULLMANN_HCL]. The base unit is nickel 200 [PERRY9_MAT]. Fasteners outside the wetted zone are ISO 3506 A4-70 on the acid unit and ISO 898-1 class 8.8 on the base unit.

## 8. HCl in the acid vapour

From the Perry tables, the vapour over the 20 wt% body liquid at 101.325 kPa carries 17.09 wt% HCl, more than the 7.07 wt% feed, so a well-mixed body cannot send its condensate back to BMED_ACID. The vapour over the feed carries 0.343 wt%. Table 8.1 compares the ways to keep the HCl out of the condensate.

Table 8.1. Options for the HCl in the acid vapour

| Option | Description | Vapour against the base case | Finding |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| a | rectifier with reflux on the vapour line (feed to the body) | 6.7 | minimum reflux 5.7 (pinch at the body vapour 17.1 wt%); blower power at least x6.7 |
| b | two stages, final stage once-through | — | final-stage vapour runs 3.0 to 17.1 wt% HCl, above the 7.07 wt% feed; still needs a column to return HCl |
| c | lower product strength, 16 wt% | 4.19 | body vapour 5.6 wt% HCl; recycle makes the feed 3922 kg/d and the vapour x4.2; leach takes 25 % more liquor |
| d | counter-current column on the body, feed at the top (adopted) | 1.076 | no reflux; top vapour 0.50 wt% HCl against 0.34 wt% equilibrium over the feed; recycle 4.33 kg/d HCl to BMED_ACID |

Option d is adopted, as the smallest complete system. The feed enters the top of a packed column on the body frame, and the liquid runs down against the body vapour and reaches the body at 20 wt%. No reflux is drawn, so the compressor duty rises only by the recycled water. The column has 4 ideal stages above the body, from the Perry equilibrium, packed to 3000 mm at a height per stage of 500 mm times 1.5 [PERRY9_ABS] (A-M-108). It is DN150 at 1 m/s vapour and 3800 mm high, free-standing beside the body on the same frame (L-1 headroom 6 m); vapour duct from the separator to the column bottom, column bottoms back to the body.

The condensate carries 0.5 wt% HCl (4.33 kg/d). It returns to BMED_ACID with the make-up water and comes back in the feed, which rises to 1304.8 kg/d, with 865.2 kg/d of water evaporated. The BMED current does not change, because the recycled HCl is not generated again. The owner may choose another option, for example option a if a condensate with almost no HCl is wanted.

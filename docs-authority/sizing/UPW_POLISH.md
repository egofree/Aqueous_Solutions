## 1. Purpose and scope

This page sizes the ultrapure water (UPW) polish `BMED_UPW_POLISH`: a reverse osmosis (RO) stage, an electrodeionisation (EDI) stage and an ultraviolet lamp that destroys total organic carbon (UV-TOC). The flows come from the plant graph.

## 2. Flows

The feed, stream S25, is distillate from the vapour compression distiller, with about 20 mg/L of dissolved solids (A-M-176). The potable share of 175.2 kg/d goes to remineralisation `REMIN` and bypasses the polish. The ultrapure water product of 127.3 kg/d, for the fab and the header `HDR_UPW`, passes through the RO stage and then the EDI stage. The RO feed is 133.3 kg/d at a recovery of 95.5 %; the reject leaves by stream IA-UPW-REJ.

## 3. Design

The RO flux follows the solution-diffusion model J = A (ΔP − Δπ) [BAKER2012], with a permeance A of 3 L/(m2 h bar) and a design flux of 20 L/(m2 h). This gives 0.27 m2 of flat-sheet thin-film composite membrane per module; 2 modules are fitted, one of them standby. The mean osmotic pressure is 192 mbar and the feed pressure 6.9 bar.
The EDI current carries the salt left in the RO permeate at a current efficiency of 0.1, through 10 cell pairs at 4 V each [ALVARADO2014]. Two stacks are fitted, one of them standby.
The UV-TOC lamp, at 185 nm and 254 nm on the ultrapure water loop, is rated 15 W (driver `upw_uv_W`).

## 1. Duty

Non-condensables released in the EVAP_ACID body leave through the condensing side and a vent condenser at 40 C. The vent carries the air dissolved in the feed (21.7 mg/kg, WEISS1970), water at saturation and HCl at the equilibrium partial pressure over the condensate (PERRY7_HCL_VLE). Outlet target 1.0 ppmv HCl, half the ACGIH TLV-Ceiling (ACGIH_HCL).

## 2. Sizing

- Diameter: column/packing ratio at least 8 against wall channelling (PERRY9_ABS), 16 mm rings: 128 mm, DN150.
- Gas loading: 1.10 L/h, superficial 2.4e-05 m/s. This is far below the range of the packed-bed k_G a correlations, so the gas-film HTU is taken from Fick diffusion across a film of half the packing size (TREYBAL3), with the HCl-air diffusivity from Fuller-Schettler-Giddings (PERRY9_DIFF). HTU is then about 0.03 mm, and NTU 6.2 needs 0.18 mm of bed. The bed is set by the 0.5 m minimum for one distributor and support stage: 500 mm.
- Flooding: not limiting at this gas rate (PERRY9_ABS).
- Liquid: recirculation at the minimum wetting rate 0.08 m3/(m h) x 340 m2/m3 (PERRY9_ABS): 350 L/h; sump pump 24 W (10 m head, 40 % efficiency, study).
- NaOH: bleed from BMED_BASE at 2 x the design-case HCl, 1.01 mmol/d (0.54 g/d of 2 M base). The 20 L sump is charged with 2 M NaOH (40 mol), which holds about 8e+04 days of the design-case HCl. Spent liquor (NaCl in NaOH, 0.56 g/d) goes to BMED_ACID as brine.
- Material: PVC-U pipe and fittings (ASTM D1785 Sch 80), in-house cut PVC tube rings, PVC mesh demister (PERRY9_MAT: PVC to 60 C in HCl and NaOH).
- Height: bed 500 mm + sump, inlet and demister 900 mm = 1400 mm.

## 3. Findings

1. With the column working, the vent condenser alone holds the vent below the 1 ppmv target (0.63 ppmv). The scrubber covers start-up and column upsets (490 ppmv).
2. The HCl load is milligrams per day in both cases. The NaOH draw changes the NaOH balance by 1.0e-03 mol/d, far inside the 1 % gate.
3. The size is set by minimum practical dimensions (DN, bed stage, wetting), not by the mass transfer.

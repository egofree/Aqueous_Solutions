# Rectifier bus

Document number: AQS-BUS-001
Revision: B
Date: 2026-10-12
Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Cell current from the duty pages. Bar sized at 200 A/cm². Length from rectifier to cell is 2 m.

2. Size. Resistivity 1.68×10⁻⁸ Ω·m. Drop is I × ρ × L / A. Because A is I / 200, the drop is the same on every cell: 67 mV. Contact drop is 50 mV, so the lead is 117 mV. Heat in the 2 m bar:

| EW-2 | 2106 | 10.5 | 142 W |
| EW-3 | 790 | 4.0 | 52 W |
| EW-4 | 171 | 0.9 | 11 W |
| EW-5 | 177 | 0.9 | 12 W |
| EW-6 | 192 | 1.0 | 12 W |
| EW-7 | 2501 | 12.5 | 168 W |
| EW-8 | 178 | 0.9 | 12 W |
| EW-9 | 2157 | 10.8 | 145 W |

Contact heat stays the old I × 0.05 V line and is not added twice.

3. Material. Copper bar. No aluminum on a wet aisle.

4. Nozzles. This is a bar, not a pipe. Bolted at the rectifier and at the cell bus.

5. Instruments. Cell voltage includes the 117 mV. No separate bar instrument.

6. Relief. None.

7. Code. Shop.

8. Open items. The 2 m length is the aisle pitch, not a measured run.

## 1. Rules

- A number belongs to one page. The page states it in its first lines as `Document number: AQS-XXX-NNN`.
- Every page directly under `docs-authority/` has a number. Folder indexes (`README.md`) do not.
- A new page takes the next free number in its series: `python3 docs-authority/_doc_register.py --next AQS-XXX`.
- A page that is renumbered keeps its old number on a `Former number:` line, so older citations can be resolved (section 3). A former number is never reissued. Where two pages once shared a number, a citation of it before 2026-10-24 may mean either; section 3 names both.
- A number followed by a qualifier (for example `AQS-INV-005 sheet MF-01`) names a part of that document (section 4).
- `flow_graph/test_doc_numbers.py` fails on a duplicate or missing number.

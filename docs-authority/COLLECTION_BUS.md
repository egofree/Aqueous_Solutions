## Purpose and scope

The plant's PV inverters are too many to land on one 480 V bus without very large breakers. This page groups them into sections, sizes each section's breaker and step-up transformer, and connects the sections on a medium-voltage (MV) collection bus, where the plant meets the utility. It also states how the plant runs as an island when the utility is lost, and the fault level each section must withstand. The single-line diagram is AQS-ELEC-002 and the cable sizes are in the cable schedule.

## Terms

- Section: a group of inverters on one 480 V breaker and one transformer (CB-1, CB-2 and so on).
- Collection bus: the MV bus that ties the section transformers together.
- Pad-mount transformer: a ground-mounted, enclosed distribution transformer (IEEE C57.12.34).
- Arc-energy reduction: the NEC 240.87 requirement for extra protection on breakers rated 1200 A or more.
- Islanding: the plant running from its own sources while disconnected from the utility; unintentional islanding must be stopped within 2 s (IEEE 1547-2018).
- Point of common coupling (PCC): where the plant connects to the utility grid.

## 1. Basis

Inverter AC output 60 kW = 72.2 A at 480 V. A section breaker is kept at or below 1000 A so that no breaker reaches the 1200 A arc-energy-reduction threshold of NEC 2026 240.87; breakers at 125 % of continuous current to NEC 240.6(A). At most 11 inverters per section. Each section feeds a standard pad-mount transformer (IEEE C57.12.34, 5.75 % Z) to the 12.47 kV collection bus; the MV bus carries the point of common coupling (AQS-ELEC-002 item 5, owner input A-M-113 for the service voltage). Switchboards to UL 891. Storage energy is allocated electrically over the hybrid inverters, so each section charges and discharges its own share; the ZnBr2 stacks stay physically in the outdoor ZNBR2 yard under NFPA 855-2026 (outdoor installation, separation and the 3 ft spacing between groups apply to the stack layout).

## 3. Plant loads and islanding

The plant bus (MCC-1, PP-1, RECT-EW and the process feeders, 156.6 kW on the graph) is fed from CB-1 at 480 V. On loss of the utility (NEC 705.40, IEEE 1547-2018 unintentional islanding within 2 s) the PCC breaker opens; the sections stay tied on the MV bus and carry the plant as an intentional island on storage, under a microgrid controller (IEEE 2030.7). Export resumes after the utility returns and the 5 min reconnection delay of IEEE 1547-2018 4.10.

## 4. Fault level

Every 480 V section sees its own inverters plus the others through two transformers; bounded by all inverters at 2 x rated, 5340 A (AQS-ELEC-006). Section breakers are rated for that with margin (65 kA interrupting class). Utility contribution at the MV bus is held (A-M-113).

# Welder module

Document number: AQS-TOOL-003
Revision: A
Date: 2026-10-11
Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Weld the frame tube. Steel to steel. 200 A.

2. Size. Arc at 25 V and 200 A is 5.0 kW. That is two SB350 connectors, not one. Duty 60%. Open-circuit voltage 50 V. Wire is 2.4 mm, drawn from electrowon iron on the wire-draw unit. Stick length 300 mm. Transformer sits in the base of an 800 × 600 × 800 A500 frame.

3. Material. Frame A500. Secondary winding copper. Electrode holder is a shop clamp, not a bought stinger.

4. Nozzles. Two SB350. Work clamp is a cable, not a pipe nozzle. No fluid.

5. Instruments. Current. Overcurrent opens the contactor.

6. Relief. None. Not pressurized.

7. Code. Shop.

8. Open items. Flux for the iron wire is not made on site. Bare wire welds if the joint is clean. A coated electrode is a later mix.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This page gives the production plan of each building-block family in the inventory AQS-INV-004. A building-block family is a group of identical small parts used across the plant, such as fasteners, valves, instruments, bearings and seals. Pipe and wire families have their own pages, AQS-INV-006 and AQS-INV-007.

## 2. Summary and rules

The inventory has 83 families: 36 fastener, 17 valve, 13 instrument, 10 electronics, 2 bearing, 2 support, 1 fabricated part, 1 protection, 1 seal.

1. Each of the 82 bought families is designed by its standard class: it is bought to a standard class, its internals are those of the class, and no datasheet content is invented for it (the rule of AQS-PRO-002). The datasheet row of each family in AQS-PRO-002 is cited in Table 4.1 and not repeated.
2. The plan of each category, given in section 3, covers the receipt check of identity and count, storage, the work order that issues the parts to an assembly or installation, and the check after installation. Shop execution stays the open part of the work; this page books no production run.
3. The fabricated family OF-13 is made in house. Its design and production plan are the issued sheets `models/mfg/<unit>/bom.csv` and `WORK_ORDER.md`, to which this page refers.

## 3. Production plan by category

### 3.1 Fastener

This category has 36 families. Fasteners are bought to the thread standard of the joint they serve: ASME B16.5 stud bolting for the pipe classes, and machine screws to the standard of the host assembly. They are torqued at assembly to that standard, and spares are held under task X-OPS-03.

### 3.2 Valve

This category has 17 families. Valves are bought to their valve class with the seal class named in section 14 of AQS-STD-002, and the fail-safe position of each actuator is set with AQS-CTL-003. Each valve is checked for identity and count on receipt, issued with the line it was counted from and operated open and closed during the hydrostatic test of its class. Its position switches are checked against their tag in AQS-CTL-001 at commissioning, and spares are held under task X-OPS-03.

### 3.3 Instrument

This category has 13 families. Instruments are bought to their device class. The range, signal and loop of each tag are given in AQS-CTL-001, not here. Each device is checked for identity and count on receipt, stored dry, issued with its tagged loop, checked against its range in AQS-CTL-001 when installed and checked again at commissioning. Spares are listed in AQS-INV-004 under task X-OPS-03.

### 3.4 Electronics

This category has 10 families. Electronic modules are bought to their IEC class and checked for identity and count on receipt. They are stored dry and protected from electrostatic discharge in their sealed packing, and issued to the panel or controller build they were counted for, using the module counts per controller of AQS-CTL-002. Their wired channels are checked against the I/O tables of AQS-CTL-002 at build and at commissioning, and spares are held under task X-OPS-03.

### 3.5 Bearing

This category has 2 families. Bearings are bought to their ISO class and checked for identity and count on receipt. They are stored dry in their sealed packing and issued with the work order of the motor or fan build they were counted for. Press fits follow the ISO 15 seat that the class fixes, and spares are held under task X-OPS-03.

### 3.6 Support

This category has 2 families. Pipe supports are fitted during piping erection on the routed line they were counted from (AQS-ISO-002). Their material and coating match the class sheet of the pipe they carry (AQS-PIP-003), and spares are held under task X-OPS-03.

### 3.7 Fabricated part

This category has 1 family. Fabricated parts are made in house, not bought. Their design and production plan are the issued sheets `models/mfg/<unit>/bom.csv` and `WORK_ORDER.md` of each unit, made by the fabrication routes of AQS-PLAN-005 with their hold, witness and review points. The shop makes the parts and the quality traveller records them; this page does not repeat those sheets.

### 3.8 Protection

This category has 1 family. Protective devices are bought to their class, checked for identity and count on receipt and issued to the panel they were counted for. Their trip coordination is given in AQS-CTL-003, not here.

### 3.9 Seal

This category has 1 family. Seals are bought to their seal class, which section 14 of AQS-STD-002 sets together with the EN 12756 mechanical-seal rule. The lines of the maker-table that are still open in AQS-STD-002 section 14, the FKM grade and the EPDM compound, must be closed before the seals are ordered. Seals are fitted under the work order of the pump or mixer they serve, and spares are held under task X-OPS-03.

## 1. Summary and rules

83 families: 36 fastener, 17 valve, 13 instrument, 10 electronics, 2 bearing, 2 support, 1 fabricated part, 1 protection, 1 seal.

- **Open design, stated once**: for the 64 bought families the standard class IS the open design (AQS-PRO-002 rule: bought to a standard class, internals of the class, no invented datasheet row); the per-family datasheet row of AQS-PRO-002 is joined below by reference and not repeated.
- **Production plan per category** (section 3 families, section 2 plan text): receipt identity and count, dry storage, the issue path to the assembly or installation work order, and the post-installation tie (loop check to AQS-CTL-001/002, seal class to AQS-STD-002 section 14, bolting to the joint standard, spares per X-OPS-03). Shop execution stays the open part of the work; nothing here books a run.
- **The fabricated family (OF-13)**: its design and production plan are the issued models/mfg/<unit>/bom.csv and WORK_ORDER.md sheets; this sheet points there.

## 2. Production plan by category

### fastener (36 families)

Bought to the threaded standard of the joint they serve (B16.5 stud bolting in the pipe class sheets, machine screws per the host assembly standard); torqued at assembly to the host standard; spares per X-OPS-03.

### valve (17 families)

Bought to the valve class with the seal class named (AQS-STD-002 section 14); the actuator family fail-safe orientation settled with AQS-CTL-003. Receipt identity and count, issued with the line the count was booked from, function-checked open/close at the class hydro; position-verification devices verified against their AQS-CTL-001 tag at commissioning. Spares per X-OPS-03.

### instrument (13 families)

Bought to the device class; the per-tag range, signal and loop stay on AQS-CTL-001 (a family here carries the physical device class and the sizes in use, not loop data). Receipt identity and count, dry storage, issue with the tagged loop, function-checked against the AQS-CTL-001 range at install and swept once more at commissioning (AQS-CTL-002/CL-004 tie). Spares: the AQS-INV-004 spares roster (X-OPS-03).

### electronics (10 families)

Bought to the IEC class, receipt count and identity check, stored dry and electrostatic-safe in the sealed original packing, issued to the panel or controller build the count was booked for (AQS-CTL-002 module counts per PLC); wired channels verified against the AQS-CTL-002 I/O tables at build and commissioning; spares per X-OPS-03.

### bearing (2 families)

Bought to the ISO class, receipt count and identity check, stored dry in the sealed original packing, issued with the motor or fan build work order they were counted for (AQS-INV-005/A-M-126 lineage); press fits follow the ISO 15 seat the class fixes; spares carried per X-OPS-03 (900 building-block spares booked fleet-wide).

### support (2 families)

Fitted during piping erection on the routed line the count was booked from (AQS-ISO-002/CAD); material and coating match the pipe class sheet they support (AQS-PIP-003 letter); spares per X-OPS-03.

### fabricated part (1 families)

In-house work, not a purchase: the design and production plan are the issued models/mfg/<unit>/bom.csv and WORK_ORDER.md sheets (A-M-183 unit BOMs; fabrication routes per AQS-PLAN-005 with H/W/R hold points); the shop fabricates, the QC traveller records it. This sheet points there and does not duplicate.

### protection (1 families)

Bought to the class, receipt identity and count, issued to the panel the count is booked on; the trip coordination itself is AQS-CTL-003, not the device sheet.

### seal (1 families)

Seal class (EPDM/FKM/PTFE seat class) and the EN 12756 mechanical-seal tie are AQS-STD-002 section 14; the maker-table lines still open there (FKM grade, EPDM Q3 compound) stay named on this sheet as gate items ahead of order. Fitted with the pump or mixer build work order; spares per X-OPS-03.

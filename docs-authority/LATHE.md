# Lathe module

Document number: AQS-TOOL-001
Revision: C
Date: 2026-10-11
Not a PE stamp.

1. Basis. Cut shafts to 40 mm and leadscrews at 3 mm pitch, 750 mm long. This lathe makes the next lathe.

2. Size. Swing 300 mm. Centers 750 mm. Bore 38 mm. Motor 1.1 kW. Cutting force 200 N at 0.2 mm depth. Torque at 20 mm radius is 4 N·m. At 500 rpm that is 0.2 kW at the cut. The motor covers it. Spindle is 50 mm diameter, 4140, running in two tapered roller bearings. Leadscrew is 20 mm, 3 mm pitch, 800 mm long. Drive is three belts and a two-speed motor. Speeds 60, 120, 250, 500, 1000, 1500 rpm. No gears on the first machine.

3. Material. Frame A500, 1200 × 600 × 800, 100 × 100 × 6. Bed is two of those tubes. Spindle 4140. Leadscrew 4140.

4. Nozzles. One SB350. No process fluid. Coolant is a jug.

5. Instruments. Spindle speed. Motor overload trips the contactor.

6. Relief. None.

7. Code. Shop.

8. Open items. Bearing part numbers are not selected. The spindle diameter and the leadscrew are.

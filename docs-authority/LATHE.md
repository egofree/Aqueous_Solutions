# Lathe module

Document number: AQS-TOOL-001
Revision: A
Date: 2026-10-10

Swing over bed 300 mm. Distance between centers 750 mm. Spindle bore 38 mm. Spindle 1.1 kW, 60 to 1500 rpm. Leadscrew 3 mm pitch. Bed is two 4x4 tubes on the module frame.

This lathe cuts the shafts and the leadscrews for the next lathe. That is the copy path.

Mill. 300 x 200 mm table, 1.1 kW, on the same frame. Welder. 200 A, on the same frame. Both are separate modules. The three together are the machine-tool bay.

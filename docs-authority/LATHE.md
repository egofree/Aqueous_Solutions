# Lathe module

Document number: AQS-TOOL-001
Revision: B
Date: 2026-10-10

1. Basis. Cut shafts and leadscrews for the next lathe. Steel, up to 40 mm diameter, 750 mm long.

2. Size. Swing 300 mm. Centers 750 mm. Bore 38 mm. Motor 1.1 kW. Spindle 60 to 1500 rpm. Leadscrew 3 mm pitch. Cutting force at 0.2 mm depth and 0.1 mm/rev is about 200 N. Torque at 20 mm radius is 4 N·m. The 1.1 kW motor covers that at 500 rpm.

3. Material. Bed is two 4 × 4 tubes on the module frame. Headstock casting is iron or a welded box. Spindle is 4140.

4. Nozzles. Power connector only. No process fluid. Coolant is a jug, not a circuit.

5. Instruments. Spindle speed. Motor overload trips the contactor.

6. Relief. None. Not pressurized.

7. Code. Shop.

8. Open items. Gearbox ratios are not drawn. The swing, the bore, and the motor are.

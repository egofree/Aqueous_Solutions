## What this plant is

One building. Closed mass, water, gas, and energy after startup. External energy is solar and geothermal. Mineral feedstock in. No net waste.

Locked wet line: hopper/shredder and vibratory conveyor dump into LEACH CSTR 1. No pre-leach filter. CSTR 2 is the float weir. CSTR 3 is the PVC/FG sink auger. Float and sink liquor return to CSTR 1. Float solids to dryer then induction pyrolyzer. Sink solids to vibratory split, then the glass branch. Pregnant liquor CSTR 1 to EW-1, overflow EW-2…EW-9. Four process gas headers stay separate: dust, acid gas, H2, oil vapor. Life headers are a different rack.

Stack, AQS-BLD-001: L-2 catch tank, L-1 process basement, L0 manufacturing and dock and fab, L1 habitation west and farm east, L2 roof tank. H2 riser has no traps. CRYO and ZnBr2 are not in a pit. Central core for egress and life utilities. Process risers in their own shaft. Roof tilt is latitude, equator face. Dallas default latitude 33°.

## Do not repeat these mistakes

C29_RECYCLE is the pipe from the PROD_C29 cut tank to the induction pyrolyzer inlet. It is already a cylinder in models/freecad/oil_line.py. It is not the wet line. It does not go to leach. It does not go to the afterburner. NCG does not ride it.

CONTINUOUS_RECYCLE is the continuous-line liquor return to LEACH CSTR 1. It is not the heat ladder.

There are no aliases. Do not rename one unit onto another.

The heat ladder names are Q1_A, Q1_B, Q2_A, Q2_B, Q3_A, Q3_B, Q4_A, Q4_B. Q4_BUS is the Q4 water header only.

## Heat ladder

Document: docs-authority/HEAT_LADDER.md.

Two ladders, A west of the core and B east. Tiers Q1 flue, Q2 glass/pyro jacket, Q3 HX-8 residual, Q4 life water under 80 °C. Batteries at each tier. Q1 is oil or ceramic, not a water tank. Q4 is the only tier that may feed Q4_BUS. GEO_HP is the only lift, and it feeds Q4 and the farm, not back up the ladder. Failed sensor fails the valve shut. Deadband 5 K.

## Studies

On main. docs-authority/MVS_STUDY_CLOSE.md.

Blow mold is a Precious Plastic class add-on, 0.5 kW average. A 14–28 kW industrial molder is refused. Electrode coat is carbon or nickel, 0.3 kW; DSA deferred. Litho is 1–5 starts/day, about 2 mL resist per 150 mm wafer. Solar cell train is texture, emitter, PSG etch, ARC, contact, fire, IV, 1 kW study share, not the yard array. VCD is MVC: at 340 K and 8 K lift, w = λΔT/T = 15.7 kWh/m³. Remineralizer is 10 L/d, 0.25 g Ca/d and 0.10 g Mg/d from the hydroxide crib. ORC pad is reserved; output is zero if Q4 takes the heat.

## FreeCAD

docs-authority/FREECAD_PROTOCOL.md on the repo still locks /usr/bin/freecadcmd 1.0 and tells the operator not to use the 1.1 AppImage. The 1.1 failure on this project was the Ribbon addon, not a Part API break. Published 1.1 notes have empty Removed/Changed/New Python API sections. PartDesign origin datums do not round-trip to 1.0. Plant builders use Part::Feature.

models/freecad/freecad_host.py accepts 1.0.x or 1.1.x and refuses a 1.1 install whose Ribbon addon is missing RibbonStructure.json. This sandbox has neither binary.

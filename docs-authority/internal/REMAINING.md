Date: 2026-10-10

Lathe gearbox. Nine speeds, 60 to 1500 rpm, ratio 25. Three belts and a two-speed motor cover it. No gear cutting on the first lathe. Belts are the copy path.

Anode. Titanium mesh, 1.5 mm. PbO₂ coat 0.5 mm. Total about 5 mm. Current density stays at 200 A/m², under the 500 A/m² commercial limit.

Furnace capacitor. Coil is 15 turns, 200 mm diameter, 250 mm long. L = μ0 N² A / l = 35.5 µH. At 10 kHz, C = 1/(4π²f²L) = 7.1 µF. Water-cooled, across the coil.

Device. One diode. 100 µm window. Anode and cathode pads 1 mm. That is the first device the bay makes. A transistor is the same flow with a second implant. No layout beyond this.

# One-line

Array, 400 kW peak, to the curtailment contactor. Contactor to the 60 kW inverter. Inverter to the bus. Zinc-bromine battery on the bus, not above 30% duty. Electrowinning rectifiers on the bus: EW-1 2105 A, EW-2 2106 A, EW-7 2501 A, EW-9 2157 A, the rest under 200 A. Protection is the inverter breaker and a fuse on each rectifier. Conductor from the array is sized to 400 kW at the bus voltage. Shop, not stamped.

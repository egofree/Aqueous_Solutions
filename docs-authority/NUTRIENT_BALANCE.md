# Nutrient balance by stage

Document number: AQS-DUTY-034
Revision: A

Authority: graph Rev AH. Study. Not a PE stamp. Milligrams per day.

Feed is larvae 0.40, duckweed 0.50, azolla 0.10 kg/d wet. Fish retain 30 % of nitrogen and 20 % of the other elements, 15 % of potassium. The biofilter oxidizes excreted nitrogen to nitrate. Uptake is the named crops plus duckweed growth.

Iron is long on a total basis and short on a soluble basis. At pH 6.8–7.2 it precipitates. The makeup is iron from the electrowinning aisle, dissolved in bipolar-membrane hydrochloric acid, and held as ferric citrate. The citrate is Aspergillus on a sugar slip. Ethylenediaminetetraacetic acid is not made. Compost humic from the mycelial stage is the second ligand. No chelate is bought.

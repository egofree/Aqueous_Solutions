## 1. Purpose and scope

This list gives the manufacturing shop and the small production units beside it, the shop machine tools, the streams that join them to the rest of the plant, and their mass balance. The metal-forming units are in AQS-EQP-MFG-MET-001. The polymer units that make pellet, resin, film and stock are in the polymers line, `docs-authority/sizing/POLYMERS_LINE.md`.

## 2. Terms

- Average load: the continuous electrical load of a unit on the graph (`continuous_kw`), equal to its daily energy over 24 h.
- Connected load: the sum of the nameplates of the tools.
- Coincident peak: the load when the tools that run together are all on, from AQS-MFG-010.
